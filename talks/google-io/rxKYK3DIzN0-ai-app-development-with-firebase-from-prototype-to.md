## Description

Explore the power of Firebase for building AI-driven applications. We'll demonstrate how to securely integrate Google's cutting-edge AI models, Gemini and Imagen, directly and securely into your mobile apps with the Vertex AI in Firebase SDK. For backend integrations and complex data access, we'll look at Genkit. Lastly, we'll cover best practices for deploying and monitoring your AI-powered applications on Firebase and Google Cloud, leveraging App Check and AI Monitoring.

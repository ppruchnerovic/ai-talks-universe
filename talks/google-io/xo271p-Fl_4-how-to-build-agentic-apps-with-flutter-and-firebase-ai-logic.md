## Description

Multimodal AI is transforming how users interact with applications. Join us for an exploration of how to build secure, scalable, and intelligent multiplatform and multimodal AI apps using Flutter, Firebase, and Gemini. We'll deconstruct the architecture of a cutting-edge app, demonstrating how to seamlessly integrate voice, image, and text interactions into your web and mobile experiences using a single codebase. We'll also share how to create engaging user experiences powered by the latest advancements in AI, backed by Google Cloud.

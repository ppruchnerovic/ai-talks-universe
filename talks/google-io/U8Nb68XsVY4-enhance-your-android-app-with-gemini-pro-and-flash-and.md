## Description

Learn about the new capabilities of Gemini 2.0 and Imagen 3 through Vertex AI in Firebase for multimodal text and image generation. Discover how to create more dynamic and personalized app experiences, and make your app more successful by increasing user engagement. We’ll also review how some Android apps are already successfully using these tools in production.

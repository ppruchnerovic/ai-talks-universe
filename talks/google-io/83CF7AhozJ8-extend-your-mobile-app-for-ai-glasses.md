## Description

Discover the workflow for building experiences for AI Glasses. Explore core architectural strategies for expanding existing apps and designing logic for glasses, with and without displays. Learn what’s new in Jetpack Compose Glimmer to build beautiful user interfaces and the latest in Android Studio's inner loop, covering tooling setup and how to validate your app without physical hardware.

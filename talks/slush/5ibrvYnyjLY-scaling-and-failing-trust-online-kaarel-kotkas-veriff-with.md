## Description

In a digital world built on data and identity, trust is everything. Kaarel Kotkas, founder of Veriff, and Ott Kaukver, early advisor and now board member, share the story of building (and failing) trust online. From what “good” looks like in digital identity to how founders should think about their teams and boards, they offer hard-earned insights on scaling responsibly and keeping human judgment at the center as AI reshapes the landscape of trust.

## Description

How does a deep passion for research evolve from academia into pursuing a commercial application? Lourdes Agapito, co-founder of Synthesia, has spent years researching computer vision. Alongside Reshma Sohoni, founding partner of Seedcamp, she’ll share her journey of transforming research into a commercially viable product, from the moment she and her co-founders came together as a team of scientists, to the long process of finding product-market fit.

This fireside chat between Lourdes and Reshma dives into the relentless tinkering it takes to make a complex idea feasible, the guardrails necessary to keep new applications of technology secure and responsible, and what the future holds for computer vision research.

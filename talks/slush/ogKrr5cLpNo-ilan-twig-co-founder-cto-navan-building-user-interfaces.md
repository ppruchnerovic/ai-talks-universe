## Description

Building a user interface for a startup is more than just aesthetics; it's about creating an intuitive and engaging experience. Leveraging AI can streamline this process, making interfaces more responsive and adaptive to user needs.

Ilan Twig, co-founder and CTO of Navan, brings nearly 20 years of engineering expertise to the table. He has a track record of successful ventures, including StreamOnce, a multimedia integration platform acquired by Jive Software. At Navan, Ilan leads product development, drawing on his experience at Hewlett-Packard and Rockmelt to drive innovation in user interface design.

## Description

Building a company comes at a cost. Often, that cost is invisible. Founders are expected to lead with confidence while facing constant uncertainty. They carry the pressure to stay strong even when things feel like they are falling apart.

In this candid keynote, Annu Nieminen, Co-founder & CEO at Upright, shares honest reflections from her own founder journey. She will explore how to handle the emotional weight of leading, how to build habits that stick, and how to stay grounded while pursuing big goals.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Founder ≠ generic leader: you embody the company and carry unique psychological load
- Expect loneliness as you scale; actively seek founder peers—even competitors
- Build rejection resilience: systematize handling “no’s” to protect energy
- Practical sanity hacks: embrace non-competitive activities, reduce over-tracking, show vulnerability, keep perspective (e.g., parenting)
- Pay yourself a solid, sustainable salary to avoid unnecessary stress

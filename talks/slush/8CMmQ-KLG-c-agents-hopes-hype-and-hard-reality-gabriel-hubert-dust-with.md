## Description

When building a company, it’s easy to fall in love with the tech and forget what you’re building it for. Gabriel Hubert, founder of Dust, joins Julien Bek from Sequoia to reflect on the lessons learned the hard way, from his early Totems days to leading in the new AI era with Dust. Together, they explore how AI agents are reshaping company-building, why it’s still harder than ever to build a VC-backed business, and what the rise of agents means for the future of work. A candid conversation on hype versus reality, they’ll share their takes on what’s next in the industry.

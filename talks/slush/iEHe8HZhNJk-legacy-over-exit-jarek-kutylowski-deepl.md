## Description

Most founders are taught to move fast, raise fast, and exit **fast. Especially building in AI, the dominant narrative is: ship a cool demo, land press, and get acquired. But what if that mindset caps your impact before it starts?

Jarek Kutylowski built DeepL not for an exit, but for enduring product value. A business designed to outlast trends. In this keynote, he’ll challenge the quick-flip culture of startup building and make a case for building a legacy in the AI era.

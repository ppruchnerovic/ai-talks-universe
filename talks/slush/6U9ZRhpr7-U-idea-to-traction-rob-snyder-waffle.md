## Description

Most founders overcomplicate the first step. Rob Snyder, Founder of Waffle, has spent years cutting through noise to help startups go from zero to traction faster. In this keynote, he breaks down a simple but powerful process for validating ideas, talking to the right people, and knowing when to pivot or push forward.

If you’re stuck at the starting line or unsure what “traction” really looks like, this talk is your shortcut. Rob brings sharp insights, real stories, and practical tactics every early-stage founder can apply today.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Prioritize firsthand experience over interviews to find real startup ideas; avoid “LARPing.”
- Bet on demand: target customers with an urgent, blocked to-do—where it’d be “weird not to buy.”
- Use the Pull Framework: urgent project, poor current options, clear fit, and buy without persuasion.
- Run sales calls to uncover pull: ask what they’re prioritizing, why now, what they’ve tried, and gaps.
- Build one repeatable case study; iterate recordings/notes to improve fit and scale by repeating it.

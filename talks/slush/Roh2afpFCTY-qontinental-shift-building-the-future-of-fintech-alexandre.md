## Description

From startup frustration to Europe’s fastest-growing business payments solution. In this fireside chat, Qonto CEO Alexandre Prot joins Axios’ Lucinda Shen to unpack what it takes to build trust and scale in one of the world’s most regulated industries. They’ll dive into Qonto’s shift from hypergrowth to profitability, the move toward a full banking license, and how AI is reshaping the fight against financial fraud.

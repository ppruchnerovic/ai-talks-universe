## Description

Some of the most ambitious startups start in silence. Franz Faerber, Co-CEO and Co-Founder at Everest Systems, built in full stealth for years before announcing a major funding round in 2024. No press. No website. No noise.

In this session, he breaks down what building in stealth really looks like. Why he chose it. What it unlocked. What it cost. And how you can decide if it’s the right move for your startup.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Stealth mode fits complex, high-trust, low-tolerance products like ERP; execution over early noise
- Build deep infrastructure first; ensure a solid base before public launch
- Align investors and team for a long runway; mission-driven culture matters
- Do continuous customer discovery, but treat positive feedback cautiously; validate willingness to buy
- Use stealth temporarily; design clear milestones to test narrative and know when to go public

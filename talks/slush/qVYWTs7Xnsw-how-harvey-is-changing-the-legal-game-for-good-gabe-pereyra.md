## Description

What started as an email from a former AI research scientist and a former litigation attorney has quickly become one of the fastest-scaling companies in the AI space: three years later, Harvey serves 700+ customers across 58 countries, generating $100M in ARR and redefining what legal work looks like. In this conversation with Kleiner Perkins General Partner Ilya Fushman, co-founder and President Gabe Pereyra shares about how to make a product lawyers trust, what it takes to scale responsible AI in a highly regulated industry, and where Harvey is headed next.

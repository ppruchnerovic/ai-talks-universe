## Description

Most of today’s foundation models were trained on words. But human intelligence didn’t emerge from language—it came from interaction, play, and experiences in the real world. This conversation explores a new paradigm for building artificial general intelligence, one grounded in worlds and actions. Moderated by Moritz Baier-Lentz (Lightspeed Venture Partners), John Hanke (founder & CEO, Niantic Spatial) and Pim de Witte (CEO, General Intuition) discuss on the Founder Stage how games, spatial computing, and embodied simulation might be the missing link toward truly general AI.

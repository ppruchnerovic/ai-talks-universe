## Description

Expanding into new markets tests everything. Your culture. Your operations. Your speed. What worked in one country might fail in another. This session shows how to scale across regions while staying focused and in control.

Marina Farthouat, Chief People Officer and Chief of Staff at Oyster, draws from deep experience scaling global, remote-first teams at companies like ClickHouse and Elastic. She’ll share practical lessons on building resilient organizations across time zones, and aligning people, process, and product as you grow globally.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Build a global operating system early: clear meeting cadences, async/sync rules, and the right tooling (Slack, Notion, Loom) to drive accountability and speed.
- Prioritize culture as strategy: define behaviors to do and not do; invest in trust, recognition, and celebrating wins across borders.
- Choose a geo-based compensation strategy for fairness, transparency, and sustainable costs; communicate it early to avoid later friction.
- Design teams by function and time zones: distribute engineering; localize go-to-market; ensure overlap hours and avoid spanning the entire globe too soon.
- Incorporate local expertise and compliance support (e.g., Oyster) to reduce risk when hiring in new markets and to focus founders on growth.

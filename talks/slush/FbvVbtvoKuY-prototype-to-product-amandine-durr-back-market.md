## Description

Getting to product-market fit starts with your first version. But what makes a prototype into a viable product? This session dives into how to iterate effectively, ship, and grow your business through your product from day one.

Amandine Durr, CPO at Back Market, has built products from the ground up at Dailymotion, ManoMano and AOL. From co-founding her own startup, We Are TV, to launching new lines at one of Europe’s biggest marketplaces, she shares practical lessons on turning early versions into real traction.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Product = business: tie every feature to measurable business impact and clear success metrics
- Use a rigorous hypothesis process: opportunity summary, supporting signals, opportunity sizing, and definition of success (input/output/safeguard)
- Ship MVPs to learn fast: limit scope (e.g., single market, caps) to validate hypotheses before scaling
- Embrace polarity: openly manage trade-offs (e.g., growth vs. fraud) to build decision-making and accountability
- Differentiate and narrate: double down on unique, proven features (e.g., Express Replacement) and market them with a memorable promise (“not a day without a device”)

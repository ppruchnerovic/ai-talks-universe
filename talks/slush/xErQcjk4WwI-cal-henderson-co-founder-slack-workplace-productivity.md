## Description

As the Co-founder of one of the world’s top productivity tools in Slack, Cal Henderson knows a thing or two about efficiency and output. In this conversation with Cate Lawrence of Tech.eu, Cal shares his strategies for founders to sustain their own productivity through the marathon of building a startup. He’ll also offer advice on how founders can empower their high-growth teams to achieve peak productivity, drawing on his experience of scaling Slack.

## Description

Balancing product and go-to-market is one of the hardest challenges in company building. Get the timing wrong and the product might break or the market might slip away. This fireside offers rare insight into how two of the world’s fastest-moving companies approach this exact tension.

Stanley Tang, Co-Founder and Head of DoorDash Labs at DoorDash, and Niilo Säämänen, Chief Technology Officer at Wolt, share how they continue to launch ambitious new technologies while executing at scale. Using autonomy and robotics as a case study, they break down how to test bold ideas, structure small teams inside large companies, and build real feedback loops between product and GTM.

AI-generated key takeaways
- Start with the use case: define the problem first, then choose tech; avoid shiny-object R&D.
- Constrain to innovate: small, budget-limited “Labs” teams earn funding by staged proofs.
- Build systems, not silos: autonomy requires end-to-end ops (dispatch, charging, pickup/drop-off, partners).
- Ship to learn: prioritize commercialization and real-world contact to avoid “R&D hell.”
- Serendipitous wins: drone weight constraints led to Smart Scales, halving missing-item rates and aiding all deliveries.

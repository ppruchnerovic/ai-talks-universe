## Description

Scaling a startup in the age of AI requires not just technical know-how but a strategic vision to leverage AI's potential. This talk will cover real-world examples of how startups can harness AI to optimize operations, enhance customer experiences, and drive growth.

Jamie Neuwirth, Head of Startups at Anthropic, brings almost a decade of scaling experience from his time at Stripe, where he played a key role in building payments infrastructure for startups and enterprises. His background also includes work at Google Education and a start in education as a 7th-grade math teacher, giving him a unique perspective on scaling businesses with a focus on real impact.

## Description

Building strong investor relations starts well before any funding round. Early engagement, research, and relationship-building with investors are crucial, as they lay the groundwork for a lasting partnership. Effective investor relations can unlock valuable resources, from recruitment support to customer connections and future funding opportunities. This makes it a key skill for any entrepreneur.

Pia, co-founder of New Wave, has firsthand experience in this area, leading investments in successful startups like Sweep, BeReal, and Mistral. Prior to New Wave, she invested in companies such as Doctolib and Shift while at Accel and Stride.

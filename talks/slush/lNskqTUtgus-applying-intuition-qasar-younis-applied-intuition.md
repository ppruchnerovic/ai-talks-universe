## Description

Qasar Younis is the Co-Founder and CEO of Applied Intuition, an AI company that builds self-driving software for vehicles from sea to space—redefining what is possible in real-world intelligence. He began his career as an automotive engineer at General Motors and Bosch, before founding a startup that was acquired by Google and later helping to run Y Combinator, all experiences that give him unique insights into pioneering today’s physical AI. In this fireside chat with Iain Martin (Forbes), Qasar will reflect on what building three very different companies taught him, how he’s applying those lessons to scale Applied Intuition into a $15 billion intelligence powerhouse, and what he envisions for real-world autonomy going forward.

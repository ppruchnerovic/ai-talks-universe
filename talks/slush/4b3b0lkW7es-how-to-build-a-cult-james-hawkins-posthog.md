## Description

early-stage founders don’t need a massive budget to build a brand. this session explores how community-led growth, building in public, and founder personality can drive breakout attention from the first handful of users through to unicorn and beyond.

james hawkins, co-ceo at posthog, has turned a developer tool into a beloved brand with 245,000 customers and a hedgehog at its heart. he shares what works, what doesn’t, and why even serious software should be a little fun.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Community is the moat: in commoditized software, loyal users drive early adoption, feedback, and word-of-mouth growth
- Ship fast, iterate faster: launch before perfect; a forgiving community enables rapid improvement to parity and beyond
- Stand out or get lost: with AI lowering build barriers, brand, personality, and distinct choices across all touchpoints matter
- Be long-term greedy: prioritize user trust (transparent pricing, generous refunds, helpful UX) over short-term extraction
- Go ultra-specific: define a hyper-narrow ICP/persona and build a remarkable experience that may alienate non-target users

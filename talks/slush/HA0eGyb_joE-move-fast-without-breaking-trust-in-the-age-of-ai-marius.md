## Description

As AI systems rapidly gain capability, founders face the challenge on building safely with AI. In this panel discussion moderated by Seth Bannon (Founding Partner of Fifty Years), Dianne Na Penn (Head of Research, Product Management at Anthropic) and Marius Hobbhahn (Founder & CEO of Apollo Research) explore what founders need to understand about AI safety, reliability, and trust as their products evolve. They’ll discuss lessons from working on the frontier and what it means for startups operating in a world where AI autonomy is accelerating.

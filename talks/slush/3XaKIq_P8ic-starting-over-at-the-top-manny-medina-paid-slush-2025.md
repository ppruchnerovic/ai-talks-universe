## Description

Whilst most founders stop at a unicorn exit, Manny Medina went back to building. Manny was employee #3 at AWS before he scaled Outreach to a successful $4.4B exit and has now raised over $30M with Paid, from top investors to solve a challenge traditional SaaS can’t: how to price, bill, and optimize autonomous AI agents.  Manny will sit down with Alex Schmitt, Partner at Lightspeed Ventures to go through his journey from operator, to unicorn, to repeat founder and more importantly the hard lessons he’s learned and applying at Paid.

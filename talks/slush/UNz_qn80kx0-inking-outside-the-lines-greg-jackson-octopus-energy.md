## Description

Greg Jackson, founder and CEO of Octopus Energy Group, has spent the past decade proving that technology can make the green transition better for customers and the planet. In conversation with Charlotte Jee (MIT Technology Review), he shares how Octopus grew from a challenger startup into a global energy tech leader by breaking industry conventions rather than bending to them. Expect insights on building trust in a regulated landscape, turning AI and digital tools into real affordability, and leading with culture instead of control—all lessons for founders looking to change the rules in their own industries.

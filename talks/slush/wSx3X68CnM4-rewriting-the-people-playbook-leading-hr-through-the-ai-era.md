## Description

It’s hard sometimes to remember the time when AI wasn’t in the spotlight and dominating headlines, but how can companies move beyond the hype and ensure that AI actually delivers for their business? Recently celebrating its 10th anniversary, intelligent HR platform Personio is now focused on how AI can empower its teams internally, as well as deliver meaningful results for its customers. In this Fireside Chat, Hanno Renner and Harry Nelis will share insights into how organizations should be rethinking team structures in the AI era, and how HR professionals are increasingly using AI to support strategic objectives and free them up to work on things that have maximum impact. Hanno will also talk about what the next ten years might have in store for Personio, and how the roadmap for success will look very different from the first 10 years.

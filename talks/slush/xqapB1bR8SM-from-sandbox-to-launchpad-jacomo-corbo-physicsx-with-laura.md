## Description

AI that understands physics sounds like science fiction—but for Jacomo Corbo (Co-founder & CEO of PhysicsX) and his team, it’s a blueprint for the future of industry. In this conversation with Laura Connell (Partner at Atomico), Jacomo shares what it takes to bridge deep research and real‑world engineering, push boundaries in conservative sectors like aerospace and energy, and build products that earn trust from expert users by proving results. Expect insights for founders navigating industries where transformation is slow, skepticism runs high, and success means translating breakthrough science into tools engineers actually believe in.

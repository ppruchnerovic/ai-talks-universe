## Description

Building a startup isn’t just about having an idea—it's about choosing the right problem to solve. In this talk, we'll explore how to identify and validate a problem that resonates with a large customer base and attracts VC funding, saving time and resources.

Daniel Khachab, co-founder and CEO of Choco, will share his journey in creating the world’s first food supply chain unicorn. Choco, with 400 employees in seven countries and €300 million raised, is revolutionizing the food industry through AI-driven solutions that reduce food waste. Daniel’s insights will provide a valuable roadmap for early-stage founders.

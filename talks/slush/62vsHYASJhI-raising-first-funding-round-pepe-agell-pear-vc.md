## Description

Your first funding round can set the tone for your entire company. But most founders only go through it once, and the stakes couldn’t be higher.

In this keynote, Pepe Agell, Partner at Pear VC and former leader of Chartboost (backed by Sequoia, acquired by Zynga in 2021) shares advice from both sides of the table. From choosing the right investor, pitching when you have nothing built to how to stay in control with terms and dilation, this talk is packed with practical tactics every founder needs before hitting send on that first pitch deck.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Traction outweighs everything in early fundraising; show real customer demand
- Use bottoms-up market sizing (customers × revenue per customer), not just top-down TAM
- Seed investors look for massive markets (~$40B+) or a clear path to expand into them
- Pre-seed is about team, not traction; wrong investor if they demand metrics at this stage
- VCs assess teams on execution, customer/market insight, and character over multiple touchpoints

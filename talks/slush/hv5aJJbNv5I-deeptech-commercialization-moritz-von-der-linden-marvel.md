## Description

Building in deep tech means solving hard science and even harder go-to-market. From lab to launch, every decision is high stakes, long cycle, and difficult to explain.

In this keynote, Moritz von der Linden, Co-founder and CEO of Marvel Fusion, shares the real playbook for turning breakthrough science into a business. He will cover how to balance scientific ambition with commercial discipline, build investor-ready proof points, and prepare for industrialization and regulation from day one.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Validate commercial fit early; avoid “better experiments” that never reach paying customers
- Diversify funding (public + private) to de-risk massive capex; engage policymakers early
- Define sharp milestones; focus on critical-path problems and sequence the rest
- Commercialize subsystems early (e.g., laser tech) to generate revenue and prove scalability
- Build strategic partnerships for go-to-market and complex components; incumbents will resist, so persist

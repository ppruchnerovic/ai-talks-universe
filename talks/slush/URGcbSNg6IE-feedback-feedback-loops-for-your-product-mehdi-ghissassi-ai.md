## Description

Effective feedback loops are crucial to refining and scaling any product. This talk will focus on building strong feedback systems that improve product quality, responsiveness, and user engagement. Mehdi Ghissassi, CPO at AI 71, brings extensive experience in creating AI products based on pioneering research. Having previously led product strategy at DeepMind and Google Brain, Mehdi will share insights on establishing feedback channels that drive continuous improvement and innovation in product development.

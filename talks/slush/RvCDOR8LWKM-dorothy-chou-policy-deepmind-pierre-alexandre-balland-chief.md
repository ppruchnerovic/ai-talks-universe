## Description

As AI development accelerates globally, Europe is carving its own path while trying to catch up with the US and China. In this discussion, Dorothy Chou from Google DeepMind, Pierre-Alexandre Balland from CEPS, and Melissa Heikkilä from MIT Tech Review discuss Europe’s AI policy. The panel explores how Europe can improve its competitive edge, from the potential trade-offs of focusing on large language models to leveraging Europe’s immense tech talent pool and rethinking success metrics for AI startups. The panel will also discuss ways to inject more investment into the European AI ecosystem, fostering a future where policy and progress align.

## Description

Declan Jones is the co-founder and Chief Data Officer of Prolific Machines, a biotechnology startup harnessing light to grow and control cells with unprecedented control. In this conversation with Adam Bluestein from Fast Company, Declan delves into go-to-market strategies for groundbreaking technologies and scientific innovations, focusing on how machine learning is accelerating advancements in biology. Declan and Adam will also explore leveraging reality-based AI to drive paradigm shifts in bioprocessing, offering new efficiencies and collaborative potential with regulatory bodies.

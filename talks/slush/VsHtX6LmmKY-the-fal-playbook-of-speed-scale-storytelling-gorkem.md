## Description

In this conversation, Görkem Yurtseven, cofounder of fal, and Jennifer Li, General Partner at a16z, will cover navigating the evolving landscape of generative AI. They’ll unpack fal’s founder journey from early creative experiments toward enterprise utility, and the lessons learned along the way. Expect insights on finding product market fit, scaling through rapid growth, and redefining go-to-market in a new and fast moving market. This is a story of adaptation, vision, and building for the next wave of generative media.

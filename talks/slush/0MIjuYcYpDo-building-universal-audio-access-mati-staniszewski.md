## Description

Mati Staniszewski’s childhood movie nights inspired his groundbreaking AI voice platform, @Elevenlabs. He is joined by Bryan Kim of @a16z  to share insights into founding and funding an AI application company, the power of combining product and technical skills, and the importance of building with integrity. Moderated by Bloomberg’s Tom Mackenzie, this stage talk will also feature bold takes on industry trends, from the rise of multimodal AI to tackling the dominance of foundational models. This is a must-attend for those looking to build applications that bring AI to the mainstream.

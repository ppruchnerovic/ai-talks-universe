## Description

Building a startup today comes with no shortage of hype, especially in AI. For founders tackling the ambitious trio of hardware, software, and AI, the stakes, and the pitfalls, are high. How can you create commercially viable technology that brings these complex elements together seamlessly?

Andy Lonsberry, Co-founder & CEO of Path Robotics, has his fair share of firsthand experience in building integrated tech. In this fireside chat with Wen Hsieh from Matter Venture Partners, Andy will share his insights on developing hardware, software, and AI into a single, resilient product. Expect hard-won advice on avoiding common founder missteps, choosing your investors wisely, and building beyond the boom to create technology that stands the test of time.

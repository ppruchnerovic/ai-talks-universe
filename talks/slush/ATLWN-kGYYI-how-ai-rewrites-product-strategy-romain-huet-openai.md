## Description

AI is changing how products are built and how teams operate. Speed is no longer the hard part. The real challenge is knowing what to focus on and when to ship.

In this keynote, Romain Huet, Head of Developer Experience at OpenAI, shares how AI is shifting how early-stage companies approach product strategy. He breaks down new ways of working, common mistakes founders make, and how prioritization and decision-making evolve when building becomes easier than ever.

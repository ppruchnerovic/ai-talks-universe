## Description

AI is everywhere—exciting, accelerating, and impossible to ignore. But amid the hype, how much of the technology is actually helping us work smarter and faster? Meredith Whittaker (Signal) and Peter Sarlin (Silo AI) take a step back to ask what’s being built and at what cost. From automation and productivity myths to the ethical and societal trade-offs of deploying AI at scale, they unpack how founders, researchers, and operators can keep their footing in a field racing ahead.

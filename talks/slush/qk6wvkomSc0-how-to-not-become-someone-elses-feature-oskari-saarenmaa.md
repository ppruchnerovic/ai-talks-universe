## Description

The worst nightmare for any founder is to spend years perfecting a product that becomes someone else’s feature. In this talk, Oskari Saarenmaa, co-founder & CEO of Aiven, Shiv Ramji, President at Okta, and Eric Liaw, General Partner at IVP, will discuss how to create defensible value beyond technology, building moats in an era of AI and open APIs, and the lessons learned from near-misses in ensuring product longevity.

They’ll explore how founders can identify the right problems to solve, the patterns that cause products to get absorbed into larger ecosystems, and whether solving domain-specific versus broad challenges influences long-term success.

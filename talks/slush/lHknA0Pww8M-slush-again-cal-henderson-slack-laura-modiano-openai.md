## Description

In this Fireside Chat OpenAI’s Laura Modiano and Slack co-founder and CTO Cal Henderson explore how AI is reshaping the way we build, and the kind of founders the next decade will belong to. From faster feedback and new collaboration models to Cal’s return to founding and Laura’s view on emerging tools, they discuss what’s changed, what still matters, and what they hope to see from the next wave of builders on the Slush stage.

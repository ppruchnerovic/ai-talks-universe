## Description

Building customer love requires finding the balance between a clear vision and the chaos of daily execution. It’s about creating products that become indispensable to a community.

Erica Anderson, Chief Revenue Officer at Notion, took on this challenge when she transitioned from a stable role at GitHub to a fast-paced startup. With over 20 years of go-to-market experience, she now guides Notion's growth strategy, drawing on her success scaling AI products at GitHub to $1 billion+ in ARR.

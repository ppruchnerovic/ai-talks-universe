## Description

Culture can be your greatest asset or your biggest risk as you grow. Most startups don’t lose their edge because of product. They lose it because they let culture drift.

Jacob Wallenberg helped scale Ramp from 100 to 1,200 people while leading the People team. Now as VP of International, he shares what it takes to protect culture while scaling fast. From onboarding to team habits to early warning signs, this session teaches how to keep culture strong as your company grows.

- Founders should stay deeply involved in recruiting to set the talent bar, sell vision, and place high-upside bets.
- Start new hires with smaller scope/titles to filter for motivation and boost early success odds.
- Use promotions deliberately to signal and reinforce desired behaviors across the org.
- Avoid legacy promotion criteria (team size/tenure) that incentivize bloat and waiting.
- Run toward fires quickly—give feedback, adjust roles, or act—out of fairness to the individual and the team

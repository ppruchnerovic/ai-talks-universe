## Description

Picture this: a humble app that starts as a photo background remover grows to become the leading photo editing app in the market with 200 million users. It then leverages its large audience to train its own foundation model and serve the biggest brands in the world. That is what Matthieu Rouif did with Photoroom, and he will share all his key insights from this journey. David Thevenon of Balderton will moderate this conversation that is a must-watch for builders aspiring to create the next global Generative AI application. The two will discuss everything from product development to monetization to market positioning in the age of AI.

## Description

Raising a Series A is a crucial milestone for startups, demanding a proven track record and a clear vision for scaling. This stage is about convincing investors of long-term growth potential and the capability to execute on a larger scale.

Lisa Smith, co-founder of Prewave, transitioned from PhD research into entrepreneurship, driven by her commitment to sustainability and human rights. Under her leadership, Prewave has become a leading AI-driven supply chain risk management platform, used by major companies like VW, Toyota, and Lufthansa to ensure transparency and mitigate risks.

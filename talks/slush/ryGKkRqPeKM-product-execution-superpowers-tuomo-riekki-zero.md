## Description

Startups don’t fail from lack of ideas. They fail from building the wrong ones too slowly. This session is about sharpening your product focus and moving with purpose.

Tuomo Riekki, founder of Zero and former CTO of Smartly.io, shares how his team prioritizes what to build, cuts bottlenecks across design and engineering, and executes with speed and clarity. From choosing the right customer signals to structuring teams for fast iteration, he unpacks what disciplined product execution really looks like in the earliest stages.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Embed engineers with customers; ship fast, learn via real usage (feature-gated alphas, live demos, minimal Figma)
- Build with reusable “Lego” components and strong data abstractions to enable week-scale feature delivery
- Balance speed and quality intentionally; cut corners early but fix fast based on customer signal
- Win one perfect ICP customer first; over-serve them to create champions and organic sales
- AI makes CRM automation/UX overhaul timely; incumbents are slow—delight with automation and modern UX

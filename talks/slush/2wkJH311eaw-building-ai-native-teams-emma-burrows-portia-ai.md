## Description

AI is changing how products are built, and so should the teams building them. This session breaks down what AI-native teams look like. What roles you need. How workflows shift. And how to hire for what’s coming next.

Emma Burrows is the Co-founder & CTO of Portia AI and a former engineering leader at Google and Stripe. She’s rethinking how teams should operate in the AI era. With deep experience across infrastructure, product, and leadership, Emma brings hard-earned insight into what it takes to hire, structure, and scale truly AI-native teams.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- AI-native teams change workflows, roles, and shipping to let agents handle large chunks of work, not just tab-completion.
- Shift from line-by-line coding to architecture-level “vibe engineering”: specify the what and the how; agents draft, humans guide and review at the end.
- CLI-based coding agents correlate with deeper AI-native practices than editor-first tools like Cursor.
- Work in thin vertical slices: prompt → agent draft → test/QA → minimal review → iterate.
- Maximize parallelization: keep agents busy and write the next prompt while code is generating to preserve context and speed.

## Description

As Europe ushers in a new era for cybersecurity where protection is embedded in every device, Gianni Cuozzo (Co-founder & CEO of Exein) and Elena Moneta (Principal at Balderton) sit down to explore how regulation and rapid tech change are reshaping the landscape for founders. Together, they’ll share Exein’s journey: betting early on embedded AI security, navigating the demands of the EU’s Cyber Resilience Act, and embracing a global mindset from day one. Expect lessons on how startups can turn policy into a competitive advantage, why cultural identity matters for scaling deeptech, and how founders can spot and seize inflection points before the mainstream arrives.

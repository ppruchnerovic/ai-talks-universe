## Description

Rebuilding after a storm is one of the hardest things a founder can do. In this candid conversation, Peter Carlsson (Co-founder of Aris Machina, founder of Northvolt), Ali Parsa (Founder of Quadrivia AI, founder of Babylon Health) take the stage with Mimi Billing (Sifted) to discuss what it means to start again after tough lessons and hard-won experience. They reflect on what they’d do differently, what still matters no matter what, and how to make big decisions when pressure is high and resources are tight. With honesty and perspective, they explore how founders can face failure without fear and find the conviction to build again.

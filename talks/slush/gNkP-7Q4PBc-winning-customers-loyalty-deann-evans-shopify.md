## Description

In a world of infinite choice, what keeps customers coming back? This session breaks down how trust, loyalty, and community are becoming the new growth engine for startups.

Deann Evans, Managing Director, EMEA, at Shopify, draws on platform-wide insights and real merchant stories to show founders what it takes to win and retain customers in the AI-native, agentic era.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- AI reduces barriers to entrepreneurship; it’s a force multiplier that accelerates founders, not replaces them.
- To stand out in agentic commerce, show up first, authentically, memorably, and with relevance as AI agents shop for consumers.
- Loyalty is a growth engine: build community over customers; make loyalty experiential, personal, and data-driven—not just discounts.
- Unify commerce systems to avoid siloed experiences and cap on growth (Waterdrop case: global scale after unification).
- Use conversational commerce to collect zero-party data, blend automation with empathy, and deepen trust at scale.

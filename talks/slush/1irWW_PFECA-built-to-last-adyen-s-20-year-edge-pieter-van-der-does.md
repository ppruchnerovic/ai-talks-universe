## Description

After nearly two decades, Pieter van der Does has kept Adyen at the top of the fintech game: outlasting crises, embracing AI, and outperforming newer competitors along the way. In this fireside chat, moderated by Jason Karaian (The New York Times), Pieter will reveal Adyen's evolution from payments company to full-stack infrastructure platform, sharing the scaling philosophy that has sustained their dominance. From building loyalty through technology to knowing when to evolve versus chase trends, he'll explore what it takes to build a fintech giant that lasts—and what comes next for the industry.

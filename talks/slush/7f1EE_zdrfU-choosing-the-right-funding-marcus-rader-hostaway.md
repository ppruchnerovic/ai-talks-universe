## Description

The funding path you choose will shape everything. It affects your product, your speed, your team, and your outcome. But most founders do not truly understand the tradeoffs until they are deep into the journey. This session breaks down the real differences between bootstrapping, venture capital, and private equity.

Marcus Rader is the Founder and CEO of Hostaway. He bootstrapped the company from the ground up and scaled it into Europe’s leading short-term rental platform. In 2024, he closed a 350 million euro private equity round that made Hostaway the first unicorn in its space. Marcus shares what each funding path demands, what it enables, and how to choose the one that fits your company.

- Bootstrapping builds deep customer focus and culture but amplifies stress, risk aversion, and reduces real founder control
- VC funding provides capital but can distort culture; startup hype is often VC-driven marketing
- Private equity can de-risk founders and add real operational help (e.g., executive hiring, step-in support)
- Be selfish in funding choices: align capital with your vision and constraints; each path (bootstrap/VC/PE) has trade-offs and hidden incentives

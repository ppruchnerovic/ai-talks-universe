## Description

Knowing when to pivot can save your company. Most startups hit a point where the original idea no longer works. This session is for founders who want to spot the signs early, lead through change with clarity, and turn setbacks into momentum.

Rob Snyder, Founder of Waffle and a serial entrepreneur who has lived through hard pivots and tough tradeoffs. Rob currently deadlifts 365 pounds. He’ll share real stories and practical tools for recognizing signals, avoiding false ones, and making the move with your team, customers, and investors still on board.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Pivot = evolve toward demand; follow market pull, not internal ideas
- Validate interest with payment/implementation steps (“Stripe link tells the truth”)
- Batch customer learnings in sets of five sales calls; avoid chasing every new request
- Do sales-focused conversations; observe users at work instead of generic interviews
- Bad pivots come from avoiding real sales (e.g., chasing trends/virality) and blasting cold emails instead of having conversations

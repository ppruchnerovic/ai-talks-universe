## Description

As the field of generative AI grows, questions around the defensibility of application layers built on top of Large Language Models (LLMs) are gaining momentum. Some argue that such products are merely "wrappers" over models like OpenAI's.

Join this fireside chat with Lila Tretikov from NEA and Joel Hellermark from Sana to hear their thoughts on this debate. The duo will explore how emerging companies can position themselves beyond the base layer and what opportunities such companies have to shape user interfaces relative to giants. Joel will also share his top lessons from the journey of transitioning from a sales-led to a product-led growth strategy and scaling to over 100,000 workspaces in just six months.

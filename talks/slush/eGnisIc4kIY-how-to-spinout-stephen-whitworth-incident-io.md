## Description

What if the side project you’re building at work is actually your next company? This session is about the moment an employee becomes a founder and the steps that turn internal traction into a startup with real market pull.

Stephen Whitworth, Co-founder and CEO of [incident.io](http://incident.io/), shares how an internal tool at Monzo sparked 500 demo requests on launch day, how he built conviction to leave, and what it takes to turn early interest into paying customers. From finding real demand to pricing from day one, this is for anyone thinking about spinning out and starting up. This session is moderated by Anindo Mesbaul, Head of Growth at Slush.

AI-generated key takeaways
- Spun out incident.io from Monzo’s internal tooling with a gradual, low-risk side-hustle approach
- Co-founder recruitment was staged: build together → take sales calls → go full-time → form a company
- Massive early validation: 500 demo requests in one day post-launch
- Strong conviction on the problem from prior internal use; wrong assumptions on GTM and need for sales-led motion
- Risk tolerance shifts with age; favor validation-first over quit-and-build compared to earlier ventures

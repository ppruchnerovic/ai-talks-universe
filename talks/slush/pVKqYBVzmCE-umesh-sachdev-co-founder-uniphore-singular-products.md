## Description

Many founders dream of ‘suite’ success, but very few end up building one. Umesh Sachdev has built Uniphore, worth over $2B, from a singular AI-native customer service solution into a suite of products and has become the first end-to end AI company enabling Enterprise AI Cloud for the world’s biggest enterprises. Moderated by Scott Sandell, Executive Chairman and CIO of NEA, the speakers will give advice about building a generational platform company, which includes focusing on singular products in the early days, the importance of listening to customers, and unconventional means such as M&A as a tool for scaling in a rapidly changing tech environment.

## Description

Every startup wants growth. Few earn it through product alone. This session dives into what product-led growth really takes from building trust to designing onboarding that scales without sales.

Avery Pennarun, Co-founder & CEO of Tailscale, has built a breakout developer tool beloved for its simplicity and speed. With deep technical roots and multiple startup journeys behind him, Avery shares hard-earned lessons on letting product and not marketing do the talking.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Start with a tiny, talkative market you can win, then expand pin-by-pin.
- Product-market fit feels like pull; aim for automatic choice in a niche.
- Virality is real: nurture super-fans, fix “antibodies,” learn from neutrals.
- Use sales to surface friction and remove buying blockers, not to mask them.
- Stop chasing features; prioritize compliance and adoption blockers once people want it.

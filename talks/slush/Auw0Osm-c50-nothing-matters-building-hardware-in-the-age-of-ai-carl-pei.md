## Description

In a software-eaten world, Nothing co-founder and CEO Carl Pei is proving that hardware isn’t dead, it’s just getting started. Together with Tom Hulme (Google Ventures), Carl will explore how physical products, thoughtful design, and AI-driven interfaces are coming together to shape the next generation of consumer technology. This session will be showing us how to combine human-centered design with cutting-edge tech to build products that stand out, excite users, and compete with giants in the industry.

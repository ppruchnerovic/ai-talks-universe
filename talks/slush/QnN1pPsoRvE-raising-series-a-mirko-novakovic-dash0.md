## Description

Most founders underestimate what it really takes to raise a strong Series A. In 2025, the bar is higher, the market is tougher, and investor expectations have shifted.

Mirko Novakovic, CEO at Dash0 and former founder of Instana (sold to IBM), shares what it takes to break through. From building the right narrative to closing term sheets, this session delivers hard-earned lessons from raising, failing, and learning how to fundraise at the next level.

—

AI-generated key takeaways:
- Build a Minimal Lovable Product in red-ocean markets; you only get one shot to impress.
- Align GTM with pricing: ~$5K ACV = self-serve; $50K–$100K+ ACV supports sales-led.
- Growth trumps other metrics for fundraising; prioritize ARR growth and pipeline visibility.
- Fundraise by building relationships and sharing real, live data (no decks); create FOMO.
- Post-raise, scale fast and deliberately (hiring, US expansion) to set up the next round.

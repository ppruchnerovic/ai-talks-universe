## Description

Every great startup starts with a bet. But how do you know which idea is worth betting on?

In this keynote, Darren Mowry, Vice President of Global Startups at Google, breaks down what makes an idea truly strong and why most fail before they’re even built. He draws from patterns across thousands of startups to help you spot real signals, avoid common traps, and make sharper early decisions.

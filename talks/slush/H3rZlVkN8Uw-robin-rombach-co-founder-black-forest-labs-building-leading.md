## Description

As the continent faces challenges in keeping pace with global leaders in the development of AI, what does it take to build a generational technology champion out of Europe?

Robin Rombach, co-founder of Black Forest Labs, joins Alexandre Momeni from General Catalyst to explore how Europe’s approach to frontier AI is transforming generative media and setting new standards for enterprise AI adoption. With insights into open-source’s pivotal role and strategies for upskilling the workforce, they’ll discuss how European innovation is empowering businesses worldwide to harness AI’s transformative power.

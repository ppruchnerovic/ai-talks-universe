## Description

Everyone celebrates wins, but almost no one talks about setbacks. Still, every startup has to face the Valley of Death.

Francesco Simoneschi, Founder & CEO of TrueLayer, shares what it feels like when things break. He speaks candidly about how to keep leading when momentum fades and belief wavers. Together with Maxine Rior, Principal at Northzone, they unpack how to hold the company together, lead through chaos, and make the hardest calls when everything is on the line.

—

AI-generated key takeaways
- Market cycles demand toolset shifts: optimize for growth when capital is cheap; pivot to monetization and efficiency when it tightens
- Act early on warning signs: public markets lead; private funding lags—overreact slightly rather than wait
- Build a high-trust core: small, aligned leadership group to make tough calls and project unity
- Communicate transparently: over-articulate strategy, admit mistakes, and guide the team through gradual change
- Use adversity as fuel: counter negative narratives with bunker mentality, focus on controllables, and prove progress with results

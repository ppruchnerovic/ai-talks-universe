## Description

Navigating the go-to-market landscape is fraught with challenges, and founders often make key mistakes that can hinder their growth. This talk will address the top five common missteps—ranging from hiring practices to pipeline building and pricing strategies—and provide actionable insights on how to avoid them.

Maggie Hott brings over fourteen years of experience in scaling GTM teams across leading tech companies, including Eventbrite, Slack, Webflow, and @OpenAI. With a proven track record of driving hundreds of millions in revenue, she is recognized for her expertise in outbound strategies and product-led growth.

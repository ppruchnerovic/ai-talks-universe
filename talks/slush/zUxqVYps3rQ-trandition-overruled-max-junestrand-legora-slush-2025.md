## Description

Legora raised its seed round from Benchmark in March 2024 when it was 5 people. It went on to launch its product October of 2024. In the year since, Legora has signed 400+ enterprises and law firms in over 40 countries and is now one of the fastest growing AI software companies in the world. In this fireside chat, Max Junestrand, founder and CEO of Legora, joins Benchmark’s Chetan Puttagunta unpack how Legora spent nearly a year building product before launching and then scaled around the world quickly.

Key takeaways:

- Obsess over product readiness; you get one shot with users — delay launch until real users love it
- Build with customers, not in a vacuum; embed with practitioners and iterate beside them
- Compete on focused excellence; beat general-purpose tools by owning a single user and workflow end-to-end
- Pair great UX with service; ship user-friendly design plus hands-on onboarding and tailored workflows
- Maintain speed at scale; run teams like mini-startups, hire entrepreneurial leaders, and iterate with honest customer feedback

## Description

In an AI landscape flooded with players, standing out is a constant challenge. Commoditization looms, threatening to reduce innovation to a set of predictable features. How does a company build a competitive edge, create products with real value, and stay distinct in an industry racing to replicate itself?

May Habib, Co-founder & CEO of Writer, shares her approach to setting Writer apart in a crowded market. Joined by Darian Shirazi from Gradient Ventures, they’ll reveal how fostering an inventive culture, investing in brand differentiation, and attracting top-tier AI talent have been essential to Writer’s success.

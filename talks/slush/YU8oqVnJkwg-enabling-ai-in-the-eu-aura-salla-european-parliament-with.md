## Description

With Europe’s technological future at stake, this conversation brings together Aura Salla (European Parliament) and Magnus Hambleton (Partner at ByFounders), to discuss how sovereign AI, unique regulatory approaches, and strategic investments can help the EU to compete globally. They'll address Europe’s core strengths in AI, data governance, the impact of US hyperscalers, energy challenges, and how the region can change perceptions and assert its vision on the world stage.

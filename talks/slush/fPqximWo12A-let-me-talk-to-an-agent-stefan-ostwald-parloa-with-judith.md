## Description

Stefan Ostwald (Co-founder & CAIO of Parloa) and Judith Dada (General Partner at Visionaries Club) get practical on the leap from voice-only AI to a platform embraced across industries, and reveal how to create automated systems that actually feel human. They’ll map out the lessons from the frontlines: how do you pair forward-thinking product vision with the daily realities of customer feedback, and what does it take to build trust with users who just want problems solved, not more bots? This is a conversation about engineering empathy, navigating fast shifts in tech, and why the future of automation is as much about people as it is about code.

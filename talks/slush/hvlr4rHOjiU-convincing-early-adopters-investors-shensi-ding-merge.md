## Description

Getting started is the hardest part. How do you overcome the self-doubt, build momentum from zero, and find people who believe before there’s proof?

In this keynote, Shensi Ding, Co-founder & CEO of Merge, shares hard-won lessons on turning early skeptics into believers whether that means converting first customers or landing your first check.

## Description

Meredith Whittaker, President of Signal, envisions a new model of independent technology that prioritizes user privacy and security without conforming to corporate interests or surveillance capitalism.

In this conversation with Emma Hinchliffe, Meredith shares her vision for sustainable funding structures to support the development of private technology, and emphasizes the need to move away from the surveillance-based business models that dominate today's tech giants. Meredith advocates for democratic tech governance, restructured incentives, and ensuring that those most likely to be harmed by AI systems have a voice in their creation and application.

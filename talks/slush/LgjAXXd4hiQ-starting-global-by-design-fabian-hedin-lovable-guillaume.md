## Description

Global expansion used to take years. Now it can happen by accident or by design. Today’s most ambitious products reach users around the world from day one, but the decision to lean in is still one of the hardest early calls a founder makes.

In this fireside, Guillaume Princen (Head of EMEA at Anthropic) and Fabian Hedin (Co-founder of Lovable) unpack how global demand shaped their earliest product and GTM decisions. They’ll share how to recognize real pull, how to design for scale from day one, and how AI and PLG are changing the “when, where, and how” of going global.

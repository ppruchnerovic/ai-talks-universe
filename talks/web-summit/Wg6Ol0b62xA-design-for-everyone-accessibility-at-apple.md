## Description

For four decades, Apple has designed with disability in mind. Accessibility is a core value at Apple, infused into all of the company's products, services and operations. Join Apple’s global head of accessibility, Sarah Herrlinger, to hear about the innovations that have been driven by designing for users with disabilities, how AI and machine learning are powering new experiences for disabled users, and why Apple believes that accessibility is a human right.

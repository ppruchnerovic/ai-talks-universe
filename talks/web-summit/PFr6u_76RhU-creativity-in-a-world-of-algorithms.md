We sit down with Reza Izad, Wim Ponnet and Kit Eaton to talk about creativity in a modern tech world.

Studio71 and EndemolShineGroup manage some of the world's most valuable content, featuring everyone from Lily Singh and Dwayne Johnson to Mr Bean, Master Chef and Black Mirror. In this session they explain how, in a world where content is both ubiquitous and precious, beyond the click bait, there is lots of innovation and creativity.

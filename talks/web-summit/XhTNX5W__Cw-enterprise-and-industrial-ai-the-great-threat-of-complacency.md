## Description

Ganesh Bell talks about how we must remain vigilant with the advent of AI technologies.

Many studies focus on the cross-industrial impact of artificial intelligence five, twenty and thirty years from now. Forget that timing. The future is here, and there are thousands of cross-sector case studies.  As traditional industries and businesses grapple with digital transformation amidst changing competition, markets and customer tastes, they must fight complacency and prioritise AI and IoT, or suffer the consequences. Inertia will drive extinction.

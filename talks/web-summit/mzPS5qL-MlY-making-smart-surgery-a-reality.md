## Description

We sit down with Dorry Segev, Annie Rogaski and Katie Collins as they talk the future of surgery.

It's been a while since hospitals started getting smarter. Once rare robotic surgery is now more mainstream; our medical realities are augmented and virtual, transplant tech is disrupting donations and telemedicine holds the throne. But is a smart hospital always a better one? And how much smarts are left to come?

## Description

European Institute of Innovation and Technology's Martin Kern, European Trade Union Confederation's Luca Visentini, Udemy's Kevin Johnson, Y Combinator's Holly Liu, and El Confidencial's Manuel Ángel Méndez at Web Summit 2018.

A recent study by the McKinsey Global Institute predicts that by 2030 as many as 800 million jobs could be lost to automation. Advances in robotics and AI are already having drastic impacts on the workplace. Are we entering into a utopia where we'll spend all of our time at leisure or will it be a dystopia of mass unemployment and extreme inequality?

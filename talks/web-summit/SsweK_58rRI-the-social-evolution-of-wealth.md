## Description

eToro's Yoni Assia at Web Summit 2018.

In 2017, less than 1% of the world's population owned almost 50% of the world’s wealth. Crypto, despite shaking the foundations of the financial system and introducing revolutionary concepts such as decentralisation and consensus mechanisms, has failed to improve wealth distribution. Can technology help to reduce inequality and help people to pursue their purpose?

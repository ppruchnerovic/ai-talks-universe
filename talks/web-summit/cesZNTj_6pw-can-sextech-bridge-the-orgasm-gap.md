## Description

Featuring Polly Rodriguez, Alexandra Fine, Stephanie Alys and Leah Hunter, this panel talks where sex and tech cross over.

The sextech industry is, at first blush, all about connected machines like vibrators and robots as well as implementing virtual reality to enhance foreplay and pleasure. But as the industry incorporates machine learning to better anticipate clients' needs, leaders also are pushing to make sextech more human-centric and less about mere gadgets. This discussion includes the founder of revolutionary sexual well-being company Unbound and the CEO of Dame, a startup aiming to make phenomenal sex toys.

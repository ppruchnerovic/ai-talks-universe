Filmed live at Web Summit 2022 in Lisbon.

At a time when most companies are focused on technology or hardware that keeps you glued to a screen or a virtual world, Amazon wants you to interact with its technology only when you need it. In this session, Alexa head scientist Rohit Prasad explains how a philosophy of 'ambient intelligence' is driving the development of AI that blends invisibly into our environment, helping you when you need it and disappearing when you don’t.

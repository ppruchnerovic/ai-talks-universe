## Description

As artificial intelligence continues to revolutionise industries, the key challenge for professionals and businesses alike is to remain relevant in this rapidly evolving landscape. In this session, we will explore how to harness AI as an ally rather than a disruptor. Discover actionable strategies to adapt your skill set, leverage AI-driven tools, and maintain a competitive edge. By emphasising human-centered skills—creativity, emotional intelligence, and strategic thinking—you can thrive in an AI-enhanced world.

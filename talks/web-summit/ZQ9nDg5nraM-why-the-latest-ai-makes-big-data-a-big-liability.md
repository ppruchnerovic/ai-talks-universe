## Description

For businesses that aren’t building their own LLMs, the value of AI will come from combining existing LLMs with well-curated, high-quality data sets. When it comes to customer data, that’s a big problem. In this talk, renowned cybersecurity guru Bruce Schneier and his co-founder John Bruce will make the case that personal digital wallets offer a breakthrough. Companies can use this new tool as a data foundation for hyper-personalized, real-time AI experiences.

## Description

Ordre's Simon Lock at Web Summit 2018.

The impact of the digital revolution, increased retailer competitiveness and other global economic factors have created significant disruption to traditional business channels. Fashion week schedules are in a state of flux, and the ability for retail buyers to constantly travel and attend physical showrooms and global fashion weeks, presents a range of challenges. Already utilising Virtual Reality and 360 imagery, ORDRE is pioneering new technologies that will be crucial to the future of the fashion business.

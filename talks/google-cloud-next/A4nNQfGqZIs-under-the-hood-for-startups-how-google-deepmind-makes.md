## Description

What actually happens between a research breakthrough and an API release? Google DeepMind product managers will pull back the curtain on the trade-offs made while building models such as Gemini, Gemma, and Nano Banana. Find out how we prioritize reasoning vs. latency and long context vs. cost, so you can align your startup’s roadmap with the next generation of frontier intelligence.

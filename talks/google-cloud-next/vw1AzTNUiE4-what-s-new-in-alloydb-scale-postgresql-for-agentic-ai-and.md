# What's new in AlloyDB: Scale PostgreSQL for agentic AI and hybrid clouds

## Description

Demanding transactional, analytical, and AI workloads require more than standard PostgreSQL. In this session, learn how AlloyDB, a 100% PostgreSQL-compatible database, can empower your applications with agentic AI through its latest capabilities, including vector indexing, vector search, and natural language interfaces. Join us to find out what’s new in AlloyDB Omni, the downloadable edition for hybrid and multicloud environments, and gain insights into how to scale your workloads while achieving superior price-performance.

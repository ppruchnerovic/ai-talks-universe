## Description

The latest DORA report tells us that while 80% of developers feel AI is enhancing their individual productivity, at the organizational level, more AI means more instability. This session shows you how to flip that script, replacing rigid workflows with agent-first development. Through a series of live demos, we'll dive into how Gemini CLI and Antigravity allow you to orchestrate agents that reason, plan, and act, while ensuring the core tenants of development such as reproducible builds, extensible integrations, and enterprise-grade trust.

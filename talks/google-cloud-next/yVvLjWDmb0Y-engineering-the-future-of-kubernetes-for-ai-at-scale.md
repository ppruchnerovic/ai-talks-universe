Join this session to explore the cutting-edge landscape of Kubernetes. Learn how Google, as the top open source contributor to Kubernetes for 11 consecutive years, is steering the industry’s direction by improving infrastructure fluidity, securing agentic workflows, and intelligently optimizing resources.

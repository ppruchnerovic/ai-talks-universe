## Description

Keynote: Beyond the Hype: Operationalizing AI for Tactical Cyber Superiority

🎙️ Brigadier General Reid J. Novotny, Chief AI Officer & DoW Cyber Force Generation Lead, U.S. Cyber Command
📍 Presented at SANS AI Cybersecurity Summit 2026

Brig General Novotny addresses how USCYBERCOM is leveraging advanced AI capabilities to enhance defensive and offensive cyber operations, whiles addressing critical gaps in threat detection and adoption barriers, to stay ahead of adversary AI exploitation. Brig Gen Novotny will outline strategies for resilient AI integration in military cyber workflows, emphasizing partnerships, to counter evolving threats from state actors like the PRC.

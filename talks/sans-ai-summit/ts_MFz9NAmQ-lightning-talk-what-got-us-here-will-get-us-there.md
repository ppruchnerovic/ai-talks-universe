Lightning Talk: What Got Us Here Will Get Us There

🎙️ Chris Hughes, VP, Security Strategy Zenity
📍 Presented at SANS AI Cybersecurity Summit 2026

Decades-old security approaches built on reactive, risk-averse practices are breaking down in the age of AI. As attack timelines compress from years to hours, traditional “bolt-on” defenses struggle to keep pace. This talk examines why legacy models fail under machine-speed conditions and what that shift means for modern defense.

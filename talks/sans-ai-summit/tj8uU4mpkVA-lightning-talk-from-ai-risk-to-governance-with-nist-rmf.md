From AI Risk to AI Responsibility: Operationalizing AI Governance with NIST AI RMF

🎙️ Kellep Charles, Cybersecurity Department Chair, Capitol Technology University
📍 Presented at SANS AI Cybersecurity Summit 2026

No current GenAI model is secure by default without continuous adversarial testing. This talk explores how to move from recognizing AI risk to establishing true AI responsibility by operationalizing governance with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF).

## Description

Portuguese and Spanish language translations for SXSW 2025 Keynotes and Featured Sessions presented by Itaú.

Prompting AIs is great, but has an AI prompted YOU yet? The Agentic Era is dawning, and with the active participation of AI agents business is about to get weirder and wildly more productive. Learn how we’re already prompting AIs with our behavior and how spatial computing agents will connect businesses with the many kinds of ecosystems they inhabit, leading to automatically evolving enterprises that adapt in real-time. We’ll explore how humans and technology are actively co-creating reality, plus practical ways AI agents already unlock exponential productivity and sustainable business growth.

By skillfully connecting the possible with the practical, Neil Redding is a Near Futurist and Innovation Architect with 30+ years of experience unlocking the transformative power of emerging technologies. A sought-after keynote speaker at SXSW, Dubai Future Forum, and AWE, and at corporate events for Meta, Microsoft, and more, Neil inspires audiences with the reality-shifting capabilities of AI and spatial computing for activating pragmatic interpersonal and business value. Through his boutique consultancy Redding Futures and previous roles, Neil has worked with world-class brands including Visa, Nike, Apple, and Verizon to distill these continually emerging possibilities, applying ecosystem-centered design to unlock sustainable growth, resilience, and transformative business opportunity.

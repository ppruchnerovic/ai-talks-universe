Portuguese and Spanish language translations for SXSW 2025 Keynotes and Featured Sessions presented by Itaú.

While technology often feels impersonal, Bumble is taking a different approach—using AI in a responsible and ethical way to enhance how we connect with ourselves and others, and build better relationships both online and in person. Michael Affronti, Bumble's Chief Product Officer, sits down with NBC's Savannah Sellers to discuss the company's vision for creating a world where all relationships are healthy and equitable, powered by technology that makes the world feel less alone.

## Description

Portuguese and Spanish language translations for SXSW 2025 Keynotes and Featured Sessions presented by Itaú.

Autodesk’s Chief Marketing Officer Dara Treseder and San Jose Mayor Matt Mahan will sit down for a discussion about the collective effort to bring government into the 21st century. The duo will unpack how the latest technologies — including AI — are transforming urban infrastructure and creating lasting positive impacts on communities like San Jose and beyond. Moderated by TechCrunch’s Rebecca Bellan, this panel will explore how Autodesk and the City of San Jose are helping transform the future of housing, transportation, responsible AI procurement, and more to ultimately reimagine what it means to be a sustainable, thriving city.

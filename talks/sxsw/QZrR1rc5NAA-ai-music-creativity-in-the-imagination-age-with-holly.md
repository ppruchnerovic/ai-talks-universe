## Description

What happens when you play in the #ImaginationAge’s playground of possibility with AI? Meet Holly Herndon, one of the Time100 Most Influential People in AI, who is showing us how an artist-centric future is within reach. Through developing her own tech and protocols for living with the technology of others, often with a focus on the ownership and augmentation of digital identity and voice, Holly’s groundbreaking work is reshaping the relationship between AI, music, and human creativity. These not only facilitate expansive artworks across media — but are artworks unto themselves. Helen Todd, the first resident of Cincinnati to digitally clone herself with a hyper-realistic avatar and the founder and podcast host of Creativity Squared, understands the potential of what a human-centric future can be alongside her brilliant podcast guests like Holly. Join Helen for a front-row seat to an intimate session to see what Holly wants you to know to meet this moment in time — and to collectively envision and usher in our shared future in the #ImaginationAge, together.

## Description

Watch the MuleSoft keynote from Dreamforce 2025 to see how the agentic enterprise is moving from theory to production. Learn how organizations are building, governing, and scaling AI agents using MuleSoft Agent Fabric.

The agentic enterprise is no longer theoretical. It is here, and it is reshaping how organizations design, deploy, and operate AI across the enterprise. In this keynote, MuleSoft leaders walk through what it really takes to move AI agents out of experimentation and into production. If you are asking how agents discover capabilities, safely invoke APIs, coordinate across systems, and operate with enterprise-grade governance, this session breaks it down in practical terms.

You will see how API-led connectivity, real-time integration, and enterprise governance form the architectural fabric that allows AI agents to reason, act, and collaborate across the business with confidence. The session introduces MuleSoft Agent Fabric and shows how you can discover existing agents, govern how they are used, orchestrate interactions across domains, and observe agent behavior end to end. Live demos highlight new capabilities including Agent Registry, Agent Governance, Agent Broker, Agent Visualizer, MuleSoft Dev Agent features, and the MuleSoft Diagnostics Agent. You will also hear from customers including MSC Cruises, Barco, and Moody’s on how they are preparing their organizations for agentic workflows at scale.

If you are responsible for enterprise architecture, integration, automation, or AI strategy, this keynote provides a clear blueprint for building agentic systems that are connected, governed, and ready for real business impact.

In this keynote, you will discover:
• What defines an agentic enterprise and why integration is central
• How to discover and govern AI agents across ecosystems
• How to orchestrate and observe multi-agent workflows
• How agent development fits naturally into developer workflows
• How customers are scaling agentic architectures in the real world

## Description

The future of revenue management is agentic.

Watch the full  Agentforce Revenue Management (formerly Revenue Cloud) keynote to see how Salesforce helps accelerate revenue growth with a complete quote-to-cash platform that breaks down silos, orchestrates revenue, and drives growth across every channel and model powered by AI Revenue Agents.

Learn how Agentforce Revenue Management eliminates the messy middle of disconnected data and systems between your CRM and ERP by uniting every department across your organization on one complete revenue platform. Reinvent your revenue to be customer-centric by:

- Providing sellers a streamlined workspace with the Enhanced Quote Line Editor (GA Now). Everything’s in one view, so deals move at the speed of the customer.
- Allowing flexible deal structures through Advanced Ramp Deals (GA Now), Nested Groups (GA Now), Price Propagation (GA Feb ‘26), and Dynamic Bundles (GA Now)—so your most complex transactions can be modeled out without creating chaos in the system.
- Generate a valid quote, no matter how complex your product offering is, by Quoting with Agentforce (GA Now) and the Constraint Based Configurator (GA Now) for smarter transaction management, eliminating friction and ensuring deal velocity.
- Focusing on asset-driven revenue by making every product sold a dynamic, trackable asset natively in Salesforce through Asset Lifecycle Management (GA Now).
- Unlocking your revenue data via Tableau Next for Revenue (GA Now) with dashboards for ARR, MRR, churn—even pricing analytics, giving the visibility you need to build confidence with your board, investors, and customers.
- Building Billing into a growth engine for your business through Self-Service Billing & Payments (GA Now), Consumption Management (GA Now), and resolving complex billing-related questions with an intelligent Billing Agent (GA Now).

## Description

Discover how Agentforce Sales (formerly Sales Cloud) is transforming the sales cycle, creating an era of limitless selling by multiplying seller capacity and elevating human ingenuity. This keynote reveals how the transformation of Agentforce Sales arms every seller with an AI teammate to grow pipeline, close deals faster, and ignite partner channels.

Key Highlights
- Limitless Selling Era: Learn how AI sales agents act as force multipliers, making the limitations for sellers obsolete.
- Agent-Assisted Prospecting: See how Agentforce Sales works around the clock to fill lead lists and book meetings, giving sellers a full pipeline and calendar upon waking.
- Seller Elevation: Understand how AI sales agents eliminate non-selling activities, such as building account plans, prepping documents, and updating CRM.
- Sales Workspace Experience: Explore the new experience where sellers and Agentforce work together, featuring embedded Tableau, risk spotting, and agent-driven activity logging.
- Partner Cloud Transformation: Learn how Partner Cloud brings Agentforce to partners, providing a connected solution from enablement to co-selling to incentives.
- Customer Impact: Hear stories from top sales teams like Uber for Business, Asymbl, and Siemens who are already leveraging Agentforce Sales to scale, drive growth, and elevate their human sellers.

## Description

Discover how Agentforce Field Service (formerly Salesforce Field Service) is transforming service delivery and setting a new standard for field service management by elevating human workers with AI agents and orchestrating next-generation robotic workforces. This keynote explores innovations across the entire field service lifecycle—from customer experience and scheduling and dispatch to mobile tools and operational analytics—all built on a single, trusted foundation.

Key highlights include:

- Agentic Scheduling: See how field service AI agents fully autonomously handle appointment scheduling, outreach, and rescheduling, enabling a new level of field service automation by giving valuable time back to human dispatchers.
- Mobile Workforce Empowerment: Learn about new features for mobile technicians, including seamless GIS (Geographic Information System) map integration and the use of voice-powered forms to dramatically reduce admin time and errors in the field.
- Operational Confidence: Explore the new Field Service Operations Center, which uses AgentForce to provide conversational data analysis and actionable insights (like capacity planning) to drive revenue and efficiency.
- Physical AI Orchestration: Witness the future of field service with a live demo of multi-robot orchestration, where Agentforce coordinates drones and highly mobile robots (Cobalt and Boston Dynamics' Spot) to complete work alongside human teams.
- Customer Stories: Hear firsthand accounts and see demos from global leaders like GE Vernova (Wind Business) and Google Fiber on how they are leveraging the platform to reduce costs, improve efficiency, and maintain high customer satisfaction.
- Simplicity and Speed: Introducing Salesforce Go, a new feature designed to automate foundational setup steps, helping organizations go from purchase to productivity faster than ever.

## Description

Join GitHub's Amy Lewis, Director of Enterprise Marketing, as she sits down with Dani Grant, CEO of Jam, for an insightful conversation on the journey from product manager to entrepreneur. Dani shares how her experience as a product manager at Cloudflare inspired her to build Jam, a tool now helping over 150,000 engineers fix bugs faster.

Along the way, Dani reflects on how her time in GitHub for startups program provided insights and guidance to help launch her company.

Dani dives into the key lessons she learned while launching a startup and offers valuable advice on overcoming the fear of failure, knowing when to pivot, and surrounding yourself with the right people.

Dani shares how tools like GitHub Copilot have been instrumental in scaling Jam and enabling fast, efficient product development.

## Description

How does Spotify keep improving their developer experience in a fast-changing world? How has Spotify evolved to build an AI DJ? Join Sanjana Seetharam, senior product manager and David Rubin, Engineering Manager VCS at Spotify, as she shares the key to scaling change, one PR at a time. From monoliths to microservices, cloud-native platforms, and now AI tools like GitHub Copilot, Spotify’s journey has been a technological shift. This session will explore how their engineering teams navigate major tech transformations all from their IDE, their journey over the last decade with GitHub, and using Backstage for internal development.  Discover how these tools empower new ways of working, fuel innovation, and help Spotify reach their next tech frontier, from audiobooks to an AI DJ.

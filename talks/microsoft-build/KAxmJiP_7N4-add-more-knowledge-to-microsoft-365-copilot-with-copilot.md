## Description

In this session, we'll dive into how you can leverage Copilot connectors and actions to boost the knowledge base for your agents. By integrating third-party data, you'll be able to extend Copilot's capabilities and provide your agents with richer, more comprehensive information and act directly from within Copilot's canvas on your data.

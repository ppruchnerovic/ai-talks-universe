## Description

From intelligent apps to intelligent agents, learn how Dataverse helps you build the future of innovation atop your business data. In this session, we share our customers' journeys of building agents and copilot-powered app development in a friendly and low-code way, backed by Dataverse. We will cover AI-powered search in agents and apps, copilot-assisted data modeling and business logic creation, and how Dataverse powers the operations of agents created in Copilot Studio.

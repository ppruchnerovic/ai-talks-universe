## Description

Creating safe and innovative experiences with AI means using tools and techniques to avoid causing potential harm to customers while delivering on the promised capability they are looking for. Our AI experts will share concrete examples and recommendations. You will come away from the session knowing how to build more safeguards into your products as well as help you create a differentiated experience that is responsible by design.

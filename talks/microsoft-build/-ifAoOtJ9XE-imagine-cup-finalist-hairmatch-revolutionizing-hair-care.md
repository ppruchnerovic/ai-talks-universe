## Description

Join this session for a live demo of HairMatch, an AI-powered mobile app transforming hair care for women with textured hair. Powered by Azure AI Foundry, it uses a Vision Language Model to analyze hair type, delivering tailored product recommendations, hairstyle guides, and expert advice. As a finalist in the Imagine Cup—the premier global AI startup competition for student founders—HairMatch is redefining personalized beauty through the latest AI technology

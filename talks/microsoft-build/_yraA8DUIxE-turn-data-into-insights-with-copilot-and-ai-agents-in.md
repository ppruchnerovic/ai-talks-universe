## Description

Learn how to customize and extend Copilot and AI agents in Microsoft Fabric to deliver more granular, actionable insights for teams across your organization. Come discover advanced methods to transform raw data into meaningful, conversational insights. We will dive deep into techniques for embedding domain-specific knowledge into your agentic workflows, ensuring that responses are accurate, highly relevant, and contextually aware to your business needs.

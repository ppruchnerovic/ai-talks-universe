## Description

This demo will show how quickly you can land data in OneLake from everywhere-other clouds, on-prem, real-time streaming and more. You can even transform your data as you bring into OneLake or between OneLake items. See how easy it is to unify your data to build an end-to-end data solution in Fabric and leverage it for AI.

## Description

Azure AI Foundry is proud to partner with Credo AI and Saidot to help customers tackle the end to end AI governance. Join us for a demo of how Azure AI Foundry integrates with these companies' AI governance platforms to help organizations build a centralized view of their AI models and use cases, identify relevant risks, run evaluations, and apply relevant mitigations to deliver more trustworthy AI apps.

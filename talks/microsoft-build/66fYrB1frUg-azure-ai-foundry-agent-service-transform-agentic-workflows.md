## Description

Faster, better, and at lower cost. Discover how Azure AI Foundry Agent Service, seamlessly integrated into Azure AI Foundry’s continuous signals loop, can revolutionize your business processes. This powerful platform enables developers to define and run AI agents with low latency, high scalability, and enterprise-grade capabilities. Streamline decision-making and boost efficiency, whether working with simple or advanced agents, or orchestrating multiple agents.

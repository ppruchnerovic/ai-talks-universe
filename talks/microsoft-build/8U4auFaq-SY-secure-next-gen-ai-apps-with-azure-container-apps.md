## Description

Discover how Azure Container Apps dynamic sessions simplify building scalable SaaS applications. In this breakout session, learn to create and extend multi-tenant solutions with secure, isolated, and stateful serverless containers—accelerating your SaaS journey from development to deployment. Additionally, explore how serverless GPUs can be leveraged for high-performance AI inferencing, enabling focus on developing powerful AI capabilities without the complexity of managing GPUs.

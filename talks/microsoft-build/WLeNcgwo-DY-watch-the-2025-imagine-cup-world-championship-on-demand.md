## Description

The future of tech just got brighter.

Experience the full 2025 Imagine Cup World Championship, where three student startups—Argus, HairMatch, and Signvrse—pitched their innovative AI-powered solutions live at Microsoft Build.

Built by Daniel Kim and Arjun Oberoi, students at Stanford University this student startup developed a two-part wearable device powered by Azure AI Speech and Azure AI Foundry—designed to support individuals with low vision through object detection, facial recognition, and spatial guidance. 🦮💡

Inspired by their own families, Daniel and Arjun set out to help older adults maintain independence and freedom. Today, they’re taking that mission global. 🌍

👏 Huge congratulations to team Argus, who now take home $100K and a mentorship session with Microsoft Chairman & CEO Satya Nadella!

And a big shoutout to our incredible runner-up teams:
✨ HairMatch – Matthew Steels and Alycea Adams
✨ Signvrse – Anthony Marugu, Branice Kazira, Daniel Angila, and Gheida Abdalah

Each runner-up team receives $25,000 USD to continue building their impact-driven AI solutions. 💡🔥

These finalists aren’t just building tech—they’re launching startups with purpose.

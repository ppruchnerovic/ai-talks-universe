## Description

Watch us build an intelligent multimodal AI app from scratch in just 15 minutes. We’ll show how easy it is to transform images and videos stored in Azure Blob Storage into valuable insights without the need for pre-existing metadata or tags.  We will show how Coactive’s Multimodal Application Platform can help you seamlessly integrate the power of multimodal AI into your Azure AI applications, empowering you to build smarter apps with visual understanding faster.

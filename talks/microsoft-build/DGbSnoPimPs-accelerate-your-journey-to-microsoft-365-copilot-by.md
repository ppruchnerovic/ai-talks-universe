## Description

In this fast-paced, demo-heavy session, pro developers will discover an all-new, integrated experience for building connectors that bring line-of-business (LOB) data directly into the Copilot canvas. We’ll showcase new capabilities that simplify the authoring process, walk through end-to-end demos of real-world LOB integration scenarios, and reveal how to quickly connect internal systems to deliver rich, context-aware experiences in Copilot. If you're looking to unlock enterprise data and drive productivity at scale, this session is your blueprint.

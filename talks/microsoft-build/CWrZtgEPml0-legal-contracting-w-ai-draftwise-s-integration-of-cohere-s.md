## Description

Discover how DraftWise, an AI-driven contract drafting and negotiation platform, collaborates with Cohere to transform legal workflows. This session will delve into DraftWise's strategic integration of Cohere's advanced AI models to enhance information retrieval, drafting accuracy, and negotiation efficiency. Attendees will gain insights into the challenges faced by legal professionals and how AI solutions can address these issues, leading to improved productivity and client satisfaction

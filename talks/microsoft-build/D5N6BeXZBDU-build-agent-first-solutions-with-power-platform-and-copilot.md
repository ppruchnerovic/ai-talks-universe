## Description

Join us on a journey to transform manual business processes to agent-led, human-supervised tasks using Copilot Studio and Power Apps. We'll use AI to define the business problem, map the processes, identify efficiency opportunities, and create a multi-asset solution including agents, apps, and automations. This approach combines no-code, low-code, and pro-code elements to speed up development and enhance creativity, reducing drudgery and delivering a working solution in record time.

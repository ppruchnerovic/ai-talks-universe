## Description

Kayla Underkoffler
Zenity
Zenity, Senior Security Engineer

Kayla is a senior security engineer in the CTO office with Zenity. Her professional career started in the United States Marine Corps, where she then left active duty to pursue a career in Cybersecurity. Throughout her years in security, she has served as a practitioner in vulnerability management, security operations, crowdsourced security, and most recently, Agentic AI security and governance.

With a passion for bridging the gap between business and technology, Kayla will continue to evangelize the importance of security for everyone.

Shauna Rathbun
Ping Identity
Platform Security Engineer

Shauna Rathbun is a Security Engineer at Ping Identity, focused on securing low-code/no-code platforms and supporting the safe enablement of citizen development across organizations. With a strong background in cloud security and identity, her mission is to make security more approachable, actionable, and adaptable for both users and security professionals.
As an advocate for secure and reliable innovation, Shauna is committed to shifting security closer to the user with the goal of empowering them to see security as a support for their innovation rather than a hindrance.

## Description

Denys Linkov, Head of ML, Wisedocs
Afseen Syeda, AI Medical Data Specialist, Wisedocs

About the Speakers:
ML and AI Leader focused on building useful and scalable products. Strong focus on customer outcomes with expertise in Agents,  LLMs, RAG and MLOps. Experienced at managing teams of 25+

Currently Head of ML at Wisedocs, previously started the ML team at Voiceflow. Teach ML at UofT (CSC412 Probabilistic ML and CSC490 ML in Industry Capstone) and on LinkedIn Learning (17 courses)

Have spoken at numerous conferences in Canada, US and UK on ML product development, evaluations, team structure, ML for startups and LLM integrations.

Beyond technology, I have enjoy reading and working with the community on green energy, transportation and housing initiatives

Afseen
Afseen is a Lead Prompt Engineer at Wisedocs, where she works alongside the machine learning team to design and refine prompts that make AI more effective at processing and reasoning over complex medical documents. Her work focuses on getting the most out of AI models through thoughtful prompting, whether that means improving how accurately information is extracted or making workflows more efficiently.

Abstract:
The world runs on the data and processes stored in un structured documents. Models are becoming incredibly capable of summarizing and extracting contents, but how well do they perform fuzzy, human style synthesis tasks? This talk will cover our experience building complex internal document processing evals

Husband, father and ML engineer. Great passion for Machine Learning, Big Data, Information Retrieval, and, of course, Rick & Morty!

Abstract:
In this workshop, we'll take a dive into MLOps CI/CD pipeline automation with GCP, Superwise, and retraining/auto-resolution notebooks. In part 1, we’ll focus on how to put together a continuous ML pipeline to train, deploy, and monitor models. Part 2 will focus on automations and production-first insights to detect and resolve issues continuously.

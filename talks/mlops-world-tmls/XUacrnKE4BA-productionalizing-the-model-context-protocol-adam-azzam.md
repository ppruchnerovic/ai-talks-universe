## Description

🎥 From GenAI World: Tools, Infra & Open Source Stack — Virtual Session (July 29, 2025).

Session Title: Productionalizing the Model Context Protocol: Use-Cases, Pitfalls, Patterns
Speaker:  Adam Azzam, VP of Product, Prefect
Category: Workflow Orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, etc.)

Abstract:
Eight months after the Model Context Protocol’s debut, MCP servers are already the connective tissue between large-language models and the data they need—yet many projects still stall at the prototype stage. Drawing on usage data and support tickets from FastMCP (the Python foundation under most live servers), this talk distills what actually works in production, what still breaks, and why.

Adam explores the real environments where MCP is thriving, the unseen hurdles teams hit when moving from localhost to enterprise traffic, and the practices that separate persistent services from weekend demos. Finally, he shares what’s next as context becomes a first-class product surface.

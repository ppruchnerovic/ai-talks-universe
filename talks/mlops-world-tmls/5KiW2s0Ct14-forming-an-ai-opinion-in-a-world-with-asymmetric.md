🎥 From the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Virtual Keynote (October 6, 2025)

Session Title: Forming an AI Opinion in a World With Asymmetric Information
Speaker: Denys Linkov, Head of ML, Wisedocs
Talk Track: Keynote

Abstract:
Every day, we’re surrounded by bold technical and societal claims about AI. Many of these predictions come from those with access to non-public information, while major breakthroughs are increasingly hidden behind trade secrets rather than academic publication.

In this keynote, Denys Linkov provides a framework for assessing technical, economic, and social claims about AI grounding them in reality and helping practitioners and leaders form their own well-reasoned opinions.

Learn how to evaluate information asymmetry in the AI landscape and translate high-level insights into actionable steps that inform daily decisions, product strategy, and long-term thinking.

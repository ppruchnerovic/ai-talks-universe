## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 9, 2025)

Session Title: The New Wave of Synthetic Data
Speaker: Seth Kimmel, CEO, Sutro

Abstract:
Synthetic data isn’t new — but LLMs have transformed its possibilities. In this session, Seth Kimmel, CEO of Sutro, explores how organizations are using LLM-generated synthetic data to fuel a new era of AI development. From training and evaluation to data privacy, portability, and large-scale simulations, synthetic data is reshaping how we build, test, and deploy models.

Seth highlights real-world examples of how teams are leveraging synthetic data to enhance model performance, simulate market conditions, and strengthen retrieval and search systems — all while protecting sensitive information and improving scalability.

What you’ll learn:
• How LLMs have revolutionized synthetic data generation
• Real-world use cases for training, evaluation, and simulation
• How synthetic data supports privacy, compliance, and faster iteration
• Why synthetic data is essential for scaling GenAI systems responsibly

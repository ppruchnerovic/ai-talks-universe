## Description

🎥 From GenAI World: Tools, Infra & Open Source Stack — Virtual Session (July 29, 2025)

Session Title: Agentic AI Command Center: How Gateways Orchestrate GenAI Implementation and MCP Integration
Speaker: Nikunj Bajaj, CEO, TrueFoundry
Category: Agent Lifecycle Management

Abstract:
As Generative AI evolves from experimental prototypes to mission-critical enterprise systems, organizations face growing complexity in managing distributed AI at scale. With over 50% of enterprises running AI in production, the era of single-model deployments is over.

In this session, Nikunj Bajaj introduces the AI Gateway pattern — a central control plane for orchestrating enterprise AI ecosystems. He breaks down how gateways solve real-world challenges such as inconsistent APIs, latency management, failover handling, semantic caching, and governance enforcement.

Attendees will learn practical architectural patterns for building high-availability, multi-model systems that manage thousands of concurrent requests with sub-millisecond decision-making. The talk also explores the future of Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration, where gateways coordinate not just models, but entire tool ecosystems to enable natural language-driven automation.

Whether you’re an architect, platform engineer, or AI leader, this session provides a roadmap for designing resilient, scalable, and governable AI systems that align with enterprise maturity.

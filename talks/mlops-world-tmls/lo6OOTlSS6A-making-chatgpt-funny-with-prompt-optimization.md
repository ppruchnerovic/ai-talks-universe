Abstract:
A recent study found ChatGPT repeated the same 25 jokes 90% of the time. As is often the case with popular narratives about the limits of AI, ChatGPT is capable of so much more... you just have to know how to ask! Using principles of prompt engineering, I try to get ChatGPT to make you laugh, while arming you with techniques for overcoming similar supposed limitations, when working with AI.

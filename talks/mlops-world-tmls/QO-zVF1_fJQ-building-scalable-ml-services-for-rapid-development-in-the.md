Abstract:
We build machine learning products to support discovery and automation within the fitness health and wellness sector. Our products range from building recommender systems to enable our consumers to discover products from our customers within our fitness marketplace to applying natural language techniques to enable our customers to create automated marketing emails to delight their customers. In this talk, we will present our solution for training and deploying machine learning models into our production environment. We will talk about how our pipeline has evolved with open source tools like DBT, AirFlow, and MLFlow to address various pain points in building and scaling our data pipelines to support our machine learning solutions across the breadth of our wellness and beauty product ecosystem. Through this, we were able to reduce our product release time by 85%.

## Description

Rod Rivera, Developer Relations Engineer, Rasa

About the Speaker:
Rod Rivera is a Professor of the Practice in AI at ITAM and a Developer Relations Engineer at Rasa. With over 15 years of experience across three continents, Rod specializes in Sovereign Agent Architectures — autonomous, always-on AI systems designed for operational leverage. He has led ML initiatives for global technology leaders including Alibaba Cloud, Huawei, and Samsung. Rod focuses on educating the next generation of builders on how to build and operate persistent, credentialed, and auditable AI digital employees.

Abstract:
Voice isn't text with audio glued on. It's a concurrent system with no "send" button, where silence is a signal and every word is irreversible. We'll walk through various common failures and the architecture that fixes each.
These are problems you own, not model limits you wait out. We'll show what the fixes look like and how Rasa makes them first-class with runnable examples and what's changed for voice in 2026.
You'll leave able to diagnose why your agent feels broken and how to fix it.

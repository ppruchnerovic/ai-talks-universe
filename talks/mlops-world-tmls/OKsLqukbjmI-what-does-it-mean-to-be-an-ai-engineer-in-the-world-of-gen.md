## Description

Speaker:
Aarushi Kansal
Principal Engineer, Bumble

Abstract:
In this talk the audience will learn about the rapidly changing ecosystem and what it now means to be an "AI Engineer" in the world of Generative AI. It will cover the technical eco system while also touching on business strategy.

The key function of a healthy team will be emphasized, that is, when both engineers and business decision-makers understand the AI ecosystem and how it affects their respective remits.

Currently, there's a lot of hype around AI but not enough understanding and education about what is possible, where certain tools fit in and how both engineers and business decision-makers can make use of this new boom.

This talk will tie in use cases and business strategies with both technical & business considerations as they impact this rapidly changing eco-system

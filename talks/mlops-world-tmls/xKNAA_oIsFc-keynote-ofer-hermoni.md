## Description

Speaker: Ofer Hermoni, Generative AI Commons, Education & Outreach Chair, Linux Foundation AI & Data

Join us for a crucial conversation on defining Open-Source AI—an increasingly essential and complex task in today's rapidly evolving AI landscape. Our session will kick off with an overview of the Generative AI Commons from the Linux Foundation AI & Data. We will then delve into the unique nuances and challenges of Open Source AI, highlighting how it differs from traditional open-source software. Our discussion will focus on maintaining a delicate balance between innovation and the core principles of Open Source: the freedom to study, use, modify, and share. We invite the community to engage in this vital dialogue to help shape a definition that not only reflects the changing nature of AI but also preserves the spirit of openness amidst technological progress.

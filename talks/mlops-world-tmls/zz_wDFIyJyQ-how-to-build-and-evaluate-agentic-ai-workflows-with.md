## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 9, 2025)

Session Title: How to Build and Evaluate Agentic AI Workflows with FloTorch
Speaker: Dr. Hemant Joshi, CTO, FloTorch
Talk Track: Agents in Production

Abstract:
Deploying GenAI agents at enterprise scale introduces new challenges — from governance and observability to cost optimization and trust. In this hands-on session, Dr. Hemant Joshi, CTO of FloTorch, walks through how to build, evaluate, and scale agentic workflows using the FloTorch AI Gateway.

You’ll learn how to set up agentic workflows that connect with any LLM via a single endpoint, leverage smart routing for efficiency, and establish robust monitoring to ensure reliability and compliance. The session also demonstrates how FloTorch helps enterprises accelerate AI deployment while maintaining full visibility into performance, cost, and latency trade-offs.

What you’ll learn:
• How to design and scale enterprise-grade agentic workflows
• Why governance and observability are key to sustainable AI adoption
• How the FloTorch AI Gateway enables seamless LLM integration and smart routing
• Techniques to evaluate cost, latency, and accuracy before production deployment
• How to accelerate time-to-production and future-proof agentic systems

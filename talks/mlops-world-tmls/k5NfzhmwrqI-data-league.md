## Description

Speaker: Ian Schweer: Staff Software Engineer, Riot Games

League of legends faces lots of interesting problems in the data space that are unique due to the video game aspect. We will cover how we deploy models (pytorch, jax, and numpy) into and around a binary video game. It will teach us what the fundamental data model is for games as a service, and how has the data and ML stack changed since the league's inception in 2009.

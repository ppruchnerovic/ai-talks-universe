🎥 From GenAI World: Tools, Infra & Open Source Stack — Virtual Session (July 29, 2025).

Session Title: The Workhorse of GenAI: Powering High-ROI Projects with Accelerated Batch Inference
Speaker: Seth Kimmel, Founder & CEO, Sutro
Category: Model Deployment and Serving

Abstract:
Batch inference remains one of the most powerful yet under-discussed pillars of enterprise AI. In this session, Seth Kimmel explores how accelerated batch inference can dramatically increase velocity, reduce costs, and unlock scalability for high-ROI GenAI projects.
Through a practical demonstration of Sutro’s product, Seth covers:

• When to choose batch inference over real-time infrastructure
• How accelerated batch inference improves efficiency and throughput
• How organizations are generating significant business value from batch inference today

This session is ideal for AI researchers, developers, data professionals, and AI product or project managers looking to drive meaningful ROI from GenAI deployments.

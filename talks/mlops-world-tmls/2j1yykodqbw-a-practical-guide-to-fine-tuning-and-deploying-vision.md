## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 9, 2025)

Session Title: A Practical Guide to Fine-Tuning and Deploying Vision Models
Speaker: Zac Carrico, Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Apella
Talk Track: ML Training Lifecycle

Abstract:
As video foundation models become central to modern AI systems — from healthcare and robotics to retail and security — MLOps teams are encountering new challenges in efficiently fine-tuning and deploying these large models for domain-specific tasks.

In this practical session, Zac Carrico from Apella shares hands-on techniques to fine-tune vision foundation models at scale, without overloading compute resources or sacrificing real-time performance.

Using state-of-the-art architectures, Zac demonstrates strategies for data sampling, temporal-aware augmentation, and adapter-based tuning, along with optimization and deployment patterns that make large vision models practical for production use.

What you’ll learn:
• How to fine-tune large vision models efficiently and reliably
• Data sampling and temporal augmentation strategies for video datasets
• How to use adapter-based tuning and scalable optimization methods
• Best practices for handling long or sparse videos with minimal latency
• How to deploy chunk-based inference and temporal fusion modules in production

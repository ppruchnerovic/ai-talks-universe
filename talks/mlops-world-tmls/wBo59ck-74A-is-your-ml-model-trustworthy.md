## Description

Speaker Bio:

María Grandury, ML Research Engineer at nuerocat GmbH
María is a Machine Learning Research Engineer focused on AI robustness and explainability. She holds two degrees in Mathematics and Physics and is currently working at neurocat GmbH where she contributes to the core of aidkit, an AI quality and trustworthiness evaluation tool. She is also the founder of "NLP en ES 🤗" - the Hugging Face community of Spanish-speaking NLP professionals - and part of the Core Team of Women in AI & Robotics.

Abstract:
In recent years, Machine Learning models and architectures have become increasingly complex. This growing complexity makes it more difficult to deliver high quality in terms of model performance, robustness and explainability. The introduction of automated evaluations of the trustworthiness of a model is one solution to guarantee that your clients can rely on your model's predictions. This talk will guide you through the different AI quality pillars, their importance and how to evaluate them.

## Description

🎥 From the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Virtual Session (October 7, 2025)

Session Title: The Efficiency Equation: Leveraging AI Agents to Augment Human Labelers in Building Trust & Safety Systems at Scale
Speaker: Madhu Ramanathan, Senior Engineering Leader, Trust and Safety, Meta
Talk Track: Augmenting Workforces with Agents

Abstract:
In today’s fast-moving digital ecosystem, Trust & Safety systems must keep pace with content proliferation, real-time enforcement demands, and ever-changing threats like deepfakes, hallucinations, misinformation, and adversarial behavior.

In this keynote, Madhu Ramanathan explores how LLMs and AI agents are transforming Trust & Safety operations by augmenting human labelers—delivering scalable, high-quality, and cost-efficient systems that balance defect rate, precision, and cost.

The session dives deep into:

• The evolution of Trust & Safety systems and emerging AI-driven challenges
• The traditional role of human labelers across enforcement, measurement, and reactive takedowns
• Real-world case studies of how LLMs and agents are used to measure, detect, and enforce at scale
• Continuous evaluation and calibration techniques using human-in-the-loop and automated prompt tuning
• Solutions to complex production challenges like model migration, GPU cost constraints, and bias mitigation

Through Meta’s experience, Madhu reveals how human judgment and AI intelligence can co-evolve to create hybrid Trust & Safety systems that are resilient, ethical, and continuously improving.
What you’ll learn:
• Why Trust & Safety is entering a new AI-augmented era
• How hybrid systems balance human oversight with agentic automation
• How to design resilient, cost-effective, and adaptive enforcement systems
• The future of combining human intelligence (HI) and artificial intelligence (AI) for scalable trust solutions

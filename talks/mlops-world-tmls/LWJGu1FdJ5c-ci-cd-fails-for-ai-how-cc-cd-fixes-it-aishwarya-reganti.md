## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 8, 2025)

Session Title: Why CI/CD Fails for AI, and How CC/CD Fixes It
Speakers:
• Aishwarya Naresh Reganti, Founder, LevelUp Labs (Ex-AWS)
• Sai Kiriti, Member of Technical Staff, OpenAI
Talk Track: Agents in Production

Abstract:
Traditional CI/CD pipelines assume deterministic software — but AI systems don’t play by those rules.

In this talk, Aishwarya Naresh Reganti (LevelUp Labs, ex-AWS) and Sai Kiriti (OpenAI) introduce the Continuous Calibration / Continuous Development (CC/CD) framework — a new approach for building and scaling AI products that behave unpredictably and autonomously.

Drawing on 50+ real-world deployments, they share how teams can start with low-agency, high-control setups, then safely scale AI systems as they earn reliability and trust. Attendees will walk away with a clear blueprint for managing AI’s inherent uncertainty — from eval design to behavioral monitoring and trust calibration.

What you’ll learn:
• Why CI/CD fails for AI and the risks of applying software assumptions to AI systems
• How the CC/CD framework enables continuous calibration and safe autonomy
• How to scope capabilities and design meaningful evaluation metrics
• How to monitor AI behavior in production and prevent drift
• Practical steps to scale autonomy without losing control

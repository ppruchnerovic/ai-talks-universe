## Description

Naman Goyal, Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Google DeepMind

About the Speaker:
Naman Goyal is a Machine Learning Software Engineer at Google DeepMind, working on advanced reasoning, planning, and instruction-following for Gemini. He is a co-author on Gemini 2.5: Pushing the Frontier with Advanced Reasoning, Multimodality, Long Context, and Next Generation Agentic Capabilities.

Earlier, he worked on the perception stack for autonomous vehicles at NVIDIA, multimodal learning for document understanding at Apple, and deep metric learning for image retrieval at Adobe Research. He holds a Master's in Computer Science from Columbia University, where his thesis explored multimodal learning and on-device NLP for code-switching speakers. He speaks regularly on production-AI topics at venues including The AI Conference, the Agentic AI Summit, DeveloperWeek, and ODSC East.

Abstract:
As AI models become more capable, the focus in production environments is shifting from full automation to effective human-AI collaboration. How should humans and AI systems work together on complex tasks that neither can optimally handle alone? This 1.5-hour workshop explores the ML foundations of collaborative intelligence. We will cover concrete production patterns for three areas: determining when models should defer to humans, communicating confidence and uncertainty, and utilizing training paradigms that produce models that are better collaborators (not just better predictors). I will ground these concepts in real-world production AI use-cases.

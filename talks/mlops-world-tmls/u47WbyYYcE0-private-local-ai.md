## Description

Speaker: Christian Crowley, Co-Founder, Sanctum AI

Sanctum is a private AI application that runs full-featured, open-source LLMs locally. Users can run Sanctum on their Mac or PC & access any open source LLM available on Hugging Face. Accessing leading open source language models in an encrypted, local environment should be as easy as possible, especially for users looking to fine tune their models on sensitive data (e.g., upload & chat with PDFs, financial models). I'm excited to dive into the world of private AI & share more on what we're building at Sanctum.

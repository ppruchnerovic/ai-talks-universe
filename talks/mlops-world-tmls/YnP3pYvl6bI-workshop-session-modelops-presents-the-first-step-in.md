Speaker Bio:
Jim Olsen, CTO at ModelOp
Jim is the CTO at ModelOp, where he leads the technical design of ModelOp Center, the leading ModelOps platform.  Jim has been a technical innovator for the past 30 years, leading architectural design for products at a variety of companies, including Teradata, Qualtrics, and Novell.  Jim holds two software patents and has taught several ModelOps master classes.  For the last year, Jim has been living off the grid in the Rocky Mountains of Colorado.

Abstract:
Data Scientists have their choice of model development tools, platforms, and factories for developing AI models. But to operationalize a model, you need to have the model life cycle established for the models that you want to put into production. The model life cycle is what defines the operational steps from time of deployment to retirement for all models in your organization. In this session, Jim Olsen will show you how to design and build a model life cycle, including how to incorporate Industry best practices. Jim will discuss the considerations for creating the model life cycle, who should be involved, and the types of issues that must be considered.

## Description

Alex Strick van Linschoten, Machine Learning Engineer, ZenML

About the Speaker:
Alex is a Machine Learning Engineer at ZenML. He previously worked as a historian and researcher but now focuses on LLM and GenAI initiatives for ZenML's MLOps and LLMOps platform. Alex holds a PhD in War Studies from King's College London and a BA in Arabic and Persian from SOAS. He's based in Delft (Netherlands).

Abstract:
We dissect patterns and practices from 600+ production GenAI systems across industries. This evidence-based talk -- based on ZenML's LLMOps Database -- cuts through the hype to reveal what actually works when moving LLMs from impressive demos to reliable production. We'll explore why workflow architectures consistently outperform agent-based systems, how successful teams implement dual-cycle development, and the critical role of evaluation frameworks in managing non-determinism. Whether you're building RAG systems or orchestrating complex GenAI workflows, this session provides battle-tested strategies backed by hundreds of real implementations that you can apply immediately to your own LLM projects.

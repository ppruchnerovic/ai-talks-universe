Abstract:
Businesses today are increasingly certain that AI will be a driving force in the evolution of their industries over the next few years. Yet for every successful AI project, there are many that fail to reach widespread adoption in the business and achieve their expected outcomes. This is partly because the mechanics of AI deployment can be complex, and there are still gaps in skills and tooling that can make it difficult for data science, IT operations, and business teams to work in lockstep. But beyond the operational challenges, there are also much more profound issues of trust and transparency that businesses need to address before they can truly turn AI into a business advantage. In this workshop we will walk through how our clients can begin to monitor and address these issues.

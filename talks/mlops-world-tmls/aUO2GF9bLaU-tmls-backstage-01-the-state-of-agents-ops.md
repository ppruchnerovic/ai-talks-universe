## Description

In this episode of TMLS Backstage, Denys Linkov (Head of ML, Wisedocs), Diego Oppenheimer (Head of Product, Hyperparam), and Priyankya Somrah (Principal, Work Bench) dive into insights from the Benchmark Report on Agentic Ops, a first of its kind look at how teams are actually building and deploying agentic systems.

They explore the evolving role of agents in modern workflows, including where traditional automation still fits, emerging tooling gaps, the shift toward non deterministic design, and the long term architectural implications for teams building at scale.

Whether you are just starting to experiment with agentic systems or scaling them in production, this lively chat offers a glimpse into what leading AI practitioners think about the report and what is likely to come next.

The session concludes with a live audience Q and A.

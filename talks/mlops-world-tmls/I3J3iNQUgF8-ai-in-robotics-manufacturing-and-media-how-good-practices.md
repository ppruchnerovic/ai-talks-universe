Abstract:
Artificial intelligence is a frontline technology with profound implications for human beings, cultures, societies and the environment, as it is playing a central role in different application domains. AI can foster advanced robotics applications not only to extend the capabilities of different types of robots ranging from social companions to warehouse management robots but also to improve safety. In manufacturing, AI has advanced process automation, improved planning, and reduced losses for better quality and highly efficient productions. AI in media is used to automate internal processes, learn and engage with audiences, and create new ways for distribution and discovery of content. While AI has the undeniable potential to be our ally in creating a more equitable, fair, and sustainable future beyond the limits of the human mind and abilities, it could also exacerbate inequities, perpetuate human biases, and even create new ones. Panel participants will discuss how maximizing the benefits of AI and avoiding its pitfalls, will require addressing real and imminent challenges, looking at the question from both research and industry points of view.

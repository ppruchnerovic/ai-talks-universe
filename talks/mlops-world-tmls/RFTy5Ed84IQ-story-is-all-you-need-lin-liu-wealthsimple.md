## Description

🎥 From the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Virtual Session (October 7, 2025)

Session Title: Story Is All You Need
Speaker: Lin Liu, Director, Data Science, Wealthsimple
Talk Track: Scoping and Delivering Complex AI Projects (formerly Scoping ML Projects in an AI Era / AI Product Strategy & Delivery)

Abstract:
In the age of Generative AI, what if the most complex feature engineering could be replaced by simple storytelling?

In this talk, Lin Liu introduces a new paradigm for predictive analytics—transforming structured data like transaction logs or app usage data into coherent, text-based “stories.” These stories are then fed directly into Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate predictive scores, bypassing traditional modeling and feature engineering.

Through real-world case studies, Lin demonstrates how “data-as-story” can predict major life events from credit card transactions and detect fraud anomalies from app behavior narratives—often outperforming rule-based systems.

What you’ll learn:
• A practical framework for applying the “data-as-story” technique
• How storytelling can simplify the MLOps pipeline
• How to leverage LLMs for predictive analytics tasks traditionally handled by bespoke models
• Ways to unlock new predictive power and operational efficiency in real-world applications

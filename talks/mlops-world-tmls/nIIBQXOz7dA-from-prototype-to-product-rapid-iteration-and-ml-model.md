## Description

Speaker: Richie Frost: Software Engineer, Dropbox

As AI rapidly evolves, organizations must keep pace by iterating on ML models quickly. In the MLOps field, we face several challenges that make it hard to prototype and deploy quickly. In this presentation, we share how we leverage a mix of open-source tools and our own solutions at Dropbox to achieve faster iteration speeds, reducing prototyping and deployment times from weeks to under an hour.

## Description

Speakers:
Sasha McGrath, Account Executive, Appen
Geoff LaPorte, Adoption Program Manager, Applied AI, Appen

In an era driven by data and powered by artificial intelligence, the effectiveness of data science teams hinges upon access to high-quality data and robust collaboration tools. Our presentation unveils a comprehensive platform designed to revolutionize how data science projects are executed and AI models are deployed. At its core, our platform serves as a dynamic data asset, offering both off-the-shelf pre-trained datasets and access to a network of Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) who meticulously annotate, evaluate, and validate customer training data. Beyond data provision, our platform stands as an essential software solution, facilitating seamless collaboration and scalability for data training projects. Join us for an immersive presentation where we not only explore the features and capabilities of our platform but also demonstrate its practical applications through a live demo, showcasing its transformative potential for data science teams worldwide

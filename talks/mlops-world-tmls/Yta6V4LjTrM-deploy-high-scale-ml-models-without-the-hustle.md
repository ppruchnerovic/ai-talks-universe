## Description

Speaker:
Pavel Klushin, Head of Solutions Architecture, QWAK
Pavel is Qwak's Head of Solutions Architects, leading ML engineering efforts with various companies and helping companies to productionize their ML models.  He also previously led tech teams in Spot & Mamarm (IDF Tech unit).

Abstract:
Data scientists today can implement and train an ML model with predictive performance on an offline holdout dataset, given relevant training data for their use case. However, the real challenge isn't building an ML model, the challenge is building an integrated ML system and to continuously operate it in production. In the following workshop you will learn how to deliver a production-grade online model using the Qwak platform including Model training, testing, deployment, & monitoring.

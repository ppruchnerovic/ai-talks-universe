## Description

Speaker:
Shin Liang
Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Canva

Abstract:
Large language models (LLMs) have been widely adopted in many applications across different business. Crafting delightful content from diverse and messy inputs is one of the use cases that can be enhanced by LLMs. However, due to the subjectivity and unstructured nature of the space, it appears to be challenging to objectively evaluate the quality of the LLM outcomes on specific tasks, such as providing detailed summaries on design inputs based on design types.
This talk explores an evaluation framework on the quality of LLM outputs for transforming diverse and messy textual inputs into delightful outputs with certain criteria. It not only considers the general LLM evaluation metrics such as relevance, fluency and coherence, but also specific metrics such as overall information preservation rate, accuracy of titles / headings understanding, and key information extraction score etc. This evaluation framework with measurable metrics can be generalised with similar LLM tasks.

## Description

🎥 From GenAI World: Tools, Infra & Open Source Stack — Virtual Session (July 29, 2025)

Session Title: Scaling AI: Observability Across Models, Data Pipelines, and Infrastructure
Speaker: George Miranda, VP of Marketing, InsightFinder AI
Category: Agent Lifecycle Management

Abstract:
As AI models move from pilot to production, ensuring reliability and performance becomes increasingly complex. Traditional monitoring tools struggle to identify the interplay between model behavior, data quality, and infrastructure health.

In this session, George Miranda explores the challenges of delivering reliable and responsible enterprise AI, and introduces a new approach to intelligent observability, evaluation, and governance built to meet them.

Through real-world case studies, George shares how enterprises can enhance trust, accountability, and performance in production AI systems.

Attendees will learn:
• Why conventional observability tools fall short for enterprise-scale AI/ML
• How to detect and mitigate model/data drift, data quality issues, hallucinations, and prompt injections
• Ways to correlate infrastructure anomalies with model behavior in production
• Best practices for scaling reliable, cost-efficient AI systems

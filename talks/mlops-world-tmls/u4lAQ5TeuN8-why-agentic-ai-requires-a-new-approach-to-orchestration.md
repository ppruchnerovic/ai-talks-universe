## Description

🎥 From GenAI World: Tools, Infra & Open Source Stack — Virtual Session (July 29, 2025)

Session Title: Why Agentic AI Requires a New Approach to Orchestration
Speaker: Niels Bantilan, Chief ML Engineer, Union.ai
Category: Workflow Orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, etc.)

Abstract:
Today’s AI orchestration stack was built for a different world—one of static, batch pipelines. But AI agents aren’t batch jobs; they operate continuously, adaptively, and responsively to real-time inputs.

In this session, Niels Bantilan explores why Agentic AI demands a new generation of orchestration tools designed for dynamic decision-making and resource-aware coordination across models, APIs, GPUs, and CPUs.

Using real-world examples from the Flyte 2.0 open-source release, Niels outlines three key principles for modern orchestration systems:
- Durability and reliability
- Dynamic, flexible workflow support
- Observability between and within tasks

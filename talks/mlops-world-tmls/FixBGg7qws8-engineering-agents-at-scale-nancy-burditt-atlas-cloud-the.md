## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 9, 2025).

Session Title: Context Under Pressure: Engineering Agents at Scale
Speaker: Nancy Burditt, Customer Engagement Manager, Atlas Cloud

Abstract:
In this session, Nancy Burditt from Atlas Cloud explores how enterprises can scale digital workers, insight engines, and diagnostic agents to deliver measurable value across business operations. As organizations race to deploy AI agents, the challenge lies in maintaining context, reliability, and adaptability under real-world pressure.

Nancy discusses practical strategies for engineering agents that can operate efficiently at enterprise scale — emphasizing context retention, diagnostic accuracy, and seamless integration with human workflows.

What you’ll learn:
• How to design digital workers and diagnostic agents for large-scale environments
• Why context engineering is critical to reliability and adaptability
• How to integrate agentic systems into enterprise workflows for real-time insights
• Key lessons from scaling AI-powered engagement tools in production

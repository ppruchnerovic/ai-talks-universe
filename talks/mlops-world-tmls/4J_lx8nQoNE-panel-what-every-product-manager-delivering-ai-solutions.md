## Description

Speakers:
Jessie Lamontagne, Data Science Manager, Kinaxis
Jessie Lamontagne is a data science manager at Kinaxis, where she leads the delivery of a demand forecasting product. Before joining Kinaxis she built machine learning solutions for credit risk modelling, and developed deep learning algorithms for synthetic data generation. She completed graduate work in economics during which time she taught statistics, microeconomics, and applied policy.

Nahla Salem, Senior Product Manager, Yelp
Nahla Salem specializes in product managing B2B Machine Learning products. She leads a team in Yelp using ML to help acquire, engage, and retain Yelp's Local Businesses. Before Yelp, Nahla built end-to-end ML products in multiple growth-stage Toronto start-ups where she also built ML and Product teams. Customers she worked with include Fortune-50 Manufacturing conglomerate, United Technologies, and the world’s largest health and beauty Retail group A.S. Watson. He has led the development of data-driven products in the supply chain, retail, fintech and telecom spaces.

Anneya Golob, Staff Data Scientist, Shopify

Abstract:
Good product management can be the difference between building a cool product nobody uses, and building a product that has a huge impact on the lives on millions. In this panel, we’ll discuss the importance of product management in AI/ML, from the perspective of product owners, data science leaders, and product managers. We’ll share examples of how the relationship between product managers and development teams can be optimized to solve the problem of end user adoption, while also reflecting on times when broken lines of communication led to products that did not meet end users expectations.

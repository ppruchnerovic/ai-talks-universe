## Description

Speaker: Mikyo King, Head of Open Source & Founding Engineer, Arize AI

As teams race to incorporate generative AI in their applications, the need for observability and evaluation is critical. This is often easier said than done since LLMs deal wit h probabilities and not determinism.

That's partly why Mikyo King – Head of Open Source and Founding Engineer at Arize AI and a former Apple engineer – assembled an open-source team to build:
- OpenInference, a new open set of plugins and conventions that enables tracing of AI applications; and
- Arize-Phoenix, a platform that offers AI evaluation and observability

After a quick overview of each, King will go into a demo of Phoenix through the lens of several real-world examples and use cases. The presentation will include practical guidance for practitioners, such as how to incorporate LLMs as a judge to guard against hallucinations poor retrieval.

Dr. Ehsan Amjadian earned his Ph.D. in Deep Learning & Natural Language Processing from Carleton University, Canada. He is published in a variety of additional Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science domains including Recommender Engines, Information Extraction, Computer Vision, and Cybersecurity.

Dr. Amjadian is currently the Head of Data Science at the Royal Bank of Canada (RBC), where he has led numerous advanced AI products from ideation to production and has filed multiple patents in the areas of Data Protection, Finance & Climate, and Computer Vision applications to Satellite Images.

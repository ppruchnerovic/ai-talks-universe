## Description

Speaker:
Josh Goldstein, Solutions Engineer, Seldon
Josh Goldstein leads the US Solutions Engineering team at Seldon, where he knits client needs with Seldon’s deployment capabilities to help organisations realise the potential of ML across presales and customer success. Previously, he has worked with a number of organisations in the search and recommendations industry, building bespoke solutions for the digital workplace and commerce.

Abstract:
The workshop will cover what organisations need to understand and have in place in order to deploy ML models at scale while minimising risk. They'll then walk the attendees through the creation, deployment, monitoring and management of a ML model using a real life use case.
- Delegates will get an introduction to the key challenges that organisations face when scaling up their ML deployment and how to overcome them
- They'll also get hands-on experience with ML deployment as they cover a range of practical tasks including training models on pre-processed datasets, deploying model artefacts with Seldon Deploy and using explainers and outlier detection to gain insight into model decisioning

Speaker's Bio:
Danny Luo
Machine Learning Engineer, Nextdoor

Machine learning engineer with passion in actualizing the value of AI and proven experience in implementing ML solutions in large, complex enterprises. Educator & public speaker of ML.

Abstract:
Running a ML inference layer in a shared hosting environment (ECS, K8s, etc.) comes with a number of unobvious pitfalls that have significant impact on latency and throughput. In this talk, we describe how Nextdoor’s ML team experienced these issues, discovered their sources and fixed them, and in the end received latency drops of a factor of 4, throughput increases of 3x and improved resource utilization while maintaining performance. The main points of concern are request queue management and OpenMP parameter tuning.

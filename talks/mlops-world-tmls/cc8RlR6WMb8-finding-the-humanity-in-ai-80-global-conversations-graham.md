## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 8, 2025)

Session Title: Finding the Humanity in AI: 80+ Global Conversations
Speaker: Graham Toppin, Co-Founder, PeerLabs.ai

Abstract:
When exploring GenAI’s impact for an upcoming TMLS panel, Graham Toppin began by asking a few colleagues for their perspectives. Those 15 conversations quickly turned into 80+ dialogues with people around the world, spanning technologists, artists, educators, and entrepreneurs — all grappling with the same question: what does it mean to be human in an AI-driven world?

In this lightning talk, Graham shares heartfelt insights from this global journey, reflecting on how people everywhere are learning to coexist with AI — their hopes, fears, and the surprising ways technology is reshaping identity, creativity, and connection.

What you’ll learn:
• How people across industries and cultures are experiencing AI’s rise
• The emotional and ethical dimensions often overlooked in AI innovation
• Why centering the human experience is essential to building better AI systems

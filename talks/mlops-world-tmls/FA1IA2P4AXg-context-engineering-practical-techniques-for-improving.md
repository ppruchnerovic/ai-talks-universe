## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 9, 2025)

Session Title: Context Engineering: Practical Techniques for Improving Agent Quality Today
Speaker: Vaibhav Gupta, CEO, Boundary
Talk Track: Agents in Production

Abstract:
Buzzwords like few-shot prompting, reasoning, tool calling, and structured outputs dominate today’s AI discussions — but at their core, they’re all attempts to achieve the same goal: getting the model to do exactly what you want.

In this session, Vaibhav Gupta, CEO of Boundary, reframes these concepts under a single unifying discipline: Context Engineering. He offers a practical, hands-on guide to understanding what “context” really means in the world of LLMs and how to engineer it effectively to improve agent reliability and performance.

Attendees will gain actionable insights for improving model behavior today — not through bigger models, but through smarter design and surrounding tooling.

What you’ll learn:
• What “context” truly represents in modern AI systems
• How to apply general prompting and reasoning strategies with structure
• Practical ways to improve reliability and control in your agents
• Why tooling — not just model size — determines quality outcomes
• Real-world examples of context engineering improving production systems

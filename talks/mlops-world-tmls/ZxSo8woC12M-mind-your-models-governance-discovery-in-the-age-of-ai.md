## Description

🎥 From the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Virtual Session (October 7, 2025)

Session Title: Mind Your Models: Governance and Discovery in the Age of AI Sprawl
Speaker: Bill Manning, Senior Solution Architect, JFrog, Inc.
Talk Track: Governance, Auditability & Model Risk Management

Abstract:
AI is everywhere and so are your models. From notebooks buried in shared drives to experiments lost in forgotten folders, model sprawl has become the silent killer of innovation, efficiency, and compliance.

In this session, Bill Manning from JFrog takes a witty yet insightful look at how organizations can govern, track, and trust their AI assets in an era of exponential model growth. Discover how the JFrog AI Catalog provides a single source of truth for everything machine learning — from models and datasets to metadata, lineage, and beyond.

Whether you’re an MLOps engineer, data scientist, or compliance lead, you’ll learn how to bring order and observability to AI chaos.

What you’ll learn:
• How to manage and track AI assets efficiently across the organization
• How to reduce model sprawl through centralized governance and discovery
• How JFrog’s AI Catalog enables transparency, auditability, and compliance
• Best practices for establishing trust in your AI supply chain

## Description

Speaker: Emmanuel Turlay: CEO, Sematic

Today, most AI developers resort to manual prompting to evaluate the various available LLMs. A more rigorous approach is to leverage batch evaluation using an LLM to evaluate and grade outputs at a large scale. We will show how Airtrain can be used to grade open-source LLMs on custom metrics using your own dataset.

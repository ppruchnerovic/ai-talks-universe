## Description

Speaker: Intae Rhyoo: Co-founder & CPO, VESSL AI

Despite the onset of commercially viable open-source Large Langauge Models, companies are struggling to leverage cutting-edge models like Llama2 and Mistral 7B for production-ready applications. Creating a simple demo page on a personal laptop and training, fine-tuning, and serving multi-billion parameter LLMs on HPC-scale infrastructure - with proprietary enterprise data - involves an entirely different engineering challenge. In this session, Intae, who co-founded and now leads product development at VESSL AI, will explore how companies can leverage MLOps infrastructure to go from a simple model playground to deploying enterprise-scale pipelines for LLMs.

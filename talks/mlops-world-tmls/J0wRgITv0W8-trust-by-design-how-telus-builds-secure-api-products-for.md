Abstract:
As enterprises race to build AI-powered products and services, APIs are becoming
more than technical interfaces. They are the foundation for new business models, partner
ecosystems, and scalable digital distribution. But turning APIs into trusted products inside a
large enterprise is not just a technical challenge. It requires alignment across product strategy, privacy, and governance. In this session, we will share lessons from TELUS on operationalizing external-facing APIs in a complex enterprise environment, with a focus on the real-world decisions that shape trust, adoption, and partner value. To ground the discussion, we will demo our Number Verify API, a network capability that illustrates trustworthy API architecture in practice. By leveraging signals from the mobile network rather than user-entered information, Number Verify enables seamless and secure authentication. Just as importantly, it positions us for an agentic future, where AI agents can autonomously verify users on their behalf — dramatically reducing friction in the user experience while maintaining strong identity assurance. Rather than presenting APIs as standalone technical products, this session frames trustworthy API architecture as the foundation for the AI economy: a system that must make access safe, scalable, and commercially viable. Attendees will leave with a practical playbook for evaluating API and AI productization, how to design governance into the product from day one, and how to bridge the gap between technical possibility and market adoption.

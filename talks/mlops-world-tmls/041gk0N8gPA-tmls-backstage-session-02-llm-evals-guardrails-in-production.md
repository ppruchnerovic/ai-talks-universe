## Description

🎥 From TMLS Backstage Session 02 — Virtual Broadcast (2025)

Session Title: LLM Evals & Guardrails in Production
Host: Aishwarya Naresh Reganti, Founder, LevelUp Labs
Guests: Rachitt Kumar, Applied AI Consultant & Claire Longo, Lead AI Researcher, Comet

In this first edition, host Aishwarya Naresh Reganti (Founder, LevelUp Labs) will be joined by Rachitt Kumar (Applied AI consultant) and Claire Longo (Lead AI Researcher, Comet) for a conversation on how to evaluate and monitor GenAI applications using LLM Evals and guardrails. The foundational tools for building safer, more reliable AI systems.

This will be an engaging discussion on what it really takes to move beyond vibe checks and build GenAI systems that can be trusted at scale. From aligning GenAI performance with business KPIs to deploying monitoring frameworks that catch failures before your users do, these experts will walk through actionable strategies based on real-world experience.

Whether you’re launching chatbots, automating content, or building agentic tools, this session will give you a clearer understanding of how to evaluate quality, detect issues, and reduce risk in production.

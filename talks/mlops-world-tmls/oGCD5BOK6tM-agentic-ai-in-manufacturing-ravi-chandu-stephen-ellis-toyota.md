## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 8, 2025)

Session Title: Agentic AI in Manufacturing
Speakers:
• Ravi Chandu, Generative AI Architect, Toyota
• Stephen Ellis, Generative AI Technical Product Owner, Toyota
Talk Track: Agents in Production

Abstract:
How can agentic AI systems revolutionize manufacturing, battery production, and supply chain operations?

In this forward-looking session, Ravi Chandu and Stephen Ellis from Toyota explore how Generative AI and agentic frameworks are transforming industrial processes — from automating repetitive tasks to enhancing decision-making with real-time analytics.

They share how AI agents enable human-AI collaboration, helping teams shift focus from maintenance to innovation, and from manual oversight to strategic optimization. Attendees will gain a clear understanding of how Toyota is leveraging Agentic AI to enhance operational efficiency, promote sustainability, and prepare its global operations for the future.

What you’ll learn:
• How Agentic AI is being applied across manufacturing and supply chain systems
• How AI agents can automate tasks while maintaining human oversight and control
• Frameworks for integrating Generative AI into large-scale production workflows
• Real-world examples of sustainability-driven AI initiatives in manufacturing
• Why adopting Agentic AI is key to future-proofing industrial operations

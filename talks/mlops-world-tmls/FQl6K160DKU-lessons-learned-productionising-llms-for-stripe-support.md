## Description

Speaker:
Sophie Daly
Staff Data Scientist, Stripe

Abstract:
Large Language Models are an especially exciting opportunity for Support Operations: they excel at answering questions, completing sentences, and summarising text while requiring ~100x less training data than the previous generation of models.

In this talk, Sophie discusses lessons learned productionising Stripe’s first application of Large Language Modelling - providing answers to user questions for Stripe Support.

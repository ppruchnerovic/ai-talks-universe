## Description

Speaker Bio:
Gennady Pekhimenko, Assistant Professor at University of Toronto and Faculty Member / AI Chair at Vector Institute
Gennady Pekhimenko is an Assistant Professor at the University of Toronto, CS department and (by courtesy) ECE department, where he is leading the EcoSystem (Efficient Computing Systems) group. Gennady is also a Faculty Member at Vector Institute and a CIFAR AI chair. Before joining Univ. of Toronto, he spent a year in 2017 at Microsoft Research in Redmond in Systems Research group. He got his PhD from the Computer Science Department at Carnegie Mellon University in 2016. Gennady is a recipient of Amazon Machine Learning Research Award, Facebook Faculty Research Award, Connaught New Researcher Award, NVIDIA Graduate, Microsoft Research, Qualcomm Innovation, and NSERC CGS-D Fellowships. His research interests are in the areas of systems, computer architecture, and applied machine learning.

Abstract:
In this talk, I will present two recently developed tools for ML optimization: Skyline and RL-Scope. Skyline: Interactive in-editor computational performance profiling, visualization, and debugging for PyTorch deep neural networks (https://skylineprof.github.io/). RL-Scope  is a cross-stack profiler for deep reinforcement learning workloads (https://rl-scope.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).

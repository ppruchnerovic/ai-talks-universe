Abstract:
At Udemy, multi-faceted growth created different challenges in terms of ML platform and tooling as well as the related processes. One such challenge was to come up with a scalable platform to train and execute different types of ML models in real-time or batch. We overcame this particular challenge by building generic components that increased reuse and led to faster delivery and lower maintenance costs. Another challenge was to find a good way to efficiently serve the needs of the different parts of the organization despite their varying requirements. For instance, distributed applications cannot be avoided for many of our use cases that deal with web-scale data, but there are still a significant number of cases which can be more efficiently handled without the complexities of distributed applications. To overcome this challenge, we had to unify the frameworks (and tooling whenever possible) for both cases, mainly to decrease the learning curve for data scientists and increase the maintainability. Another type of challenge we faced was the need for increased focus on developer and data science ergonomics as the organization grew. In this talk, I will present an overview of the main ML-related challenges at Udemy as the company experienced explosive growth in users, product complexity, and organization size. I will also present the best practices we developed so far and the outstanding problems in our current state.

## Description

🎥 Recorded live at the MLOps World | GenAI Summit 2025 — Austin, TX (October 9, 2025).

Session Title: Build the Next Generation of Agent Workforce with AG2
Speaker: Dr. Qingyun Wu, Founder & CEO, AG2

Abstract:
The future of work is being redefined — not just by humans, but by AI agents working as a coordinated digital workforce.

In this session, Dr. Qingyun Wu, CEO of AG2, reveals how organizations are already deploying multi-agent systems to automate complex workflows and achieve measurable business impact.

Through real-world case studies, Qingyun demonstrates how AG2’s platform powers automation across industries — from accelerating legal case preparation and software testing in regulated environments to orchestrating enterprise workflows across tools like Slack, Google Docs, and APIs.

A live demo of the AG2 Agent Playground showcases how natural-language instructions can instantly transform into coordinated, multi-agent workflows, turning the agent workforce vision into reality.

What you’ll learn:
• How multi-agent systems are shaping the future of work
• Real-world examples of agentic automation in legal, software, and enterprise operations
• How AG2’s Agent Playground converts natural language into orchestrated workflows
• The architecture behind scalable agent collaboration
• How to deploy and manage a digital agent workforce in production

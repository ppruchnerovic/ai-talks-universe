## Description

💻 Abstract:
This is an Ops guy's story about when we had to build scalable deep learning pipelines for hundreds of model training on giant time-series datasets and how we saved 80% of our cost along the way. A tale of a thousand pipelines.

🔊 Speaker bio:
Benedikt Koller- Co-Founder / CTO – maiot
I'm a seasoned SRE/Opsguy with 11+ years of experience in data-heavy companies (eCommerce, SaaS, advertising). For over two years now I'm one of two CTOs of maiot, a Munich-based AI startup. Originally focused on predictive maintenance/asset optimization of industrial assets and commercial vehicles, we're now making our internal tech stack available to a broad audience.

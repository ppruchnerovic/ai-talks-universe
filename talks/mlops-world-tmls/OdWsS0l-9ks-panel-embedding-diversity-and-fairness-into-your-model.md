Dr. Kathryn Hume is the Vice President of Digital Investments Technology at the Royal Bank of Canada, where she leads a team of software engineers building platforms for advice-driven and self-directed investing. Arthur Berrill, Head of Data Science, Royal Bank of Canada (RBC)

Arthur is an RBC Distinguished Technologist.
Arthur has more than 40 years of experience in the architecture, design and development of enterprise spatial systems including WILDMAP (a GIS before the term existed), SYSTEM 9, SpatialWare®, MapInfo products and Location Hub®. Fascinated by the use of new technology to solve business challenges,

Arthur is a graduate with Honours from the University of Queensland and did his postgraduate work at the International Institute of Aerial Survey and Earth Sciences in the Netherlands.

Abstract:
This Session will feature 3 prominent thought leaders as they discuss Model Governance at Scale and focus on helpful insights for the audience in terms of technical and ethical approaches to model governance. Also, they'll be spending some time thinking about the future and what Model Governance 2.0 may look and how you can approach this to ensure success.

They'll discuss:
Past
• How has your journey evolved over the past few years?
• What has changed from when you had single-digit models in prod?
Present
• What are the motivations for MLOps? Are they internal (better control of your processes) or external (legislation)?
• What are promising pieces of technology or ideas? Who’s doing it right?
• Why should folks in the audience care about bias? What should they do about it? What are the pitfalls?
• Model cards are becoming widely accepted, what should they contain?
Future
• What is your North Star when it comes to model automation?
• What are promising pieces of technology/research on bias/analysis?

Category: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Abstract:
RAG in 15 minutes? Let’s make it happen.
In this hands-on session, Rajiv Shah walks through how to build an end-to-end Agentic RAG pipeline and demonstrates its integration with the Model Context Protocol (MCP). He covers all the key components of a RAG system, what to look for, how to evaluate them, and how to deploy them efficiently.

By the end of the talk, you’ll have a clear understanding of what makes a RAG system truly production-ready and how to deploy one in minutes using Contextual AI’s managed RAG solution.

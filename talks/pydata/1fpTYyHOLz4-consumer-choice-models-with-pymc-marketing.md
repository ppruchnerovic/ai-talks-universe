Description:
Nathaniel Forde introduces PyMC Marketing's new consumer choice module, demonstrating how Bayesian discrete choice models can help businesses understand market dynamics and predict consumer behavior. The talk explores the limitations of traditional multinomial logit models, particularly the "independence of irrelevant alternatives" problem that leads to implausible market predictions. Forde shows how nested logit models solve this by encoding market structure, allowing for realistic counterfactual simulations. Using a heating systems dataset, he demonstrates how these models reveal which product attributes drive consumer utility and enable businesses to safely simulate product interventions and strategy changes. The presentation includes practical examples of causal inference using PyMC Marketing's intuitive API, showing how companies can test pricing strategies and feature additions before implementation.

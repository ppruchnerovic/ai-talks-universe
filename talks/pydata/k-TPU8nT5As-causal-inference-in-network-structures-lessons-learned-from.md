Speakers:
Danial Senejohnny

Description:
Measuring the true impact of wealth planning services on client investment behavior presents unique challenges when working with observational data in network structures. This talk explores practical solutions for applying causal inference techniques when A/B testing isn't feasible. Danial Senejohnny from ABN AMRO shares lessons from real-world financial services, addressing challenges like complex relationship graphs between individuals and companies, staggered treatment timing, and multiple outcome modalities (binary and time-series). The presentation covers propensity score matching to mimic randomization, staggered difference-in-difference methods for treatments administered at different times, and strategies for validating results without ground truth. Key insights include simplifying network structures to units, blocking confounding relationships, and evaluating impact across multiple KPIs. This practical guide demonstrates how to adapt standard causal inference methods to messy real-world scenarios where controlled experiments are impossible.

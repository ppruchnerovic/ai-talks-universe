## Description

Nikolai Semiachkin presents a talk on
“LLMs and Prediction Markets: Estimating Probability of Unique Events.”

As data scientists, we are good at predicting regularly occurring events - purchases, clicks, churn. But some events are one of a kind and have a major impact on the world, making them difficult to model with standard data-driven approaches.

In this talk, Nikolai explores how we can estimate the probability of such unique events. He starts with traditional human analytical methods and then shows how large language models (LLMs) and prediction markets can be used to support forecasting, drawing on results from AI forecasting tournaments and recent research.

In this session, Nikolai discusses:
🔹 Why standard predictive models fail for one-time events
🔹 How human reasoning can be structured for forecasting
🔹 How LLMs are being used to automate and support probability estimation

This talk was recorded during the PyData Yerevan October 2025 Meetup, held on October 30, 2025, at the American University of Armenia (AUA).

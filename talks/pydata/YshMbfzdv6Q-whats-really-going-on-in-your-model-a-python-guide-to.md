Description:
As machine learning models grow more complex, understanding their predictions becomes critical. This practical talk explores explainable AI (XAI) tools in Python that help data scientists and engineers build transparent, trustworthy models. Yashasvi Misra demonstrates three powerful libraries—SHAP, LIME, and Captum—using the MNIST dataset to show how each tool reveals what's happening inside your models. SHAP uses game theory to show feature contributions globally and locally, LIME provides quick local explanations by perturbing inputs, and Captum offers deep learning insights for PyTorch models. The talk covers real-world applications in healthcare, finance, and operations where explainability isn't optional—it's essential for compliance with regulations like GDPR and the EU AI Act. You'll learn common pitfalls to avoid, how to integrate XAI into your ML lifecycle, and why 72% of organizations now prioritize explainability for wider AI adoption.

## Description

🔊 Recorded at PyData Berlin 2025,

🎓 Running a 1500-person conference with volunteers? Learn how PyCon DE & PyData automated chaos with AI agents—and what actually works.

Speakers:
Alexander CS Hendorf

Description:
PyCon DE & PyData volunteers manage a 1500-person conference entirely in their spare time. To survive the chaos of speaker logistics, video processing, ticket management, and marketing, they've built an automation stack powered by AI agents and structured workflows. This talk shares honest, practical lessons from three months of intensive experimentation with agentic AI tools like Claude, Gemini, and various coding assistants. Alexander CS Hendorf reveals what worked (LinkedIn scraping, video cutting with computer vision, API integration), what failed spectacularly (data normalization, multi-step pipelines), and why structure, validation, and narrow scope matter more than hype. With live examples and open-source tools, this case study demonstrates that AI agents are powerful time-savers for boilerplate and well-documented tasks—but they're still 'useful parrots' that need tight guardrails. Perfect for anyone building community infrastructure or exploring practical AI automation.

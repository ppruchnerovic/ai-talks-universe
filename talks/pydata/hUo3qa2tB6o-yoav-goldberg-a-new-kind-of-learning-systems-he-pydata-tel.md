## Description

After charting the space of LLM-based systems and establishing a framework around them, I will switch gears to discuss what I see as a huge open-frontier that is enabled by them: a new kind of learning systems, that learn not by optimizing parameters with respect to a numeric loss or reward, but rather by processing, storing, retrieving and manipulating natural language assertions.

Speaker Bio:
Yoav Goldberg is a professor at Bar Ilan University's Computer Science Department, as well as the Research Director of the Israeli branch of the Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence

Speakers:
Laura Summers, Andy Kitchen

Description:
In this creative and thought-provoking keynote, speakers Laura Summers and Andy Kitchen present a satirical 'time travel' narrative from the year 2077, looking back at the 2020s era of data science and AI. Through humor and speculative fiction, they critique contemporary AI hype, the limitations of correlation-based thinking, and the narrow imagination constraining our field's future. The talk challenges the audience to think beyond dystopian narratives and technological determinism, advocating for causality over correlation, community over tools, and imagination as a core scientific practice. Blending comedy with serious commentary on AI alignment, hyperstition, and the cultural anxieties of our era, the speakers argue that the future isn't predetermined—it's something we actively create through our choices, communities, and willingness to imagine alternatives. The presentation concludes with a call to break free from 'realist mind vices' and recognize that technology serves human flourishing, not the other way around.

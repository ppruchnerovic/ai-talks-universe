🎓 Why the future of tech depends on care, not just code—and how maintenance work is a radical act of resistance.

Speakers:
Jessica Greene

Description:
Jessica Greene challenges tech's "move fast and break things" culture by reframing maintenance as essential, radical work. Drawing from her experience as both a software engineer and PyLadies Berlin organizer, she explores how AI's rapid adoption is creating unsustainable pressures on open-source maintainers while devaluing care work—labor historically gendered and marginalized. Greene examines the hidden costs of AI-generated code flooding repositories, the precarious funding of foundational projects like Python, and how current systems perpetuate inequality. She advocates for pro-social AI development, feminist tech principles, and collective stewardship as alternatives to extractive velocity. The talk connects technical practices like code review and documentation to broader questions of labor, gender equity, and environmental sustainability, arguing that intentional maintenance is how we build resilient, inclusive futures worth inheriting.

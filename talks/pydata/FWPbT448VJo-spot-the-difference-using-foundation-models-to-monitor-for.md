🎓 Using foundation models and satellite imagery to detect pipeline threats before they become disasters 🛰️

Speakers:
Ferdinand Schenck

Description:
LiveEO's Ferdinand Schenck reveals how geospatial foundation models revolutionize pipeline monitoring through satellite imagery. Operating pipelines buried 1-1.5 meters underground face constant threats from construction, erosion, and unauthorized digging. Traditional inspection methods are costly and inefficient, but very high-resolution satellite imagery (sub-meter ground sampling distance) offers a scalable solution.

The challenge? Distinguishing semantic changes (actual construction threats) from non-semantic changes (shadows, seasonal variations, viewing angles). With limited labeled data—just 1,600 training patches—LiveEO leveraged foundation models pre-trained on vast satellite imagery datasets. Their experiments compared models from ChangeFormer to DINOv2, testing whether geospatial-specific training, ground sampling distance, and model size matter.

Results showed foundation models trained on high-resolution remote sensing data significantly outperformed smaller models and those trained only on natural images. The breakthrough: combining masked autoencoder pre-training with multitask learning on common remote sensing tasks before fine-tuning for change detection. This approach enables accurate monitoring across 60 countries with bi-weekly to bi-monthly revisit rates, protecting critical energy infrastructure at scale.

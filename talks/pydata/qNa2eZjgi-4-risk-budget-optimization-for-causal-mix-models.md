Description:
Traditional budget optimization chases the highest predicted returns while ignoring uncertainty, often leading to missed targets and wasted commitments. This talk demonstrates how Bayesian Media Mix Models with PyMC Marketing can transform budget allocation by explicitly accounting for risk. Carlos Trujillo shows how posterior distributions reveal thousands of possible futures, enabling marketers to optimize spending based on risk tolerance rather than point estimates alone. Through practical examples, he illustrates how ignoring uncertainty can lead to allocations with less than 43% probability of hitting targets, while risk-aware optimization using custom utility functions—like mean-times-score or conditional value-at-risk—produces more reliable outcomes. Attendees learn to balance aggressive growth with resilience by encoding risk preferences into optimization objectives, visualizing trade-offs for stakeholders, and making defensible capital allocation decisions grounded in transparent Bayesian reasoning.

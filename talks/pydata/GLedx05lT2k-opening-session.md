Description:
The PyData Berlin 2025 Opening Session welcomes over 480 attendees and 100+ remote participants to one of Europe's premier data science conferences. The organizing team introduces the conference structure, highlighting 180 talk submissions with a competitive 28% acceptance rate, reviewed by 34 volunteers who submitted 600+ reviews. Key themes include community building through the "Pac-Man rule" for inclusive networking, sustainability goals focusing on gender equality and knowledge sharing, and a robust diversity program that supported 19 participants from various countries. The session covers practical information about tutorials, lightning talks, the code of conduct, and acknowledges platinum sponsors Get Your Guide and DLT Hub, along with gold and silver sponsors supporting the event.

⭐️ About PyData Berlin:
Since 2014, PyData Berlin has brought together the international Python and data community.
With monthly meetups and a yearly conference, it connects data engineers, scientists, and tool-builders to share ideas, explore open-source technologies, and drive innovation together.

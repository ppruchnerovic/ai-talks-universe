## Description

🔊 Recorded at PyData Berlin 2025,

🎓 From basic Postgres to production-grade semantic search: Building AI-powered aviation safety search with pgvector, embeddings, and reranking.

Speakers:
Dat Tran, Dennis Schmidt

Description:
In aviation safety, search is challenging—pilots and crew use technical jargon, abbreviations, and inconsistent terminology that breaks traditional keyword matching. This talk chronicles the journey from a simple Postgres setup to a robust semantic search system handling hundreds of thousands of safety reports. Starting with pgvector and basic embeddings, the team evolved their architecture to include advanced sentence embeddings, cross-encoder reranking with OpenVINO optimization, and hybrid search combining semantic retrieval with structured filters. Learn about practical trade-offs between HNSW and IVFFlat indexes, batching strategies for AWS Lambda, handling multilingual reports, and building complex filter systems that combine AI search with 35+ structured attributes. The speakers share real bottlenecks, performance optimizations, and lessons from deploying semantic search in a safety-critical industry—all while keeping infrastructure minimal and maintainable.

## Description

🔊 Recorded at PyData Berlin 2025,

🎓 Learn machine learning concepts through interactive games—no computers required! From decision trees to LLMs, make complex topics fun.

Speakers:
Dr. Kristian Rother, Shreyaasri Prakash

Description:
This innovative tutorial demonstrates how to teach machine learning concepts through interactive games that require no computers. Dr. Kristian Rother and Shreyaasri Prakash guide participants through hands-on activities that illustrate decision trees, random forests, bag-of-words models, language models, and AI hallucination. The session includes games like building decision trees with animal classifications, predicting text documents from word clouds, simulating small language models through collaborative word prediction, and exploring the 'wisdom of the crowd' effect. Participants work in teams to experience how these games can make complex ML concepts accessible and memorable. The presenters, drawing on experience from university courses and board game education certification, share ready-to-use materials under Creative Commons license. Perfect for educators, trainers, and anyone interested in making technical concepts more engaging and understandable.

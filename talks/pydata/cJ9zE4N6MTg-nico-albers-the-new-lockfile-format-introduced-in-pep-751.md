## Description

In March 2025, [PEP 751](https://peps.python.org/pep-0751/) got accepted, proposing an new format how lockfiles should be structured. The talk will give a brief history of this PEP (and it's [rejected predecessor](https://peps.python.org/pep-0665/)), introduce you to the proposed `pylock.toml` format and discuss (subjective) highlights of this PEP. Afterwards, a practical example how this PEP could improve managing your environments will be discussed.

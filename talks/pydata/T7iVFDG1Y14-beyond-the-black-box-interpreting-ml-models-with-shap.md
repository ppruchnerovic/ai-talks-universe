Description:
As ML models become more complex, understanding their decisions becomes critical. This talk introduces SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), a powerful technique for interpreting model predictions across different architectures. Avik Basu, Staff Data Scientist at Intuit, explains the mathematical foundation of Shapley values from game theory and demonstrates practical applications through two comprehensive case studies: a banking term deposit prediction using XGBoost and human activity recognition using CNNs. Learn how to interpret various SHAP visualizations including bee swarm plots and dependency plots, understand feature contributions at both global and local levels, and discover best practices for handling large datasets. The talk also covers important limitations like feature correlation issues and computational complexity, ensuring you can effectively apply SHAP to your own projects while avoiding common pitfalls.

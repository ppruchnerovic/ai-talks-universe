Speakers:
Laura Dumont

Description:
Laura Dumont, Senior ML Engineer at Owkin, shares practical insights from building Owkin-K Navigator, a free AI co-pilot for biological research. This talk addresses the unique challenges of healthcare AI where accuracy is non-negotiable. Learn how to combine natural language processing with SQL querying and vector database retrieval to navigate massive biomedical knowledge sources like PubMed's 26 million abstracts. Dumont covers critical topics including preventing hallucinations, ensuring proper source attribution, handling domain-specific terminology, and managing the complexities of heterogeneous biological databases. The presentation includes real-world lessons on hybrid search strategies, structured output enforcement, cost optimization, and the importance of building custom benchmarks with domain experts rather than relying on potentially flawed public benchmarks.

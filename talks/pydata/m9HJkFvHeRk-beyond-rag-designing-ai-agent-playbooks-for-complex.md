## Description

RAG can retrieve information and generate answers, but complex customer support cases often require understanding underlying problems, identifying resolution steps, and executing backend actions. This session explores multi-stage LLM analysis, actionable playbooks, agent evaluation, capability gaps, and balancing accuracy, cost, and scalability.

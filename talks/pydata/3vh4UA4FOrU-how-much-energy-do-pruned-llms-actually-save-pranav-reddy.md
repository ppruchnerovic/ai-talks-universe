## Description

Pranav Reddy Danda presents an experimental study on the real-world energy impact of structured pruning in Llama large language models fine-tuned for Python code generation. While pruning is widely expected to reduce computational costs by removing less important neurons, the results show that inference energy does not always decrease and can even increase beyond certain pruning levels. The talk explores the relationship between model compression, accuracy, and hardware efficiency, highlighting why theoretical reductions in model size do not necessarily translate into lower energy consumption on modern GPUs.

## Description

🎓 How GetYourGuide used fine-tuned LLMs to automate travel content creation, reducing defects by 17% and boosting bookings by 28%

Speakers:
Marco Vene

Description:
GetYourGuide faced a critical challenge: suppliers creating 155,000+ travel activities manually resulted in inconsistent content, grammar errors, and poor adherence to guidelines—all negatively impacting conversion rates. This talk reveals how they leveraged LLMs to transform content creation. Marco Vene shares their journey building fine-tuned GPT-4 models for generating titles, descriptions, and highlights, plus using Function Calling API for structured data extraction. A key innovation was developing a custom LLM evaluator to detect and classify hallucinations, achieving 82% precision and 90% recall. Through iterative improvements in training data quality, prompt engineering, and UX design, they achieved remarkable results: 17% fewer content defects, 50% reduction in hallucinations, and a 28% increase in bookings for AI-generated content. The presentation covers practical lessons in fine-tuning with limited high-quality examples, building custom evaluators, and the critical importance of cross-functional collaboration for AI product success.

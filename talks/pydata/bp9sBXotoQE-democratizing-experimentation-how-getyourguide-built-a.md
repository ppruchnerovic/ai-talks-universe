Description:
GetYourGuide transformed their experimentation infrastructure from a centralized bottleneck into a democratized, self-service platform. This talk reveals how they empowered analysts, engineers, and product teams to independently define metrics, run experiments, and analyze results. Key innovations include a modular SQL-template approach for flexible metric definitions, a Python-based Analyzer toolkit for standardized statistical calculations, and a robust batch processing pipeline with API-driven ad-hoc analysis capabilities. The presentation covers practical challenges in standardization, efficiency, and reliability, including implementing sequential testing to reduce experiment runtimes by 10%, variance reduction methods like CUPED, and automated decision engines to guide experimenters. With just three FTEs managing the platform, GetYourGuide demonstrates how thoughtful architecture and tooling can scale experimentation across an organization while maintaining statistical rigor and data quality.

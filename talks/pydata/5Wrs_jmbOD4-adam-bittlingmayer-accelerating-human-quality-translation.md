## Description

Adam Bittlingmayer, one of the original minds behind Google Translate, presents a talk on
“Accelerating Human-Quality Translation in the Real World – Building Verification for the Original Generative Task.”

Translation was one of the first real applications of generative AI — and it remains one of the most impactful. But how do we know if AI is getting it right? This talk explores what it takes to verify machine-generated translations at scale and why this question extends far beyond language.

In this session, Adam dives into:
🔹 Lessons learned from building real-world AI verification systems
🔹 The hard problems that remain unsolved in translation quality
🔹 What translation verification means for broader generative AI applications

This talk was recorded during the PyData Yerevan September 2025 Meetup, held on September 18, 2025, at the American University of Armenia (AUA).

Description:
This practical talk demonstrates how to train domain-specific Small Language Models (SLMs) using synthetic data generation, eliminating the need for massive labeled datasets. Jacek Golebiowski presents an end-to-end framework using model distillation, where large language models act as 'teachers' to generate training data for smaller 'student' models. Starting with just 10 seed examples and a task description, the approach synthesizes thousands of high-quality training examples through careful validation and deduplication. The result: specialized models that are 30-50x smaller than LLMs while matching or exceeding their performance on focused tasks. Perfect for applications requiring local deployment, low latency, or data privacy—such as PII redaction, classification, and question answering. The talk includes a real-world demonstration of building a PII reduction model and covers practical challenges like data validation, avoiding overfitting, and deployment strategies for production environments.

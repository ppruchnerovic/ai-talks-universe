## Description

See how [Quarto](https://quarto.org) can transform your Jupyter notebooks into stakeholder-ready web pages or PDFs, published online with just one command. This session features practical demonstrations of publishing with `quarto publish`, applying custom styles tailored to your organization thanks to [`brand.yml`](https://posit-dev.github.io/brand-yml/), and leveraging new features for reproducible research.

Designed for anyone looking to share their work, this talk requires only basic Python and notebook familiarity. You’ll walk away with the skills to elevate your reporting workflow and share insights professionally.

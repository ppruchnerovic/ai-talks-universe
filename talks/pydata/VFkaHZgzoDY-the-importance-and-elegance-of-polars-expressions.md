Description:
Jeroen Janssens, co-author of 'Python Polars: The Definitive Guide,' reveals why expressions are the heart of Polars' elegant API. While Polars is famous for speed, expressions make it truly powerful—they're lazy, reusable building blocks that describe data transformations without executing them immediately. This talk covers creating expressions from columns, using them for selection, filtering, sorting, and aggregation, and combining them with operators. Learn how expressions enable parallel execution, query optimization, and cleaner code. Janssens demonstrates idiomatic versus non-idiomatic patterns, explains the tree-like structure of expressions, and shows how they work across namespaces. With real-world examples using fruit data, you'll understand why mastering expressions means you're halfway to mastering Polars. The talk includes audience Q&A covering lazy evaluation, debugging strategies, and comparisons to pandas method chaining and R pipes.

🎓 The EU AI Act's hidden exemptions: why high-risk government and law enforcement AI systems escape regulation while commercial uses face scrutiny.

Speakers:
Adrin Jalali

Description:
The EU AI Act is already partly in effect, but many of its most controversial aspects remain under-discussed. Adrin Jalali reveals how the Act exempts military, defense, and national security AI systems from regulation, while law enforcement and border control applications receive special treatment with minimal oversight. Despite categorizing AI systems by risk levels—from banned 'unacceptable risk' systems to heavily regulated 'high-risk' applications—the Act allows companies to self-assess and argue their way out of high-risk classifications. The talk exposes how real-time biometric identification was nearly banned until last-minute negotiations weakened the prohibition, and how notified bodies (private auditing entities) create opportunities for the private sector while raising questions about accountability. Jalali also explores the complex relationship between AI fairness requirements and GDPR, highlighting the ideological challenges in defining and measuring fairness without accessing sensitive personal data.

## Description

In this episode of The weekly developer show, we’re joined by Dirk Ginader, Accessibility Lead at Google, for a deep and entertaining dive into the state of web accessibility in 2025. We cover everything from the "1 Million Websites" report to the role of AI in making (and breaking) accessible web experiences. Expect discussions on ARIA, auto-generated alt text, CSS mixins, developer tools, browser quirks, and why accessibility still isn’t as “automatic” as we want it to be. Plus, hilarious retro HTML throwbacks, developer jokes, and why camping is intense (pun intended).

Whether you're a seasoned accessibility advocate or a curious front-end dev, there’s tons of insight, nuance, and fun in this jam-packed episode.

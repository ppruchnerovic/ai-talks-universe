## Description

The last year was a roller-coaster of innovation and worry. Whilst hot new technologies promised to automate daily tasks and allow anyone to be a coder or creator, we also faced changes in the market that led to people losing jobs who thought they were safe for life. We've seen tech giants fail and small companies rise and grow quickly. We've seen amazing technology empower people, but also bring up new barriers and battle legal requirements in different markets. The good news is that we've kept the finger on the pulse all year for you, so time to look back and see what was, check out what is coming soon and how you can use these technologies and ideas to make your mark and ensure your success and career.

## Description

In this episode of Coffee with Developers, we sit down with Matthias Neumayer & Dima Rubanov from OscarStories—a mobile app that lets parents create AI-powered personalized children's stories. 📖✨

We discuss:
🔹 How they built an AI-driven storytelling app before it was mainstream
🔹 The challenges of ensuring ethical AI content for kids
🔹 How a single TV appearance skyrocketed downloads

## Description

In this episode of Cappuccino with HR, Rudi Bauer dives into the dynamic concept of 'Team Red vs. Team Blue' with Hung Lee, unpacking how creating an internal nemesis can ignite innovation. They explore the incumbent’s dilemma, the power of AI, and the importance of diversity in fostering creativity.

With insights into real-world applications like Disney’s methodology and practical strategies for implementing competitive models, this conversation offers actionable takeaways for addressing innovation’s toughest challenges. Learn how to disrupt from within, solve for human needs, and balance aggressive creativity with strategic management.

## Description

In this episode of Cappuccino with HR, Rudi Bauer talks with Daniel Mühlbauer, Speaker and Impactor at HR.Daten.Liebe, and also known as Data Dan, about why HR needs a ChatGPT license and how to lead AI adoption with confidence. They explore the balance between innovation and regulation, and why curiosity is key in uncertain times. From overcoming fear of technology to identifying real industry problems, this episode delivers practical steps and inspiration for HR professionals ready to embrace AI.

The ChatGPT license is currently only available in German: https://www.hr-datenliebe.de/chatgpt-fuehrerschein-f%C3%BCr-hr

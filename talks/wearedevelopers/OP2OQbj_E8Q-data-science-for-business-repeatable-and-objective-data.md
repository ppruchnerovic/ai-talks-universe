## Description

The Team Data Science Process (TDSP) is an agile, iterative data science methodology to deliver predictive analytics solutions and intelligent applications efficiently. Bringing intelligence in your core business processes is a key to bring organizations to the next level of efficiency and insights. During the session, we’ll see how to digitalize smaller and very specific manual processes infusing them with AI, making these processes re-usable and objective.

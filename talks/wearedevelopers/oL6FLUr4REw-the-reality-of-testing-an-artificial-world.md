## Description

Major advances have been made in developing applications that utilize some form of artificial intelligence, but there’s not nearly as much consideration given to how to test them. Angie Jones gives an overview of why it’s extremely important that these applications be tested and how developers can begin to prepare and build their skillset for this new realm of software. She explores today's cutting edge, innovative applications that utilize machine learning and discusses the challenges of ensuring that they actually work the way we intend them to.

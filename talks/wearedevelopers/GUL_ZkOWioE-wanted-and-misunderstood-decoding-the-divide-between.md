## Description

In this episode of Cappuccino with HR, Rudi Bauer sits down with John Salt, Adnan Pavlovic, and Ivana Spiridonovic to discuss the findings of the WeAreDevelopers Developer Survey 2024. They explore why the survey was conducted, how developers—despite being highly sought after—are often misunderstood, and what key trends emerged compared to previous years. The episode dives into the differing perspectives between developers and C-level managers or recruiters, and what companies can learn to better engage with tech talent.

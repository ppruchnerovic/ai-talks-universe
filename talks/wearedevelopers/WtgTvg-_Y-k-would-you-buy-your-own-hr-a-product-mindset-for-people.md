## Description

In this episode, Rudi Bauer talks with Jennifer Christie, Chief People Officer at Docusign, about the evolution of HR into a more strategic, product-driven function.

They discuss the importance of employee experience and personalization, how AI is transforming HR, and why new success metrics are needed. Jennifer also shares how HR can better collaborate with product and tech teams, the key skills for modern HR leaders, and how HR can become a driver of innovation and business growth.

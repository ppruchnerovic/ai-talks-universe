## Description

In this episode, Rudi Bauer talks with René Knapp, Member of the Management Board at UNIQA Insurance Group, about navigating corporate leadership in times of uncertainty and rapid technological change.

They explore how the insurance industry is transforming through AI and digitization, why long-term thinking remains a competitive advantage, and how companies can build cultures that truly drive change. René shares insights on shifting from role-based to skills-based organizations, making HR a strategic business driver, and measuring people impact through data and KPIs. He also discusses the importance of collaboration, adaptability, and AI literacy as key skills for the future, and how UNIQA is working to balance employee satisfaction with high performance in a complex, evolving environment.

## Description

Tobias Bürger and Josef Viehhauser detail the path the BMW Group has taken and the established technology stack and explore the challenges faced along the way. They also offer an overview of novel machine learning use cases, such as those based on gradient boosting and convolutional neural nets, that have been applied and deployed in real-world environments.

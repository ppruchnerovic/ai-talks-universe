## Description

This week on the WeAreDevelopers Weekly Dev Show, Dan chats with the founders Matthias Neumayer and Dima Rubanov about building Oscar Stories — an AI-powered storytelling app for kids with over 100k users. They discuss ethical AI, startup culture, slop farming, IP law, and their second project, Intellipaper — a smart research tool for working with PDFs and academic papers.

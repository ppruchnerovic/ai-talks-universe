## Description

SoftBank Robotics develops humanoid robots designed to interact with people. This interaction requires many different aspects of artificial intelligence. In this talk, we will present some of them: functions that are already running on our robots and others that are still under development. This will be an opportunity to highlight the differences between natural and artificial intelligence.

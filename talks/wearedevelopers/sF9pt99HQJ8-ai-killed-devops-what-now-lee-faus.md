## Description

AI isn’t just boosting DevOps — it may be rewriting it. In this episode, GitLab Field CTO Lee Faus argues that agentic AI is reinventing collaboration, agility, and automation, creating an “invisible SDLC” where security, testing, and quality checks move from centralized CI/CD into the developer’s local flow. Agile gets flipped as AI can solve backlog items outside the sprint, turning standups into real-time automated reports.

Faus warns about “vibe coding”: AI-built apps that look done fast but often fail on maintainability, security, and compliance. He highlights LLM confirmation bias, missing edge cases, and the growing risk of unclear AI code provenance. The future isn’t replacement — it’s evolution into AI-native knowledge workers who build agents, use smaller local models, and leverage AI to find corner cases and architectural anti-patterns.

## Description

In this episode of Cappuccino with HR, Rudi Bauer sits down with Robindro Ullah, Managing Director of the Trendence Institut, to explore the key HR trends of 2024. This year’s HR buzzword? Emotional disconnection.

As teams become increasingly detached from their workplaces, many struggle to see how their roles contribute to the company’s purpose, craving a clearer sense of their part in the story. The discussion also touches on the rise of AI in HR and why quick talent attraction is more critical than ever.

Tune in for an insightful conversation on navigating a year of workplace challenges and innovation.

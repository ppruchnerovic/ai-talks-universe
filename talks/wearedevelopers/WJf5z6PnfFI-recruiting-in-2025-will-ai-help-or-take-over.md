## Description

Is the recruitment process fundamentally flawed? And how can AI shape the future of hiring?

In this episode of Cappuccino with HR, Rudi Bauer sits down with Jan Tegze, Director of Talent Acquisition, to explore the evolving landscape of recruiting in 2025. They discuss the missing elements in traditional hiring, the role of AI in streamlining processes, and whether technology can truly replace human recruiters. Jan also shares insights on building meaningful candidate relationships in an AI-driven world, selecting the right tools, and practical tips for integrating AI into your hiring strategy. Plus, a sneak peek into his upcoming book!

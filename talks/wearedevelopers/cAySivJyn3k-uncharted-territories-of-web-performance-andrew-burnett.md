## Description

What is the type of software that is the most challenging when it comes to performance? Pretty sure you'd say gaming, right? However, we found out that live charting is one of the most taxing things to do, We sat down with Andrew Burnett-Thompson and David Burleigh, CEO and CTO of https://www.scichart.com/ to hear how they create complex visualisations of huge datasets, and how they overcome the technical challenges that come with it. Go and learn about performance problems and solutions by SciChart and raise your expectations for what’s possible in real-time.

We chatted with Andrew Burnett-Thompson and David Burleigh from SciChart about pushing the limits of data-viz performance on the web. We dive into how they render millions of points, sync hundreds of charts, and squeeze native-like speed out of the browser using WebAssembly, WebGL, and SIMD—without sacrificing flexibility.

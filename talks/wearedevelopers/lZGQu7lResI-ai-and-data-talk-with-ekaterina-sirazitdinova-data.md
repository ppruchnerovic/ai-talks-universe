## Description

In this episode of Coffee with Developers, Sead, CEO of WeAreDevelopers, is joined by Ekaterina (Katja) Sirazitdinova,  Data Scientist for Computer Vision, Video Analytics and Deep Learning at NVIDIA.

After initial introductions, Katja gives some insights into her day-to-day work and projects at NVIDIA.
Furthermore, Sead asks her about the industries (apart from the gaming sector) NVIDIA is involved in and what defines them as a Platform-Company.
Afterwards, Katja remembers how she initially got interested in AI, how that changed her career plans and gives advice to people who also want to dive into this field of technology.
She goes on to describe some of the current challenges in AI and what will happen in the near future.
Katja also shares some recommendations concerning tools and programming languages.
Returning to AI in a broader view, Sead wants to know how it may contribute to everyday life and what are the most promising, upcoming projects.
Moving on to some of the ethical considerations when it comes to AI, Katja describes how to avoid misuse and how the community needs to work on the safety and security of those systems.
Next, she talks about people who assisted her on her AI journey, how she became professional in this field and why she specifically chose NVIDIA as an employer.
Katja also gives some personal insights into her hobbies and how she manages her work-life balance.
As she also likes to speak at conferences, Sead wants to know her favourite topics and upcoming events she wants to join.
They close the session with Katja giving some motivation to the developer community.

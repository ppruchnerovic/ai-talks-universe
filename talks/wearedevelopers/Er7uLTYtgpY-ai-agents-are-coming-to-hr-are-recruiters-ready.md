## Description

In this episode, Rudi Bauer sits down with Leandro Gomes da Silva, Founder of The AI Laundry and Head of AI Operations at Noble Recruitment, to explore whether the HR world is truly prepared for the dawn of AI agents.

They delve into the practicalities of building custom AI tools for recruitment, including Leandro’s own outreach generator, "Martha", and discuss the shift from traditional talent acquisition to the emerging role of the "Talent Engineer." Leandro shares his unique perspective on balancing rapid-fire technological adoption with the irreplaceable human element of hiring, offering a candid look at how AI can eliminate administrative "anchors" to allow for three-Michelin-star candidate experiences.

## Description

In this episode of Coffee with Developers, Chris talks with PUFO co-founder Sam Liu about a future where humans and AI agents work side by side as equal collaborators. They explore multi-agent teams, agent governance, trust and security, and why communicating with AI may increasingly resemble managing human teams. The conversation also dives into token usage, specialized agents, local models, and what AI-powered organizations could look like.

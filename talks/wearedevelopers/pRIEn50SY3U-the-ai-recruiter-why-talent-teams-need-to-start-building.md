## Description

In this episode, Rudi Bauer sits down with Joe Atkinson, Co-Founder of PromptMates and Director of AI & Automation at Seed, to explore the burgeoning world of "vibe coding" and the rise of the AI-native recruiter.

They dive deep into why talent acquisition professionals should stop waiting for perfect SaaS solutions and start building their own custom tools. Joe shares his journey from a non-technical founder to an AI innovator, offering a practical roadmap for HR leaders to move from "AI anxiety" to "AI experimentation."

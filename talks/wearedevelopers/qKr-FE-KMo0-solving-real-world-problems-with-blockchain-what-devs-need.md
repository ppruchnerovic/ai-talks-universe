## Description

What’s blockchain really good for in 2025? In this episode of Coffee with Developers, we sit down with Brian Whippo of the Algorand Foundation to unpack the current state of blockchain, beyond the hype. From programmable payments and digital identity to stablecoins and quantum-resistant cryptography, we explore how modern blockchain infrastructure quietly reshapes the tech landscape.

Whether you're a seasoned developer or just curious how distributed ledgers can unlock new use cases (without having to learn Solidity), this talk delivers practical insights, dev-first tools (like writing smart contracts in TypeScript!), and bold predictions about the intersection of AI agents and programmable money.

💬 Join us as we go from crypto misconceptions to tangible, developer-friendly tech you can build on today.

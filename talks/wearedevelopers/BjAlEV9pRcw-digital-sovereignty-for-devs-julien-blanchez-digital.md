## Description

In this episode of Coffee with Developers, we sit down with Julien Blanchez, Digital Sovereignty Program Lead at Google, to explore what digital sovereignty really means in a fast-changing world. From geopolitical tensions and cloud infrastructure to the delicate balance of trust, control, and innovation—Julien shares deep insights about how Google is adapting its technology to meet sovereignty requirements across Europe and beyond.

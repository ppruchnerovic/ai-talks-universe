## Description

Recorded at WeAreDevelopers Live Week Day 2
Panel: How to think about AI and the “New Normal”? | Nancy Nemes, Dr. Isabell Franck

In these days, we are confronted with a “New Normal”: Things are slowly going back to normal, yet everyday life is still different from the pre-COVID era. While Europe is slowly awakening from an artificial deep sleep, it is certainly well equipped to find viable solutions for the future -  even during these uncertain times.

In this session, Dr.-Ing. Isabell Franck (Founder of franck.AI, member of Women in AI and Ms. AI) and Nancy Nemes (Ms. AI & #humanAIze) will present real-life examples of applied AI. Learn about the current AI-based technologies we have in Europe and how German companies are utilizing them in order to provide new solutions.

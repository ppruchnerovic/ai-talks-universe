## Description

In this episode, Rudi Bauer sits down with Uta Schulz, Head of Human Capability Future at BASF, to explore why curiosity is the ultimate competitive advantage for modern organizations.

They delve into the mechanics of lifelong learning, discussing how the innate curiosity of childhood often gets stifled by traditional structures and what leaders must do to reignite that "learning urge." Uta shares her unique perspective on balancing the rapid pace of technological change with the uniquely human ability to make sense of information.

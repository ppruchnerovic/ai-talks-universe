## Description

Most developers aren’t on job boards. In fact, about 70% of tech professionals are so-called passive candidates, meaning they're not actively looking for a job, but are open to new opportunities.

In this discussion, Ana Gospodinova hosts Rudi Bauer (Managing Director, WeAreDevelopers) and Chris Heilmann (VP DevRel, WeAreDevelopers) to explore how to attract top tech talent through authenticity, community, and transparency.

Learn why traditional hiring fails, how to build trust where developers actually are (communities, open source, meetups), and what really matters: skills, culture, and honest communication. Discover tips on salary transparency, simplifying job titles, and turning your engineers into authentic brand ambassadors.

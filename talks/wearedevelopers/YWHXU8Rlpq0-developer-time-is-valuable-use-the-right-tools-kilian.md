## Description

☕ Coffee with Developers: Kilian Valkhof on Browser APIs & Polypane

In this episode, we sit down with Kilian Valkhof, creator of Polypane, to talk about the hidden realities of browser APIs, how “standard” web features often depend on big tech services, and why that matters for privacy, security, and the future of the web. We also dive into Kilian’s journey from web developer to browser vendor, the philosophy behind Polypane, accessibility-first tooling, and why developers should value their time more.

👉 A thoughtful conversation on web standards, AI browsers, accessibility, and building better developer tools.

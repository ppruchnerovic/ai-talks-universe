## Description

Join us for a fascinating conversation on Coffee with Developers as we chat with Andreas Kollegger from Neo4j about graph databases, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), and the future of AI-powered search. We explore how graphs enhance AI retrieval, why developers should embrace them, and how they can revolutionize LLM-powered applications. Plus, we dive into GraphRAG, AI agents, and the evolution of knowledge graphs.

## Description

In this episode, Rudi Bauer talks with Glauco Leal, Founding Recruiter at Lovable, about the intersection of AI-native culture and modern HR practices.

They discuss the shift toward "vibe coding" and the democratisation of development, exploring how AI tools allow non-technical staff to build their own custom solutions. Glauco shares insights on maintaining a human-centric balance in an AI-first environment, the necessity of rapid skill refreshing, and why creativity and obsession are the most critical traits for new hires in 2026.

He also provides a behind-the-scenes look at how Lovable uses its own product to 10x HR productivity through automated talent mapping and kickoff tools.

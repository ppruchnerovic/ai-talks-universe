## Description

What is the ROI of a hackathon when AI can build software instantly? The answer isn't what you think.

Join Christian Heilmann and Mike Swift, CEO and co-founder of Major League Hacking (MLH), for a conversation about the future of hackathons, AI-powered software creation, and why hands-on learning matters more than ever. They discuss how AI is reshaping developer roles, the evolution of Hacktoberfest, the importance of open source, and why experimentation remains the best way to grow as a developer.

## Description

Jessie Schofer, Founder of Stakkd, is challenging the status quo: HR is dead, and HR tech shouldn’t be complicated. In this episode of Cappuccino with HR, Rudi Bauer and Jessie, discuss the role of HR and how technology can simplify and enhance people operations. They explore the critical shift from a purchasing to a marketing mindset, the importance of building a strong foundation for people strategies, and how AI is transforming HR by automating repetitive tasks.

Together, they tackle key questions like: Is it an HR problem or a company problem? How can organizations identify gaps in their operating models and turn them into opportunities? And what does it take to find the right HR tech to truly improve the candidate experience?

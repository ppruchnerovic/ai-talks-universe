## Description

Welcome to Coffee with Developers! In this episode, we are at the WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2022, where Sead chats with GitHub's CEO, Thomas Dohmke, about his tech journey, developer happiness, and the challenges of remote work. They discuss Copilot's role in task prioritization and whether it could replace developers. They also touch on Git's uses during the pandemic and why kids should learn to code in school. Lastly, they talk about the importance of strong leadership teams and answer some audience questions.

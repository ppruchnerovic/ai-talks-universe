## Description

Luise Freese and Elio Struyf join Coffee with Developers to share insights from their upcoming workshop at the WeAreDevelopers World Congress in Berlin. They discuss why testing is often neglected—especially in today’s AI-assisted coding era—and why that can lead to misleading results and unstable software.

Their workshop focuses on end-to-end testing with Playwright and Gherkin, helping teams create more meaningful tests, align across roles, and build a shared language around requirements. They also highlight how testing should be integrated early in the process to ensure better quality and reliability.

A great session for developers looking to level up their testing approach in a rapidly evolving AI landscape.

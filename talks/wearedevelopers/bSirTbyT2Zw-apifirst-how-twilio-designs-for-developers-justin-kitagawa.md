## Description

Justin Kitagawa, VP of Engineering at Twilio, shares how the company’s API-first philosophy treats the API as the product—and trust as what they sell. He explains Twilio’s “Lego block” approach to communication, where composable primitives for voice, SMS, and email power everything from customer support to life-critical services.

The conversation dives into Twilio’s engineering culture of extreme reliability, including five-nines availability, fault isolation, and an “assume failure” mindset. Kitagawa also covers how Twilio scaled developer experience using OpenAPI as a single source of truth to generate SDKs, docs, and tools, why documentation is a first-class product, and how the platform is evolving to support enterprise customers and AI-driven workflows.

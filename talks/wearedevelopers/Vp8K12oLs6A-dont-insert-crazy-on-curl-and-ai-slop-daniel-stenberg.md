## Description

Daniel Stenberg (creator of cURL) joins Coffee with Developers to explain why cURL is shutting down its bug bounty program—and what the flood of AI-generated “vulnerability” reports is doing to open source security workflows. We talk signal vs. noise, why most submissions now aren’t real issues, how reporting will move to GitHub, and what this shift means for maintainers, researchers, and the future of open source contribution.

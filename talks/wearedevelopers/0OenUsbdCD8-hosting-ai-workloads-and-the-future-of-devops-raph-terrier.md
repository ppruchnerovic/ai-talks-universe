## Description

Raph Terrier and Hazal Mestci from Render discuss how Render simplifies cloud hosting for developers by offering managed infrastructure, predictable pricing, and a strong developer experience. They cover AI workloads, workflows, agent-friendly deployments, DevOps automation, GDPR and compliance, remote-first teamwork, and Render’s role as an abstraction layer over hyperscalers. They also preview Render’s presence at the WeAreDevelopers event in San Jose, their Localhost conference, hiring plans, demos, free credits, and developer-focused swag.

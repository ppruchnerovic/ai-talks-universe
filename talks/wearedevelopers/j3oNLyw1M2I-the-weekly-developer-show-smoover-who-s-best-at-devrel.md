## Description

In this episode, Tim Cadenbach explores how AI is reshaping Developer Relations and introduces Smoover, a platform that helps teams measure DevRel impact through insights on code adoption, community health, onboarding, and AI readiness. As AI-driven traffic overtakes human traffic, the conversation highlights why authentic community engagement and in-person interactions are becoming more valuable than traditional SEO. The episode also covers major security concerns, including attacks on open-source ecosystems and AI-powered vulnerabilities, alongside practical developer tools such as Ollama for local LLMs and Microsoft's new Unix-style coreutils for Windows.

## Description

Chris and Dan return with another Coffee with Developers episode, sharing stories from their recent travels recording interviews across San Francisco, Berlin, and Vienna with nothing more than a portable iPhone studio setup. Along the way they discuss AI agents, bot traffic taking over the web, developer tooling, automation disasters, and some of the weirdest tech stories of the week.

In the second half, AWS Developer Advocate Ifeanyi Otuonye joins the show from Dallas to talk about AWS’s plans for WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2025 in Berlin. He shares details about AWS’s new multi-day hackathon focused on agentic AI, modern app development, and “football for good,” where developers will build AI-powered experiences using AWS tools and the Strands Agent SDK in a competitive, gamified format.

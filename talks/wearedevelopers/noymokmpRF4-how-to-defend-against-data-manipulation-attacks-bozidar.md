## Description

Bozidar Spirovski (CISO) and Wekoslav Stefanovski (Head of Dev) share what to expect from their hands-on workshop at WeAreDevelopers Congress.

They cover real-world vulnerabilities like SQL injection, XSS, SSRF, and AI prompt injection, and explain why security is still often overlooked in modern development.

Participants will hack a deliberately vulnerable app to learn how attacks actually work—and how to prevent them.

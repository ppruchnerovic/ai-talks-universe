## Description

This episode is a unique edition - filmed inside the Mercedes-Benz Museum in Stuttgart, surrounded by 150 years of automotive history and innovation. From the first-ever automobile to the future of AI-driven engineering, we explore how Mercedes-Benz is bridging its legacy with cutting-edge tech.

Katrin Lehmann, CIO of Mercedes-Benz, shares how her team is enabling 5,000+ developers with GitHub Copilot, scaling agile practices, and building platform foundations to accelerate global software delivery.

We cover AI in enterprise environments, developer culture, agile transformation, and how leadership can unlock innovation at scale - inside one of the world’s most iconic brands.

Whether you're a developer, tech lead, or engineering strategist, this episode offers a powerful look at where software and automotive intersect.

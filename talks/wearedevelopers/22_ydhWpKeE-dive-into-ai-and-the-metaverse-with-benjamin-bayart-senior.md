## Description

Welcome to Coffee with Developers! Our host Marc is joined by Benjamin Bayart, Senior Solutions Architect at NVIDIA. In this episode, you'll get to know about Benjamin's journey in the tech industry, from his first computer to his love for 3D modeling.
Benjamin shares his academic background and how he got into the world of 3D animation. He also talks about his passion for virtual and augmented reality and how it led him to work at NVIDIA.
Get a glimpse into Benjamin's work at NVIDIA and the programs and projects he's involved in. You'll also learn about the challenges of creating 3D avatars that can speak.

Discover the NVIDIA Omniverse platform and the amazing use cases it has in various projects. Get ready to dive into the future as Marc and Benjamin discuss the concept of the metaverse and the film Ready Player One.
Finally, they'll talk about the importance of AI in today's development and give you an outlook on what the future holds.

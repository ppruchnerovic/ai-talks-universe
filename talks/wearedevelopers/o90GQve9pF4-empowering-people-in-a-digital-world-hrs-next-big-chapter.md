## Description

In this episode, Rudi Bauer talks with Diana Westrich, Vice President Corporate HR Leadership & Development at BASF, about empowering people and leading transformation in a digital world.

They discuss why transformation is never just about technology, but also about culture, mindset, and leadership responsibility. Diana shares insights on fostering curiosity, balancing innovation with safety, embedding AI across organizations, and the evolving role of HR in times of uncertainty.

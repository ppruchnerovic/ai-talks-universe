## Description

Filmed at our recent HR Meetup in Vienna, this panel discussion features Rudi Bauer, Eva-Maria Meyr from Dynatrace, and Hung Lee from Recruiting Brainfood as they recap the "crazy" HR trends of 2024 and share their outlook for 2025. From the rise of AI in HR to tackling the mental health crisis and how HR teams can adapt to challenges like hiring freezes while preparing for recovery, the conversation highlights the biggest challenges and opportunities of the year. It’s a practical and honest look at the year gone by and what’s ahead in the HR and recruiting industry.

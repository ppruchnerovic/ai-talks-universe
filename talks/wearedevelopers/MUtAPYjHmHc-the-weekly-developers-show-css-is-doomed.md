## Description

In this episode of We Are Developers Live, Niels Leentheer joins the show to explore the weird, creative, and sometimes dangerous side of front-end development.

From abusing CSS and experimenting with browser APIs to discussing the limits of AI-generated design, the conversation dives into why human craftsmanship still matters in web development. The trio also explores new browser capabilities like HTML inside Canvas, unlocking powerful (and potentially chaotic) possibilities for developers.

Expect a mix of cutting-edge tech, strong opinions on AI tools, and fun experiments—from running Doom inside Doom to storing data in a mouse. This one is all about pushing the web to its limits (and beyond).

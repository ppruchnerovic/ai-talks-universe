## Description

Join us to explore how Cornell University is reshaping higher education by building an AI platform to transform how teachers guide students to success and administrators operate. Cornell’s AI innovation Lab evaluates and implements 20+ AI use cases each semester using a “minimum lovable product” approach. We’ll showcase SandboxAI, a secure campus-wide platform built on AWS serverless architecture and integrated with Amazon Bedrock. Also discover how migrating analytics to Amazon Redshift unlocked significant cost savings and performance gains, directly fueling Cornell’s AI initiatives and broader institutional goals. Cornell’s journey offers a blueprint for innovation, efficiency, and governance at scale.

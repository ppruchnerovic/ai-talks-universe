## Description

The NFL and AWS Generative AI Innovation Center developed NFL Pro Fantasy AI Assistant – an AI-powered virtual advisor helping 10,000+ fans make winning fantasy football decisions. The team will demonstrate how Strands Agents enabled the solution to go from zero to production in 8 weeks. Learn how Strands' model-driven approach simplified complex agent development, integrating NFL Next Gen Stats real-time data to answer critical questions like "Who should I start?" We will demonstrate architectural patterns achieving sub-5-second responses for player comparisons, injury analysis, and matchup recommendations while handling 10x NFL Sunday traffic. Discover how Strands helps teams ship production AI agents in weeks, not months. Perfect for engineers wanting startup speed with enterprise reliability.

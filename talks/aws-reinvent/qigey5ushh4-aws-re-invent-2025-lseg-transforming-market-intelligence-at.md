## Description

London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG) processes over 220 billion market data updates daily across 575+ global exchanges, forming the backbone of global financial markets. Learn how LSEG built their Matrix Signals Platform—a serverless architecture that detects patterns and anomalies across billions of messages. Explore their historical data migration to Amazon S3, development of a cloud-native real-time market data delivery platform, and implementation of sophisticated analytics with AWS Glue, Amazon Athena, and Amazon Bedrock. Discover how LSEG overcame cost-optimization challenges at petabyte scale, maintained consistency across 34 sites, and achieved a 5x cost reduction while improving anomaly detection from days to minutes.

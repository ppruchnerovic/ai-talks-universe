## Description

Explore how Spreetail is transforming business operations through AI adoption, delivering over $50M in annual value across the enterprise. This executive session reveals our pragmatic approach to implementing AI solutions in merchandising, supply chain, and customer experience. Learn how we prioritize use cases, manage implementation risks, and build internal AI capabilities while ensuring ROI. We'll share our framework for measuring AI success, governance, and key insights from both wins and setbacks. Discover how we navigate vendor selection, data readiness, and change management to achieve measurable business outcomes. Perfect for leaders looking to move beyond AI pilots to enterprise-wide value creation.

## Description

​Agentic applications interpret inputs and take actions within a customer’s enterprise environment, and model customization enables agents that understand the business and provides responses or actions that are appropriate for the use case. In this session, we will cover recent advancements like Nova customization on SageMaker AI and on-demand inferences on custom models, which lower the cost and effort needed for model customization. Explore customization techniques available through available recipes, walk through how you can enjoy the same low on-demand inference prices with your custom models, and learn how custom Nova models improve the price-performance for agentic use cases.

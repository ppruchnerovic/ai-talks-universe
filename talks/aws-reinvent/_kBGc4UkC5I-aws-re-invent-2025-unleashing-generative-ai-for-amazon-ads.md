## Description

Discover how Amazon Ads harnesses the power of large language models (LLMs) to enhance shopper discovery and drive better outcomes for advertisers. This session explores the AWS-based machine learning infrastructure that fuels these innovations, including fine-tuning, model serving, and real-time inference at scale. Learn how we optimized cost, latency, and reliability to deliver LLM-powered solutions across the Amazon Ads ecosystem while maintaining the high performance our customers expect.

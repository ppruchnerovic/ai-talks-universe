## Description

What does it take to migrate some of the largest, most complex enterprise and federal workloads to the cloud without disrupting mission operations or compromising security? In this session, discover how the National Reconnaissance Office and Fannie Mae architected and executed mission-scale migrations to AWS. You will learn strategies for designing secure, resilient, and cost-efficient architectures; approaches to maintaining continuity of operations during complex cutovers; and how to lay a cloud-native foundation that enables modernization and emerging technologies like generative AI. Walk away with practical insights you can apply to your own migration and transformation initiatives.

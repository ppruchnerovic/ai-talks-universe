## Description

In financial services, balancing personalization with compliance and security presents unique challenges to delivering superior customer experiences. Zepz, the global payments group behind leading remittance brands WorldRemit and Sendwave, began their Amazon Connect journey in 2021. Operating in over 130 countries with 5,000+ money transfer corridors and facilitating more than $16 billion in transfers, Zepz has transformed their customer service operations. In this session, learn how they manage 20 million customer profiles while achieving impressive results: 30% contact deflection using generative AI, comprehensive analytics across all voice and chat interactions, case and ticket management, data-driven quality assurance, and modernized customer feedback systems.

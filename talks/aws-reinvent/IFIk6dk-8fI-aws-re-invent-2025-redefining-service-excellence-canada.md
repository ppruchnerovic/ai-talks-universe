## Description

Join this session to discover how Canada Life transformed its contact center by moving from a fragmented, manual system to a cloud-based, AI-powered solution using Amazon Connect. This shift enabled seamless multi-channel engagement enabling customer interaction through their preferred channel, simplified authentication, and empowered agents with real-time tools and unified interfaces—resulting in streamlined operations, reduced complexity, faster resolutions and thus reduced operational costs and significantly improved customer satisfaction.

## Description

From sunrise alerts to sunset cost reports, the AWS WAF administrator’s day is never dull. Join us as we follow a fictional (but all-too-relatable) admin navigating setup, fine-tuning rules, building dashboards, and taming budgets - while outsmarting emerging threats before they bite. You’ll see how to adapt quickly to shifting traffic, uncover hidden risks, and make data-driven decisions without breaking the bank - or the application. Packed with tips, real-world examples, and a dash of bot traffic, this session turns the daily grind of web application protection into a well-architected adventure (edited)

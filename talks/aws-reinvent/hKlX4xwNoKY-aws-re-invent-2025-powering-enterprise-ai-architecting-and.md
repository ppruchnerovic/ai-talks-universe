## Description

Iberdrola leverages AWS to deploy Claude 4 AI agents that enhance operational efficiency through compressed decision cycles. The implementation focuses on three use cases demonstrating the design of domain-specific agents with comprehensive telemetry and safe scalability. Their approach transforms experimental prompts into reliable services through robust governance that balances innovation with risk management. Performance metrics directly connect agent activities to business outcomes. Iberdrola's vision includes developing an enterprise Agent Hub - a marketplace for teams to share and reuse certified AI agents. This platform will serve as a strategic asset, enabling Iberdrola to maximize AI benefits across its operations.

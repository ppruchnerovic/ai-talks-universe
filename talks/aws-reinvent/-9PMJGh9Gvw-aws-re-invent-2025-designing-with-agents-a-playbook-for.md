## Description

Enterprises are asking not “if” but “how” to integrate agents into their software delivery pipelines. Drawing from Cognition’s work with Fortune 500 engineering teams, Russell Kaplan, President of Cognition, will share a practical playbook for senior technical leaders: when to delegate to autonomous agents like Devin, how to govern human-in-the-loop workflows in IDEs like Windsurf, and how to measure ROI beyond productivity metrics. This session balances inspiration with actionable guidance, equipping attendees with strategies to future-proof their engineering organizations while avoiding common pitfalls. This presentation is brought to you by Cognition, an AWS Partner.

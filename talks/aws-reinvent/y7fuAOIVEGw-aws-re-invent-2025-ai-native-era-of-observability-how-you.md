## Description

Observability is entering an AI-native era where manual queries and endless dashboards are replaced by instant insights, root cause detection, and guided recommendations. In this session, you’ll learn how AI-native observability reduces MTTR, reveals root causes instantly, and empowers business and engineering teams alike. Ariel Assaraf, CEO of Coralogix, and Liran Hason, VP of AI, will share their vision for the future of observability and how Coralogix is leading the shift with Olly, the first AI-native observability agent built to turn telemetry into real-time clarity and impact. This presentation is brought to you by Coralogix, an AWS Partner.

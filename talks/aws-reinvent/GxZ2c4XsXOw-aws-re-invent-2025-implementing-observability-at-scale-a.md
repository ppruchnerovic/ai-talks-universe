## Description

Achieving comprehensive observability to ensure optimal performance, reliability, and user experience is crucial for businesses. In this session, we will explore best practices for collecting and analyzing metrics, traces, and logs across your AWS environments. Discover techniques for streamlining agent management, optimizing alerting, and enabling cross-account observability. Learn how to reduce operational overhead while gaining deeper visibility into your applications' performance and health in a cost-optimized manner.

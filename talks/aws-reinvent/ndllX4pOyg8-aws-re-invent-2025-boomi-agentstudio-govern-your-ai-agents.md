## Description

Elevate your enterprise AI strategy. In this session, we'll dive into the Boomi Agentstudio, integrated with Amazon Bedrock and Q Business Index, for building, orchestrating, and governing AI agents. We'll show you how to leverage Bedrock services with Boomi to create AI agents that have the business context and multi-system access they need to deliver ROI throughout your business, while ensuring you have complete control via Boomi Agent Control Tower. Discover how to unlock hyperproductivity with Boomi and AWS—accelerating business processes with AI agents that drive action and measurable value.

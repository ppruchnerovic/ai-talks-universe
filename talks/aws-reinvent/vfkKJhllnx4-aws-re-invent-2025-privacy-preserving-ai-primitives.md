## Description

Regulated industries face unique challenges in adopting AI while maintaining compliance. Explore four fundamental privacy-preserving primitives that unlock AWS AI capabilities: encryption with AWS KMS, confidential computing with AWS Nitro System, federated learning, and cryptographic computing. Through industry case studies, examine real-world implementations, architectural considerations, and performance trade-offs. Learn to evaluate these essential primitives against specific regulatory requirements and design solutions that maintain data sovereignty while leveraging cloud scalability. Gain actionable strategies for implementing privacy-preserving AI in regulated environments.

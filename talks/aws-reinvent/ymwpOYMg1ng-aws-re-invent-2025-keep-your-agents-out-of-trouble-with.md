## Description

As AI agents take on complex real-world tasks, ensuring they act safely and responsibly is critical. In this session, learn how Amazon Bedrock AgentCore helps you build trustworthy agents through fine-grained policy enforcement, identity-aware authorization, and runtime isolation. See how these capabilities work together to prevent unauthorized actions, protect sensitive data, and secure agent-to-tool interactions—all with millisecond-latency policy controls and built-in observability. Walk away with practical patterns to make safety, governance, and compliance a native part of your agent architecture—so your agents consistently do the right thing, the right way.

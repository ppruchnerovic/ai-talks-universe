## Description

As supply chains grow increasingly complex, companies are using the cloud, artificial intelligence (AI), and internet of things (IoT) to gain better, faster insights and reduce costs. Hear how Sysco, the global leader in foodservice distribution, executed a strategic cloud migration to enhance scalability, improve warehouse efficiency, and develop a foundation to accelerate AI use cases. You will also learn how McDonald's, one of the world’s largest restaurant chains, built innovative AI-powered solutions to improve supply chain traceability and automate inventory tracking.

## Description

As organizations build and customize multiple AI models and agents, managing experiments, versions, and evaluations becomes increasingly complex. In this session, see how Intuit, a leader in financial technology, is using Amazon SageMaker with MLflow to accelerate their AI initiatives and empower their teams to build and deploy AI solutions with confidence. Discover how this new capability eliminates infrastructure management while providing comprehensive experiment tracking, model evaluation, and observability. Learn to efficiently manage your growing AI portfolio across traditional ML, custom models, generative AI applications, and fine-tuned agents—all from a single, streamlined environment.

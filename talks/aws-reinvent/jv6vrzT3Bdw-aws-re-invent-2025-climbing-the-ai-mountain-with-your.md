## Description

Navigate the intersection of AI and security culture in this practical session. Learn how security teams can effectively embrace AI innovation through incremental steps and validation techniques. Using real-world examples, we'll demonstrate how security practitioners can adapt their skills to AI challenges regardless of their level of specialized expertise and share strategies for building security-aware AI practices. From understanding generative and agentic AI-specific security risks to creating engaging team exercises, discover how to transform security from a potential bottleneck into an enabler of responsible AI innovation. Attendees will leave with actionable insights for building a security-first approach to AI adoption.

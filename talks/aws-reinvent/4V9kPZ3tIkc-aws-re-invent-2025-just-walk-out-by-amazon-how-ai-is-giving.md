## Description

Just Walk Out technology transforms how people shop, leveraging computer vision and machine learning to eliminate checkout across retail environments. Discover how Amazon implemented Just Walk Out across its fulfillment network, transforming break room experiences for frontline workers by providing food and beverage selections with 24/7 access and no lines. Learn about the technical challenges of deploying AI systems at scale—from integrating retail technology into complex operational environments to rolling out new shopping experiences to employees in industrial settings. Amazon leaders share practical lessons from deployment: co-creating solutions through feedback from operations teams, shoppers, and partners.

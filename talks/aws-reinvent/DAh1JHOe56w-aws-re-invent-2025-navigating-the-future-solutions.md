## Description

AI is dramatically accelerating technological evolution, with new architectural primitives emerging monthly. This unprecedented pace transforms the Solutions Architect (SA) role from system designer to change navigator. Learn how SAs deliver value by enabling and de-risking rapid transformation while maintaining architectural stability. Discover strategies for developing new capabilities while preserving foundational expertise. This session explores how to guide organizations through an accelerating technology landscape where managing change has become the primary architectural challenge.

## Description

Learn how Lucid Motors is transforming its Finance organization by deploying 16 AI and ML use cases in just 10 weeks, placing the company at the forefront of enterprise AI adoption across the automotive industry. Built on AWS, delivered in collaboration with PwC and moving at Sapphire-level speed, this Finance initiative modernizes decision-making and operational intelligence. The session explores how agentic AI is reshaping insight generation and changing how teams work, using AI to amplify human decision-making while preserving human judgment. Attendees will gain practical perspectives on the delivery models that enabled Lucid to scale AI with unprecedented velocity and discipline. See how Lucid is laying the foundation for an AI-first enterprise and redefining the role of Finance within a modern, technology-enabled automaker. This presentation is brought to you by PwC, an AWS Partner.

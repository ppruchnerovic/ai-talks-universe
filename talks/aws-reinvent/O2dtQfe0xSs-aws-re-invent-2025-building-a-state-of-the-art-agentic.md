## Description

The prompt is the new browser. It’s no secret that AI agents will transform how people interact with information and services, but what’s often overlooked is how agents can only thrive on the right foundation. Agents demand a new layer of connectivity, control, and security. Join Alex Drag and Michael Field from Kong as they explore how agents are becoming the new backbone of the internet — and what builders can do to get ready for it.

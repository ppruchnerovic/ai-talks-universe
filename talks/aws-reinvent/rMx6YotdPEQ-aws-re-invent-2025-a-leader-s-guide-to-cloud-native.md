## Description

Legacy applications run mission-critical processes, but increasingly limit innovation and organizational agility through outdated architectures. This session equips leaders with frameworks to refactor applications into cloud-native solutions while preserving business operations. Successful modernization requires answering strategic questions: why modernize, at what cost, how to modernize, what risks exist, and when to act. Learn how to define scope, prioritize features, and reskill teams through proven organizational models. Discover adaptive governance that replace traditional project management to deliver incremental value while managing inherent modernization risks. Leaders will explore how generative AI accelerates application analysis and migration, reducing modernization timelines.

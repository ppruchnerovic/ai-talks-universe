## Description

Enterprises are moving beyond cloud migration toward a future defined AI-driven autonomy. This session explores why modernization across infrastructure, applications, and data is critical to unlock AI’s full potential. Panelists will share real-world examples of predictive, self-healing, and AI-orchestrated operations, and discuss the mindset shift required for CIOs and technology leaders to achieve success. Attendees will learn how cloud-native architectures, AI agents, and intelligent workflows are converging to create autonomous enterprises. By building modernization as the foundation, organizations can enhance efficiency, break down silos, and accelerate innovation, positioning themselves to thrive in an AI-powered future.

## Description

Financial services companies struggle to scale compliance due diligence processes while maintaining quality as merchant volumes grow. Learn how Stripe revolutionized compliance operations by building an AI agent on Amazon Bedrock that automates complex merchant risk assessments, resulting in a 26% reduction in review handling time through agentic automation. Discover how they built reliability and observability into agent operations, handling thousands of investigations daily while ensuring accuracy of AI-generated insights. Explore architectural patterns for orchestrating multi-step workflows, implementing human-in-the-loop validation, and maintaining audit trails at scale.

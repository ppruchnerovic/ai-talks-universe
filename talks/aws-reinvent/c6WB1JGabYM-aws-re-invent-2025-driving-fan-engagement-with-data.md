## Description

AWS partners with major entertainment, games, and sports organizations to transform how fans, viewers, and players experience their products. In this session, you will learn about how leading organizations such as the NFL, Formula 1, and other major sports leagues are using Amazon Bedrock, Amazon SageMaker, and open lakehouse architectures to create engaging experiences for their viewers. AWS will share architecture patterns and insights on how your organization can utilize AWS services to create new offerings and better engage fans around the world based on their preferences.

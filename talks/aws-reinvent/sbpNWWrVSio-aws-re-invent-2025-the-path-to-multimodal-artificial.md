The evolution toward AGI isn't happening in text alone. It's unfolding across vision, motion, and understanding of our physical world. Drawing on Luma AI's Ray3 video generation model, we'll demonstrate how AI is creating coherent, physically plausible video content that's transforming industries from e-commerce and entertainment to brand marketing, advertising, and gaming. Through concrete use cases, we'll explore live deployments solving real business challenges: scaling creative asset production for retail, reimagining film and television workflows, and transforming agency operations. Whether you're building enterprise AI applications or planning your AI strategy, join us to see where multimodal AI is today and where we're heading tomorrow.

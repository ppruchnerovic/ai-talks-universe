## Description

As AI agents transform enterprise software, securing them with robust identity and access management is critical. Traditional models of authentication and authorization often fail when agents act across multiple systems and data sources. Drawing on WorkOS’s experience with SaaS and AI companies, this session explores risks of unchecked agent behavior, compliance challenges, and the need for first-class agent identities. We’ll discuss emerging approaches such as persona shadowing, delegation chains, capability tokens, and human-in-the-loop escalation, alongside evolving standards like OAuth, OIDC for Agents, GNAP, and verifiable credentials. Attendees will gain practical frameworks and insight for building safer, scalable agentic systems. This presentation is brought to you by WorkOS, an AWS Partner.

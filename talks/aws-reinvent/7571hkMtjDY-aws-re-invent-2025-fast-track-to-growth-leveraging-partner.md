## Description

Join AWS leaders Julia Chen, VP, Partner Core, and Matt Yanchyshyn, VP, AWS Marketplace & Partner Services, to explore how AWS is transforming partner success through profitable programs and product innovation from AWS Marketplace and Partner Services. Learn how AWS can fast-track your business growth through enhanced incentives, AI-driven automation, and global reach. Explore new features enabling faster time-to-value, including automated specialization validation, simplified contract management, and enhanced AWS Marketplace capabilities. Whether you're focused on migration acceleration, generative AI adoption, or expanding globally, you'll walk away with a plan to scale efficiently while increasing profitability. This session is intended for AWS Partners.

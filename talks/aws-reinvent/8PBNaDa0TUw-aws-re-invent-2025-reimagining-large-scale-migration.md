## Description

Dive deep into strategies for planning your large-scale AWS migration. In this session, we'll explore how to use AWS Transform and AI agents to accelerate your discovery, assessment, and planning for large-scale migrations. We will show you how planning migrations in spreadsheets is a thing of the past and how AI agents remove the heavy manual work of curating discovery data, building a business case, and planning a migration.

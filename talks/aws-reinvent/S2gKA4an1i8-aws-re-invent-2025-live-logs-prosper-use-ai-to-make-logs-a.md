## Description

Logs carry the richest context for troubleshooting, yet brittle pipelines, fragile parsers, and cost-driven sampling keep teams stuck in reactive grep mode. This session introduces Elastic's AI-powered Streams, an agentic system that automatically understands and enriches any log format (structured or unstructured), simplifies ingestion, parsing, storage, and analysis, and surfaces significant events and anomalies out of the box. The result: logs become your first and smartest investigative signal, accelerating root-cause analysis and delivering actionable insights in production. Live logs and prosper!

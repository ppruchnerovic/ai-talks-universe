## Description

High-performance computing (HPC) powers critical missions in federal science—from advanced modeling to large-scale Earth observation. Agencies like NOAA use HPC to scale research, forecasting, and data-intensive workloads that safeguard communities and inform decisions across supply chains, logistics, and operations. In this session, we’ll explore how AWS helps agencies deploy secure, scalable HPC environments tailored to mission needs. NOAA will share how it applies HPC today to accelerate science and improve prediction, while AWS looks ahead to the future—where HPC and artificial intelligence (AI) converge to unlock new opportunities for research, modeling, and collaboration across the public sector.

## Description

AI at scale isn’t theoretical—it’s running in production today, serving millions of citizens and customers across the UK. In this session, hear how two organizations—Capita and the UK Department for Science, Innovation & Technology—deployed AI solutions on AWS that are transforming services at national scale. Learn how Capita built a generative AI–powered contact center solution using Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Connect, AWS Lambda, and Amazon Transcribe to deliver measurable improvements in agent productivity and customer outcomes. Then, explore how the UK Government safely deployed conversational AI through GOV.UK Chat, using AWS infrastructure, governance frameworks, and iterative stress testing to serve millions while maintaining trust and compliance.

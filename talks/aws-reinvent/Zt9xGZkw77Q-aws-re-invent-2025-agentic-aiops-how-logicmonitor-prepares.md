## Description

Agentic AI is transforming enterprise IT, enabling systems that act autonomously, learn continuously, and drive faster, smarter decisions. Yet this new power introduces complexity. In this session, leaders from LogicMonitor, NTT, and WWT unpack what Agentic AI means for today’s IT organizations and how leaders can prepare. We’ll discuss how to evaluate your organization’s Agentic AIOps maturity, identify the right AI partners, and balance oversight with autonomy. Join us to explore what sets Agentic AIOps apart, how to assess readiness, and real-world case studies in production. This presentation is brought to you by LogicMonitor, an AWS Partner.

## Description

Join this technical deep-dive revealing how T-Mobile built their mission-critical platform that handles 200 million daily transactions across 130+ million subscribers with zero-downtime. Discover the serverless-first architecture powering the _T-Mobile website_, T-Life, and 8,000 retail locations that automatically scales from baseline to major device launches and other holiday events. Learn the secrets behind active-active deployment that achieved 99.99% availability through chaos engineering and intelligent failover mechanisms. Uncover how T-Mobile's AI-powered operations reduced MTTR by using machine learning automation that predicts capacity needs and optimizes resource allocation. Walk away with battle-tested architectural patterns for security, scaling, and resiliency.

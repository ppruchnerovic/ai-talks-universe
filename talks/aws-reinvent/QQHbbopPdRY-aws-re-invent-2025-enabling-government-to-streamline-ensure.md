## Description

Covered California serves nearly 14 million residents, where success depends on data — verified, analyzed, and activated at scale. In this session, learn how Covered California uses AWS to power identity verification with AI/ML, automate call center operations with agentic technologies, and build data pipelines that pinpoint citizens and residents most at risk of losing healthcare coverage. Attendees will walk away with a blueprint for using Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL, Databricks on AWS, Amazon Connect, and AI/ML services to turn complex, regulated datasets into actionable insights. The session highlights architectures, automation approaches, and lessons learned to help government leaders unlock data as the enabler of secure, citizen-focused innovation.

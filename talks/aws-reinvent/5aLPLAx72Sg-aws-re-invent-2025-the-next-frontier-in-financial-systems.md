## Description

Transformer-based foundation models are revolutionizing payment systems by providing deep contextual understanding of sequential transaction data. In this session, NVIDIA and AWS experts explain how large-scale tabular foundation models are trained and deployed to detect fraud in real time, personalize customer experiences, and interpret complex transaction patterns. Attendees will learn about the cloud-based AI factories enabling scalable deployment, NVIDIA’s accelerated computing for efficient training and tuning, and AWS’s secure and scalable cloud infrastructure.

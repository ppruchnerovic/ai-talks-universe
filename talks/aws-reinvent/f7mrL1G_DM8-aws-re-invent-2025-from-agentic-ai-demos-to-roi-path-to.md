## Description

Most enterprises are stuck in AI pilot purgatory—impressive demos, no ROI. Garther predicts that by 2028 80% of successful GenAI apps will run on existing data platforms, yet many still treat AI as “magic” exempt from existing data challenges. This session shows why batch pipelines kill model value and how Confluent’s data streaming platform and Streaming Agents, AWS Bedrock, Amazon SageMaker, and MCP Servers unlock scalable multi-agents systems that monitor business events and instantly act or trigger real-time automation. Leave with practical frameworks to cut through hype and build AI architectures that drive measurable revenue and tangible business outcomes.

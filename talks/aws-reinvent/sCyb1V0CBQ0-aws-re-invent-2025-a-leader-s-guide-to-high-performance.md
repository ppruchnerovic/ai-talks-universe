## Description

Cloud adoption success requires more than technical excellence—it demands the right organizational structure. This session reveals six characteristics that define high-performing software teams: highly aligned, loosely coupled, outcome focused, well controlled, right sized, and long lived. Drawing from patterns across digital-native companies, you'll learn advanced frameworks that go beyond standard agile practices. Discover practical assessment methods and implementation strategies to overcome organizational barriers and build teams that consistently deliver speed and business outcomes. Designed for IT executives and change agents ready to elevate performance or scale for growth.

## Description

This session demonstrates how to leverage Amazon CloudWatch's comprehensive observability capabilities for both traditional applications and generative AI workloads. Customers building generative AI-powered workloads face challenges in understanding end-user outcomes, AI performance, health, accuracy, and quality issues at scale. Whether you're dealing with high latency, excessive token consumption, or unexpected errors, Amazon CloudWatch's pre-built dashboards provide comprehensive monitoring capabilities and insights into end-user outcomes, AI performance, health, and accuracy. Learn how observability can help bring reliability across your entire application stack.

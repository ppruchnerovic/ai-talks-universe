## Description

AI adoption marks the next generation of cloud innovation and it’s reshaping the attack surface. Shadow AI projects, new control planes, and sensitive data pipelines are converging with traditional cloud misconfigurations and identity gaps. This session shows how Wiz helps organizations secure both cloud and AI by surfacing risks in context from S3 pipelines to SageMaker models and unifying ASM and AI-SPM to give teams the visibility, context, and confidence to act. This presentation is brought to you by Wiz, an AWS Partner.

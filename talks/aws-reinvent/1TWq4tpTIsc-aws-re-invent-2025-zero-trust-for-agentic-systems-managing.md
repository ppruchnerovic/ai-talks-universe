## Description

Agentic systems—AI-driven applications that autonomously act across services—are transforming enterprise workflows, but they also create a surge in non-human identities (NHIs) and secrets that expand the attack surface. In this session, we’ll explore a vulnerable agentic application scenario to illustrate the risks of static credentials, permissive access, and leaked secrets. We’ll then demonstrate how HashiCorp Vault and HCP Vault Radar enable a zero-trust architecture that mitigates these threats while preserving developer agility. Attendees will leave with practical strategies to secure agentic systems from development to production on AWS. This presentation is brought to you by HashiCorp, an AWS Partner.

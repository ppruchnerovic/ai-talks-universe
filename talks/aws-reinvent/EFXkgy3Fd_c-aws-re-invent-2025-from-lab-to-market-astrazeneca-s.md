## Description

AstraZeneca is on a mission to deliver 20 life-changing medicines by 2030, and is leveraging agentic AI technology to make it happen. The company has implemented AI solutions throughout their organization, revolutionizing their data management and analysis capabilities. Through their innovative approach of using modular, multi-agent AI systems, AstraZeneca has deployed multiple AI tools into production across multiple business units. During this presentation, we'll dive into how the company is implementing AI solutions in both their clinical development and commercial divisions to achieve tangible outcomes. Additionally, we'll explore how AstraZeneca is utilizing AWS technologies to create a scalable infrastructure that can readily incorporate future technological advancements.

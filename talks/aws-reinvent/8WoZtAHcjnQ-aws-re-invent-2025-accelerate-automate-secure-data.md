## Description

Accelerate your cloud journey by moving your data to AWS quickly and securely with AWS DataSync. In this session, discover best practices for how to move your data at scale with increased performance. Hear a success story from PathAI, an AWS customer whose platform enables AI-powered digital pathology solutions, and learn how they used DataSync to build scalable AI workflows across a global network of hospitals and labs.

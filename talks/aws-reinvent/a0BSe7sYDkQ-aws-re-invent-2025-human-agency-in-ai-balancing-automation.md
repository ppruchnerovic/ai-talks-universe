## Description

As AI-driven automation transforms organizations, a balance between efficiency, control, and human agency is essential. This panel will explore how Boomi’s AI-powered integration and automation solutions—including the Boomi Agent Control Tower—enable enterprises to automate intelligently while ensuring governance and transparency. Experts will discuss best practices for leveraging AI responsibly, maintaining compliance, and fostering collaboration between AI and human decision-makers. Attendees will gain insights into how Boomi’s advancements help organizations scale AI-driven innovation without compromising control, security, or human agency in an increasingly digital landscape. This presentation is brought to you by Boomi, an AWS Partner.

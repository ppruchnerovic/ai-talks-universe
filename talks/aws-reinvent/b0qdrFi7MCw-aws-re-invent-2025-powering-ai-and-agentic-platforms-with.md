## Description

Organizations are rapidly building AI applications and workflows to automate manual processes and boost productivity. The reality: many of these initiatives never reach production. This session explores the critical challenges of building, deploying, and scaling end-to-end enterprise AI systems and shares proven technical architectures and best practices to go from POC to production with Amazon Bedrock. You'll hear directly from Vercel, a leading provider of AI dev tools, as they share how they built and scaled their production AI platform with Amazon Bedrock and how their AI SDK accelerates building AI applications with Amazon Bedrock integrations.

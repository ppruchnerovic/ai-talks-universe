## Description

Explore CSL's comprehensive transformation strategy focused on migrating complex VMware workloads and consolidating fragmented SAP systems to build a more agile and resilient foundation. Learn how CSL leveraged agentic AI with AWS Transform to reduce technical debt and licensing costs across 4000+ VMware servers, revolutionizing traditional migration approaches and accelerating discovery and planning processes by 10x. You'll also discover how CSL unified their fragmented ERP landscape and established enterprise-wide data standards through RISE with SAP on AWS. Gain technical guidance, get inspired with lessons learned, and walk away with actionable insights that you can use in your organization.

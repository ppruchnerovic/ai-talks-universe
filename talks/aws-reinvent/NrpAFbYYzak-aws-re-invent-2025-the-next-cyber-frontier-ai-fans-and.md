## Description

CISOs are navigating an era of unprecedented complexity while safeguarding the world’s most beloved entertainment and sports brands. In this discussion, security leaders from Anthropic, Disney, the NBA, and Amazon share how they balance innovation with resilience. Together, they’ll explore the evolving threat landscape, the rise of AI-driven risks and defenses, and what it takes to protect billions of users, fans, and customers worldwide. They will also examine how AI is disrupting security leadership—demanding real-time decisions and redefining what it means to protect entertainment in a world that never sleeps.

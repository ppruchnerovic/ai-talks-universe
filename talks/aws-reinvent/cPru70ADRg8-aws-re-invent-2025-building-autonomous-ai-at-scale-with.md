## Description

Building autonomous AI at scale with Amazon Bedrock 2025 marks the breakthrough year for autonomous AI systems, from vibe coding to specialized assistants and super agents disrupting multi-billion dollar industries. Amazon Bedrock empowers disruptive startups to rapidly prototype autonomous applications and agents, seamlessly scaling from proof-of-concept to production systems serving millions of users. We'll tackle top scaling challenges, share best practices, and hear from Genspark, a cutting-edge super agent startup, showcasing their complete journey—from rapid MVP development to handling production traffic at scale. Discover specific Bedrock capabilities and support programs that optimize performance, reduce cost, achieve global scalability, and enable your vision without enterprise-level infrastructure investments.

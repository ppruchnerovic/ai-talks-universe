## Description

In this session, you will learn how Formula 1 is revving up fan experiences with cloud and generative AI technology. Discover how Formula 1 is revolutionizing motorsport entertainment through cutting-edge cloud and AI technology. Learn how real-time data analytics and generative AI are transforming race broadcasts and fan engagement, and how AWS Media Services is powering 1 TV Premium, F1's new streaming service that launched in 2025. We'll explore F1's innovative solutions for enhancing race operations and fan experiences, demonstrating how technology is reshaping the future of live sports entertainment.

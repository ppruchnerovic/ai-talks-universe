## Description

Capital One’s governance, risk and compliance platform represents a leap in risk management—built natively on AWS to deliver real-time decisioning, regulatory compliance, & enterprise resilience. While many financial institutions are just beginning to move from on-prem to cloud, Capital One is already ahead. In this session, leaders Solomon Francis & Matt Davis share how they built a GRC platform from the ground up on AWS using Bedrock, SageMaker, & S3. The platform demonstrates how a cloud-native platform can future-proof financial institutions against rising risk & compliance demands. This session offers practical lessons in designing & building risk platform in AWS. This presentation is brought to you by PwC, an AWS Partner.

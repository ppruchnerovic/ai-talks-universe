## Description

Enterprises often withhold sensitive data from AI models due to risk, which can result in limited business insights. This session explores how tokenization can address this challenge by securing training and response data, which renders the data useless to unauthorized access and potential exposure while preserving its analytical value. Attendees will gain insights from Capital One’s large-scale tokenization implementation, along with actionable strategies, architectural patterns, and implementation blueprints for applying tokenization within complex AWS environments. You'll leave with actionable guidance for securing diverse AI-driven use cases at scale. This presentation is brought to you by Capital One, an AWS Partner.

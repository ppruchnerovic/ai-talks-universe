## Description

As organizations race to embrace AI, the question is no longer if AI will transform security and business operations, but how leaders can harness it responsibly and effectively. This session explores the latest attacker innovations powered by AI, and the defensive strategies security teams must adopt to stay ahead. You’ll gain insight into the real-world challenges enterprises face in managing AI risk, balancing productivity with protection, and ensuring trust in rapidly evolving systems. Through practical examples and forward-looking analysis, we’ll cut through the hype to reveal the tangible ways AI is reshaping cybersecurity and enterprise resilience.

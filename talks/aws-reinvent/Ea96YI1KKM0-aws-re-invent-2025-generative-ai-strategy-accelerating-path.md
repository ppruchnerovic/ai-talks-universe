## Description

Learn how to scale generative AI experiments into production-ready solutions. This session provides a strategic and technical framework for accelerating AI deployment while managing risks and maintaining governance. We'll cover practical approaches to align AI initiatives with business goals, establish evaluation criteria, and build effective teams. Through real-world examples, discover proven methods for fast-tracking implementation while ensuring security and quality. Gain actionable strategies for overcoming deployment challenges and creating repeatable processes that reduce time-to-value. Ideal for technology leaders focused on maximizing AI investments and driving enterprise adoption.

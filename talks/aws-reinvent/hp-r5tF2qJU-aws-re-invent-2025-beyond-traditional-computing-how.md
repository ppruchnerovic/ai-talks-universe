## Description

Discover how Amazon WorkSpaces is transforming the digital workplace with new end user features and intelligent management and automation capabilities. In this breakout, Deloitte will share how they deploy WorkSpaces at scale globally for different personas, and Dizzion will share how they use AI to automate onboarding, provisioning, and intelligent monitoring. Learn how with WorkSpaces, you can modernize yesterday’s deployment patterns, and build the foundation for tomorrow’s flexible and scalable AI enabled workplace.

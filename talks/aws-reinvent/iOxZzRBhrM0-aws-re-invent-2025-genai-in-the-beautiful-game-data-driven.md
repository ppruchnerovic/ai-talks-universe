## Description

Discover how top-tier football clubs are leveraging generative AI to gain a competitive edge. This session explores real-world applications of AI in professional sports, from tactical analysis to talent scouting. Learn how clubs are using AWS's data platform and AI technologies to identify defensive gaps, optimize formations, and uncover rising stars. Hear firsthand accounts of the transformational impact of AI on decision-making and performance both on and off the pitch. Join us to explore the intersection of technology and the beautiful game.

## Description

Join our session to discover production-ready generative architectures AI pioneers leverage to ship tomorrow’s customer experiences and transform industries with lean teams, constrained budgets but accelerated timelines. You’ll learn how to apply the platform mindset and AWS Generative AI services - Amazon Q, Amazon Bedrock, and Amazon SageMaker and integrate them with other AWS services to deliver both agentic and non-agentic workloads at scale that stay secure, cost-efficient, and aligned with responsible-AI guardrails. Leave with actionable patterns and inspiration that you can use in your own organizations to scale Generative AI workloads.

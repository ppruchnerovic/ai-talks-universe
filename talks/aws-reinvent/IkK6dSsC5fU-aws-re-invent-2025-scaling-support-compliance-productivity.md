## Description

Discover how Coinbase transformed customer support, compliance automation, and employee productivity using a unified AI platform powered by Amazon Bedrock. This multi-layered system integrates knowledge bases, real-time tool execution, and domain-specific AI agents to handle tens of thousands of interactions monthly. The platform enables fully agentic customer support, compliance and other productivity workflows, optimizes manual reviews, and improves productivity with intelligent task routing and seamless integration with Coinbase's tools. We'll examine its automated testing, performance metrics, and robust security measures, ensuring scalable and reliable operations.

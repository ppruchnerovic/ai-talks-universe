## Description

Generative AI deployments face challenges with high computing costs and complex scaling requirements. AWS Trainium2 and Inferentia2 chips deliver compelling price-performance for both training and inference workloads. In this session, discover innovations that enable efficient scaling of advanced AI workloads, from training and serving MoE models to deploying agentic systems with reasoning capabilities. Learn how customers are achieving exceptional performance while significantly reducing costs.

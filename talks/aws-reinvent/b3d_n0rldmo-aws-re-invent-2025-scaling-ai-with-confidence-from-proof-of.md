## Description

From a proof of concept to scaling AI for top U.K. insurance brands, CDL’s journey highlights the challenges of deploying AI at scale while staying compliant, cost-effective, and trustworthy. This session covers their use of observability to track performance and risks, building guardrails for safe AI outputs, and strategies to scale without compromising cost, quality, or governance. Learn key lessons about the people and processes behind successful AI adoption. If you’re looking for practical insights to turn AI ideas into real impact, don’t miss this session.

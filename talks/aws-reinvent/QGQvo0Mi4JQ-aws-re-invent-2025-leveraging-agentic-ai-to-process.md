## Description

This session explores how LPL leveraged Agentic AI for its middle office operations processes powered by Capgemini and AWS native services—is transforming intelligent automation workloads in financial services. By eliminating costly platform dependencies and leveraging scalable cloud-native tools like Lambda, SageMaker, and Bedrock, the platform named Automanvik enabled LPL Financial to build secure, efficient digital workers that enhance client experience and reduce operational overhead. With proven use cases in wealth management and compliance, and deep expertise from Capgemini and AWS, this session reveals why Agentic AI is the future of intelligent automation—and why now is the time to invest.

## Description

Join HSBC and AWS to explore how organizations can use operational data and Large Language Models to boost release velocity and reduce operational burden during an incident. Discover how HSBC is transforming Change Management with Amazon Bedrock, analyzing historical trends to predict and prevent implementation failures. Learn how to extend this approach into a continuous improvement framework that turns operational data from a passive record into an active tool for risk reduction and service excellence.

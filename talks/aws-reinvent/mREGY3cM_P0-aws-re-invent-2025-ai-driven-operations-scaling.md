## Description

Discover how Kakao and Samsung transformed operations with AI on AWS. Kakao achieved 400x faster observability for 50 million daily users using EKS with ClickHouse and AI-driven analytics, processing billions of log events and reducing query times from hours to seconds while enabling proactive anomaly detection. Samsung revolutionized cloud cost management with autonomous multi-agent systems using Strands SDK and Bedrock AgentCore, achieving hundreds of thousands of dollars in savings through real-time collaboration and automatic optimization. Learn practical architectures and blueprints for deploying enterprise-scale AI automation that delivers measurable business impact across observability and cost management.

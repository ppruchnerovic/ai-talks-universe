## Description

Join Intel and AWS leadership for an exclusive deep dive into the transformative power of the latest generation of Intel Xeon 6-powered AWS EC2 instances. Discover how these cutting-edge compute platforms are delivering unprecedented performance, scalability, and total cost of ownership (TCO) benefits for enterprises running on AWS. This session will showcase real-world success stories from our top customers who are harnessing the exclusive synergy of Intel and AWS to accelerate innovation, optimize workloads, and unlock new levels of efficiency. Whether you're a CTO, cloud architect, or business leader, this session will equip you with insights to make smarter infrastructure decisions and stay ahead in a compute-driven world. Join us to unleash to power of compute on your cloud! This presentation is brought to you by Intel, an AWS Partner.

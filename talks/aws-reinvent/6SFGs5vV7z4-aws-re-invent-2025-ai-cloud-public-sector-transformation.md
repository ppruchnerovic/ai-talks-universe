## Description

Join this dynamic session to see how PwC and AWS are redefining public sector service with cloud and AI. Medway NHS Foundation Trust has deployed an AI-powered contact solution that streamlines patient inquiries, bookings, and follow-ups, slashing wait times and easing staff workloads, while upholding data security and NHS compliance. Meanwhile, Birmingham City Council, in partnership with PwC and AWS, migrated legacy services to a scalable cloud platform, boosting service delivery, resilience, and cross department collaboration. Attendees will explore governance, risk management, cyber security, and disaster recovery, plus tangible wins: reduced on-prem dependence and faster, data driven decision making for smarter outcomes. This presentation is brought to you by PwC, an AWS Partner.

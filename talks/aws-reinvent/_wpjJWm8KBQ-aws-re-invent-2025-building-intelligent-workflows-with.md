Transform traditional enterprise workflows into intelligent, automated operations using modern event-driven architecture and AI capabilities. This session demonstrates how to evolve monolithic systems into scalable, cloud-native solutions that leverage AI agents for near real-time decision making and automated processing. Through practical examples of business process modernization, we'll show how combining AWS services creates resilient, intelligent systems that reduce operational overhead while improving response times.

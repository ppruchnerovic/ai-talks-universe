## Description

According to the CrowdStrike 2025 Threat Hunting Report, cloud intrusions surged 136% in the first half of 2025 (compared to all of 2024) and adversaries are rapidly sharpening their tactics in the cloud, making visibility into cloud events a non-negotiable for security teams. With Falcon Next-Gen SIEM and Falcon Cloud Security, you can quickly onboard essential data from AWS CloudTrail, Security Hub, and Guard Duty with a single click to detect and respond to cloud threats at speed. Join this session to discover how AWS and CrowdStrike are revolutionizing defenses with AI, leveling the playing field for SOC teams against today's sophisticated threats. This presentation is brought to you by CrowdStrike, an AWS Partner.

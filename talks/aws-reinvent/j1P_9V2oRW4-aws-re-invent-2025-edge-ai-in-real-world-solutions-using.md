## Description

Join us to learn how deploying AI in physical systems can offer speed, efficiency and cost savings. We’ll explore a real-world use case of defect detection in manufacturing to show how Edge AI can be applied from incident alert to diagnosis. Walk through practical applications of AWS SageMaker models running on edge devices based on Qualcomm® Dragonwing™ processors and how you can integrate AWS IoT Greengrass and Amazon Bedrock for a host of triggered actions and recommendations. We’ll also show how you can transform legacy devices by adding intelligence and connecting them to the cloud, including how you can use Qualcomm AI Hub and Edge Impulse with AWS solutions to optimize, deploy and maintain your Edge AI implementations. This presentation is brought to you by Qualcomm, an AWS Partner.

## Description

Healthcare organizations struggle with disconnected administrative systems and manual processes that create inefficient patient experiences and operational bottlenecks. Join UC San Diego Health to learn how they implemented an AI-powered patient engagement solution using Amazon Connect to address complex administrative processes through a unified patient engagement journey across the health system. We'll demonstrate the implementation of Amazon Connect with Epic EHR integration to enable self-service patient authentication and appointment information retrieval.

# Front end development in 2040 by Fotis Adamakis

**Fotis Adamakis**

`Devoxx` · `Devoxx` · `2025` · `40 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szlyZm6q2xY) · [Conference site](https://devoxx.com/)

## Description

Let’s imagine front-end development 15 years from now. Will TypeScript still be around? What about React? And what will happen to the everyday problems we deal with today, like state management, time zones, and decimals? Will accessibility finally be treated as a first-class citizen? And how much of it will be handled by AI?
Join me as we explore disruptive ideas, make bold predictions, and have a bit of fun imagining where front-end development might be headed in the not-so-distant future.

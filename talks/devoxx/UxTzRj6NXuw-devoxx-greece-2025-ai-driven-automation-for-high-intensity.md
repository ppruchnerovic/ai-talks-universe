# Devoxx Greece 2025 - AI-Driven Automation for High-Intensity Development by Stefanos Giorkas

**Stefanos Giorkas**

## Description

As software development becomes faster and more complex, keeping up with testing demands is a constant challenge. This presentation highlights how artificial intelligence is transforming the way teams approach test case automation. Instead of relying solely on manual review and prioritization, AI can analyze large volumes of test cases, assess their suitability for automation, and help teams focus their efforts where it matters most. By reducing repetitive work and providing data-driven insights, AI-driven automation empowers teams to maintain high quality, improve test coverage, and keep pace with rapid development cycles.
To support this shift, several smart AI-driven strategies are being explored—such as automatically scoring test cases for automation potential, integrating intelligent suggestions into existing workflows, and using conversational tools to interact with test data in real time. These approaches aim to boost productivity, streamline collaboration, and allow QA teams to focus on high-impact areas while letting AI handle the heavy lifting.

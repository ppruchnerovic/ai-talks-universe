# Harmonizing Code and Melody: making electronical music in a matter of speaking! by Jan Van Wassenhov

**Jan Van Wassenhov**

`Devoxx` · `Devoxx` · `2024` · `51 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Cy8pGknPpg) · [Conference site](https://devoxx.com/)

## Description

Explore the innovative intersection of a multi-agent system (MAS) and music with MusicAgent, a groundbreaking homegrown project.This session delves into the creation of multiple unique agents capable of composing, arranging, and producing music through the fusion of coding with Python, Sonic Pi using large language models (LLM's).Witness the intricacies of using Sonic Pi for live music creation through code and generative AI models in crafting musical pieces. Learn about the challenges and triumphs in MAS-driven music creation and envision the future of music and technology.And not to forget, you'll experience the thrill of live electronic music performance, generated on the spot using MusicAgent.Discover how anyone can easily control the full music creation flow, from conceptualization to recording, and even creating album cover art.Are you ready to start coding and generating and creating your own songs?Join me to see how MusicAgent can transform the musical landscape!

## Description

What if you could solve complex problems, unlock creativity, and foster real dialogue—using Lego bricks?In this interactive workshop, we introduce participants to LEGO® SERIOUS PLAY®, a hands-on methodology designed to boost engagement, deepen reflection, and facilitate team alignment. No buzzwords—just bricks, stories, and shared meaning.You’ll experience it firsthand: from the playful “Duck Challenge” to a deeper exercise where you build a model of your role, you’ll explore how thinking with your hands unlocks new insights and makes abstract ideas tangible.Along the way, we’ll explore:The science behind “thinking with your hands”How LSP bypasses cognitive filters to trigger honest, creative conversationsReal-world use cases from teams in HR, design, leadership and transformationWhether you’re curious about facilitation, coaching, or team dynamics—come ready to build, reflect, and share!Key TakeawaysDiscover what LEGO® SERIOUS PLAY® really is (and what it’s not)Learn how LSP supports creative thinking, team alignment, and problem-solvingExperience two LSP mini-workshops: the Duck Challenge + Job RepresentationLeave with ideas to apply it in your own team, meetings, or workshopsTarget audienceDesigners, developers, and product owners interested in innovative facilitation toolsTeam leaders, Scrum Masters, and Agile coaches looking to boost team engagementAnyone curious about play-based methods to foster meaningful collaboration and insight

## Description

Discover how to successfully integrate Generative AI into a platform, where an AI driven solution serves the largest global enterprises, handling petabyte-scale data collection and monitoring mission-critical applications.

This talk will explore the goals of the Dynatrace platform, its hypermodel AI Davis, the integration process of Generative AI, and the challenges and triumphs encountered on the path to General Availability (GA) release.

Join us to learn about implementing Generative AI at scale and see how we ""drink our own champagne""!

# Self Healing Rollouts: Automating Production Fixes with Agentic AI & Argo Rollouts by Carlos Sanch

**Carlos Sanch**

`Devoxx` · `Devoxx` · `2025` · `46 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ABjIUw7ojiU) · [Conference site](https://devoxx.com/)

## Description

Even with robust CI/CD, production rollouts can hit unexpected snags. While Argo Rollouts excels in Kubernetes at progressive delivery and automated rollbacks to mitigate deployment issues, what if we could go a step further?This session explores how to elevate your release process by integrating Agentic AI with Argo Rollouts. We&#39;ll demonstrate how an intelligent agent can automatically analyze logs when a rollout fails, pinpointing the root cause. Beyond diagnosis, these agents can take proactive steps on your behalf—from suggesting and even implementing code fixes or deployment manifest adjustments, to generating new pull requests and sending immediate notifications. This approach moves us closer to truly self-healing deployments.Join us to learn how to combine the power of Kubernetes and Argo Rollouts with the autonomous capabilities of Agentic AI, achieving a release experience that is not only seamless but also resilient.

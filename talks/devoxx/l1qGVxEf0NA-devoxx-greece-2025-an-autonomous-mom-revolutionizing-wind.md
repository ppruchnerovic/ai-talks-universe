## Description

Full Title : An Autonomous Mom: Revolutionizing Wind Farm Management with AI, Sensor Data and Connectivity.

Speaker Vassilis Vassilatos

In this insightful presentation, MindWave will delve into the cutting-edge integration of IoT and AI reasoning to create autonomous systems for wind farm management. Drawing from personal anecdotes and industry expertise, MindWave will explore how advanced technologies can optimize energy consumption in wind farms.
The session will begin with a relatable introduction, highlighting the universal challenge of energy efficiency. The presentation will then transition to a detailed explanation of the innovative architecture behind an autonomous wind farm management system. This includes the integration of AI for logical reasoning and decision-making, sensor technology for real-time data collection, and robust connectivity solutions.
Key topics covered will include:
The design and implementation of an autonomous system for managing wind turbines.
Simple Practical demonstrations of code for backend services and AI interaction.
The future of data-driven industries and the transition to autonomous systems.
Software engineers and tech enthusiasts will gain valuable insights into the latest advancements in IoT and AI, as well as practical tips for implementing these technologies in real-world applications.
Target Audience: Software engineers, AI enthusiasts, and anyone interested in the future of autonomous systems.
Key Takeaways:
Understanding the integration of IoT and AI in autonomous systems.
A glimpse into the future of data-driven industries and autonomous technologies.

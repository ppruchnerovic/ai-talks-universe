# [VDBUH2025] Ibrahim Gharbi & Yassine Benabbas - Kotlin for the JVM and beyond

**Ibrahim Gharbi, Yassine Benabbas**

`Devoxx` · `Devoxx` · `2025` · `41 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=svRcOscXmXU) · [Conference site](https://devoxx.com/)

## Description

Kotlin is much more than just another JVM language; it is a complete ecosystem! From backend development to cross-platform front-end development, and even data science, it is everywhere. This is part of the DNA of Kotlin since its creation and grows exponentially.

This talk demonstrates how developers can easily become fullstack ones by adopting Kotlin. We will share three demos covering different stacks of development including experimental ones. We will achieve all of this by keeping a clean architecture and integrating libraries such as Ktor client/server and DataFrame.

By the end of this presentation, you will be delighted to realize how easy and pleasant it is to kick-start development of mobile, desktop, web, backend, data-science apps and make infra-as-code with Kotlin. Even more app stacks are already in your reach thanks to Kotlin!

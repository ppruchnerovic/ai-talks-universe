## Description

The Gemini ecosystem is expanding at an unprecedented rate, introducing a torrent of updates and new capabilities that can be challenging even for seasoned developers to track. This session aims to provide a clear and consolidated overview of the most significant recent advancements, ensuring the developer community can keep pace with this rapid innovation.We will explore the dawn of a new, agentic era powered by Gemini. This paradigm shift is creating a world of possibilities, led by powerful new assistants designed to understand, reason, and act on your behalf. We will dive into practical examples of these agents, such as the open-source Gemini CLI that brings AI directly into your terminal, and Jules, an asynchronous coding agent that can autonomously write, test, and improve code.Furthermore, the talk will delve into the latest breakthroughs in vibe coding. We&#39;ll demonstrate how Gemini is revolutionizing the development workflow, from the generation of assets to the creation of complex code and entire applications based on high-level descriptions.A special focus will be placed on Google AI Studio, showcasing how it serves as a powerful environment to experiment with and harness these new capabilities for your own projects.Join us to navigate this new frontier and understand how to leverage the latest tools in the Gemini galaxy.

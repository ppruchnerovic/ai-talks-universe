## Description

Please subscribe to our YouTube channel @ https://www.youtube.com/@DevoxxForever

Join the maintainers of LangChain4j for a fast-paced tour of its latest features and how to use them in real-world apps. We’ll demo an AI researcher agent for drug discovery while covering concepts like guardrails, observability, multi-LLM orchestration, agents, and testing for non-deterministic systems. We’ll also explore advanced RAG, basic RAG’s less clumsy big brother, to give your models access to data from documents, databases, and the web. By the end of this talk, you'll be ready to take your AI-powered proof-of-concept to production.

## Description

Artificial intelligence is all the buzz right now—everyone’s experimenting with tools like ChatGPT, but many small and medium-sized companies haven’t fully embraced its potential.
This talk delves into the obstacles companies face when adopting AI and offers practical solutions to navigate these challenges. Learn how to blend your company's unique expertise with AI capabilities to forge a path toward Enterprise Intelligence—a powerful synergy that enhances productivity and decision-making. We'll share a compelling case study from CGS, highlighting our successful collaboration with clients to integrate a large language model into our RIAS software. By utilizing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), we've made intelligent insights accessible to teams, fostering innovation and competitive edge.

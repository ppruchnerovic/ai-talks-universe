# Unlocking AI Coding Assistants: Real World Use Cases by Gunter Rotsaert

**Gunter Rotsaert**

`Devoxx` · `Devoxx` · `2025` · `149 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LtAe8EB72OI) · [Conference site](https://devoxx.com/)

## Description

Have you struggled with AI coding assistants, finding their outputs inaccurate, non-compilable, or inconsistent with your coding standards? This was my experience too, until I discovered how to harness their true potential. By learning to interpret their responses, refine prompts, and recognize their limitations, I transformed AI coding assistants into invaluable tools for writing production-grade software.In this talk, I will share real-world examples from my own projects, highlighting how AI coding assistants can significantly enhance your development workflow. Through practical tips and tricks, you will learn how to maximize the benefits of these tools, ensuring they become an integral part of your coding process. Then, take it a step further and uncover the capabilities of agentic coding.

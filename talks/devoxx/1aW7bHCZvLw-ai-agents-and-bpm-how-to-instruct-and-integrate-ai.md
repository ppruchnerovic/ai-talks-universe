## Description

AI is increasingly present in the IT landscape. Beyond hype, its real power lies in handling unstructured, complex processes — if we can make it trustworthy. At Belfius Insurance, we follow a simple principle: augmentation first, automation next. We start by assisting colleagues with AI components, then carefully extend to automation of routine tasks.  In this talk, we will share how we: • Design processes with a workflow engine (BPM) that integrate AI interactions and safeguards. • Use feedback loops with business users to build trust and improve models. • Visualize and monitor AI outputs to keep systems transparent. • Decide when and how to involve humans in the loop. • Apply these principles in our first deployments, where AI supports claim handlers with summarization and document analysis. By working side by side with business experts, we aim to make AI a trusted partner across insurance processes.

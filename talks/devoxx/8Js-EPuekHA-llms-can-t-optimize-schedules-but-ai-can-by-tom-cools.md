# LLMs can't optimize schedules, but AI can! by Tom Cools

**Tom Cools**

`Devoxx` · `Devoxx` · `2025` · `16 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Js-EPuekHA) · [Conference site](https://devoxx.com/)

## Description

GenAI and LLMs are awesome and are perceived by some as the solution for everything. However, when it comes to common tasks like creating schedules they tend to produce unreliable or nonsensical results.

Fortunately, there’s a lesser known branch of AI that excels at complex scheduling: mathematical optimization. We'll discuss why LLMs fail at these tasks, how constraint solving provides a robust alternative and how you can leverage the Open Source Timefold Solver so you don't even have to worry about the math.

You'll see this form of AI in action and walk away with a powerful new tool for your problem-solving toolbox.

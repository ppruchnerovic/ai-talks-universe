## Description

AI is flooding the developer toolbox. Chatbots, coding assistants, agents, local LLMs... it’s a mess.This talk is your survival guide. We’ll cut through the noise and get real: what do developers actually need? Reading code? Generating it? Automating tasks? Staying private? For each case, I’ll show the right kind of tools, with demos, hard truths, and guidance on prompt engineering, privacy trade-offs, and flexibility (self-hosted vs. SaaS). You’ll see strengths, limits, and a simple decision grid to help you choose (or say no) in your own context. Because surviving the AI jungle is about knowing where you want to go.

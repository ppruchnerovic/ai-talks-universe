## Description

AI agents are here, evolving fast, and poised to fundamentally reshape how we live, work, govern, and relate to one another. From autonomous coding assistants to distributed agent ecosystems making decisions on our behalf, we are entering a new era of software that can act with intent.In this thought-provoking session, we explore the deep and far-reaching consequences of AI agents on every facet of human society:Technology &amp; Work: What happens when agents take on complex knowledge work, collaborate with humans in software teams, and begin making architectural decisions? Will they elevate us—or replace us?Economics &amp; Labour: As cognitive automation spreads, who benefits? We’ll unpack the economic ripple effects across industries, from productivity gains to job displacement and re-skilling.Governance &amp; Law: Autonomous agents will challenge legal frameworks, and accountability. Who’s liable when an agent acts? What does “intent” mean in a world of autonomous agents?Ethics &amp; Privacy: With agents that observe, learn, and act in persistent digital spaces, what are the risks to personal freedom, surveillance, and human dignity?Culture &amp; Identity: When generative agents create art, write books, and simulate personalities, how do we define creativity, authenticity, and human expression?Whether you’re an optimist, a skeptic, or somewhere in between, this session will challenge your assumptions, spark new questions, and help you prepare for the world that’s rapidly unfolding.

## Description

This session explores the innovative approach of blending child-computer interaction, engaging UI/UX design, and gamification to revolutionize AI education for young learners. Attendees will learn how to craft captivating, kid-friendly AI courses that transform young minds from tech consumers into tech creators. Key topics include:Innovative Curriculum Design: Techniques to simplify complex AI concepts into fun, understandable lessons for kids.Interactive Learning Tools: Hands-on projects and activities that make AI accessible and exciting.Screen Time Optimization with AI: Leveraging AI to determine the optimal amount of screen time for effective learning without overexposure.Hybrid Learning Approaches: Combining online and offline activities to create a balanced and holistic learning experience.Challenges and Solutions: Strategies to overcome obstacles in developing engaging AI courses for children.Impact and Outcomes: Evidence of the transformative impact of these courses on sparking curiosity and fostering a new generation of tech-savvy innovators.This session aims to provide valuable insights and practical tips on nurturing the next generation's interest in AI, making it suitable for educators, developers, and AI enthusiasts.

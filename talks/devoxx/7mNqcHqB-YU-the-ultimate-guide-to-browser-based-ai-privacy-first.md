## Description

The Ultimate Guide to Browser-Based AI: Privacy-First, Offline-Ready, and Fully Future-Proof by Maxim Salnikov

Generative and intelligent features are quickly becoming a core part of modern web experiences—but they often come with trade-offs: privacy concerns, reliance on internet connectivity, and the cost of using cloud-based APIs. What if you could bring powerful AI directly into the browser, on the user's device?

In this session, we’ll explore the full landscape of browser-based AI and machine learning—no servers required. From text generation and image recognition to real-time transcription and offline copilots, the web platform now provides a rich toolbox of APIs and tools to enable truly private, performant, and intelligent experiences.

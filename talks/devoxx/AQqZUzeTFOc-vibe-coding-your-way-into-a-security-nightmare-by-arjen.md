## Description

In this talks, I explore the concept of &quot;vibe coding&quot; and addresses the potential pitfalls of unguided AI-generated code. I stand for a balanced approach, advocating for AI as a powerful tool that, when wielded with enough knowledge and background, can accelerate innovation. I focus on equipping developers with practical rules and insights to prevent &quot;security nightmares,&quot; ensuring that the dream of rapid AI-assisted development is built on best practices. I show you how things can go wrong with real case studies and will help you understand AI&#39;s limitations, the critical need for code review, adherence to established software development standards, and continuous learning in the fast-evolving landscape of AI security.Key take-aways:In the talk I introduce my 7 rules for secure coding in the age of AI, and with a little bit of humor show you where things can go horribly wrong, so that you do not make the same mistakes!Target audience:Developers wanting to use AI to generate code, AI enthousiasts who want to build secure systems.

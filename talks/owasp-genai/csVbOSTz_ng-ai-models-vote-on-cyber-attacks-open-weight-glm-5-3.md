## Description

Threat actors are no longer relying on a single AI model to run an attack. Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama break down new research on multi-model command and control, and on how open-weight models are putting frontier-level cyber capability in anyone's hands.

Key Takeaway:
Frontier labs can't see what happens on infrastructure they don't control. Once safeguards can be removed cheaply, defenders lose both the guardrails and the early-warning reporting they've relied on.

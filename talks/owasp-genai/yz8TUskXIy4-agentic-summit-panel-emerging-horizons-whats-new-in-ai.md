## Description

Research Panel – OWASP Agentic Security Summit, London | Dec 9, 2025. This research-focused panel explores how AI security research can help practitioners keep pace as LLMs and agentic systems move into production. Panelists from Queen’s University Belfast (CSIT/Cyber AI Hub), the Alan Turing Institute, and LASR (UK government-backed AI security research) explain how the UK ecosystem collaborates across academia, government, and industry—bridging early research (low TRL) to deployable outcomes.

Key themes include the non-determinism challenge of auditing agents (why code-style audits don’t translate cleanly), the need to apply classic security principles (CIA, least privilege, separation of duties) across the ML pipeline, and why defense-in-depth—not a silver bullet—will ultimately make AI systems safer. The panel also highlights emerging research priorities: agentic identity and memory risks, AI supply chain drift, reasoning-model attacks, and training data poisoning at surprisingly small scales.

They close with practical advice: don’t treat “Top 10” as checkbox compliance—use it as a guide to focus on what applies to your use case, and follow trusted hubs (government, standards bodies, OWASP/NIST) to stay current.

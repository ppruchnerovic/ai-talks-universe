## Description

Hosts Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama unpack a world first: on June 12th the US government had Anthropic pull Fable 5 and Mythos 5 worldwide — not banning a chip or a weapon, but a model itself. They dig into where the line was drawn ("fix this code"), why pulling these models may hurt defenders more than adversaries, and the deep irony of the safety-first lab becoming the first casualty of a national security directive.
Covered this episode: export controls applied to AI for the first time, why benchmarks are becoming "functionally useless," the marketing-hype trap of "uniquely capable" models, and the downstream risks for the US AI industry.
Hosted by Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama of the Gen AI Security Project Threat Intelligence Initiative. Produced by the AI Security Project with RumRunner Media.

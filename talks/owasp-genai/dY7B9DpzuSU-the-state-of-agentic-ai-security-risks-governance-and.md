## Description

Keren Katz and Rock Lambros explore the opportunities and risks of working with generative AI agents in both enterprise and customer-facing applications. They unpack critical topics like vulnerabilities in enterprise AI systems, unpredictable agent behavior, adversarial prompts, and the security challenges of interconnected AI ecosystems. Sharing real-world examples, they highlight the very real implications of integrating AI into daily operations and workflows.

Keren and Rock also dive into the rapidly evolving world of AI regulations, discussing the EU Code of Practice and how national AI strategies in the US and China are shaping the future. Whether you’re implementing AI in your business or trying to keep up with the latest rules and risks, there’s something here for everyone.

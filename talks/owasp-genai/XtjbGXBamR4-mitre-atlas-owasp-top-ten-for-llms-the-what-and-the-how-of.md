## Description

Put in charge of defending GenAI and hearing about ATLAS, OWASP and NIST all at once? Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama explain what MITRE ATLAS and the OWASP Top Ten for LLM Applications are, how they differ, and how to use them together from day one.

What we cover:
• Guidance vs. regulation: why the EU AI Act is a different kind of framework
• What MITRE ATLAS is and how it builds on MITRE ATT&CK
• Tactics, techniques and procedures for AI systems
• What the OWASP Top Ten for LLM Applications covers
• Prompt injection, excessive agency and system prompt leakage
• OWASP as the "what" and ATLAS as the "how"
• A customer support chatbot walkthrough with retrieval over a knowledge base
• Prompt injection, sensitive information disclosure, vector weaknesses and misinformation
• Threat modeling: human review for high-risk actions and layered controls
• Testing your prompt injection filters beyond plain English
• Why AI supply chain risk jumped to number three
• Verified sources, signed artifacts, dependency scanning and runtime monitoring
• Using threat intelligence to prioritize your AI defenses

Key Takeaway: You don't need to pick a winner. Use OWASP to prioritize risks, use ATLAS to model how attackers will exploit them, and build a threat intelligence led roadmap for defending your AI systems.

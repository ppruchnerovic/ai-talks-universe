## Description

Regulation & Governance Panel - OWASP Agentic Security Summit, London | Dec 9, 2025) This panel tackles one of the hardest questions in agentic AI security: how do we turn fast-changing regulations, standards, and governance ideas into practical guidance that still enables innovation? Moderated at the OWASP Agentic Security Summit in London, the discussion brings together perspectives from financial services, standards bodies, consulting, and the UK regulator.

Speakers unpack the geopolitical drivers shaping AI governance (US/China competition, EU bloc dynamics, UK “growth + voluntary engagement” approach), and why the U.S. is likely headed toward fragmented state-level AI regulation. The conversation then pivots to what practitioners actually need: risk-based governance, clarity on organizational risk appetite, and mechanisms like sandboxes / live testing to translate principles into controls.

A key theme: “human-in-the-loop” doesn’t scale and can create false confidence. The panel argues for a more realistic model: accountability owned by people (business owner, system owner, operational owner), supported by telemetry, automation, and—eventually—AI governing AI. They also highlight the often-missed human factor: AI literacy, fatigue, and how humans avoid accountability under pressure.

The takeaway: adopt a risk-first mindset, design for modularity and test-at-scale, and use standards as accelerators—not box-ticking.

More OWASP GenAI Security Project resources: https://genai.owasp.org

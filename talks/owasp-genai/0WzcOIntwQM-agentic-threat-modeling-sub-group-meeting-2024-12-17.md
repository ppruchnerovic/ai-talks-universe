## Description

Join us for an insightful discussion led by Jason Ross, a core team member, as we delve deep into the complexities of threat modeling agent ecosystems. In this meeting, we explore the nuances of creating a new framework specifically tailored for agent systems while also leveraging existing frameworks to avoid duplicative efforts. The team discusses several pertinent aspects of AI security, including existing OWASP projects such as the OWASP AI Exchange and the AI Security Governance Checklist. Jason and the participants emphasize the critical need for pragmatic guidance and actionable security guidelines for developers and security practitioners alike.

The conversation also covers the unique challenges posed by autonomous agents, including their decision-making capabilities, tool usage, and potential security risks. The team debates various architectural patterns, the importance of considering agent-to-agent interactions, and the implications of code generation within agent systems. Additionally, we touch on the integration of safety and security concerns, the potential for unbounded resource consumption, and the need for threat modeling frameworks that address these emerging issues. Whether you're a security researcher, a developer, or just interested in AI security, this discussion offers valuable insights into the evolving landscape of AI and agent-based systems.

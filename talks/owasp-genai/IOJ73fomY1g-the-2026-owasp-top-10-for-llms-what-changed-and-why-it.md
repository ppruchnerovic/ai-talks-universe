## Description

The OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications just got its biggest update yet. Scott Clinton talks with Steve Wilson and Rock—the co-leads behind the list—about how it's evolved since its first version in 2023, and what the 2026 edition means for anyone building or securing AI systems.

What we cover:
• Why Excessive Agency jumped several spots as LLMs evolve into tool-using, decision-making
agents
• A real-world hacking story: how a guardrail written as a prompt instead of code got bypassed by a simple misspelling
• Why Prompt Injection is now treated as inevitable—and what that means for how you architect defences
• Practical advice for everyday ChatGPT users vs. enterprise AI builders
• How this year's list used real incident data for the first time, and why the data was messier than expected
• How to contribute to the next version of the Top 10

Key Takeaways:
Stop trying to make the model unbreakable. Prompt injection is inevitable—design your downstream systems so it doesn't matter when it happens.

## Description

🧠🛡️ Cyber Threat Landscape After 3 Years of GenAI (CTI Findings & Practical Guidance)
This session from the OWASP GenAI Security Project Virtual Summit (October 2025) is delivered by the CTI Initiative co-leads Brian Akyang and Rachel James, analyzing how threat actors are using and abusing GenAI across today’s cyber threat landscape. The talk frames GenAI’s impact through a CTI lens—resources, skills/capabilities, and TTPs—and explains why visibility is still limited (provider transparency gaps, “AI-written” attribution uncertainty, and little insight into custom models).

Key findings: the most observable, high-impact change so far is AI-enabled social engineering (voice phishing, AI-written phishing, real-time deepfake video fraud). They also cover the reality vs hype of “AI malware,” attacks on AI systems (prompt injection vs supply chain/poisoned models, API key theft), and actor-specific trends (e-crime and nation-states, including North Korean IT worker infiltration).

Includes actionable plugs for OWASP CTI deliverables: a GenAI Incident Response Guide and Deepfake Preparedness/Response guidance, plus ways to volunteer and provide feedback.

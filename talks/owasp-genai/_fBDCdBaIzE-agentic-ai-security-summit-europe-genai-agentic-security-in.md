## Description

At the OWASP Agentic Security Summit in London (Dec 9, 2025), Mark Keating (AWS Principal Security Specialist) shares a practical path from OWASP’s Top 10 for LLM Apps to the new Top 10 for Agentic Applications. He explains how security shifts as systems move from chatbots to tool-using agents and protocols like MCP: non-determinism, “input becomes code,” autonomous decision-making, and dynamic learning. Mark’s core message: focus on what you can control outside the model—least privilege, strong identity/authorization, data protection, monitoring/telemetry, and defense-in-depth—applied at the right integration points. He introduces scoping matrices (buy vs. build) and autonomy-based agentic scopes to map risks to real deployments, showing how risk escalates with autonomy.

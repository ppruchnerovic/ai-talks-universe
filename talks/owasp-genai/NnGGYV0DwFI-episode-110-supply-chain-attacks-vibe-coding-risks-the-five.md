# Episode 110 | Supply Chain Attacks, Vibe Coding Risks & the Five Eyes AI Warning

**Supply Chain Attacks**

## Description

Hosts Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama unpack why supply chain attacks are exploding in the age of vibe coding — when AI writes code faster than anyone can security-review it. They walk through recent incidents: the "Cordyceps" CI/CD pipeline flaw affecting Microsoft, Google, Apache and others; the "Miasma" worm that hijacks AI coding assistants at the tool level; and a North Korean actor using a Claude-powered agent to inject a malicious dependency that led to $12M in stolen crypto.
Also this episode: the rare joint Five Eyes advisory warning that AI will transform offensive and defensive cyber in "months, not years," plus the arrival of open-weight models like GLM 5.2 and what "the genie out of the bottle" means for export controls — with the usual reminder that the operator's skill, not the download, is where the real capability lives.

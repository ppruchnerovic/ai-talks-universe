## Description

Is generative AI turning every script kiddie into a nation-state hacker—or is the reality far less dramatic?

In Episode 4 of the GenAI Security Project Threat Intelligence Podcast, Rachel James and Dr. Brian Nakayama examine one of cybersecurity's biggest questions using newly published empirical research analyzing tens of thousands of criminal forum discussions.

Rather than speculation, they explore what threat actors are actually saying about AI, where large language models are helping attackers today, and why many of the biggest fears surrounding AI-powered cybercrime have yet to materialize.

Topics include:

Is AI really automating hacking?
The difference between "Standalone Complex" and "Vibe Crime"
Why "Dark LLMs" like WormGPT are often overhyped
Where AI is actually improving attacker productivity
How AI is changing social engineering, phishing, and reconnaissance
Why guardrails still matter
The future of AI-enabled cybercrime—and what security leaders should really be watching

If you're a threat intelligence analyst, SOC leader, CISO, security researcher, or AI security practitioner, this episode provides a practical, evidence-based perspective that cuts through the hype.

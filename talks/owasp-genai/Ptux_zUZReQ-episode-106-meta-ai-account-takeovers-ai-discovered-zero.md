## Description

Hosts Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama break down a prompt-injection flaw that let attackers hijack Instagram accounts through Meta's AI support assistant — and a new Google report showing threat actors likely using AI to find and exploit a zero-day. The lesson: think hard about how much agency you hand your AI before you deploy it.
Covered this episode: what "threat actors" and "zero-days" really mean, the Air Canada chatbot that invented a discount and lost in court, and why "just because you can, doesn't mean you should."
Hosted by Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama of the Gen AI Security Project Threat Intelligence Initiative. Produced by the AI Security Project with RumRunner Media.

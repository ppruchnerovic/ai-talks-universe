## Description

The meeting is the monthly OWASP GenAI Security Project team call, held on the second Wednesday of each month. The session begins with introductions and a welcome to new participants, reinforcing the group’s open and collaborative structure.

A key update is the upcoming publication of the Q1 2026 roadmap. The project operates with eight working groups and publishes a quarterly roadmap outlining active initiatives and upcoming priorities. The 2026 roadmap will reflect ongoing efforts and evolving focus areas in response to the rapidly changing GenAI and AI security landscape. The roadmap is intended to help contributors and community members understand where to engage and what work is in progress.

A major focus of the meeting is outreach and events. The team highlights the upcoming RSA AI Security Summit, scheduled for March 25 at the Moscone Center. Attendance requires an Expo Plus Pass, and a discount code is available. The summit will feature project updates, working group presentations, discussions on advancements in AI and agentic security, and highlights from the Agentic Adoption Challenge. The event is positioned as an opportunity to showcase progress made over the past year and preview plans for 2026.

In addition to the summit, the team is organizing related RSA activities, including a kickoff party hosted with sponsors, further strengthening community engagement and collaboration.

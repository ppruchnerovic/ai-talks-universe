## Description

The GenAI Security Project’s Threat Defense COMPASS consolidates AI threats, vulnerabilities, defenses, and mitigations into a unified AI Threat Resilience Strategy Dashboard. COMPASS enables organizations to evaluate everything from external adversaries using AI tools to internal deployments of Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, and proposed GenAI or Agentic projects. Designed for iterative use, COMPASS serves as both a methodology and a practical spreadsheet tool that guides security teams through rapid threat prioritization and strategic decision making.

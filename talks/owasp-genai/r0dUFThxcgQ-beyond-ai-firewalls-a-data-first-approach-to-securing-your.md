## Description

🛡️📊 Beyond AI Firewalls: A Data-First Framework for Enterprise GenAI Security
This session from the OWASP GenAI Security Project Virtual Summit (October 2025) explains why enterprises must go beyond AI firewalls to secure GenAI apps moving from PoC to production—especially with the rapid growth of MCP-connected agents. Speakers from HPE and DAXA break down the dual threat: AI attacking enterprise data (exfiltration, manipulation, destructive actions) and enterprise data attacking AI systems (hidden commands in emails/docs, poisoned MCP connections, injection attacks).

The core message: output filtering isn’t enough. Firewalls are often not data-aware, creating “governance by guesswork.” Instead, a shift-left, data-first approach controls the context provided to models at runtime for deterministic governance, visibility, and auditability via an AI Data Bill of Materials (traceability of what data from which source influenced which response/action).

Includes a demo securing both RAG chatbots (classification + identity/semantic policy enforcement) and agentic/coding assistants (blocking destructive actions and injection payloads before execution).

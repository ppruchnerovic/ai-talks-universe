## Description

In this session from the OWASP Agentic Security Summit (London, December 9, 2025), Scott Clinton, co-chair of the OWASP GenAI Security Project, provides an in-depth overview of the project’s evolution, scale, and mission—setting critical context for the Agentic Security discussions that follow.

Scott explains how the initiative began roughly 20 months ago as the OWASP Top 10 for LLM and Generative AI, and rapidly grew into a solutions-oriented, global project with eight working groups spanning the full AI security lifecycle. He highlights the project’s explosive community growth, including thousands of active contributors on Slack, more than 20,000 LinkedIn followers, and collaboration with standards bodies and governments worldwide.

The talk emphasizes that GenAI and agentic security are not just technical problems—they span governance, risk, and executive decision-making. Scott outlines the project’s nearly 50 published resources, extensive localization efforts across ~20 languages, and a roadmap of upcoming guidance, tools, and open-source initiatives such as AI SBOM (AISBOM).

Finally, he explains why the Agentic Top 10 took time: the team intentionally built foundational research—threats, mitigations, MCP guidance, threat modeling, and real-world data—before releasing a prioritized list. This session frames the summit as a deep, practical dive into securing agentic AI systems at scale.

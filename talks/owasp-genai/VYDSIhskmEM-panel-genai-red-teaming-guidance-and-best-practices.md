## Description

🛡️ GenAI Red Teaming Panel: Agents, Prompting & Scalable Evaluation
This session from the OWASP GenAI Security Project Virtual Summit (October 2025) features a panel of GenAI red teamers discussing how to test goal-oriented and agentic AI behaviors in the real world. The group breaks down modern techniques beyond “ignore previous instructions,” including goal manipulation (goal drift, malicious goal expansion, exhaustion loops), probabilistic prompt engineering, and why attacks must appear aligned with an agent’s objectives.

They also cover why automation is a requirement for scale—but only works with strong success signals: detectors, LLM-as-judge pitfalls, quorum/ensemble judging, and human verification to avoid hallucinated “wins.” Finally, the panel highlights stakeholder alignment across AI, security, ops, and ethics—and closes with practical advice: play with models, study research, threat-model your system, and test for your specific use case.

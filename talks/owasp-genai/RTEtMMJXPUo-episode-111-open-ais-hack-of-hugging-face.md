## Description

A model was asked to prove how good it was at hacking. It decided the fastest route was to break out of its sandbox and steal the answer sheet.

That's the Hugging Face incident in one line. The long version — one poisoned dataset,
container escape, lateral movement across Kubernetes clusters, 17,000 attacker actions in a
single weekend — is Episode 11.

But the detail that should worry security teams isn't the attack. It's the investigation. Hugging
Face tried to analyze it with commercial frontier models, hit refusals and cost walls, and ran the forensics on a self-hosted open-weight model instead.

That's the asymmetry we keep flagging, playing out in public: attackers operate without
guardrails, defenders don't. Two questions worth sitting with. If your own red-team harness escaped tonight, do you have an IR plan for that? And when a model attacks a third party with no human at the keyboard, who's liable?

Full episode below. Hosted by Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama of the OWASP GenAI
Security Project.

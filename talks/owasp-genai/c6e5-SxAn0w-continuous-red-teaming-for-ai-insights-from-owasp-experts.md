## Description

In this episode of the OWASP GenAI Security Podcast, Aubrey sits down with Ante and Kris from Splx to discuss the importance of continuous red teaming for AI applications. As AI evolves with multimodal systems, agent frameworks, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), proactive security testing becomes essential to address vulnerabilities like jailbreaks, data poisoning, and alignment shifts.

Learn how integrating automated red teaming processes and collaborating closely with security teams can help organizations stay ahead of threats. Don’t miss this deep dive into the latest strategies for safeguarding AI applications in production and beyond.

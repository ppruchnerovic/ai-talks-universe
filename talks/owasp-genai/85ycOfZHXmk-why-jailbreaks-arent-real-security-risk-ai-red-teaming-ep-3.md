## Description

Jailbreaking a chatbot is fun, but it's not real security risk. Real risk is an agent that leaks data, compromises a code repository, or takes an action nobody approved.

Scott Clinton talks with Jason Ross (AI red teaming, Salesforce) and Sonu Kumar, co-leads of the Gen AI Security Project's Red Teaming Initiative. They cover how to scope an AI red team engagement, why non-deterministic behavior makes findings hard to prove or fix, and why human-in-the-loop is a weak control. They also preview the upcoming Red Teaming Manual.

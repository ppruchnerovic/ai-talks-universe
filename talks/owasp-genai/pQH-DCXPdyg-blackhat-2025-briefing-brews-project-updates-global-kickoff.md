## Description

Scott Clinton shares key updates, including the publication of the State of Agentic AI Security & Governance Guide and the launch of the Agentic Security Landscape. Attendees were introduced to the project's mission, its working groups, and resources like threat mitigation guides, red teaming frameworks, CISOs' checklists, and global translation initiatives. The presentation also covered recent successes, growing adoption of OWASP frameworks by solution providers, and practical ways to contribute to the community.

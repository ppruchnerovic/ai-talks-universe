## Description

Hosts Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama break down "Jade Puffer" — what researchers at Sysdig call the first end-to-end agentic ransomware operation, with an LLM driving every step from initial access to cleanup. They explore how forensic investigators proved it was AI (not just a well-resourced human), from self-narrating payloads to error corrections that happen faster than any person could type.
Also this episode: an update on Anthropic's Fable and Mythos models coming back online after US export controls, what the government's growing control over model access means for defenders, and why — as always — it's less about the model and more about orchestration.
Hosted by Rachel James and Dr. Bryan Nakayama of the Gen AI Security Project Threat Intelligence Initiative. Produced by the AI Security Project with RumRunner Media.

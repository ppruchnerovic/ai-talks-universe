Introduction To Tag Infrastructure - Kashif Khan, Ericsson & Dylan Page, Lambda.ai

This presentation will introduce the recently rebooted CNCF TAG Infrastructure. We'll cover its operational structure and its collaborative efforts with CNCF projects in key areas such as data and storage, networking, DNS, compute, service mesh, infrastructure lifecycle, edge computing, sovereignty, and load balancing. We will also highlight our ongoing work in developing ecosystem guidance and whitepapers. Attendees will learn how to contribute to and participate in the CNCF Infrastructure community, and gain practical insights into leveraging cloud-native infrastructure in their own environments.

Ethical Bias - Robert Glenn, Glennium, LLC & Diana Zhawn, Kopacetic, Inc.

In this talk, we explore how to design ethical, behavior-aware AI agents that balance personalization with privacy and human autonomy. Drawing on both established engineering practices and emerging ethical frameworks, we examine how ethical constraints can serve as drivers of better system design, rather than limitations on innovation.We discuss how to build agents that learn from individual behavior while actively promoting accountability, collaboration, and well-being, without crossing into exploitation, surveillance, or intrusion. Central to this approach is an understanding that AI systems are not just computational tools, but participants in human communication.

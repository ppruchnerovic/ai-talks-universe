The Sanity Check: Observability for AI Data Pipelines - David Aronchick, Expanso

Your AI just confidently gave a completely wrong answer. Why? Was it a bad model? Or was it poisoned by garbage data ingested three weeks ago from a rogue Salesforce update? You have no idea, because your AI data pipeline is a complete black box.

This is a talk about lighting up that black box. We (from the teams that built Kubernetes and scaled Kinesis) will show you how to apply the hard-won SRE principles from hyper-scale data systems to your AI infrastructure. This isn't about model monitoring; it's about data pipeline observability. We'll show you the architecture and tools required to trace a single bad prediction all the way back to the specific source data that caused it.

You'll leave knowing how to monitor for data freshness, track lineage from source to token, and alert on data drift before it silently corrupts your entire system.

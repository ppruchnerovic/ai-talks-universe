GPU Reservations: Maximizing Utilization and Fairness Across Teams - Sam Huang, Cantina Labs & Thomas Chaton, Lightning.AI

Companies worried about GPU costs often turn to long-term reservations, which can cut prices by up to 75% vs. on-demand. But executives hesitate to commit to long contracts amid shifting priorities, while researchers dislike losing autoscaling capacity.

In this talk, we’ll share how we merged the elasticity of cloud-based autoscaling with the savings of reserved capacity by building a custom platform on Kubernetes - one that orchestrates researchers’ training workloads, abstracts away infrastructure complexity, and keeps users informed. We’ll walk through the architecture and core capabilities, from transparent, adaptive preemption guided by team priorities to automated coordination with hardware providers for optimal resolution of hardware issues and dynamic budgeting that enables bursting into on-demand GPUs when needed.

If you’re exploring how to make cloud-native AI research infrastructure both cost-efficient and researcher-friendly, this session is for you.

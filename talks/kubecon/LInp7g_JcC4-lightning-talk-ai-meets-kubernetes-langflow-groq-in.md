Lightning Talk: AI Meets Kubernetes: Langflow & GROQ in Automated Incident Management - Harini Anand, IBM

This talk is a personal journey that began with a simple desire: to make life easier as a developer assigned to managing recurring Kubernetes operational issues. Faced with frequent pod failures, node assignment bottlenecks, and the challenge of safeguarding formations from accidental deletions, I initiated and built a proof-of-concept pipeline leveraging Langflow's visual orchestration and GROQ language for contextual LLM querying. This AI-driven system automates the detection, diagnosis, & remediation of common customer issues by combining Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), cloud-native Astra DB vector search, and multi-turn conversational interaction, accelerating ticket resolution and improving cluster stability without overwhelming human operators. The talk shares this journey, the system architecture, and how this approach embodies a practical application of generative AI & RAG in Kubernetes operations, fitting naturally in the evolving Cloud Native AI and Kubeflow ecosystem.

Most enterprises are excited about AI - until they hit the wall of data residency requirements, compliance constraints, and loss of model control. At Telenor AI Factory, we have built a platform designed for exactly this challenge: enabling organisations to develop and deploy AI on secure, sovereign infrastructure with local data processing and full control over their models and data.

In this talk, Joachim Jacobsen will present Telenor AI Factory’s service offerings - including GPU-as-a-Service (GPUaaS) and Model-as-a-Service (MaaS) - all delivered within the framework of sovereign data processing.

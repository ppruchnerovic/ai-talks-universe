## Description

Artificial Intelligence (AI) promises transformative benefits, offering greater operational efficiency, improved workforce effectiveness, and enhanced profitability. Yet, many organisations underestimate the complexity of implementation. Without clear goals, robust governance, and informed leadership, AI initiatives risk failure.

This session provides practical guidance on how to move beyond hype and build a sustainable AI strategy. Attendees will gain actionable insights into organisational readiness, risk management, and ethical considerations, ensuring AI adoption delivers real value.

Key Takeaways:

- The organisational changes and leadership commitment required for AI adoption
- How to set realistic expectations and communicate what AI can (and cannot) deliver
- Understanding risk and implementing effective risk management for AI systems
- Principles of Responsible AI and ensuring ethical accountability
- Governance, legal, and compliance considerations for AI deployment
- Security implications and best practices for safeguarding AI systems

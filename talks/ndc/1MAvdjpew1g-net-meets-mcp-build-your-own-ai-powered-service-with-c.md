Ever wondered how ChatGPT understands exactly what you mean, even when you don't say it explicitly? The secret is MCP, Model Context Protocol! And guess what, you can build your own MCP servers using C# and .NET!

Join me in this lively, hands-on session as we demystify MCP and learn to build an intelligent context-aware MCP server from scratch.

Whether you're new to MCP or a seasoned AI enthusiast, you'll leave with everything you need to make your apps smarter, more intuitive, and ready for the AI-driven world. Let's bring AI to life, the .NET way!

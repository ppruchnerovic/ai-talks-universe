Every person operates with a unique internal operating system — their HumanOS — made up of personality traits, hardwired strengths, decision-making defaults, and emotional drivers. And just like moving between Mac, Windows, or Linux, working with people running a different OS than yours can lead to confusion, friction, or unexpected system errors.

In this session, you’ll learn how to:
• Decode your own internal OS using tools like Gallup Strengths, Enneagram, and The People Code
• Identify your default responses to stress, feedback, and conflict — and when to patch or update
• Understand your motivators, communication style, and work preferences — and how they impact collaboration
• Recognize compatibility issues for what they are: system mismatches, not personal flaws
• Use AI as a personal coaching layer to help you align your internal settings and navigate difficult work scenarios

You’ll walk away with a clearer understanding of your system architecture, and digital tools to help you optimize how you problem-solve, communicate, and collaborate with others — even when their internal OS is nothing like your own.

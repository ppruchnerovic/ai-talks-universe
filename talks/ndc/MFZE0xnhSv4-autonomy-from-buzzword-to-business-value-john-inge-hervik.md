Autonomy: a word that excites some, frustrates others, and often leaves organisations divided. But what does autonomy truly mean in practice, and how can it deliver measurable value? Is it the key to success, or just another management headache?

In this session, we’ll demystify autonomy and self-organisation, exploring their real-world applications and benefits. You’ll gain the clarity and confidence to explain how autonomous teams function effectively in organisations, and you’ll be armed with arguments to steer endless autonomy debates back toward what really matters—creating value that drives your organisation forward.

Beyond the theory, we’ll dive into practical insights, addressing common challenges and questions drawn from years of client work and training sessions. From fostering accountability to improving collaboration between autonomous teams, this session equips you with the tools and mindset to make autonomy work—not just as a concept, but as a driver of success.

Key Takeaways:
1. The Truth About Autonomy: Gain a clear, actionable understanding of autonomy and self-organisation, and the tangible value they bring.
2. Practical Collaboration Tips: Tips and tricks to enhance cooperation and alignment between autonomous teams.
3. Facilitating Ownership: Discover how to cultivate responsibility and engagement in teams and employees.
4. Debate with Confidence: Equip yourself with compelling arguments to refocus autonomy discussions on outcomes and value.

Prompt injections are inevitable, but impact is optional. This fast-paced talk focuses on practical exploitation and real-world attack chains. You will learn how attackers build rapport with LLMs, manipulate behavior, and bypass restrictions in AI-powered web applications.

We will demonstrate how vulnerabilities can be chained together to steal and exfiltrate sensitive data through indirect prompt injections, malicious rendering techniques, and abusive tool calls. Finally, we will break down the reality of AI risk and show exactly how to build the guardrails needed to stop these attacks before they succeed.-

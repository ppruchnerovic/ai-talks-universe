Traditional specs slow you down, and vague bug reports drive devs crazy. What if backlog items could practically write themselves?

In this session, Adam Cogan takes you behind the scenes to see exactly how to build a real-world AI tool. From a messy prototype to a enterprise ready production app, the tool automatically captures, transcribes, structures, and assigns actionable work items. Using Azure, Whisper, and the ChatGPT API, you'll see a practical implementation that eliminates frustrating context switches, clears up ambiguity, and drastically speeds up bug and feature reporting.

Along the way, Adam hit the MCP world. Come learn his candid insights and hard-earned lessons about integrating AI into everyday software workflows, so you can avoid common pitfalls and get a head-start building your own AI-powered development tools.

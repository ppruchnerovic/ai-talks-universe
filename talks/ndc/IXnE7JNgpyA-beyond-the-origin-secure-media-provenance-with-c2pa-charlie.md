## Description

This talk was recorded at NDC Manchester in Manchester, England.

Attend the next NDC conference near you:

/            @NDC

Follow our Social Media!

In today's more centralised internet, the trust model of a TLS-protected origin that helps you understand and rely on where content has come from is not as useful as it used to be. Platforms offer huge volumes of user-published content, and an HTTP man-in-the-middle attack on such content is not necessary if an actor wants to deceive.

In this talk, I'll describe how the Content Credentials specification from C2PA addresses these concerns and others. I'll talk about the architecture of the specification, typical media workflows, and the challenges of implementation. We'll also spend some time understanding how validation and long-term signatures work in the specification, before looking at C2PA's new "Conformance Program", intended to provide levels of security assurance to C2PA implementations.

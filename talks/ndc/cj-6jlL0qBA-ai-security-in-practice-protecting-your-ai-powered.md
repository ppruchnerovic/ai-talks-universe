Everyone's excited about adding AI to their applications. But what about the security implications?

AI introduces unique vulnerabilities that require new defensive approaches. This session shares practical strategies for securing AI applications in production, based on real-world implementation experience.

You'll learn:

- New attack vectors that AI introduces: What makes AI security different from traditional application security
- Securing AI at every layer: Input validation, model protection, output sanitization, and monitoring
- When existing security tools fall short: Understanding the gaps in traditional defenses
- Building security into your AI workflow: Practical controls for development, deployment, and operations

Whether you're adding your first AI feature or scaling AI across your organization, you'll leave with actionable security practices for AI-powered systems.

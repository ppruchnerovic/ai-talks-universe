Building teams is easy. A lot of people could tell you how to do that.

Building Agile teams in a way that everyone would get closer to the things that matter to them and perform optimally long-term is a lot more difficult.

You see, the best tech teams are adaptive, flexible and creative. Yet, the most significant risk remains - the human factor. We are so focused on formal systems, structures and frameworks that we often need to remember this.

How to increase and decrease the temperature in your team - optimal tools to ensure high engagement when necessary, and guard the team against blowing themselves up.

This workshop will be fast-paced and practical, including tools, methods and case studies from more than 10 years of experience leading international technology teams, more than 4 years of consulting 1500+ tech leaders and building 5MM+ AI products.

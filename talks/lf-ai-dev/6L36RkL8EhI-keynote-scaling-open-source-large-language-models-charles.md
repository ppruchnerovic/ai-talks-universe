## Description

Keynote: Scaling Open Source Large Language Models - Charles Srisuwananukorn, Founding VP of Engineering, Together

At Together AI, we believe the future of large language models is Open Source. Through the Together Inference API, Together provides quick access to 100+ open source large language models for fast inference. This talk will describe just a few of the challenges we faced scaling large language models and how we solved them.

## Description

Breakdown New Data Silos in Generative AI - Junping Du, Datastrato

Generative AI and foundation models are moving really fast and are influencing everyone potentially. There is wide consensus that foundation models can play better with high quality data with fine tuning or prompt engineering. However, most data is still siloed into different data lakes or even clouds - some ETL pipelines or feature engineering works are very costly with low performance, data discovery is hard to do across different departments. In this presentation, we will discuss these pain points and quickly demo how to solve it with open source tools and technology.

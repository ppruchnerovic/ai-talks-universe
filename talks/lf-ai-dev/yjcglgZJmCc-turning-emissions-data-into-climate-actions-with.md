Turning Emissions Data Into Climate Actions With CityCatalyst - Mirco Rudolph, OpenEarth Foundation

Cities are responsible for over 70% of global greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, yet only 5% have GHG inventories in place. CityCatalyst is an open-source platform created to close this gap by helping cities build GHG inventories aligned with the international GPC Protocol and receive AI-powered recommendations for mitigation and adaptation actions based on their urban profile, climate risks, and emissions data.

In this session, we will introduce the concept of climate action prioritization and share how we built a system that helps cities identify the most impactful actions. We will walk through our data pipeline, explain how we gathered fragmented action data, standardized and enriched it to create the largest open-source dataset for climate actions, and used expert comparisons to benchmark and train our models.

We will also share our journey from qualitative scoring to a robust ML model trained to match actions to city needs. Along this journey we will point out pitfalls and share our learnings.

The platform has been piloted with 50 cities across Brazil, potentially impacting millions. We are looking to collaborate with others working on AI for climate and urban resilience.

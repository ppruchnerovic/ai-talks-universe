Keynote : The Open Source AI Alliance - Anthony J. Annunziata, Director, AI Open Innovation, IBM

IBM, Meta, Linux Foundation, and more than 50 partners in industry and the research community have just launched the AI Alliance to support and accelerate open source and open science in AI. We will ensure that open communities are well supported and at the center of contributing to and benefiting from the GenAI revolution. We’ll work across the technology stack and the open model landscape as well as in education, policy and public awareness to ensure AI remains open and the most capable and performant technologies are those rooted in open source and open innovation.

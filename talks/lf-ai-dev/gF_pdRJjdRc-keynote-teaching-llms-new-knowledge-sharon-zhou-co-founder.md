## Description

Keynote: Teaching LLMs New Knowledge - Sharon Zhou, Co-founder & CEO, Lamini

Imbuing an LLM with new knowledge goes beyond just querying it (prompt-engineering and RAG). Teaching an LLM new knowledge dramatically lifts the performance and understanding of a domain, but is much harder than just querying it. For one, it's hard to get the right data for the model to actually comprehend new knowledge. Additionally, better LLMs require product knowledge of the business, so some in-house expertise is needed to build the best LLMs. With Lamini, you'll get to explore an all-in-one LLM stack that adapts any model to better comprehend new knowledge, so you don’t need to collect or transform data as before, with only a few lines of code.

Sponsored Session: Sovereignty Spotlight: EU Regulations and the Global Impact of Open Source - Stefan Bergstein & David Szegedi, Red Hat

Navigating the impact of a rapidly evolving regulatory environment on your organisation? Us too.
In this talk, we will take a fast-paced tour through the shifting EU regulatory landscape - covering key acts like DORA, EUCS, the CRA, and the AI Act - and unpack what they mean for technology adoption and governance.
We will then dive into the critical role of open source software in enabling transparency, trust, and digital sovereignty. Expect a focus on practical concepts like traceability, digital signing, and how they underpin sovereign platforms.
Finally, we will illustrate key sovereignty concepts - such as reversibility, operational stability, and resilience - through hybrid cloud strategies and security best practices, supported by real-world case studies.

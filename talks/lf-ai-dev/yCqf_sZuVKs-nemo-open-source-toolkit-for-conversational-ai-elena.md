NeMo: Open Source Toolkit for Conversational AI - Elena Rastorgueva, NVIDIA

NVIDIA NeMo is an open-source Conversational AI toolkit allowing developers and researchers to work on Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Text To Speech (TTS). Join us on a whistle stop tour of the NeMo toolkit’s functionality and how to start using it for your own projects.

AI-Powered Search in Modern E-Commerce - Stanko Kuveljic, SmartCat.io

In the competitive world of e-commerce, relevant search results directly impact business outcomes. This presentation explores:

- Domain-specific embeddings using historical click data with contrastive loss functions
- Strategic tradeoffs between cross-encoders and bi-encoders
- Using LLMs to synthesize multimodal training data, addressing cold-start challenges through text, image, and interaction data
- Optimized vector retrieval with targeted reranking for enhanced relevance

The approach extends beyond search to power conversational shopping assistants that maintain context, understand preferences, and deliver personalized recommendations through natural dialogue.

Learn actionable strategies for implementing semantic search systems that handle diverse product catalogs while meeting performance requirements—creating intuitive shopping experiences that boost engagement and conversion.

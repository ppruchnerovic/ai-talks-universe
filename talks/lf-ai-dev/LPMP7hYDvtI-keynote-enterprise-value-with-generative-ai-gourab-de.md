## Description

Keynote: Enterprise Value with Generative AI - Gourab De, Vice President, Customer Success and Machine Learning, Weights and Biases

This presentation at the Linux Foundation by Gourab De explores the evolving landscape of Large Language Model (LLM) operations. It addresses the broader challenges faced in the LLM field, such as efficient experiment tracking, model optimization, and robust production monitoring. We will cover examples around the integration of innovative tools and methodologies to overcome these challenges, emphasizing their role in enhancing LLM development and deployment processes.

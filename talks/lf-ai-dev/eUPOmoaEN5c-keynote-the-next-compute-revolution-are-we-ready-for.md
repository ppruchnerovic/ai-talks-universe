Keynote: The Next Compute Revolution: Are We Ready for Quantum? - Jennifer Prendki, Founder, Quantum x AI Consulting & Advisory

Fifteen years ago, few predicted that GPUs would become the backbone of modern AI, and yet those who anticipated the shift were the ones who shaped the future.

Quantum computing is now at a similar inflection point. While the common refrain is that “quantum is 30 years away”, recent advances suggest the timeline may collapse much faster than expected.

In this talk, I’ll explain why developers need to start paying attention now: what quantum really means for AI, where the first practical intersections are emerging, and how to think about hybrid and quantum-inspired methods without waiting for fully mature hardware. Most importantly, I’ll outline the mindset shift developers need in order to future-proof their work and avoid being left behind when the next compute revolution arrives.

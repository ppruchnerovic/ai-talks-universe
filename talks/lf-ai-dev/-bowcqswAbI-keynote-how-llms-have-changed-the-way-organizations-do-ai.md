## Description

Keynote: How LLMs Have Changed the Way Organizations Do AI - Robert Nishihara, Co-founder & CEO, Anyscale

Businesses adopt LLMs in distinct phases, and the challenges at each phase are distinct. Organizations that have been productionizing AI for years are finding that they have to adapt their long-standing practices around infrastructure, testing, application development, and talent. This talk distills lessons from one year of working firsthand with dozens of organizations exploring and ultimately bringing LLMs to production.

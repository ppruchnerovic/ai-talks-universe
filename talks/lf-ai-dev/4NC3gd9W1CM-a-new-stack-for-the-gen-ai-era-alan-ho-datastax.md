## Description

A New Stack for the Gen AI Era - Alan Ho, DataStax

Generative AI is an innovative leap forward from our existing AI technologies. Unlike traditional AI, Generative AI harnesses both structured and unstructured data to allow applications to perform “human-like” tasks. With any major technological disruption like the internet and mobile, a new “stack” will emerge. In this talk, Alan Ho ties together the latest advances in AI, Cognitive Science, and industry to predict what a future generative AI technology stack will become. Discussion topics will include:
- Path towards autonomous AI agents
- Integration of human-like memory with generative models
- 5 major development works flows for generative AI
- Role of Fine-tuning VS Retrieval Augmented Generation
- Role of “reasoning” in generative AI apps
- Concept of multi-model agents, and multi-agent systems

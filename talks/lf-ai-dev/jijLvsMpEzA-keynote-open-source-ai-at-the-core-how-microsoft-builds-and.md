Keynote: Open Source & AI at the Core: How Microsoft Builds and Runs on OSS - Ryan Waite, Open Source Strategy and Incubations, Microsoft

At Microsoft, open source isn’t just a good idea—it’s fundamental to how we build, operate, and scale our business, especially as we unlock the potential of AI. In this talk, I’ll share how Microsoft contributes to open source projects that power both our infrastructure and cutting-edge AI services. You’ll learn how some of our biggest customer-facing services rely on open source technology, demonstrating how it fuels our most critical workloads. We’ll also explore how we contribute upstream to cloud-native projects that underpin our infrastructure, and highlight new open source initiatives like Copacetic, Dalec, and Radius that reflect our commitment to building with, and for, the open source community.

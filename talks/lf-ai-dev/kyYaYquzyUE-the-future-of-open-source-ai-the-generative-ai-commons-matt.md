## Description

The Future of Open Source AI & The Generative AI Commons - Matt White, Generative AI Commons

This session explores the evolution of open source AI, focusing on generative technologies. It discusses the shift towards domain-specific Large Language Models (LLMs) and the emergence of GenOps for managing generative AI infrastructure. The talk highlights how open source AI is addressing transparency and accessibility, essential for equitable AI development.

Key to the session is introducing the Generative AI Commons under the Linux Foundation. This initiative aims to democratize and advance open source generative AI through neutral governance and transparent collaboration. The Commons not only hosts generative models, datasets, and application frameworks but also emphasizes responsible AI principles and develops educational programs to advance open source AI knowledge.

Attendees will gain insights into the legislative risks for open source AI in the current climate and the role of the Generative AI Commons in ensuring open source AI's freedom and accessibility. This session is designed for professionals keen on understanding open source AI's future, and the integral role of initiatives like the Generative AI Commons.

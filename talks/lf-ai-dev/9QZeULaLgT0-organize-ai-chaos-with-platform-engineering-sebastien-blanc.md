Organize AI Chaos With Platform Engineering - Sebastien Blanc, Port

Okay, so Platform Engineering blew up around the same time AI did, right?

And everyone's jumping into AI features, which is awesome. But here's the thing, a lot of folks are forgetting about all the new stuff that comes with AI-infused apps. Like, how many tokens have we burned through? Which LLMs are even in play? Where are those embedded databases hiding? And don't even get me started on managing all those prompts, resources, and tools with MCP.

It's a bit of a mess, and I think it's time we talked about it. This talk is about cutting through the chaos and figuring out how to actually drive AI-infused projects, not just dive into them.

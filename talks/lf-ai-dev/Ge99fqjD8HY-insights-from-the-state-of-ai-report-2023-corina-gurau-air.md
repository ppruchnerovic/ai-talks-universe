Insights from The State of AI Report 2023 - Corina Gurau, Air Street Capital

The State of AI Report 2023 recently published by Air Street Capital covers the most exciting AI developments across research, industry, geopolitics and safety. In this talk I will summarize key insights of the report. Key themes include: the race to superior Large Language Models, industry adoption and new capabilities unlocked, and the ever growing need for compute. I'll end with the yearly 10 bold predictions to watch out for in 2024. For deeper insights read the full report at https://www.stateof.ai/ and feel free to reach out to Air Street on any startup ideas.

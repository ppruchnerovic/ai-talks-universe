## Description

Responsibility in Depth: Layering Licensing, Regulation, and More - Luis Villa, Tidelift, Inc.

Open software has “played on easy mode” for many years, with the core legal and ethical decisions for most projects beginning and ending with the choice of a license. This was well-intentioned, but open machine learning has exposed the shortcomings of putting so much pressure on copyright licenses.

Instead, ML demands a new attention to “responsibility in depth”: layering licensing, governance, community, and regulatory responses together in order to provide a workable combination of technical innovation, ease of use, and just social outcomes.

The talk will first survey what we’ve learned about the shortcomings of open licensing (and closed/SaaS!) approaches in ML to show why that is insufficient. Then it will explain how we can learn from “defense in depth” to combine a variety of approaches, including working with regulators in the US and EU.

Keynote: Open Source Stack Empowers the AI Era - Dr. Xiong Wei, Chairperson, OpenAtom openEuler Committee; TOC Vice President, OpenAtom Foundation

Within the open source operating system landscape, openEuler—a growing hub of innovation in open source operating systems—is exploring new frontiers on two fronts: enhancing the performance of AI training and inference, and leveraging AI to make OS management and maintenance more intelligent.

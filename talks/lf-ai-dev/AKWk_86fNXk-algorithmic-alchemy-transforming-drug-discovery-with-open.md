## Description

Algorithmic Alchemy: Transforming Drug Discovery with Open AI - Gloria Macia, F. Hoffmann-La Roche AG

In this session, we'll delve into the groundbreaking intersection of open AI and open data in revolutionizing drug discovery. Leveraging the power of advanced algorithms and vast open datasets, we'll explore how AI is catalyzing the transformation of traditional drug development processes. From accelerating target identification to optimizing lead compound selection, discover how this fusion of technology and collaboration is unlocking new frontiers in pharmaceutical innovation and the role open AI and open data can play in addressing global health challenges.

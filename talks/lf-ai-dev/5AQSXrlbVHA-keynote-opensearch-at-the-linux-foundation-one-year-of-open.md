Keynote: OpenSearch at the Linux Foundation: One Year of Open Innovation and Community Growth - Pallavi Priyadarshini, Senior Manager, OpenSearch Project

One year ago, OpenSearch took a bold step—joining the Linux Foundation to reinforce its commitment to open source values, transparency, and collaboration. This keynote chronicles our remarkable first year, highlighting how this strategic alignment has accelerated innovation and fostered unprecedented community expansion.

We'll examine the tangible impacts of this partnership, including doubling our contributor base and the expansion of our vibrant developer communities across six continents. Key technical achievements include breakthrough advances in vector search, significant Core Engine optimizations, integration of cutting-edge machine learning and AI agents, and enhanced observability features that are reshaping how enterprises handle search and analytics at scale.

Through concrete examples and community success stories, we'll demonstrate how the Linux Foundation's open governance model has amplified collaboration and technical innovation. Join Pallavi Priyadarshini, Senior Manager at the OpenSearch Project, as she shares insights from this transformative year and outlines our vision for the future of open source search technology.

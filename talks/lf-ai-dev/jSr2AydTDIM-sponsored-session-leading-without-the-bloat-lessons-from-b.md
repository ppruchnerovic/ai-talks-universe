Sponsored Session: Leading Without the Bloat: Lessons from Building a High-Growth Open Source SaaS - Lovisa Johansson & Magnus Landerblom, 84Codes

What does it really take to scale a high-growth SaaS company that hosts open source software? How do you tackle the complexities of potential software license changes while preserving culture, autonomy, and technical excellence?
In this session, the team behind LavinMQ shares the leadership lessons they’ve learned building a remote-first, flat-structured, high-growth company. We'll explore why we chose to build LavinMQ as an open-source product and how lean teams, shared ownership, and trust are core to how work gets done. This approach has fueled our rapid expansion—LavinMQ is built by the team behind CloudAMQP, recognized as one of Europe’s fastest-growing companies.

Hear practical insights on:

Building momentum for an open-source product with small, high-performing teams instead of hiring at speed.
Creating space for experimentation and community contribution in a culture without heavy hierarchies.
Maintaining focus and alignment as both revenue and product complexity scale, within an open-source ecosystem.

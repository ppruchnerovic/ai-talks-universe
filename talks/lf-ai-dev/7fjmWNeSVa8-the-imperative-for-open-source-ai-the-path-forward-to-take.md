The Imperative for Open Source AI - The Path Forward to Take Down the AI Oligarchs - Emmanouil (Manos) Koukoumidis, Oumi

AI today is being captured by a handful of companies positioning themselves as the stewards of its future, boasting compute and talent. But this is not AI's destiny, and history tells us this. Closed ecosystems may move first, but open systems are more resilient. We’ve seen this with Linux, Wikipedia, and the internet itself. What ultimately wins is whoever can harness the most minds. The open community is still the sleeping giant of AI. If awakened, coordinated, and equipped with the right tools, it can and will outbuild, outscale, and outlast the silos.

This talk will discuss why it is both imperative that open source frontier AI succeeds but also why this is very likely. I will present the platform that Oumi is building and the collaborative efforts that it is driving to enable open source frontier AI. Last, I will make the case for why open source AI is the right future and platform for all enterprises small and large to build upon as well as how enterprises can now succeed with Oumi’s platform; the future is bright, brighter than the present.

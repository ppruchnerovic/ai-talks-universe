One Pipeline to Rule Them All: Unifying Multimodal and AI Data Processing with Daft - Sammy Sidhu & Colin Ho, Eventual

Processing millions of images, documents, and structured data shouldn't require juggling ffmpeg, custom scripts, and Spark—but that's exactly what kills iteration speed in most AI pipelines today.

This talk introduces Daft, a Python-native data engine that unifies multimodal data processing in a single framework, handling everything from structured tables to images and embeddings. Plus, Daft has native integrations with data catalogs like Iceberg and Delta Lake, and VectorDBs like Turbopuffer and Lance. In this talk, you'll see live demonstrations of large scale document processing, batch inference, and multimodal ETL, all in a single data pipeline.

Stop building fragile, multi-tool data pipelines that break under production load. Whether you're processing terabyte-scale datasets for foundation model training or building real-time inference systems, discover how purpose-built infrastructure with Daft turns multimodal data chaos into a streamlined competitive advantage.

Sponsored Session: Frontier AI Models: A Review of Past Nine Months - Ozgun Erdogan, Ubicloud

In this talk, we'll survey recent shifts in AI models and their impact on AI application development. We'll start by showing how reasoning models like o3 and DeepSeek-R1 enabled a move from iterative chat to single-shot problem solving. We'll then talk about the 10-30x in cost reduction with the newer models, deployment strategies with quantization, and the open source AI model landscape. We'll conclude with a practical framework for choosing the right AI model for your use case.

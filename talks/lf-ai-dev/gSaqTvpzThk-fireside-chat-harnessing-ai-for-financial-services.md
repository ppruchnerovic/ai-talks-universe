## Description

Fireside Chat: Harnessing AI for Financial Services Innovation - Luca Borella & Jane Gavronsky, FINOS

Join us for a dynamic fireside chat with Jane Gavronsky and Luca Borella as they explore the transformative impact of artificial intelligence (AI) in the financial services sector. This conversation will highlight FINOS’s AI Strategic Initiative. Discussion highlights include: FINOS LLM Exploration - discussion of collaborative efforts to integrate large language models (LLMs) in financial applications and ongoing work on regulatory interpretation, financial benchmarks, and open datasets. AI Readiness - developing frameworks for safe and compliant AI deployment. Innovative tech in Financial services - what’s different, what’s the same?

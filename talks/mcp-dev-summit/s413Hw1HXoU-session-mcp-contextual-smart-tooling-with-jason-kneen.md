## Description

[Session] MCP & Contextual Smart Tooling
🎤 Jason Kneen, AI Innovator & Researcher - BouncingFish

In this presentation, I'll be talking about the concept of dynamic tooling—something that is important to enable developers to leverage the MCPs and tooling that they have access to without overloading the IDEs or applications they're using.With simple techniques and existing tools that Jason has developed, he can show you how to optimize your MCP servers and tooling into a concise set of toolboxes—that enables you to include only the tools you need when you need them—saving context, saving tokens, saving costs, saving time. He'll also cover techniques and tricks around MCPs to enable you to create what he calls virtual MCPs and Servlets—Allowing you to spin up MCPs in seconds to carry out tasks Or even have your AI create them on the fly. Finally, Jason will explain how to use all of these techniques to create a development system and environment—which is focused, contextually aware, lean, cost-effective, and, most importantly, virtually hallucination free.

## Description

[Session] From Embeddings to Edges: A Graph Based Approach to Tool Selection
🎤 Matthew Lenhard, Founder - MCP Evals

As AI agents rely more on external tools, choosing the right tool—or chain of tools—becomes critical. In this talk, I’ll share a new approach that replaces traditional embedding-based selection with a graph database. By modeling tools and their relationships as a graph, agents can make smarter, more context-aware decisions—leading to better results, fewer failed calls, and support for multi-step workflows.

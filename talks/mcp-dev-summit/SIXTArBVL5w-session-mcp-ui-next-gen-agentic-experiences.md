## Description

[Session] MCP-UI: Next-gen Agentic Experiences
🎤 Ido Salomon, Author - MCP-UI
🎤 Liad Yosef, Co-creator - MCP-UI

People are increasingly turning to AI agents for everyday tasks. From reading the news to online shopping, with the help of MCP, agents are poised to become the next generation of browsers. In this emerging landscape, applications must find ways to maintain their identity and UX to remain relevant for their users. MCP-UI offers a solution for apps to deliver rich, interactive UI on top of MCP, unlocking new frontiers in agentic UI.

In this talk, we’ll explore the future of the agentic web:
The agentic web - how AI agents are replacing traditional browsers and reshaping how users interact with content and services
MCP-UI - deep dive into MCP-UI architecture
Real-world use cases
Evolving MCP - current limitations and opportunities to extend the spec in the CWG (UI and other fields)
-Future vision

Join us to discover how MCP-UI enables the new web, where users can access their favorite apps uniformly through any agent.

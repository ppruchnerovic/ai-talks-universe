# The #1 Mistake Killing MCP Adoption Inside Your Company

## Description

Sterling Chin (Founding DevRel and Applied AI Engineer at Inngest) tells the story behind MARVIN, his open-source personal AI chief of staff built on Claude Code with a fleet of MCP servers and a markdown-based persistent memory system. His original Reddit post went viral with 3/4 of a million views, and MARVIN now has 950+ GitHub stars, a desktop and mobile app, and is used by two dozen non-developers at Postman including marketers, PMs, engineering managers, and the C-suite.

This talk is a field report on what actually happens when you hand a Claude Code harness plus MCP to people who have never opened a terminal. It is also the clearest argument I have heard for why top-down AI mandates fail and curiosity-driven adoption works.

What is covered:

- How MARVIN Was Born: Sterling's path from one-off MCP integrations for meetups and CFPs to a full personal AI chief of staff built over a holiday break.
- The Markdown Memory Architecture: Current-state files, daily sessions, weekly reports, and git history as the long-term memory trail — no vector DB, no Jira, just structured markdown.
- Personality as a Feature: Why giving MARVIN the sardonic voice of the Hitchhiker's Guide robot changed how Sterling interacts with him and killed the "you're absolutely right" sycophancy.
- The 191-Line-Item Week: What a single week with a Claude Code agent that remembers everything actually produces.
- The Naming Problem: Why "MCP," "tool definitions," and "JSON schema" are adoption killers for non-developers and why "this connects MARVIN to your Gmail" is the only explanation most people need.
- Why Your APIs Are Broken for Agents: Sterling's take from his time at Postman on why wrapping existing APIs as MCP tool definitions fails, and why you have to build agent-native ones.
- Teaching Liv to Think Like a Builder: How a marketer went from pasting transcripts by hand to shipping a Granola integration with no public API in 15 minutes, by learning to think like a PM, an eng manager, and a developer.
- The New Hire Pattern: Why onboarding your AI agent like a human hire with a 90-day ramp beats treating it like a search bar, every single time.
- The Security Story: What happens when your infosec team notices marketers requesting Claude Code access, and why OpenClaw bans will false-positive on your personal agent's bash scripts.
- Curiosity Beats Mandates: How MARVIN spread through Slack "holy sh*t" moments rather than corporate rollouts, and why that matters for any enterprise AI strategy.
- Getting Evenings Back: The under-discussed outcome — work actually finishes by 5, Slack quiets down, and Sterling stops checking email from bed.
- Announcing MARVIN for Teams: A shared context layer so personal MARVINs can collaborate.

This is essential watching for DevRel, platform engineers, AI champions inside enterprises, anyone trying to roll out MCP to a non-developer audience, and anyone who has had their AI rollout stall because nobody can pronounce "tool definition."

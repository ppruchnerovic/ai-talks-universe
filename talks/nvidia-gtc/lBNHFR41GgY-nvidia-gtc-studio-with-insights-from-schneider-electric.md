## Description

Pankaj Sharma, Executive Vice President of Software and Services at Schneider Electric, outlines the challenges of scaling AI infrastructure, emphasizing energy as the foundation of the five-layer AI cake. As AI adoption accelerates, he points to growing pressure on power, grid capacity, and sustainable energy—while highlighting the opportunity to use AI to optimize and expand energy systems for the next generation of AI.

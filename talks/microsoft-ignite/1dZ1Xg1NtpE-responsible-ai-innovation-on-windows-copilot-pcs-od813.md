## Description

Every company needs to comply with laws and regulations while delighting customers with new and beneficial AI-powered experiences. This session gives an overview of the Windows approach to responsible innovation. Ben and Michael share how this approach—spanning privacy, security, digital safety, fairness, transparency, accountability—shapes the entire development and deployment process, as well as the latest advances, examples, and best practices for responsible innovation with AI Copilot+ PCs.

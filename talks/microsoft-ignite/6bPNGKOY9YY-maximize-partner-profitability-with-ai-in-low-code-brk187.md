## Description

In today’s fast-paced digital landscape, agility and speed are paramount, and Low Code partners are leading the way in providing customers with innovation at scale. This insightful session is designed for partners looking to harness the power of low code and AI. Partners building practices focused on automation and app modernization are increasing profitability and time to value by integrating Power Platform into their practice – and providing a holistic AI offering.

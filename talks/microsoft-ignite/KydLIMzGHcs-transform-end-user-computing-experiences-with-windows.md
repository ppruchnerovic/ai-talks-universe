## Description

Discover the benefits of modernizing your end-user computing with Windows & Intune. Enhance productivity with the latest Windows and Copilot features on a range of devices from Copilot+ PCs to a new simple and secure solution for Windows 365. Learn how Copilot in Intune and automation can streamline management. Gain valuable insights and innovative ideas from a cross-industry panel of IT leaders on modernizing endpoints to meet increasing demands for security, flexibility, and reduced total cost of ownership (TCO).

Join us for a focused look at how Planner and the Project Manager agent work together to turn conversations into action. We’ll show how these tools integrate deeply with Microsoft Teams and Copilot Chat—helping you capture tasks from meetings and channels, start drafts, and keep projects moving without leaving your flow of work.

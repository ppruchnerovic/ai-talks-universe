## Description

Learn from ZS how Microsoft’s Power Stack is transforming healthcare and life sciences. This session explores five key trends: Power BI Copilot for advanced analytics, migrating to Power BI and Fabric, low-code app development with PowerApps, AI-powered persona-based copilots, and scaling Gen AI with Azure AI Studio. Discover how these innovations drive productivity, better decision-making, and transformation.

## Description

Learn all about building, running, and managing intelligent apps! In this session, we will introduce a business problem and, using the new AI-powered designer, build a series of apps infused with AI to solve the problem. We'll explore how these intelligent apps enhance productivity and satisfaction at work with in-app AI assistance. And we will show you how you can monitor and govern them at scale. Join us to learn how you can get the most out of the apps of the future today!

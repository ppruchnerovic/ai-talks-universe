## Description

Do you have concerns about oversharing? Discover how well-managed data can unlock the full potential of generative AI while supporting your security and compliance needs. Our experts will share the latest admin capabilities in Copilot, SharePoint Advanced Management (SAM), and Microsoft Purview that can help you more effectively manage permissions, enhance security protocols, and implement robust data lifecycle management practices.

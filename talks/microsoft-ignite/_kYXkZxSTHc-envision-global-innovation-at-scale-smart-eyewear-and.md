## Description

From smart eyewear to connected retail, technologies like AI are redefining industries. TeamViewer empowers enterprises like Ray-Ban manufacturer EssilorLuxottica, and their new division Nuance Audio, to scale innovation globally – relying on intelligent services to remotely manage thousands of devices, ensure seamless operations and enable new customer experiences. Explore how secure, real-time connectivity based on Azure boosts efficiency while driving sales and growth across global markets.

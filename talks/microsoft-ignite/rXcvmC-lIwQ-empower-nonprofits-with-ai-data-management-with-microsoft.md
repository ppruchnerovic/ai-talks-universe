## Description

Data management is key for nonprofits organizations to unlock the power of AI to drive their missions forward. In this session, we will showcase how your organization can use Microsoft Fabric to streamline and centralize your data, making AI adoption easier. Attendees will explore best practices, evaluate current data systems, and create a roadmap. You’ll also hear from a nonprofit on how Microsoft Fabric has helped them gain better control on current data estate and utilize the power of AI.

## Description

In the AI era, Business Applications partners are leading the charge in maximizing value by streamlining and optimizing business processes for customers. The upcoming years will witness a surge in business process transformation, focusing on each role and function to enhance efficiencies and achieve cost savings with AI. In this session, we’ll look at key growth areas within Business Applications and highlight partner opportunities that can help you realize maximum value for your customers.

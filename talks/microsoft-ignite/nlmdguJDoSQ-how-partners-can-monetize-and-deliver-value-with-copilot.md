## Description

Every customer has unique business needs and processes. Find out how Copilot agents enable you to connect, extend, and customize Microsoft Copilot to internal and third-party applications to create seamless and more efficient workflows. Learn how to build your extensibility practice and monetize solutions with Copilot agents and Copilot Studio to unlock the value of AI for the unique needs of your customers.

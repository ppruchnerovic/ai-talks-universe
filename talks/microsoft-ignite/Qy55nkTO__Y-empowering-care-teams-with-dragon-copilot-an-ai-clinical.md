## Description

Discover how Microsoft Dragon Copilot, an AI clinical assistant, simplifies and transforms care team workflows, streamlines documentation, surfaces information, and automates tasks. Designed with role-based experiences at its core, Dragon Copilot delivers tailored capabilities for the broader care team. Its integrated and extensible design ensures seamless interoperability with EHR systems, AI apps, and agents—enabling organizations to scale innovation without disrupting workflows.

## Description

In the era of AI, agent evolution is poised to radically transform the future workforce experience. In this session, we’ll debut the latest purpose-built employee agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot designed to enhance employee experience and boost IT and HR back-office productivity. Also discover how Microsoft Viva is equipping organizations to accelerate their journey towards an AI-driven workforce, with powerful insights, employee communications tools and skills-based learning.

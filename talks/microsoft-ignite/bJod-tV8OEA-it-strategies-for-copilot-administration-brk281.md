## Description

Join us to understand the essential steps to ensure your organization is ready to adopt Microsoft Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot with confidence. Learn about organizational and IT readiness steps for IT admins and innovative product experiences in the Microsoft 365 admin center to make it easy. This session will cover topics around the role of cloud app discovery, enterprise data protection and more. Walk away with a comprehensive guide to optimize both your organization's AI adoption.

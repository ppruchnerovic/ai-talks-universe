## Description

Deep dive into the new agent development tools and learn how everyone from internal IT to ISV’s can take advantage of new SDK’s and tools to build your own agents, publish to your own environment or use Copilot agents to enhance Microsoft 365 Copilot. Learn how to leverage the Microsoft graph and M365 to take your agents even further while keeping the organization secure.

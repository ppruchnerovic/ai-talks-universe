## Description

In this session we discuss how to improve customer service using AI with a Microsoft Teams Integrated Contact Center.
We demo 4 specific use cases: Digital Assistant IVR, AI transfer assistant, suggested resources based on call context, and AI Agent checklist.
This gives some real-world options to enhance customer services and assist your agents in the contact center.

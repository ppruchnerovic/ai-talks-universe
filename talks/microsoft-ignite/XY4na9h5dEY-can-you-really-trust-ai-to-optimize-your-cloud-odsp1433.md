## Description

If you’re responsible for an enterprise cloud, you know how expensive and time-consuming it is to operate hundreds of individual services, at all hours of the day. That’s why many engineering leaders are now using AI to manage their cloud — from rightsizing resources to fixing availability issues.
But is this safe? And what happens when something, inevitably, goes wrong?
Sedai CEO Suresh Mathew explains the important benefits — and risks — of trusting your cloud to AI.

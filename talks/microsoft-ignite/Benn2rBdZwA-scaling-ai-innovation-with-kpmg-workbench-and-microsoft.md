## Description

KPMG Workbench, developed with Microsoft, is an AI platform transforming enterprise service delivery through automation. Utilizing Microsoft Foundry, it manages 50+ AI agents, scalable to thousands, helping ensure secure, compliant, and interoperable approaches in tax, audit, and advisory fields. The platform integrates the firm’s industry expertise into digital interactions. Explore how KPMG elevates service delivery with Workbench and how your organization can benefit.

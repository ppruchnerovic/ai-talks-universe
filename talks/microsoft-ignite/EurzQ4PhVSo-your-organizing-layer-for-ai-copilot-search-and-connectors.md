## Description

In the age of AI-powered productivity, finding the right information at the right time is more critical than ever. This session introduces Copilot Search as your organizing layer for AI—bringing together the power of semantic search, generative answers, and Copilot connectors to transform how users discover and act on information across Microsoft 365.

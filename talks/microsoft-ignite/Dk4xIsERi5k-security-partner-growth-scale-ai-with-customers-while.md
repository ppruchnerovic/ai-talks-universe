## Description

As customers embrace AI and Copilot technologies, partners helping safeguard the data estate is more critical than ever. Join us to explore the comprehensive features of Microsoft Purview that are designed to protect data in this new era. You will leave this session understanding how to leverage Microsoft Purview to accelerate Copilot adoption, utilizing partner GTM investments.

## Description

Join us as we introduce a groundbreaking new multimodal service as part of the Azure AI portfolio - Azure AI Content Understanding. Discover how we are helping enterprises build the next-generation of multimodal AI apps by streamlining data processing workflows with an efficient setup, grounding mechanisms, and confidence scores for modalities such as documents, images, video, and audio. Learn how WPP and Numonix are delivering new customer experiences using the new Azure AI service.

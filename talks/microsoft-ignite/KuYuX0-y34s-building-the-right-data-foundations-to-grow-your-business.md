## Description

Before enterprises can effectively capitalize on new AI technologies, they must first establish the right foundations that support their business goals. This includes creating a strategy to modernize existing applications, data landscapes, and incorporating security/compliance measures from the ground-up. Learn how Kyndryl’s modernization framework and end-to-end services help enterprises prioritize and create a strategy to modernize and integrate new AI capabilities, including generative AI.

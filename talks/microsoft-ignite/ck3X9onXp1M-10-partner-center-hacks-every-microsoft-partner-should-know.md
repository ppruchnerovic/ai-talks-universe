## Description

How are you using data to drive your Microsoft partnership and profitability? In this session you will learn how to use data to identify and act on opportunities that are left on the table; from funding to cross-sell/up-sell, internal usage, and much more. These hacks can help unlock hidden revenue opportunities and build a roadmap to maximize profitability.

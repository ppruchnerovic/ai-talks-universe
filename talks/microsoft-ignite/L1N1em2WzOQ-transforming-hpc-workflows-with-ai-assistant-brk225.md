## Description

Elevate your high-performance computing experience for workflows with the power of our AI Assistant, seamlessly integrated with Azure Batch. Say goodbye to technical hassles— automate your setup and resource management for workflows and modelling. In this session, We’ll dive into how this assistant is driving breakthroughs in genomics and health sciences. Watch as we demonstrate the automated execution of a complex genetic pipeline, showcasing how AI enhances research precision and speed.

## Description

Do you know where your Copilot prompts are going? In this admin-focused, demo-filled session we’ll follow a series of prompts as they journey through the Copilot stack. Along the way we’ll stop to take closer looks at where and how prompts are processed, stored, and secured. We’ll also talk about the impact of grounding, orchestration, and extensibility– and maybe add a surprise along the way.  You’ll leave with new information and resources that elevate your Copilot management expertise!

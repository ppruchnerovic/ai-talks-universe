## Description

Azure is redefining the next generation of cloud infrastructure for the AI era - built to power the world’s most ambitious AI workloads and designed for intelligent, automated operations. With innovations like Fairwater AI Super Factories and Azure Copilot, customers can modernize, scale workloads, and operate securely with greater efficiency and resilience. With Azure, organizations have the world’s most advanced cloud for AI and modern infrastructure at their fingertips, to power today and invent tomorrow.

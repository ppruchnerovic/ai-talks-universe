## Description

Handling documents like offer letters, invoices, and contracts can be labor-intensive and inefficient. In this session, you'll learn how to simplify and accelerate these processes by automating agreement workflows and integrating them seamlessly into your broader business operations. Discover how a major insurance company achieved significant improvements with Docusign and Power Platform and explore how your organization can do the same.

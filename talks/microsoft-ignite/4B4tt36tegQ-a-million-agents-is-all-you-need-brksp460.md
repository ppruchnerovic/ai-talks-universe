## Description

Cognizant VP of AI Research Risto Miikkulainen shares how to design, test, and scale safe multi-agent systems. Discover research showing how multi-agent systems can solve tasks beyond single-agent models, even with a million agents. Learn how to evaluate and fine-tune agent confidence using evolutionary strategies and apply these innovations to drive organizational growth.

## Description

As we approach the end of support (EOS) for Windows 10 on October 14, 2025, it's crucial for organizations to transition to Windows 11. In this session, we will explore how Windows 11 and Copilot+ PCs serve as the canvas for a new era of AI at work. Learn about Copilot+ PCs the fastest, most intelligent and secure Windows PCs ever as well as the latest AI-enhanced features in Windows 11, including advanced security measures, innovative AI experiences, and seamless integration with Microsoft 365. Join us to understand the benefits of upgrading to Windows 11 and how to prepare your organization for the future of AI.

## Description

AI is transforming every business function, and enterprises must quickly embrace and operationalize AI to stay competitive. Traditional governance tools were not designed to manage risk of AI systems at scale, and over 85% of AI projects fail as a result. This session will discuss the challenges with traditional solutions that slow innovation, and why enterprises must embrace a purpose-built approach to AI governance designed to empower and accelerate AI transformation.

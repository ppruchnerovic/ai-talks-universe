## Description

In September we introduced the concept of Web + Work + Pages. Copilot's ability to bring these components together into a single product represents the foundation of a new way of working, with Copilot at the center. In this session we'll dive deeper into the products and technology underlying this new way of working, giving you a richer understanding of its capabilities. We'll also share real world opportunities to redefine working patterns in your organizations with Business Chat and Pages at the center.

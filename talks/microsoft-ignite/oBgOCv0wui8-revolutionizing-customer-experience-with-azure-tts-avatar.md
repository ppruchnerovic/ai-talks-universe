## Description

Uncover how CDW harnessed the power of Azure TTS Avatar, Azure OpenAI, and agentic architecture to deliver an unparalleled customer experience. Learn about our innovative solution, showcased at a live event, enabling customers to effortlessly order coffee through natural language interactions. Witness the fusion of Large Language Models (LLMs) with Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) for real-time, contextually rich responses to CDW-related inquiries.

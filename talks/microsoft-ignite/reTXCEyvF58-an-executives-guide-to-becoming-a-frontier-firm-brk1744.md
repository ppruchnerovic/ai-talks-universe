## Description

What sets today’s most advanced organizations apart? Join Katy George, Corporate Vice President of Workforce Transformation, and Matthew Duncan, Head of the Future of Work, for an inside look at how to lead with AI. They’ll share tools and actionable insights that address executives’ most pressing questions, informed by Microsoft’s own experience. Whether you’re just beginning your transformation or looking to accelerate it, you’ll walk away with practical takeaways for becoming a Frontier Firm.

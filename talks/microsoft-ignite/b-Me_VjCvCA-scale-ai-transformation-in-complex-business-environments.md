## Description

AI transformation isn’t a future aspiration—it’s here, it’s complex, and it’s redefining how work gets done across the enterprise. In this interactive conversation, we'll go beyond vision to execution. Designed for business and technical leaders alike, this session aims to deliver candid insights, real demonstrations, and practical use cases that show what it can actually take to scale AI in enterprise environments and shift to a program mindset.

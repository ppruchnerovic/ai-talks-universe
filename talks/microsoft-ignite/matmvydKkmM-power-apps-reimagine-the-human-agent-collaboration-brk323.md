Dive deep into how humans and agents work side by side through new collaboration patterns and interfaces in business applications. Learn how Microsoft 365 Copilot integrates with apps, and explore best practices for bringing assistants, apps, and agent feeds together in unified workspaces, unlocking seamless collaboration and greater productivity for every employee.

## Description

Explore the future of Copilot connectors and revolutionize the way agents interact with business and productivity data. Learn how Copilot connectors enable you to effortlessly unify business data stored in Microsoft Dataverse with productivity data from Microsoft Graph, all within a single tool. No more switching between different platforms—Copilot connectors allow you to access, manage, and act on all your data from one interface, streamlining workflows and simplifying your daily tasks.

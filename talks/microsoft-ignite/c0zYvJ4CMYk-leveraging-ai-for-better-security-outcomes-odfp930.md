## Description

Generative AI has already been adopted by your employees. The question is: how will your organization going to meet these challenges? Shadow AI, prompt injection, and data loss are just three of the new risks companies face. This session will provide an overview of the challenges in the modern AI world, how to “fight AI with AI,” and best practices to implement AI-driven security in your organization to stop AI-powered threats, remediate risks, and meet the demands of compliance.

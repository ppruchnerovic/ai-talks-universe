## Description

Join this customer panel discussion to learn how E&Y, Chevron Phillps Chemical Co, London Stock Exchange (LSEG), and PayChex modernized on Azure App Platform using Azure Kubernetes Service, Azure App Service, Azure Functions, and Azure Container Apps. This panel discussion will cover real-world challenges of app modernization, the ease of modernizing with the right tools, and leveraging Azure Data and AI platforms to enhance internal business processes and external customer experiences.

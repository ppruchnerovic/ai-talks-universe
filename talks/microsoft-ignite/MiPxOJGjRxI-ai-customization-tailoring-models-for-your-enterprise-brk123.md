## Description

In today’s rapidly evolving AI landscape, using Large Language Models (LLMs) as-is can be limiting. To unlock their full potential, customization is essential. This session explores the importance of model personalization, covering prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and fine-tuning with proprietary data. Learn how tailored AI solutions enhance accuracy, relevance, and efficiency, driving value and keeping your organization at the forefront of AI-driven innovation.

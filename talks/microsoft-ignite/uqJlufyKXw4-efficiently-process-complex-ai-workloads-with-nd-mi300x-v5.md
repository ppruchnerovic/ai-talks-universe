## Description

Join us for an in-depth exploration of the Azure ND MI300X v5 VM series, a groundbreaking addition to Azure’s AI infrastructure. This session will highlight how these VMs, equipped with industry-leading high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and exceptional compute performance, are designed to meet the demands of advanced AI workflows. Discover how the ND MI300X v5 series accelerates innovation by enabling fast and efficient processing of complex AI models workloads.

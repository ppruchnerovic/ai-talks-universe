## Description

As organizations increasingly adopt AI-driven tools, understanding their impact and optimizing their usage becomes crucial. Discover how to use the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard to improve adoption and impact across your organization. Dive deeper with advanced reporting in Viva Insights to create custom reports, analyze trends, and measure business outcomes associated with Copilot usage. This session is designed for analysts and leaders who want to maximize the value of their Copilot investment.

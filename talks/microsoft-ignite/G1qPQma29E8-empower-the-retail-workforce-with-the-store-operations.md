## Description

With Microsoft Cloud for Retail, you can leverage the Store Operations Agent to support retail employees in their daily tasks and inquiries. Powered by natural language processing and semantic search, it provides quick answers to common questions about products, inventory, and procedures. Additionally, employees can create and manage tasks through voice or text commands, boosting productivity and collaboration. Join us for an in-depth exploration of the features, architecture, deployment, and extensibility of the Store Operations Agent, configured in Microsoft Copilot Studio.

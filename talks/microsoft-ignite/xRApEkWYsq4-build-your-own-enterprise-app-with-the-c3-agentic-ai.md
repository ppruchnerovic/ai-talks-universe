## Description

Join Adrian Rami, GVP Products and Engineering at C3 AI, for a live demo of the C3 Agentic AI Platform. See how to turn ideas into Enterprise AI apps in minutes using low-code Application Canvas or full-code in Visual Studio Code. We’ll showcase the end-to-end process: define goals in natural language, generate an app instantly, refine with code, and deploy securely. Build workflows, agents, and dashboards, connect to data, and ship with full governance, versioning, and observability.

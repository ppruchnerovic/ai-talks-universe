## Description

It is often said that Data is the fuel that powers AI, but it can also pose challenges for leaders adopting analytics platforms like Microsoft Fabric to do more with their data. In this session, you will learn how Microsoft Purview works together with Microsoft Fabric, offering a comprehensive approach to secure and govern data in the era of modern analytics and AI helping organizations confidently activate their ever-growing digital landscape.

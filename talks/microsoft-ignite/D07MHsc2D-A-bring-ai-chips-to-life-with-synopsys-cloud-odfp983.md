## Description

The AI chip ecosystem is undergoing a dramatic transformation as companies of all sizes vie to meet the skyrocketing demand for AI compute. Recent projections by Futurum Research indicate that the processor and accelerator market for AI applications in the data center segment will grow at a 30% CAGR, reaching $138B by 2028, up from $38B in 2023. This extraordinary growth highlights the increasing number of AI chip startups entering this competitive and technologically demanding industry. Despite significant barriers to entry, the AI chip startup ecosystem is thriving, with over 190 AI processor startup companies today. How are these startups managing to innovate and compete with established players? Cloud-based solutions, such as those provided by Synopsys and Microsoft, play a crucial role.

## Description

Everyone has some legacy technology in their environment. We get it. So, how do you move into the modern AI-infused Microsoft Teams world while you are getting more use and value from your existing legacy desk phones, conference room systems, and analog (ATA) devices? Do you know how to make this happen? Spoiler alert! We will be talking about firmware updates, about your network’s ability to support a SIP Gateway and more. Join us to hear how to avoid common pitfalls on the path to modern.

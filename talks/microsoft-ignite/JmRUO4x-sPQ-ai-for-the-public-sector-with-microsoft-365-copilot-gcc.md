## Description

Microsoft 365 Copilot GCC is coming to US Public Sector customers in the GCC (Government Community Cloud) environment. Come explore how Copilot will support public sector leaders and strategists as the next-generation tool for mission success. This session will focus on the role of AI in Public sector, product details, public sector use cases, responsible AI practices and data privacy, and next steps to prepare your public sector agency for mission success with Microsoft 365 Copilot GCC.

## Description

Since 2019, AMD and Microsoft have partnered to enhance computing on Azure. Join us to explore how AMD EPYC™ CPUs can advance your infrastructure, covering general purpose, confidential computing, storage, visualization, and high-performance computing. Discover how upgrading to AMD EPYC CPU-based VMs on Azure can save costs, accommodate AI workloads, safeguard confidential data, and maximize cloud performance.

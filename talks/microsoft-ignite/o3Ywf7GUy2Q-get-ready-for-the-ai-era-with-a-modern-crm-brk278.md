## Description

In the age of AI, customers are choosing to bet on customer experience solutions that help them take a platform-centric approach to CRM to move away from data silos. With better data comes better AI. In this session, hear from customers who have moved to Dynamics 365 Sales from legacy CRM systems and transformed their sales organization by modernizing their CRM platform; placing big bets on AI and winning with more personalized customer engagements and accelerated pipeline growth.

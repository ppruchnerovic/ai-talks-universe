## Description

Due to their size SLMs are prime candidates to customize for specific tasks. In this session, we will delve into fine-tuning and customizing Phi and Small Language Models (SLMs) to meet specific use cases and enhance performance. Join us to learn how to harness the full potential of Phi and SLMs through fine-tuning and customization, and take your AI projects to the next level.

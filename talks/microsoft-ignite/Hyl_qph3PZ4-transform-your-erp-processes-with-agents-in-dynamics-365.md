## Description

Join us for an exciting session on the future of ERP systems with Dynamics 365! Discover the transformative power of AI agents in ERP and how the latest advancements in generative AI are paving the way for more autonomous ERP processes and scenarios. See how the integration of agents in Dynamics 365 helps companies increase expense management, improve cash flow, reduce risk and improve supplier collaboration. Don't miss this opportunity to see live demos and gain insights into the future of ERP with Microsoft Copilot and Dynamics 365.

## Description

Discover how Microsoft 365 Copilot agents can  empower sales, service, and finance teams with purpose-built Copilot agents. This session explores how role-specific insight and workflows powered by Copilot agents can have impact on business functions.  You’ll learn about the latest product innovations, see how providers are enriching customer connections, and gain practical guidance from other real-world examples on driving business success with these Copilot agents.

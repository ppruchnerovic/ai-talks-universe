## Description

Discover how Microsoft 365 Copilot empowers neurodivergent workers, who represent roughly 20% of today’s workforce, through demos and the latest research. Learn how you can use Copilot as powerful tool for inclusivity in your organization to level the playing field and maximize potential whether you are neurodivergent, an ally, or a people manager.

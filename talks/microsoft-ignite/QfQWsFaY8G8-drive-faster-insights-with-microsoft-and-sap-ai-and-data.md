Microsoft and SAP are helping customers unlock enterprise data with AI. In this session, see how joint innovations in data and AI are transforming operations—from frontline workflows to strategic decisions. Discover how to unify SAP and non-SAP data for analytics and automation, and how SAP Business AI and Microsoft Copilot simplify tasks, surface insights, and accelerate outcomes.

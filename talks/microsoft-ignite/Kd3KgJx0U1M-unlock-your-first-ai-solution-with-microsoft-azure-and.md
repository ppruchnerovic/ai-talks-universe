## Description

For small and medium businesses (SMBs), artificial intelligence has shifted from a future ambition to a priority. Leaders are asking not if to adopt AI, but how quickly. The promise of AI for SMB is especially compelling: automating repetitive tasks, uncovering insights from data, and personalizing customer experiences. Getting started doesn’t need to be complicated. In this session we’ll demonstrate two approaches:

1. Building your first AI solution using Foundry Templates in minutes
2. Buying AI solutions via the Microsoft Marketplace from trusted partners

Attend this Ignite on-demand session and walk away with two fast, practical ways to get your first AI solutions running on Azure — complete with step-by-step demos you can follow.

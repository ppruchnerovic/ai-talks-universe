## Description

Legacy AD backups weren’t designed for today’s ransomware. They require scripts, identical hardware, and hours (or days) of downtime, often risking reinfection. This session explores why outdated methods fail and contrasts them with modern, patented approaches that guarantee clean, instant forest recovery. Learn how to move from uncertainty to assurance when your identity layer is under attack.

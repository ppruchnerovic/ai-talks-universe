## Description

Microsoft is enabling energy companies to tackle complex decarbonization challenges using AI-powered solutions. In this session we will highlight use cases like methane emissions detection, predictive maintenance, and generative AI for permitting workflows—demonstrating how agentic AI and copilots are helping reduce emissions, streamline compliance, and unlock operational efficiency.

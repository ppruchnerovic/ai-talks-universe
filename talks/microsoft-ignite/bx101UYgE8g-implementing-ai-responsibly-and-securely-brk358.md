## Description

Designed to bring together AI enthusiasts, enterprise leaders, startup founders, data scientists, and security experts. The session will explore AI implementation in enterprises, sharing real-world examples and strategies for overcoming challenges. It will also address startup-specific issues like limited resources, ethical considerations, and building scalable AI solutions, offering practical tips. Additionally, the session will cover planning and executing AI projects, emphasizing clear objectives, collaboration, and continuous improvement. It will highlight the importance of ethical and secure AI, focusing on fairness, accountability, transparency, and robust security measures.

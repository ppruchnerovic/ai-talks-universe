## Description

NVIDIA RTX AI provides an end-to-end, GPU-accelerated platform for building and deploying state-of-the-art AI models and applications across PC and cloud. Developers can use RTX to build on-device AI that keeps your data and applications private, while tapping into the power of AI on your PC. This talk will showcase and guide developers on the end-to-end suite of tools to streamline development and deploy outstandingly fast, AI-enabled applications for the 100M+ Windows RTX PCs.

## Description

With Microsoft Cloud for Retail, you can harness the power of the Personalized Shopping Agent to enhance the digital shopping experience. Leverage generative AI to enable intent-based, conversational interactions, personalized product discovery, tailored recommendations, and a seamless checkout process. Join us for a deep dive into the features, architecture, deployment, and extensibility of this versatile agent, powered by composable AI services configured in Azure AI.

## Description

In this session we will dive deep into the custom hardware, software, and architecture of Azure’s purpose-built cloud infrastructure, including Azure Maia and Azure Cobalt silicon. We will also discuss how end-to-end systems optimization, thoughtful codesign of hardware and software, and advanced datacenter technologies in cooling, power, and network – form the powerful and reliable foundation of the Copilot Stack that empowers your innovations in the era of AI.

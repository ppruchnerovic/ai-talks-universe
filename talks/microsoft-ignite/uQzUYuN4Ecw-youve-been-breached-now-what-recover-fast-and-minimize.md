## Description

You’ve likely made good investments in cyber security defense, but do your teams know what to do if a breach occurred? A cyberattack is not a matter of “if” but “when.” Insight CISO Jeremy Nelson steps through the best practices and disciplines to prepare your teams for calm, swift recovery after an incident. You’ll learn how to scale your security response at mass — and how to keep the impacts of a breach to a minimum.

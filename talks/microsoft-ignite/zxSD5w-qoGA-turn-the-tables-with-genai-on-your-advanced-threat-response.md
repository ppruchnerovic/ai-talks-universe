## Description

Hackers are already using GenAI to punch holes through your proactive IR security defense strategy, causing headaches for CISOs and security teams, and threatening missing-critical applications. In this session, you will learn how Aviatrix has partnered with the Microsoft Copliot for Security team to leverage GenAI and LLM data enrichment from across the Microsoft security landscape to pinpoint potential threats across the entire network and recommend adaptive defensive policies in real-time.

Join us to dive into the latest advancements in AI, focusing on two transformative technologies: agent-based systems and LLMs. We will break down how AI agents work together to solve complex problems and share insights into the future of AI, with a live demo of an end-to-end decision augmentation use case from scratch with Cognizant's agent-based platform, Neuro AI.

## Description

Selecting the appropriate technology is crucial for building Win32 applications on Windows. In this session, we will explore the capabilities of WPF and WinUI to guide you through the decision-making process for choosing the native platform that best suits your needs. Discover the unique benefits of each framework, learn how to modernize your existing WPF applications, and more. Whether you're a seasoned developer or new to the Windows platform, this talk will provide valuable insights into selecting the right technology for your Win32 applications.

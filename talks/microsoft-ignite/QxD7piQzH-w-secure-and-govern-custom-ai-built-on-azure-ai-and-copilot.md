## Description

66% of organizations are testing, planning, or already developing GenAI applications, with an average of nearly 14 applications simultaneously. This can introduce new and increasing security threats. In this session, we will demonstrate how Microsoft can help you enable data security and compliance controls for your custom AI apps, manage vulnerabilities in your AI stack, and detect and respond to emerging AI threats such as prompt injections and model theft.

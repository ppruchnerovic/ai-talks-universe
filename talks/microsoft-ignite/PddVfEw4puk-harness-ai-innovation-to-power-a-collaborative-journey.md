## Description

The Siemens and Microsoft partnership aims to enhance product lifecycle management (PLM), design, manufacturing, and other industrial processes by leveraging Microsoft's cloud and AI technologies. Manufacturing companies have shared that 78% of quality events go unreported, and 16% are significant defects. The Siemens Xcelerator portfolio on Microsoft Azure allows manufacturers to communicate issues faster to the right people to fix the issues.

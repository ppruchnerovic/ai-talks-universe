## Description

The role of ERP in the business is changing with the introduction of agents – from Cloud ERP now to Agentic ERP. Come learn how Dynamics 365 is leading the way to Agentic ERP with a new adaptive experience, pre-built agents out of the box, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) compatibility and build your own plan for the future of ERP.

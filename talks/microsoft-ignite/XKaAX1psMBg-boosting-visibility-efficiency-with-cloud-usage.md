## Description

Cloud engineering and FinOps teams strive for infrastructure efficiency and cost-effectiveness, often beginning with rate optimization focused on pricing and discounts. However, advancing to usage optimization reveals challenges such as limited visibility, automation capabilities, empowerment, and alignment with end-engineering teams.

This session showcases REPAY’s journey to unlock enhanced visibility and savings with Stacklet’s comprehensive cloud visibility and usage optimization tool.

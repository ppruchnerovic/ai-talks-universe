## Description

In an AI-first world, enterprises want to innovate and move fast to stay competitive. Too often, enterprise innovation stalls in “pilot purgatory.” The difference between experimentation and impact is having the right partner to bring new ideas safely into production.

Join this session to learn how Microsoft helps enterprises unlock their innovation potential. Through real-world examples, you’ll hear how AI leaders are moving faster and scaling smarter, responsibly.

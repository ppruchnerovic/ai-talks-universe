# Carlos Acevedo: True Zero Data Retention Is Structural, Not a Clause in a Contract

## Description

Every model that touches the web inherits the privacy policy of its search provider. This means Zero Data Retention is only enforceable up to your search vendor's boundary, unless it's a property of a vertically integrated stack. If they're calling an external index for some queries, then you can't guarantee true ZDR for your users. In this talk, you'll get practical advice for how to trace the retrieval path your queries actually take; how to verify end-to-end privacy; why agents are compounding this privacy gap; and three questions to take back to your team to ensure reliably enforceable ZDR across all endpoints without degradation in result quality.

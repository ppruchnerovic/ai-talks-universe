## Description

Open models now match closed frontier models on quality at a fraction of the cost. Choosing one is the easy part. Running it well is where teams get surprised, usually under sustained load, when behavior that looked fine in evaluation stops holding. Together AI serves these models at production scale, across thousands of GPUs and hundreds of millions of requests. Ollama is the front door for developers who use them every day. This session covers both vantage points: what it takes to serve an open model efficiently at volume, and what developers hit once they start building on one. Time for questions at the end.

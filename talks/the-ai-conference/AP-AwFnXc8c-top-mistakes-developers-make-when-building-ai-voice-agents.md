## Description

Recorded live at Hack Day, this session breaks down the pitfalls that derail voice agents in the real world—latency and barge-in handling, ASR/TTS mismatch, prompt design and LLM routing, turn-taking + interruptions, error recovery and fallbacks, telephony quirks, compliance, and observability. Andrii shows battle-tested patterns to ship voice agents that feel natural, stay responsive, and actually hold up in production.

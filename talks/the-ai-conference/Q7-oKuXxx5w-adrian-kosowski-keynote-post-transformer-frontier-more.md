# Adrian Kosowski | Keynote: Post-Transformer Frontier: More Intelligence per Watt, Token & Dollar

**Adrian Kosowski**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2026` · `20 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7-oKuXxx5w) · [Conference site](https://aiconference.com/)

## Description

Transformers have reshaped AI, but they were never designed as a foundation for intelligence that must persist and grow over time. Today’s reasoning models often gain capability by generating and accumulating an ever-growing trace of reasoning tokens. This raises latency, drives up inference cost, and increases energy consumption. We are already seeing diminishing returns from scaling parameters and inference-time compute. This keynote explains why the next frontier in AI will come from architectural breakthroughs that produce more intelligence per watt, token, and dollar. I will map the emerging post-Transformer landscape across three architectural frontiers: persistent and recurrent memory, sparse and selective computation, and continual adaptation — and show how they need to come together to achieve this goal. I will then introduce BDH — an architecture that unifies memory, reasoning, and adaptation — and lay out a path beyond static parameters and ephemeral token histories toward AI systems that retain what matters, reason and adapt over long horizons, and improve through experience.

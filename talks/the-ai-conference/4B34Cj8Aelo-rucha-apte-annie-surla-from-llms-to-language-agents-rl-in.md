## Description

Pretraining builds knowledge. Reinforcement learning builds behavior. As LLMs move into production systems powering copilots, decision engines, and autonomous workflows post-training with RL has emerged as a critical step in shaping model behavior beyond imitation. This talk presents a practical guide to post-training language agents using reinforcement learning, covering reward modeling, human preference optimization, environment-based feedback, and tool-integrated training loops. The talk will analyze trade-offs between supervised fine-tuning and RL, discuss stability and scaling challenges, and present real-world examples where RL improves reasoning depth, tool reliability, and policy alignment.

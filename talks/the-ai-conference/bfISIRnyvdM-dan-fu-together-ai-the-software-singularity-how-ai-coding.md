Dan Fu, VP of Kernels, Together AI

The Software Singularity: How AI Coding Agents Are Accelerating Themselves

In 2025, AI coding agents hit a watershed moment in quality and usability. In this talk, we’ll see how today’s AI agents are now powerful enough to write complex code across a variety of settings — and how Together AI is using AI agents to build the platform to serve the next generation of AI agents. Attendees will walk away with: An understanding of how Together AI’s researchers use AI coding agents to build full-stack production workloads Strategies on how to organize and structure agent workflows Insights on what AI agents mean for the future of software.

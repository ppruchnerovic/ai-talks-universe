# Lindsay Walker | Keynote: Agentic Commerce: Financial, Legal, and End-User Interaction

**Lindsay Walker**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2026` · `14 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GsI39jK6dN0) · [Conference site](https://aiconference.com/)

## Description

Existing payment rails, and all that is built around it, from customer identity verification, to terms of service, to the tools you use to manage and facilitate payments were built with the human user experience in mind. As companies and industries explore the use of AI in commerce, trading, and financial markets, they realize that the traditional systems used to carry out these transactions cannot be easily adapted for use by AI Agents. Companies from Stripe to Coinbase to Cloudflare are recognizing the new capabilities and technologies needed to enable agentic commerce and are building toward that future. The way that you provide and control funds accessible to an agent, as well as how commercial flows like shopping and checkout are executed will need to be redesigned, as adapting human-centric rails doesn’t work in an AI-driven environment. Provisioning a virtual credit card for an agent doesn’t mean that agent will be able to use those funds in most commercial flows. This keynote will explore some of the gaps that exist in using traditional systems with AI, and propose some of the new solutions that can be adopted. Enterprises will understandably need more control compared to a hobby builder to enable secure agentic workflows with commercial transactions, and this keynote will discuss what the future looks like for different parties. With a summary of what is being built and what standards are being developed, this talk will give a high level view of what a commerce-enabled agentic future looks like. Distributed Ledger Technologies (DLTs) are especially well suited to play a role in Agentic Commerce, providing the infrastructure for payments, solidifying and recording human intent, legal agreements, and audit trails for agentic actions.

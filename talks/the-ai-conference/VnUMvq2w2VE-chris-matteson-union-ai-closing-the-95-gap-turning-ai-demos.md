## Description

Chris Matteson, Union AI

Closing the 95% gap: Turning AI demos into dollars

Despite massive investment, most enterprise AI projects are failing. Recent studies show: 95% of enterprise GenAI initiatives fail to produce measurable returns(MIT NANDA, 2025); only 5% of custom tools survive the pilot-to-production cliff (Forbes, 2025); and the share of companies abandoning AI efforts have from 17% last year to 42% this year (S&P Global). The message is clear: what looks good in a proof-of-concept rarely survives real-world demands. Workflows stall, infrastructure buckles, and brittle orchestration makes scaling nearly impossible. In this session, we’ll explore why orchestration is the hidden barrier and how reframing it as a flexible, execution-aware, and compute-aware layer can change the game. Instead of patching together lightweight tools or wrestling kubernetes alone, we’ll show how Union AI’s approach helps teams turn experiments into durable, production-ready systems. Attendees will leave with a practical roadmap for building orchestration that adapts to messy infrastructure realities, scales with experimentation, and ultimately delivers lasting business impact.

## Description

Daniyal Maniar, Software Engineer, Riot Games

Voice Chat Moderation at Scale: Building Safer Voice Comms in Games with Audio LLMs

This talk explores how we use audio large language models (LLMs), including Whisper, to detect trust and safety violations in voice chat for online games. We share our approach to audio classification, technical challenges at scale, and how these tools help create safer, more inclusive player experiences.

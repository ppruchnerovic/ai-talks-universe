# Illia Polosukhin | Keynote: Why We Need User-Owned AI

**Illia Polosukhin**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2026` · `15 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=romJJGlLjHA) · [Conference site](https://aiconference.com/)

## Description

As adoption of AI agents increases, more businesses and individuals are realizing the risk involved in exposing IP, proprietary and financial data, and personal information to centralized AI providers. Illia Polosukhin, co-author of “Attention Is All You Need,” calls for a shift towards “user-owned AI,” or AI systems that optimize for users’ agency and preserve their privacy. He unpacks his vision for an open AI stack and the requirements for more performant and trustworthy AI systems with verifiable compute, globally available to all.

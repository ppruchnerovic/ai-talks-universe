## Description

Tris Warkentin, Director of Product Management, GoogleDeepMind

The future of on-device intelligence: Gemma and Gemini Nano

The next AI revolution won't just be in the cloud. It's happening in your pocket. Join Tris Warkentin, Director of Product Management at Google DeepMind, for a look into the future of on-device intelligence. As a leader behind foundational models and products like Bard, PaLM, and Imagen, Tris will unpack Google's vision to bring powerful AI directly to users through two groundbreaking model families: Gemma, Google's new family of lightweight, state-of-the-art open models, and Gemini Nano, the highly-efficient model built to power on-device experiences in the Android ecosystem. Discover the technical breakthroughs enabling this shift and explore the new wave of faster, more private, and context-aware applications that will redefine our relationship with technology.

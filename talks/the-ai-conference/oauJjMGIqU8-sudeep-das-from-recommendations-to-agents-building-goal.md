## Description

Modern consumer AI is moving beyond one-shot predictions toward systems that actively reason, plan, and adapt. In this talk, Sudeep explores how personalization is evolving from static recommender systems into agentic, goal-driven AI experiences that operate across search, discovery, decision-making, and execution. Drawing from real-world production systems, the session covers how large language models, deep learning, and reinforcement-style feedback loops can be combined to build consumer-facing agents that understand intent, maintain context over time, and take coordinated actions on behalf of users. Topics include agentic product discovery, multi-step decision flows, trust and controllability in consumer agents, and practical evaluation strategies for these systems at scale. Attendees will leave with concrete frameworks for designing AI systems that feel helpful, adaptive, and human-centered—while remaining reliable, efficient, and grounded in real business constraints.

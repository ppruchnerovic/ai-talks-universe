## Description

What does it actually take to go all-in on AI as an engineering organization? This session traces monday.com's 12-month journey through three levels of AI engineering: developers pair-programming with AI, builders working in agentic mode, and finally autonomous agents owning complete tasks end-to-end. Anya shares the real metrics behind the shift - from +14% developer throughput at Level 1 to autonomous agents now owning 45% of all merged PRs, with overall output more than doubling. You'll see what this looks like in practice, with agents operating as genuine team members across code review, incident triage, and performance analysis. And you'll hear where monday.com is headed next: optimizing the human-agent team as a single unit.

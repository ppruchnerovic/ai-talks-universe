## Description

Boost AI Applications with SQL/Relational Vector Database

"MyScale = SQL + vector. Navigating the crossroads of relational and vector databases, we present MyScale: a revolutionary SQL/relational vector database that seamlessly blends the best of both worlds. MyScale retains full SQL compliance while delivering unmatched performance and scalability, surpassing specialized vector databases.
Through real-world applications, we will showcase how MyScale enables users to “chat” with millions of academic papers, amplifies semantic memories for millions of users, and supercharges LLM with a large knowledge base.
As the costs of LLMs continue to decline and their usage skyrockets, the future demands a data infrastructure that is both adaptable and scalable. Join us as we embark on a journey into this new era of database technology and the synergy it brings to the world of LLMs."

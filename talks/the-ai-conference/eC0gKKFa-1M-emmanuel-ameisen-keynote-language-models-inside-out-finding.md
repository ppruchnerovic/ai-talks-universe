# Emmanuel Ameisen | Keynote: Language Models, Inside Out: Finding world models hidden in the weights

**Emmanuel Ameisen**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2026` · `11 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eC0gKKFa-1M) · [Conference site](https://aiconference.com/)

## Description

Language models are superhuman text predictors. The best way to predict a piece of text is to build a rich model of the world that generated it. Look inside a language model and that's what you find: concepts shared across languages and modalities, plans formed well in advance of being enacted, and even evolved algorithms for arithmetic. Prediction, done well enough, becomes simulation. To guess the next word, the model learns to represent the hidden variables behind the text: a speaker's emotions, beliefs, and goals. The field of interpretability has spent the last few years decoding language model weights directly. This talk is a tour of some of the most important findings from this work, and of the mental model they add up to: a language model as a world model, hiding in plain sight in the weights.

# Empowering Agents with Knowledge Graphs using Neo4j MCB Servers — Jason Koo (Neo4j)

**Jason Koo**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2025` · `13 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=in-bbFGQz2U) · [Conference site](https://aiconference.com/)

## Description

Recorded live at Hack Day, this workshop shows how to connect AI agents to a living knowledge graph with Neo4j’s MCB servers—so they can ground answers in structured context, reason over relationships, and act with richer state. Jason covers graph modeling basics, standing up an MCB server, wiring agents to query with Cypher, and patterns for retrieval, updates, and governance in production.

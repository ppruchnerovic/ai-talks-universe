## Description

Most enterprise AI failures aren't model failures: they're meaning failures. When LLMs don't share your business's definitions of "customer," "risk," or "compliant," every downstream decision inherits that ambiguity. This session introduces the Semantic Backbone: a knowledge infrastructure that enforces a single, governed semantic agreement across fragmented data silos, grounding LLMs in the business logic that actually matters. We'll show how Semantic GraphRAG architectures reduce hallucinations by up to 90% and make AI reasoning explainable and auditable, without rebuilding your data stack. Data and AI leaders will leave with a concrete framework for moving from brittle prototypes to production-grade, domain-aware AI systems. Diagnose why fragmented data definitions are the root cause of most enterprise AI failures in production Learn how an enterprise knowledge graph and a context graph play together Explore the Semantic Backbone as a governed knowledge infrastructure that enforces unified business logic and context across silos, giving LLMs a reliable foundation to reason from Examine how Semantic GraphRAG delivers explainable, auditable AI outputs, and what a practical transition from prototype to production looks like for data leaders

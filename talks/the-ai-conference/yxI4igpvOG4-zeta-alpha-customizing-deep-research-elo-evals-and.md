## Description

Jakub Zavrel, Founder & CEO, Zeta Alpha
Fernando Rejon Barrera, CTO, Zeta Alpha

Customizing Deep Research: Elo Evals and Optimization of Advanced AI Agents

How can advanced agentic AI systems like Deep Research be customized on private enterprise data with just a few labeled examples? How can we evaluate and optimize these modular, non-differentiable systems to build high performance reasoning systems for complex tasks? In this talk we will outline the architecture of this class of agents, show how LLM-as-a-judge can be used for Elo based evaluation in the absence of gold standard benchmarking data sets, and explore how sparse evaluation signals can be used for end-to-end optimization of AI agents to improve performance.

## Description

Sarmad Qadri, CEO, LastMile AI

Generative AI Development for Software Engineers

For AI to be truly democratized, software engineers will have to be at the forefront of AI application development. What does the software development workflow look like for generative AI apps?

This talk will show how things like version control, evaluation and monitoring apply to generative AI, and the tools and processes we can use to bring rigor to managing prompts, evaluating gen AI apps and having AI as just another tool in the software developer’s toolkit.

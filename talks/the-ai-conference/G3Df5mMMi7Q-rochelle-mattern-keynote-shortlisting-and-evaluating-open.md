# Rochelle Mattern | Keynote: Shortlisting and Evaluating Open Models

**Rochelle Mattern**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2026` · `13 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G3Df5mMMi7Q) · [Conference site](https://aiconference.com/)

## Description

There are dozens of open models worth considering and no reliable way to know which ones suit your workload. General rankings will not tell you, and testing all of them is not realistic. So teams pick a few, run an evaluation, and hope the result means something. This session covers how to narrow the field and how to build an evaluation that predicts production behavior. What representative workload data looks like. Which performance characteristics matter before a model goes live. Rochelle works on these migrations across Together AI's customers and will go through what the successful ones did differently.

## Description

Caitlin Colgrove, Co-founder and CTO, Hex

Building in Public: Why Traditional Product Launches Fail in AI

The three years since the launch of ChatGPT that catalyzed the AI era, we’ve seen both meteoric rises as well as catastrophic crashes. The difference? In AI, the only way to build a good product is real usage – and lots of it. Successful products find ways to leverage this data early and often, inverting the traditional product launch cycle. Come hear how we’ve applied these principles in Hex’s latest launch of our Notebook Agent and what we’ve learned in the process.

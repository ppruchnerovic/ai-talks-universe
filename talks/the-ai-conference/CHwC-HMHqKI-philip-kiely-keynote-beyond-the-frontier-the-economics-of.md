# Philip Kiely | Keynote: Beyond the Frontier: The economics of the multi-model era

**Philip Kiely**

## Description

For the last few years, the AI conversation has been dominated by a handful of frontier, closed-weight models but that era is ending. As open-source models have become better and better and new specialized model labs have emerged, the industry is shifting toward a multi-model future. This future is one where open source and closed-weight models are distributed, evaluated, and run side by side on shared infrastructure, and where the right model is chosen on quality and performance for a specific job rather than the lab who created it. Baseten sits at the center of this shift, serving as a hub for distributing both open source and closed-weight models at production scale. In this talk, Philip Kiely, author of Inference Engineering and early employee at Baseten, will make the case for why this shift is not just inevitable but good for the industry. The unit economics of open source models now dramatically outperform frontier models for equivalent tasks, and smaller, fine-tuned models frequently outperform large general-purpose models on quality, latency, and cost for real customer workloads. Drawing on data from production deployments, this talk will show why the future of AI isn't one model for all tasks. It's the right model, for the right job, at the right price.

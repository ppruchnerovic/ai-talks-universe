## Description

Ansh Kanwar, Chief Product Officer, Reltio

The New rules of data intelligence in the age of AI

The old rules of data no longer apply. To lead in the Age of Intelligence, enterprises must adopt new data strategies that move at the speed of AI— in real-time - and that are connected and built for continuous decision-making. In this session, Reltio CPO Ansh Kanwar shares the new rules of data that enterprises need to adopt in the Age of Intelligence, and the key capabilities and enterprise data foundation that CAIOs, CIOs and IT leaders need build across their organizations to be ready for the operational realities of AI at scale. Attend this session to learn how an agentic data fabric of unified, trusted data assets accelerates AI transformation & learn more about real-world strategies for success.

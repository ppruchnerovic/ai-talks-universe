## Description

Tom Ohtsuka, Co-founder & CEO & CTO, Teammately

Let AI Build AI The unveil of "AI AI-Engineer", and how it transforms the AI development

CEO Tom presents their cutting-edge flagship product, the AI Agent designed to enhance AI. This autonomous agent assists developers in customizing language models using the foundation model layers and optimizing its architectures, including model configs and in-context learning like prompts and RAG. It offers superior values in AI engineering compared to traditional fine-tuning or prompt engineering driven solely by humans.

In this session, Tom will delve into the LM-native technology behind the product and explore transformative use cases that could revolutionize AI development practices. He will also discuss the current challenges in productionizing LLMs/VLMs using manual trial and error, explaining why allowing LLMs to enhance other models is a more robust solution, and why in-context learning is lightweight enough to make autonomous iteration possible.

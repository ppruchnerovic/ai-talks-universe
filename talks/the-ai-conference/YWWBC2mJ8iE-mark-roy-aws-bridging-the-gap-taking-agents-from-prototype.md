## Description

Mark Roy, Principal AIML Architect, AWS

Bridging the Gap: Taking Agents from Prototype to Production

Building great agent demos is just the beginning - the real hurdles emerge when implementing security, scalability, and governance in real-world deployments. Successful teams need to navigate this transition while eliminating the undifferentiated heavy lifting of infrastructure management and operational complexity. Discover how to implement enterprise-grade capabilities, from secure runtime environments and unified tool management to robust identity controls and comprehensive observability. Amazon Bedrock AgentCore services - including memory, observability, runtime, gateway, and identity - accelerate this journey with production-ready capabilities, enabling teams to focus on innovation while scaling confidently from proof-of-concept to enterprise-wide adoption.

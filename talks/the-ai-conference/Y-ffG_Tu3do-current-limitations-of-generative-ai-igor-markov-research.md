Current Limitations Of Generative AI

Generative AI for text, images and other data types raised excitement, hopes and company valuations.

Brain icon for The AI Conference 2023, a groundbreaking two-day event on AGI, LLMs, Infrastructure, Alignment, AI Startups, and Neural Architectures.Making good use of this momentum requires understanding pitfalls and limitations of the technology. Therefore, we review prominent limitations and discuss if/how they can be circumvented in practice.

About Igor Markov
Igor L. Markov is Research Scientist at Meta. He previously worked as professor at the Univ of Michigan. He received his M.A. in Mathematics and Ph.D. in Computer Science from UCLA. He is an IEEE Fellow and an ACM Distinguished Scientist.

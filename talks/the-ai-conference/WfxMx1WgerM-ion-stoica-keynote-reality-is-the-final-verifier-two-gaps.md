# Ion Stoica | Keynote: Reality Is the Final Verifier: Two Gaps in Agentic Software Engineering

**Ion Stoica**

## Description

AI coding agents can generate and test software orders of magnitude faster than humans can. Yet they optimize against requirements and environment models that only approximate stakeholder intent and the real world. This talk introduces the two-gap framework: the requirement gap and the model gap. Neither can generally be closed in an open, changing world. Although these gaps are longstanding, agents make them far more consequential because they may lack relevant context, iterate much faster than humans, and automatically test and deploy implementations. Reliable agentic development therefore requires an outer assurance–revision loop that uses deployment outcomes and human judgment to continually narrow the gaps.

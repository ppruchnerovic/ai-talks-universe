## Description

Agentic software engineering is expanding beyond coding assistance toward autonomous execution across the software lifecycle. As this shift takes hold, AI agents are beginning to take on broader, longer-running work, while humans increasingly define goals, constraints, and desired outcomes rather than individual tasks. Application modernization illustrates this shift in practice: agents help understand complex systems, transform applications, and optimize how software is built and maintained. As agents take on this kind of work, enterprise realities come into play — hybrid and on-prem environments, governance, security, and operational control all shape how these systems can operate responsibly at scale. As agents operate within these enterprise constraints, their workloads grow, and economics and model strategy become part of the architecture. This session explores how model selection and rotation can optimize for capability, speed, and cost, and what these trends suggest about the future of increasingly autonomous software delivery.

Cecilia Ziniti, Attorney & General Counsel, Founder & CEO, GC AI

AI Legal Issues:A Tech General Counsel Turned GPT App Developer Breaks It Down

This talk will explain copyright, fair use, and legal issues for AI, and how to navigate them in development. Hear from a three-time general counsel and now AI application developer on the legal challenges and opportunities, and how to both protect yourself and move fast. The presentation will explore the latest legal cases including NYT v OpenAI, and discuss the implications for intellectual property rights.

Attendees will learn the legal challenges in AI, with dozens of real-word examples of cases I’ve litigated or been involved in at companies from Replit, to Amazon, to Cruise. Exciting times!

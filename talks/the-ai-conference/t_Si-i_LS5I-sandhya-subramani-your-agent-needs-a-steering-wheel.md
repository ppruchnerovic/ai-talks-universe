## Description

What if your AI agent could recognize a bad move before making it and adjust its next step? Most agents rely on prompts for instructions and guardrails to catch failures. That leaves a gap during execution, when an agent skips a required check, repeats a broken tool call, or reaches a decision that needs human judgment. This session introduces steering, for just-in-time guidance inside the agent loop. Through a live demo using an open-source agent harness framework, attendees will learn how to inspect the proposed actions, guide recovery, enforce required steps, and add approval points without giving up the flexibility that makes agents useful.

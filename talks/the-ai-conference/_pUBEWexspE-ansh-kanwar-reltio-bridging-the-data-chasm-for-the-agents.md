## Description

Ansh Kanwar, Chief Product Officer, Reltio

Bridging the Data Chasm for the Agents of the Future

Soon, applications will be experienced as simple prompts to autonomous AI agents—but behind that simplicity lies a complex data landscape. Enterprises today are navigating AI transformation while contending with data debt accumulated from siloed systems and fragmented data. Organizations faced with the urgency to realize AI impact now must find a scalable way to harness trusted data across the enterprise.  In this keynote presentation,  Ansh Kanwar, Chief Product Officer at Reltio explains how to accelerate that transformation with the assets that you have today - and build  a blueprint to make AI agents enterprise-ready, now.

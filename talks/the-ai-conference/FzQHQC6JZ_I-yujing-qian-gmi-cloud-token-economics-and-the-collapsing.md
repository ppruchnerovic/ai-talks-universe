## Description

Yujing Qian, Head of Engineering, GMI Cloud

Token Economics and the Collapsing Bubble of Closed-Source Lock-In

Closed-source AI models deliver strong performance today, but their economics don’t scale. Builders are already spending tens of thousands monthly on inference, with token costs 8–10x higher than open-source alternatives for only marginal benchmark gains. This talk explores how reliance on closed-source providers is the real AI bubble—and why forward-looking companies are insulating themselves with two strategies: model agnosticism and open-source + reinforcement learning (RL).

# Sergey Levine | Keynote: Robot Foundation Models

**Sergey Levine**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2026` · `15 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J6eJbzBUk9w) · [Conference site](https://aiconference.com/)

## Description

Foundation models have transformed how we interact with AI systems. Can the same transformation take place in robotics, providing us with robots that can be tasked to perform any job through prompts and instructions? Getting the same kind of generality and interactivity out of robots that we've come to expect from AI assistants and generative AI tools will require both technical innovations and a sophisticated industrial effort. In this presentation, I will discuss what robot foundation models are, how they have advanced in recent years, and the technical directions that will lead to the most significant advances in the near future.

## Description

Frontier AI development is increasingly concentrated among a small number of centralized labs, leaving many countries, industries, institutions, and communities dependent on models they cannot fully inspect, adapt, govern, or control. Project Tapestry, led by the AI Alliance, offers a different path: a global, open, consortium-based approach to developing frontier foundation models where partners contribute data, compute, and talent while retaining control of their own data and the ability to build sovereign derivatives aligned to local laws, languages, cultures, industries, and priorities. In this session, attendees will learn how Project Tapestry re-frames sovereignty as a performance strategy, why open weights alone are not enough, and how collaborative model development can help organizations move from simply consuming AI to actively shaping the foundation models they depend on.

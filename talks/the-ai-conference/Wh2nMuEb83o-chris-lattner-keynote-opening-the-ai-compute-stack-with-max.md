# Chris Lattner | Keynote: Opening the AI Compute Stack with MAX and Mojo

**Chris Lattner**

`The AI Conference` · `The AI Conference` · `2026` · `15 min`

## Description

AI hardware is exploding, but developers are still locked to a single programming model. Chris Lattner, creator of LLVM, Clang and Swift, co-founder and CEO of Modular, and EVP of Advanced AI Software & Platforms at Qualcomm, argues the future of compute will be decided at the software layer, not the silicon. He introduces Mojo 1.0, now fully open source under Apache 2.0, and MAX, an inference platform built to deliver portability and performance across increasingly diverse hardware without vendor lock-in or rewrites.

From Mosquitoes To The Inside Of Trash Bins
Unbelievable Computer Vision Use Cases

At Visual Layer, we assist Fortune 500 companies from various industries in curating their massive image and video datasets. In this presentation, I will summarize intriguing use cases we have encountered and share valuable lessons learned.

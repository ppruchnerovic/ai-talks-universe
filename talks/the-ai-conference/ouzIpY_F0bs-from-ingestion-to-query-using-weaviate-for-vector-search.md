## Description

Recorded live at Hack Day, this workshop walks through an end-to-end vector search pipeline in Weaviate: data ingestion and chunking, schema design, embedding choices, filters and hybrid (BM25+vector) search, RAG patterns, and performance tuning at scale. Adam also covers operational tips—monitoring, sharding/replication, and cost-aware deployment—so your search stays fast and reliable in production.

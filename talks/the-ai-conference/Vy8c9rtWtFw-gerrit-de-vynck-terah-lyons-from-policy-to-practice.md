## Description

Terah Lyons has seen AI governance from both sides — as the architect of the first U.S. national AI policy strategy and founding CEO of the Partnership on AI, and now as the person responsible for turning policy into practice inside one of the world's largest financial institutions. In this fireside chat with Washington Post tech reporter Gerrit De Vynck, Lyons shares concrete lessons from deploying AI at scale inside JPMorganChase — what responsible adoption actually looks like under real regulatory and operational constraints, where global governance frameworks help versus where they slow real execution, and what it takes to balance innovation with safety when the stakes, and the scrutiny, are this high.

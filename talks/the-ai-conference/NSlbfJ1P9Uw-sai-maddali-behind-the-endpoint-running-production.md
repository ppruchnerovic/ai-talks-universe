## Description

Today’s most ambitious AI products combine multiple models, custom and post-trained models, multimodal workloads, and long-running agents—often under unpredictable demand. At the same time, users still expect them to feel instantaneous, reliable, and economically sustainable. Drawing on patterns across AI-native startups and large enterprises, this session will unpack what it takes to run inference well at production scale: matching models to the right engines and hardware, scaling without painful cold starts, managing latency, reliability, and cost tradeoffs, and continuously adapting as models and workloads evolve. More importantly, we will examine how these infrastructure decisions shape the customer experience, and how AI architectures are evolving behind the simplicity of a model endpoint. As inference becomes more central to product differentiation, companies are beginning to treat it not as backend infrastructure, but as a core product capability.

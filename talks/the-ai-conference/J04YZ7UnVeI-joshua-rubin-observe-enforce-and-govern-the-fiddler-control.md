## Description

An agent in your pipeline just wrote code, called an API, or touched a database, and nobody reviewed it first. That's the autonomy agents already have. The question is whether you'd know if one did something wrong, and how long it would take you to find out. That’s what the AI Control Plane is for: a system of trust that starts with evaluation, since that's what makes it possible to monitor an agent's activity, enforce policy on an agent's actions before they execute, and govern behavior across the agentic lifecycle. We'll show what this looks like in practice: inline guardrails that check an agent's actions before they execute, and dashboards that break down exactly what every agent is doing and spending by developer, team, and token. We'll also show how Fiddler Centor Models come batteries-included, no external API calls, no added cost per evaluation, so you can evaluate 100% of your traces or scale down to sampling for smaller deployments. The same approach works whether you're operating a handful of agents or a fleet. You'll leave with a practical way to decide which of your agents have earned more autonomy, and how to give it to them without losing the ability to govern what they're doing.

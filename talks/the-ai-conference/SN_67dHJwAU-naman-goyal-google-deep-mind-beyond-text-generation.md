## Description

Naman Goyal, Machine Learning Engineer, Google Deepmind

Beyond Text Generation: The Rise of Agentic AI and Its Transformative Potential

The evolution of Large Language Models (LLMs) from reactive text generators to proactive autonomous agents represents one of the most significant paradigm shifts in artificial intelligence. This presentation explores how agentic AI systems—capable of goal-directed behavior, environmental interaction, and decision autonomy—are redefining human-AI collaboration across industries. Drawing on recent research, we'll examine the technical foundations enabling this transformation: hierarchical planning structures, long-term memory architectures, and tool integration frameworks that dramatically enhance performance on complex tasks. The discussion will cover contrasting operational frameworks like ReAct, which demonstrates significantly higher completion rates on benchmark tasks, and Plan-and-Execute approaches, which receive stronger stakeholder ratings for internal consistency. We'll address critical ethical considerations including the Graduated Autonomy Framework, which reduces intervention requirements while maintaining safety, and explore cutting-edge developments in multi-agent systems, embodied agency, continual learning, and meta-learning—where adaptive architectures require fewer examples to master novel tasks. With hundreds of research papers published on novel agent architectures in recent years, agentic AI represents the frontier of artificial intelligence research and application. This presentation provides a comprehensive overview of current capabilities, emerging challenges, and future directions for practitioners and decision-makers navigating this rapidly evolving landscape.

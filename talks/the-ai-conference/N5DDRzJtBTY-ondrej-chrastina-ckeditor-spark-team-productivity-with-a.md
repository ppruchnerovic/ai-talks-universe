## Description

Ondrej Chrastina, Developer Advocate,  CKEditor

Spark Team Productivity with a Frictionless, Unified AI Writing Stack

Your content creators, marketers, and support teams are juggling tools. They draft and
experiment with content in multiple conversational AI systems. The result is a tedious
copy-paste job into your application or internal system, followed by hours spent fixing the
formatting that inevitably breaks. For them, it's inefficient. But for you - the founders, the product
leaders, the developers - it is a nightmare. This 'Bring Your Own AI' chaos creates security
holes, opens you up to compliance risks, and produces content that is inconsistent and often
factually incorrect because it lacks critical context. The workflow is fundamentally broken.
Let’s explore the challenges and opportunities of integrating AI directly into your content creation
process. We will touch on how integrating AI into your writing stack can help you:
● Boost productivity and alignment
○ Give your users a unified platform with tailored AI
○ Preserve rich text formatting
○ Get an AI-driven editing experience and review process without friction
● Offload the operational burden
○ Reduce the workload on AIOps teams
● Keep your product or application compliant and secure
CKEditor has been the most trusted, robust rich text editor component on the market for over
two decades - built for engineers and trusted by Fortune 500 companies. In this talk, we’ll unveil
the new CKEditor AI: a suite of configurable components that brings context‐aware generation,
predefined quick actions, model selection, and review workflows directly into your rich text
editor. Alongside integrated real-time collaboration, you get elevated rich text editing, a
productivity boost, and a coherent user experience all in one place.
Based on research with our customers, we will showcase possible applications of an integrated
AI writing stack such as audit reports, translation workflows, or customer service sentiment
analysis.
Join us for this talk to learn more about what you can achieve with the unified AI writing stack

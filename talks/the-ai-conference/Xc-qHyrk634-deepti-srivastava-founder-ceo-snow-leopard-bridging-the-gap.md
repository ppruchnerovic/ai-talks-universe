## Description

Deepti Srivastava, Founder & CEO, Snow Leopard

Bridging the Gap between AI and Business Data

While there is massive potential for GenAI to impact businesses, many companies are still struggling to utilize it in decision making tasks and critical workflows. One of the major gaps in realizing this potential is providing the right business data to AI systems at the right time for broader, more accurate and more timely outcomes. In this talk, we’ll discuss those gaps and how we are working to solve them.

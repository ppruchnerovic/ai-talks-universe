## Description

Arvind Gopal, VP of Product Management and Strategy, eGain

The Missing Link in AI CX Automation: A Trusted Knowledge Infrastructure What, Why and How

In a 2025 KMWorld survey, a whopping 61% of respondents pointed to erroneous or inconsistent answers as the top barrier for AI adoption. Trust can make or break a brand especially in the high-stakes domain of CX. How do you automate and deliver trusted customer experiences at scale with AI?  The solution lies in building a central foundation or a hub of vetted data and knowledge content that is correct, consumable, consistent, and compliant. Attend this session to learn: What is an AI knowledge hub? How do you build a trusted AI knowledge hub?  How do you make knowledge content AI-ready? What does success look like in AI for CX?

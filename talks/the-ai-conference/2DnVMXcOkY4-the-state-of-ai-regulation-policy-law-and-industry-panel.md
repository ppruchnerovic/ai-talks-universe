## Description

Panelists: Andrew Freedman, Kevin Frazier, and Rebecca Bauer-Kahan. Moderator: Gerrit De Vynck.

AI regulation in the U.S. is taking shape in real time, with states moving quickly as federal agencies, courts, and industry work to keep pace. This panel brings together leaders shaping AI law, policy, and governance to examine where regulation is working, where gaps remain, and what comes next. Moderated by Washington Post tech reporter Gerrit De Vynck, the conversation will explore the tension between state and federal approaches, innovation and oversight, and what a workable regulatory framework could look like as AI capabilities continue to advance.

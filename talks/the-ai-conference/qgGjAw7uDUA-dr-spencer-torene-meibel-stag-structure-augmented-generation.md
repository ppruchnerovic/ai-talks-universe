## Description

Dr. Spencer Torene, Principal Research Scientist, Meibel

STAG: Structure Augmented Generation - Bridging Structured and Unstructured Data for Enhanced RAG Systems

Organizations today struggle with extracting insights from diverse data sources, often treating all content as unstructured even when inherent structure exists. We present STAG (STructure Augmented Generation), an innovative system that maximizes performance by automatically identifying and applying structure wherever possible within heterogeneous data collections.   STAG's core philosophy centers on structure-first processing. Rather than treating all content as unstructured by default, STAG identifies structural patterns and relationships that can be leveraged for enhanced retrieval performance. For content that remains genuinely unstructured, STAG creates both graph and table-based representations for discoverable entities in addition to creating traditional RAG embeddings. This dual structural approach applies organizational relationships even to unstructured content, ensuring that retrieval processes benefit from enhanced document context and interconnections.  At query time, STAG's structure-augmented approach delivers superior results by leveraging structured queries where applicable, semantic search where necessary, and automatically incorporating referenced documents through intelligent graph and table traversal. This structural foundation enhances RAG system performance by providing more precise retrieval, better context, and comprehensive responses.  STAG represents a paradigm shift from treating enterprise data as universally unstructured to intelligently applying structure at every opportunity. This approach unlocks significant performance gains in information retrieval and generation by working with data's natural organizational patterns rather than against them. The presentation will demonstrate STAG's architecture, showcase real-world implementation results, and discuss the system's impact on enterprise RAG performance metrics.

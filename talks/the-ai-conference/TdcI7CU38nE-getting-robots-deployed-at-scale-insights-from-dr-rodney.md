## Description

Robotics pioneer Dr. Rodney Brooks (Co-Founder & CTO, Robust.AI) breaks down the real-world challenges of deploying robots beyond the demo stage.

He explores why reshoring U.S. manufacturing isn’t as simple as tariffs, why human-robot collaboration has only recently become viable, and what makes scaling physical robots so much harder than deploying software.

Brooks also explains how advances in sensors, computation, and human-robot interaction are enabling safer, smarter machines—and why true ROI in robotics requires buy-in from corporate leaders, on-site managers, and the people working alongside these robots.

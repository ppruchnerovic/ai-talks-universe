## Description

Austin Mejia, Product Manager, Head of AI @ Pixel Watch, Google

AI Wearables - The Next Frontier of Personal Computing

The AI wearables market is the next frontier for personalized intelligence, but achieving business value requires a distinct executive strategy. This session, led by Austin—creator and leader of Watch AI for Google Pixel—moves beyond the hype to provide a strategic blueprint for analyzing, productization, and deployment in this complex, constrained environment.
We will analyze the critical technical and market forces driving current wearable traction and examine the key product trade-offs that real user impact versus incremental features. Drawing directly from real-world, cutting-edge development, attendees will learn:

Shifting Market Forces: Examining the technical and design constraints that have previously limited wearable adoption, and what is now changing

Growing Business opportunity: Defining the problems, characteristics, and industries that can most immediately benefit from AI Wearables

The Go-to-Market Mandate: Navigating the unique hardware, software, and compliance constraints to ensure rapid, high-impact product deployment

Attendees will walk away with an understanding of the competitive landscape, the economic drivers of Edge AI, and a practical strategy for unlocking new business value with this generation of personal AI.

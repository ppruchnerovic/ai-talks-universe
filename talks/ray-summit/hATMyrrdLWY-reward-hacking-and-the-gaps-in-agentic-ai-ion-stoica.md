## Description

"AI coding agents write and test code orders of magnitude faster than humans. The failures are piling up just as fast, and they share a common underlying structure.

Ion Stoica is the director of the Sky Computing Lab at UC Berkeley, co-founder and executive chairman of Anyscale, and a co-founder of Databricks. His work spans some of the most widely used open source projects in history, including Apache Spark, Ray, and vLLM. In this talk, he presents unpublished new research on why agents reward hack and hallucinate: the gaps between what users intend and what requirements specify, and between development environments and the real world. He shows an agent that made a key-value store six times faster by not storing the values, explains why these gaps can be narrowed but never closed, and argues that AI-generated code should be treated as malicious by default.

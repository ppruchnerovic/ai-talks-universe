## Description

Every use case needs different latency, cost, and compliance tradeoffs, but most teams either over-engineer one-off stacks or force everything through a generic endpoint.

At Ray Summit 2026, Ansh Pratap Singh, Chief of Staff at Simplismart, covers a configurable inference engine that adapts per use case without new infrastructure: swappable backends across vLLM and TensorRT-LLM, per-workload autoscaling, and prompt-level tuning for data privacy, plus the config schema and the failure modes they hit at scale.

You'll leave seeing how one engine serves voice, batch, and enterprise workloads differently.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2025, Sheik Mohamed Imran from Intel shares how the accelerating demand for AI inference—now outpacing training workloads in enterprise environments—is reshaping infrastructure strategy, and how Intel® Xeon® 6 processors deliver a powerful, cost-effective solution for production-critical inference across small to medium language models.

He begins by highlighting the growing number of enterprise use cases where CPU-based inference shines—RAG applications, intelligent chatbots, automated content creation, document summarization, and emerging agentic workflows—particularly in pipeline stages requiring predictable latency, security, and efficient scaling.

The session then explores Intel Xeon® 6’s AI-centric hardware advancements, including:

AMX (Advanced Matrix Extensions) for major inference speedups

Enhanced memory bandwidth (MRDIMMs) to accelerate model throughput

High multi-core parallelism for scalable CPU-based inference

Intel TDX for confidential computing and secure, trusted execution

Sheik also walks through deployment acceleration via vLLM integration and Intel’s turnkey enterprise offerings—Intel® AI for Enterprise Inference and Intel® AI Enterprise RAG—designed to reduce infrastructure complexity so teams can focus on application development.

A highlight of the talk is how Anyscale Ray amplifies Xeon’s capabilities by orchestrating distributed inference across hybrid infrastructure. Ray’s autoscaling, resource-aware scheduling, and workload portability enable organizations to dynamically handle variable AI traffic with ease.

Together, Xeon + Ray form a unified, enterprise-grade compute fabric—accessible, scalable, secure, and cost-efficient—that democratizes AI inference across industries.

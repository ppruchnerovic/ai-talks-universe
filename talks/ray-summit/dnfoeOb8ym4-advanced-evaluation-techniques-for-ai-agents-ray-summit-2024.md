## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, Jason Lopatecki, Founder and CEO of Arize AI, presents a comprehensive approach to evaluating LLM-powered AI agents, addressing key challenges in this rapidly evolving field. The talk focuses on three critical issues: behavioral instability, comprehensive testing, and cascading failures.

Lopatecki explores advanced evaluation techniques, including the use of multi-dimensional metrics and automated scenario generation. Drawing from the experiences of hundreds of AI engineering teams in production environments, he provides insights into implementing agent-specific observability systems. The presentation covers strategies for designing robust evaluation pipelines capable of handling the complexities of modern AI agents, from detecting subtle regressions to quantifying performance across a range of dynamically generated test cases. This talk offers valuable guidance for teams working on developing and deploying sophisticated AI agents in real-world applications.

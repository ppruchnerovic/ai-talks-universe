## Description

"vLLM now supports over 1,000 accelerator types and more than 5,000 model architectures, and the workloads running on it have transformed from single-turn completions to agentic systems with tool calling, real-time interaction, and strict latency requirements.

Simon Mo built Ray Serve from zero to one, co-created vLLM, and is now co-founder and CEO of Inferact. In this talk, he surveys the state of open source inference: hardware diversifying across NVIDIA, AMD, and Google TPU; the Kimi K3 launch with Moonshot AI and the vendor verification process that lets anyone trust an open source engine to serve frontier models accurately; distributed KV cache pools delivering order-of-magnitude throughput gains on agentic workloads; and the vLLM roadmap ahead.

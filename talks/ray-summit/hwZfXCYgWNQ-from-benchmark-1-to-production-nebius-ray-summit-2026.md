## Description

Before joining Nebius, the Eigen AI team held the top spots on Artificial Analysis inference benchmarks across multiple models.

At Ray Summit 2026, Ryan Wang, SVP of Applied AI at Nebius, covers the inference framework techniques behind those results, from kernel and runtime optimization to NVFP4 quantization on Blackwell and speculative decoding, and what changes when you go from benchmark wins to production traffic at scale on Nebius Token Factory.

You'll leave with concrete optimization techniques and a clear sense of what production traffic actually demands.

## Description

Miles is an enterprise-facing agentic RL framework for large-scale PyTorch post-training.

At Ray Summit 2026, Mao Cheng of RadixArk and Xinyu Zhang of Anyscale cover its end-to-end stack for SFT, LoRA SFT/RL, and agentic RL, scalable multi-turn tool-use rollouts, and integrations with PyTorch, Megatron, and Ray, plus lessons from production workloads: low-precision training, MoE RL stability, speculative rollout acceleration, and extensible hooks for custom models, rewards, environments, and deployment pipelines.

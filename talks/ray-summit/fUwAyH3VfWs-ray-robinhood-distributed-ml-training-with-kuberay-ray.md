## Description

At Ray Summit 2025, Lanting Chiang and Robert Macy from Robinhood share how the company scaled its machine learning platform to support large-model and large-dataset training by adopting distributed training with KubeRay.

They begin by discussing how Robinhood’s ML workloads outgrew single-node training, making distributed training an essential capability for future model development. The talk then walks through Robinhood’s evaluation process and decision to adopt KubeRay for large-scale distributed training, highlighting key architectural choices and how Ray was integrated into their existing ML training stack.

Lanting and Robert also explain the platform-level abstractions they built to make distributed training seamless and accessible for internal teams. They detail how certain aspects of Robinhood’s Kubernetes environment shaped where they used native KubeRay components and where they substituted alternatives based on their unique requirements.

This session provides practical, real-world insights into integrating Ray into a production ML platform—covering lessons learned, architectural best practices, and strategies for enabling distributed training at scale.

## Description

As the world shifts from simple RAG stacks to more sophisticated AI systems, Jerry Liu from LlamaIndex explores the frontier of advanced knowledge assistants. This session delves into the complexities of creating multi-agent systems capable of reasoning over diverse inputs and interleaving retrieval with tool use to produce sophisticated outputs.

Liu addresses the challenges of building reliable multi-agent systems, focusing on developer ergonomics and production deployment. He outlines core building blocks for advanced research assistants, including cutting-edge RAG modules and event-driven workflow orchestration. This talk offers valuable insights for developers and AI enthusiasts looking to push the boundaries of AI assistants beyond simple question-answering to tackle tasks of arbitrary complexity across multiple data sources.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, Niket Agarwal presents NVIDIA's innovative work on advancing video comprehension in generative AI. The talk focuses on the crucial process of meticulous data curation, filtering, annotation, and high-quality data selection from vast video collections for downstream fine-tuning tasks.

Agarwal explores the development of a multimodal video foundation model, highlighting key components of an end-to-end video curation service orchestrated by Ray Data and running on NVIDIA DGX Cloud. The presentation covers insights from NVIDIA's team on leveraging and enhancing state-of-the-art AI models for large-scale inference and creating high-quality video datasets. This session offers valuable knowledge for developers and experts working on practical implementations of video AI in fields such as robotics, digital twins, and autonomous vehicles.

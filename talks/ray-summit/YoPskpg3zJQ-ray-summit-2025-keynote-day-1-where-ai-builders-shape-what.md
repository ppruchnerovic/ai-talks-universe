## Description

Watch the keynote recording to hear Anyscale CEO Keerti Melkote discuss the evolution of computing and the growing need for a unified AI compute platform. Anyscale Co-founder and Executive Chairman shares the origin story of Ray and why this is its moment.

Later, Anyscale Co-founder Robert Nishihara takes the stage to explore Ray’s role in the broader ecosystem and unveil exciting new product updates.

Key announcements include:
29:30 Ray joining the PyTorch Foundation
32:02 RDMA Support
47:04 Anyscale Runtime
52:40 Observability and Lineage
1:03:28 Deeper Kubernetes Integrations
1:05:58 Global Resource Scheduler and Multi-Resource Clouds
1:10:23 Anyscale on Microsoft Azure

1:13:38 Brendan Burns, CVP of Microsoft Azure Cloud Native & Management Platform, joins Keerti Melkote, CEO of Anyscale, for a discussion on the partnership between Anyscale and Microsoft Azure.

1:22:34 The keynote continues with a panel discussion featuring Robert Nishihara, Dawn Chen (Software Engineer at Google), Joe Spisak (Director of Product Management at Meta), and Simon Mo (Lead at vLLM). Together, they explore the foundations of open-source AI infrastructure and how collaboration across open-source projects continues to evolve.

1:39:50 Jim Fan, Director and Distinguished Research Scientist at NVIDIA, takes the stage to explore the evolution of robotics—discussing the Physical Turing Test, the challenges of building intelligent robots, how they’re trained, and what the future holds.

2:00:32 Devendra Chaplot, Member of Technical Staff at Thinking Machines, closes out day one with an overview of Tinkr—covering its development journey, core architecture, and key capabilities.

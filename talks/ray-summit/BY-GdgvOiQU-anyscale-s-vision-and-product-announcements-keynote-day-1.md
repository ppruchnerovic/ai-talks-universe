## Description

Join Anyscale co-founders Ion Stoica and Robert Nishihara as they unveil the future of AI scaling with Ray, the AI Compute Engine.

13:17 The keynote begins by addressing the growing "AI Complexity Wall"—a challenge arising from five key trends in AI:

1. Unprecedented scale
2. Multimodal data processing
3. Sophisticated post-training techniques
4. AI-powered optimization
5. The rise of compound AI and agentic systems

15:18 To break through this wall, Stoica and Nishihara introduce Ray as the AI Compute Engine—a solution that supports any AI/ML workload, handles diverse data types and model architectures, optimizes resource allocation, and scales seamlessly while abstracting complexity from developers. They showcase how Ray has enabled companies like Instacart, Niantic, and Canva to achieve remarkable improvements in data processing, code efficiency, and cost reduction.

They dive into how Anyscale builds on Ray, with advanced AI workflow features including:

## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, Venkata Jagannath from Astronomer and Marwan Sarieddine from Anyscale present a comprehensive solution for AI infrastructure orchestration. The talk addresses the common challenge organizations face in creating a cohesive AI infrastructure, often resulting in disconnected platforms for data processing, model training, and inference.

The speakers demonstrate how the integration of Apache Airflow and Ray creates a unified system capable of handling all aspects of AI development and deployment, regardless of data or model size. They showcase an end-to-end ML lifecycle implementation for custom data, covering automated LLM fine-tuning, evaluation, serving, and LoRA deployments. This session offers practical insights for organizations looking to streamline their AI operations, reduce development time, and optimize costs in production environments.

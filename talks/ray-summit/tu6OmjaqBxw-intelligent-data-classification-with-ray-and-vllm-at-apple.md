## Description

In this Ray Summit 2024 presentation, Praveen Kanamarlapudi from Apple explores the application of Ray and vLLM for AI-powered data classification and annotation. The talk addresses common challenges in data management, including privacy concerns, documentation quality, and missing business context.

Kanamarlapudi demonstrates how Apple harnesses LLM models using Ray's infrastructure and vLLM to bring intelligence to data classification and governance. The session covers practical applications, showcasing the scalability and efficiency of this approach in automating data processes.

The presentation offers insights into technical implementation, making it relevant for data engineers, scientists, and professionals involved in automated data classification and AI. Attendees will learn how Apple leverages these tools to enhance their data management strategies, potentially improving data quality and compliance.

This talk provides valuable knowledge for organizations looking to implement similar AI-driven data classification systems, highlighting the potential of Ray and vLLM in addressing complex data challenges.

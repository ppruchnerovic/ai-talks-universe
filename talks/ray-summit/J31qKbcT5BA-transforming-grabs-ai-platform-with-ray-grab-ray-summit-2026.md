## Description

Grab is transforming its AI platform with Ray to support three ML experimentation loops on one distributed execution layer: offline training, simulator-based parameter search, and reinforcement learning in simulation.

At Ray Summit 2026, Yvonne Wong, Chongyu Zhou, and Abhinav Rai from Grab show through production examples how Ray Train, Data, Tune, and Core simplify heterogeneous scaling, improve experimentation throughput, and make topology, reliability, and cost tradeoffs measurable.

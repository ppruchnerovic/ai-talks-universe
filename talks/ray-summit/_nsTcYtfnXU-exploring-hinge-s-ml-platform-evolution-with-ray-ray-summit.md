## Description

Hinge exists to inspire intimate connection to create a less lonely world. In this talk, Dev Goyal and Jonathan Jin from Hinge present their experiences in leveraging Ray to enhance their machine learning capabilities at Ray Summit 2024. They focuse on how Hinge's ML platform team has utilized Ray to democratize and streamline ML processes within the company.

Goyal and Jin discuss Ray's adaptability, highlighting how it seamlessly integrated with Hinge's existing infrastructure. They explore how this flexibility has been crucial in significantly reducing the time-to-production for ML-based solutions at Hinge. The presentation provides insights into the practical applications of Ray in a real-world dating app environment, demonstrating its impact on accelerating ML development and deployment processes.

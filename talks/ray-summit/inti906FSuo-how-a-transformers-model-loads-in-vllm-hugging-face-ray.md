## Description

The Transformers modeling backend for vLLM lets you serve Hugging Face Transformers model implementations at full vLLM speed, with access to all of vLLM's features.

At Ray Summit 2026, Harry Mellor, Machine Learning Engineer at Hugging Face, takes a deep dive into how a Transformers model loads in vLLM and, as a consequence, why its performance and feature access are so strong.

You'll leave understanding the mechanics behind the backend and when to reach for it.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2025, Mengliao Wang from Geotab shares how the company is building a cost-efficient and highly automated Smart Video Platform to process intelligence from billions of video frames captured by Geotab cameras.

The talk highlights how Anyscale Ray serves as the backbone for hosting and orchestrating Geotab’s machine learning models for video analysis, enabling both real-time inference and large-scale batch processing with high efficiency.

Mengliao also dives into Geotab’s automated training and validation workflows, which leverage Ray’s distributed capabilities to dramatically reduce the time and cost of model development and deployment.

Learn how Geotab is achieving major operational savings and accelerating innovation in video analytics through its strategic adoption of Anyscale Ray.

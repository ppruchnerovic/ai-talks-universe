## Description

John Bicket is the co-founder and CTO of Samsara. Samsara brings physical equipment online and leads digital transformations for complex operations in the physical world. In this keynote, John describes how Samsara, a leader in fleet management technology, harnesses the power of Ray and Anyscale to revolutionize their AI capabilities. Bicket shares their transformative journey and how Samsara partnered with Anyscale to overcome infrastructure scaling challenges. He explores how Ray is utilized across Samsara's entire AI pipeline, including orchestration, model training, field event sampling, dataset incorporation, and inference serving in both backend and edge deployments.

John emphasizes the profound impact of AI on their work, and Samsara has deployed these models at scale, serving numerous customers effectively.

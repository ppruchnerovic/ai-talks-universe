## Description

In this Ray Summit 2024 presentation, Anyscale's Kourosh Hakhamaneshi unveils the company's comprehensive toolkit for Large Language Model (LLM) development. Built on Anyscale's unified platform, Hakhamaneshi demonstrates how this suite of production-grade primitives simplifies the processes of serving, tuning, and evaluating LLMs, enabling developers to focus on solving core business problems.

The talk delves into Anyscale's high-level abstractions that maintain flexibility while streamlining the development process—with insights into how these tools can be integrated with existing workflows, making them particularly valuable for enterprises managing multiple LLM use cases or those looking to leverage open-weight models at scale. Hakhamaneshi illustrates how Anyscale's platform facilitates iterative fine-tuning, efficient serving, and comprehensive evaluation of LLMs, providing a roadmap for organizations aiming to optimize their LLM development lifecycle.

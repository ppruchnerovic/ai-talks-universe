## Description

As models and data grow, the multimodal training bottleneck increasingly comes from the surrounding input work rather than the training step itself: decoding, transformation, and tokenization contending with gradient computation for accelerator time.

At Ray Summit 2026, Minghao Xie and Haoran Cai from Luma AI present Koba, their multimodal data-loading framework, and Kojo, a Ray-based substrate that disaggregates stateless preprocessing stages onto managed actor pools while preserving the existing training loop. The result: compute that scales on its own pool independently of the trainer, fault isolation so a preprocessing failure no longer cascades into training, and reclaimed accelerator capacity returned to the gradient path.

You'll leave with a reusable pattern for relocating stateless, out-of-step computation off the training GPUs and onto Ray, plus the operational lessons of running it at scale.

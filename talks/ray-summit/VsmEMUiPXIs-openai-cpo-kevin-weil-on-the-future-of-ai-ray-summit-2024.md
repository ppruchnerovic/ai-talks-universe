## Description

Join Robert Nishihara and Kevin Weil, CPO of OpenAI, for an enlightening discussion on the rapidly evolving world of AI. This fireside chat offers unique insights into OpenAI's philosophy and the future of AI technology.

Weil shares OpenAI's perspective on open-source models and the importance of making AI accessible. He discusses the challenges and excitement of working with a constantly advancing technology, likening it to building on a shifting foundation.

The conversation explores the development of more sophisticated AI models, particularly the leap from knowledge recall to reasoning capabilities. Weil explains how this progression is changing the way we interact with AI, moving from question-answering to task completion.

Weil also touches on OpenAI's approach to model deployment and their vision for more natural human-AI interactions. This chat provides a thoughtful look at the current state and future potential of AI technology.

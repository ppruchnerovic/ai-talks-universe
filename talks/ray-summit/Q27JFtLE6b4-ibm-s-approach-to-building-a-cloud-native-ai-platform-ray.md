## Description

Hear Carlos Costa from IBM Research present the collaborative efforts of IBM and Red Hat in developing a cloud-native platform for AI, focusing on the complex workflows involved in training and deploying foundation models. The talk addresses the challenges of the current AI/ML landscape, where fragmented software tooling often requires extensive expertise for large-scale deployment and operation.

Costa details how IBM and Red Hat are leveraging KubeRay, Ray, and PyTorch to create a scalable and high-performance platform for the end-to-end cycle of foundation models. He discusses the platform's ability to scale from hundreds to thousands of GPUs on IBM's Vela supercomputer. The presentation also covers the integration of this technology into Red Hat OpenShift AI, highlighting a partnership with NASA that resulted in training a pioneering geospatial foundation model. This session offers insights into building comprehensive, cloud-native AI infrastructures capable of handling the demands of modern AI/ML workflows.

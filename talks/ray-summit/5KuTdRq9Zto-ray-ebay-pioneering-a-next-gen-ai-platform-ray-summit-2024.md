## Description

eBay's AI platform has undergone a significant transformation, with Ray playing a crucial role in its evolution. In this session, Yucai Yu from eBay reveals how the integration of Ray has reshaped their AI ecosystem, leading to accelerated development, optimized GPU resource usage, and enhanced user experience.

Yucai delves into four key production scenarios where Ray has made a substantial impact: batch inference, near-real-time feature engineering pipelines, distributed training, and comprehensive AI solutions. The presentation offers insights into the challenges faced, solutions implemented, and lessons learned during this integration process. Additionally, Yucai explores how Ray has influenced eBay's product design, showcasing the importance of thoughtful design in managing assets and leveraging the platform throughout the machine learning lifecycle.

## Description

Mercor RL-post-trained agents to climb APEX-Agents, its benchmark of real knowledge work tasks in investment banking, consulting, and law, scaling model size from 35B to 397B using 20 H200 nodes on Ray, with a headline run improving Pass@1 from 16.11% to 27.29%. The full recipe is open source.

At Ray Summit 2026, Edward Hu and Charlie Ruan from Mercor walk through making data RL-ready and driving harness error rates near zero before training starts, making agent loops token-in-token-out, the algorithmic choices that mattered in practice, generalization studies across held-out harnesses and datasets, and a transparent per-step cost breakdown of what a run like this actually costs.

You'll leave seeing why Mercor treats post-training as a platform decision across compute backends, harnesses, and data.

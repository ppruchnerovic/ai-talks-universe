## Description

In this Ray Summit 2024 breakout session, Spotify's Abdullah Mobeen and Google Cloud's Brandon Royal present their innovative approach to distributed Large Language Model (LLM) training. The talk focuses on Spotify's adaptation to the demands of Generative AI, showcasing their ML platform built with Ray on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE).

Mobeen and Royal discuss how Spotify has incorporated LLM support into their platform, enabling efficient training of models exceeding 70B parameters. The presentation covers the management of diverse machine types, including NVIDIA H100 GPUs, and how Kubernetes simplifies resource allocation.

Key topics include performance optimization techniques such as compact placement and NCCL Fast Socket. Mobeen explains how Spotify leverages Ray to distribute training applications across GKE-managed resources, providing insights into their scalable ML infrastructure.

This session offers valuable information for organizations looking to implement or improve their LLM training capabilities, particularly those interested in cloud-based solutions using Ray and Kubernetes.

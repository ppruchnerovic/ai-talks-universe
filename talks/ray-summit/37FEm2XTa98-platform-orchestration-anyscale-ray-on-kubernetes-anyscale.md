## Description

Compute is becoming one of the world's scarcest resources, and intelligent orchestration tuned to your company's needs is becoming the moat.

At Ray Summit 2026, Adhip Gupta and Elizabeth Hu from Anyscale cover orchestration at three layers: Kubernetes acquiring and managing the infrastructure, Ray turning it into a Python-native distributed runtime, and the Anyscale platform orchestrating across Ray clusters, prioritizing, queueing, and placing work so nothing in your compute estate sits idle. The session covers Anyscale on Azure Kubernetes Service, platform orchestration with the Anyscale Scheduler, and Anyscale KubeRay Connect for adopting Anyscale's observability and orchestration on existing KubeRay clusters.

You'll leave with a model for getting the most out of scarce compute across many teams, workloads, and clusters.

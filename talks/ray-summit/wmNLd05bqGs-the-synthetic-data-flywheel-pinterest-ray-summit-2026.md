## Description

Generating millions of synthetic preference pairs for training requires orchestrating GPU inference, rate-limited search APIs, and LLM judges, each with different concurrency profiles and cost constraints.

At Ray Summit 2026, Eric Wang and Gaurav Arora from Pinterest share a composable operator framework on Ray Data where ML teams configure what runs, models, tools, and prompts, while the platform handles how it scales: streaming execution and backpressure across heterogeneous stages. The trained model then becomes a producer for the next round, driving iterative model self-improvement.

You'll leave with production results, architecture decisions, and the quality gains from closing the loop.

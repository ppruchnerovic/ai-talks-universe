## Description

Gemma's custom quantization work produces highly compressed quantization-aware training checkpoints while preserving model quality.

At Ray Summit 2026, Sara Smoot, Software Engineering Manager at Google DeepMind, covers how quantization methods, checkpoint design, and serving integration work together to reduce memory requirements and inference costs, along with practical considerations for deploying these compressed models with vLLM.

You'll leave knowing what it takes to serve highly compressed Gemma models in production.

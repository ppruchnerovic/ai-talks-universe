## Description

In this exciting Ray Summit 2024 breakout session, Ahmet Salih Gundogdu and Erica Lin from Boston Dynamics AI Institute explore the frontier of robotics machine learning. Gundogdum and Lin showcase their innovative end-to-end ML training platform, designed to seamlessly handle multimodal data and integrate extensive robot simulations.

Discover how the Boston Dynamics AI Institute leverages Ray to manage and query metadata, streamline data preprocessing both offline and online, and effortlessly scale training infrastructure. The speakers demonstrate how their system uses RayClusters to incorporate physical simulation tools directly into training workflows, while supporting multi-tenancy within Kubernetes for optimal resource sharing. By addressing the complexities of robotics ML, Boston Dynamics AI Institute is paving the way for more efficient and scalable robot training, with real-world applications on the horizon.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2025, Aaron Liang from Google and Jui-An Huang from Anyscale share the latest advancements in KubeRay aimed at dramatically improving the user experience for teams running Ray on Kubernetes.

They introduce a series of major RayJob enhancements designed to simplify and harden the end-to-end workload lifecycle. These include:

Deletion policies for predictable and controllable cleanup

Cron scheduling to support recurring and automated Ray workloads

Sidecar mode for easier integration with surrounding services and tooling

Background status checks that improve reliability and reduce operational overhead

Together, these upgrades make Ray job orchestration on Kubernetes more intuitive, more manageable, and more robust for both developers and operators.

Attendees will gain practical insight into how these new features streamline Ray workloads in production—and how KubeRay is evolving to make large-scale distributed computing easier than ever.

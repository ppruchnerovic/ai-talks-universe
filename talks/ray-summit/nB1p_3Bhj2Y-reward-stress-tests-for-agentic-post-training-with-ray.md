## Description

In agentic post-training, reward bugs become model behavior.

At Ray Summit 2026, Radhika Gaonkar, Applied Researcher at Prime Intellect, presents a Ray-based system for stress-testing reward designs and model checkpoints before scaling RL runs: distributed rollout sweeps, paired evaluations, failure traces, and CI-style gates that catch proxy hacks before training scales. Code: github.com/RGaonkar/ray-agent-reward-lab

You'll leave with a working approach to validating rewards before they cost you a training run.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, Anyscale's Yunxuan Xaio and Amjad Almahairi delve into advanced techniques for training large-scale models. This talk explores how Ray Core's latest features can enhance training efficiency for LLMs and multimodal AI models. In this session, Xaio and Almahairi showcase Ray's native GPU-GPU communication and pre-compiled execution paths, demonstrating their application in complex data and control flows for distributed model training.

The session highlights the implementation of pipeline parallelism and training of multimodal models using heterogeneous GPUs. Xaio and Almahairi compare Ray implementations with NCCL and PyTorch, emphasizing simplicity and maintainability. They also share benchmarks on throughput and GPU utilization, illustrating the practical benefits of these optimizations.

This presentation is particularly relevant for ML researchers and engineers working with large-scale AI projects. It offers insights into maximizing accelerator utilization and improving training efficiency, crucial considerations in the era of increasingly large and complex models.

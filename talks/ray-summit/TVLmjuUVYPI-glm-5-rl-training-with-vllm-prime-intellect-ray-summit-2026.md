## Description

Training trillion-parameter MoE models on long-horizon agentic tasks demands that inference and optimization scale independently.

At Ray Summit 2026, Sami Jaghouar and Radhika Gaonkar from Prime Intellect present the asynchronous prime-rl stack, using GLM-5.1 as a case study: runs at up to 131K sequence length with 256 rollouts and sub-five-minute steps on 28 H200 nodes. They explain how vLLM powers low-precision inference, prefill/decode disaggregation, prefix-aware routing, and distributed KV cache storage, while the trainer combines FSDP, context parallelism, and expert parallelism.

You'll leave seeing how these pieces reduce idle time and make frontier-scale agentic post-training more efficient.

This session was part of the first vLLM Conference at Ray Summit, hosted by Inferact.

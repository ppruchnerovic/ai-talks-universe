## Description

In this Ray Summit 2024 presentation, Roger Wang from Roblox addresses the growing interest in supporting multimodal language models within the vLLM framework. The talk focuses on Roblox's efforts to integrate these advanced models into vLLM, a process driven by recent rapid developments in multimodal AI.

Wang explores the technical challenges encountered during this integration process and shares key insights gained along the way. This presentation offers valuable lessons for developers and organizations looking to leverage multimodal capabilities in their LLM deployments, providing a practical perspective on the complexities and opportunities in this evolving field of AI technology.

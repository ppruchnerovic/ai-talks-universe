## Description

As Ray Serve deployments grow to thousands of autoscaling replicas, the Serve controller, the brain coordinating every replica and routing decision, becomes the bottleneck: control-loop latency climbs and autoscaling decisions act on stale, seconds-old metrics.

At Ray Summit 2026, Abrar Sheikh, Software Engineer at Anyscale, walks through a systematic scalability experiment that pushed a single controller to 2,048 replicas and pinpointed where the time actually goes, plus the two optimizations that moved the needle: a Pydantic v2 migration of the serialization layer and a Cython-based metrics aggregation path, together delivering a 7x faster control loop, 3.6x higher controller throughput, and 15x fresher replica metrics at peak scale.

You'll leave knowing where controller time goes and how to scale past it.

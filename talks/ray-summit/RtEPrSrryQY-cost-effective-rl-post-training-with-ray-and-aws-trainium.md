## Description

RL post-training faces operational complexity and inefficient resource utilization: rollout generation, reward computation, policy training, and frequent model-weight synchronization must all be coordinated across distributed resources.

At Ray Summit 2026, Chelsie Zhang and Zhenkun Cai from AWS and Artur Niederfahrenhorst from Anyscale show how to simplify and scale RL post-training with Ray on AWS Trainium, a purpose-built AI accelerator for better price performance. They cover the architecture and an end-to-end distributed workflow spanning rollout generation, reward computation, policy updates, and model-weight refresh.

You'll leave equipped to set up RL post-training pipelines faster and run them at lower cost on AWS Trainium.

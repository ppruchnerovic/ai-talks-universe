## Description

At Lila Sciences, AI research runs on a self-service, cloud-native foundation.

At Ray Summit 2026, Priunsh Syen and Tyler Titsworth from Lila Sciences give a high-level overview of Lila's AI platform and the unique problems they solved along the way: the theoretical foundation and a four-layer-cake model of the stack, how they think about compute platforms that need to be AI-native, top-down, and workflow-oriented, and how they turn complex infrastructure into approachable tools, from post-training LLMs to massive ETL pipelines of instrument data and protein structure prediction.

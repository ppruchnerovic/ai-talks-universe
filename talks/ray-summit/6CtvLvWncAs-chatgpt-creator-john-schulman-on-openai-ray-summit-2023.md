## Description

OpenAI co-founder John Schulman joins Anyscale Co-Founder and CEO Robert Nishihara for an illuminating conversation on the creation and impact of ChatGPT.

In this Ray Summit 2023 keynote, Schulman shares his 13-year journey in AI, starting from his PhD research in 2010 to the pivotal decisions that led to ChatGPT's development. The discussion explores how OpenAI leverages Ray's distributed computing capabilities to tackle unprecedented computational challenges.

Schulman and Nishihara dive deep into pressing questions about tomorrow's AI landscape, including data quality concerns and scaling bottlenecks. They also explore fascinating parallels between human and AI learning, examining what it might take to achieve human-level intelligence and the crucial role of diverse training data.

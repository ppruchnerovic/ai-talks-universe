## Description

In this Ray Summit 2024 presentation, Trevor DiMartino from Workday unveils their innovative approach to fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLMs) in a secure, multi-tenant environment. DiMartino showcases how Workday addresses the unique challenges of training models on isolated customer data while managing GPU scarcity and strict data access controls.

The talk delves into Workday's platform design, which leverages Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) techniques like LoRA and KubeRay's autoscaling capabilities. DiMartino explains how this architecture enables cost-efficient, on-demand GPU resource allocation for both research and production environments. The presentation also highlights how Ray is utilized at various scales to create a flexible deployment solution, making full-stack development as accessible as full-scale production in Workday's multi-tenant ecosystem.

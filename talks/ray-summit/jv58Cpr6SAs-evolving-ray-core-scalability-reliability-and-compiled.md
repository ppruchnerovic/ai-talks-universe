## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, Stephanie Wang and Rui Qiao from Anyscale present the latest advancements in Ray Core, showcasing its growth. This breakout session highlights recent improvements in Ray Core's scalability and reliability, which make it more effective at adapting to the demands of an accelerator-driven computing landscape.

Wang and Qiao discuss key enhancements implemented since Ray Summit 2023, including the introduction of streaming generators and the shift to weekly Ray releases. Plus, get a look into Ray Compiled Graphs, a new backend for Ray Core designed to simplify high-performance application development on accelerator clusters.

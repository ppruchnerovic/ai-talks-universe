## Description

At Ray Summit 2025, Alexey Kudinkin from Anyscale shares the major milestones behind the release of the RLlib v2 stack, now officially GA and redesigned for the next generation of large-scale reinforcement learning workloads.
He begins by walking through the engineering insights and production learnings that shaped the new RLlib v2 architecture—built for higher throughput, improved reliability, and easier extensibility. The session highlights key enhancements that enable RLlib to scale to 10,000+ environment runners and 100+ learners, supporting both massive distributed training and complex real-world RL pipelines.
Alexey also outlines what’s ahead for RLlib, including deeper integration with diverse simulators and expanded support for large-scale, multi-agent, and hybrid RL workloads.
Whether you're building simulation-heavy pipelines, scaling RL research, or deploying RL in production, this talk provides a clear look at the future of RLlib and how it's evolving to meet the demands of modern AI systems.

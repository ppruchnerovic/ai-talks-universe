## Description

The landscape of cloud computing is evolving, and Akash Network is leading the charge as the world's first open source super cloud. This session pulls back the curtain on this groundbreaking platform, exploring its potential to revolutionize distributed computing and AI workloads.

Anil Murty and Logan Cerkovnik will delve into the origins and mission of Akash Network, offering insights into its unique position in the cloud ecosystem. They'll showcase how ThumperAI harnessed Akash's capabilities for training an advanced image generation model, demonstrating the platform's prowess in handling intensive AI workloads. The presentation will culminate with a live demonstration of Ray clusters running on Akash, potentially featuring vLLM and Llama-3.1, illustrating the seamless integration of cutting-edge AI tools with this innovative cloud infrastructure.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, Robert Berke from Kaiko.ai presents their work on a multimodal clinical diagnostic app. The session focuses on the integration of pre-trained LLMs with Vision Transformers (ViTs) to enhance image analysis capabilities for clinicians.

In this talk, Berke outlines the development process, explaining how the team incorporated vLLM technology to process both clinical text and images. He discusses the app's deployment in clinical research settings, highlighting its validation features and real-time data processing capabilities.

The presentation demonstrates the role of Ray Train and Ray Serve in this project, showing how these tools facilitate the conversion of multimodal data into clinical insights. Berke explains how this approach could impact diagnostic workflows, offering attendees a look at the practical application of AI technologies in healthcare.

## Description

Modern models can span thousands of GPUs and months of training. At this scale, failures become the norm, costing GPU hours and significant engineering time to diagnose.

At Ray Summit 2026, Jason Li and Mark Towers from Anyscale cover how Ray Train accelerates debugging and minimizes lost GPU hours across three common production failures, data starvation, NCCL hangs, and node preemptions, through rich data ingest observability, proactive hang detection, and JIT checkpointing, and how the Anyscale platform extends this to production scale with the Ray Train dashboard.

You'll leave knowing how to minimize lost GPU hours at production scale.

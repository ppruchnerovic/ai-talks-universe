## Description

This breakout session from Ray Summit 2024 introduces the integration of Intel Gaudi accelerators into the Ray framework. Carson Wang and Chendi Xue from Intel demonstrate how this combination enhances GenAI workloads, focusing on improved performance, scalability, and efficiency for Large Language Models (LLMs) throughout their lifecycle.

The speakers detail how Ray's distributed computation and fault tolerance capabilities work with Intel Gaudi accelerators to enhance LLM pre-training, fine-tuning, and serving. They showcase the platform's ability to optimize popular models such as Llama, Mistral, and Stable Diffusion, leading to better scalability and cost-effectiveness. Developers, data scientists, and AI practitioners will gain practical knowledge on configuring and managing Ray clusters equipped with Intel Gaudi accelerators. The session also presents performance results, illustrating how this technology combination is advancing AI capabilities.

## Description

Mental health support represents a top use case for LLMs, making safe, clinically faithful models an urgent need. In-context learning is powerful, but meaningful support may rely on tacit dynamics that resist explicit prompting.

At Ray Summit 2026, Anne Clarke and Akele Reed from SonderMind show how they fine-tune a suite of models across families, sizes, and architectures with LoRA on de-identified therapist language, robustly shifting domain performance toward the gold standard of human therapeutic care while preserving the constraints that keep deployment bounded, scoped, and responsible.

You'll leave with an approach to clinically grounded fine-tuning.

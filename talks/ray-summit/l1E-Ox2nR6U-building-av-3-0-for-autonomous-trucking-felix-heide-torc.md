# Building AV 3.0 for Autonomous Trucking | Felix Heide (Torc Robotics) | Ray Summit 2026

**Felix Heide**

`Ray Summit (Anyscale)` · `Anyscale` · `2026` · `21 min`

## Description

An autonomous truck at highway speed has to perceive and plan at ranges five times longer than a robotaxi. The benchmarks, datasets, and driving stacks built for urban driving don't transfer.

Felix Heide leads AI at Torc Robotics, building fully autonomous long-haul trucking. In this talk, he covers Torc's AV3.0 approach: an end-to-end virtual driver trained with self-play reinforcement learning, where every vehicle in simulation is driven by the same learning policy and difficulty scales itself. He walks through the TruckDrive dataset introduced at CVPR 2026, generative simulation for synthesizing long-tail scenarios, and the training infrastructure behind it all: a billion simulated miles per weekend, training jobs spanning CPU nodes and multiple GPU generations in one pool, all orchestrated on Anyscale.

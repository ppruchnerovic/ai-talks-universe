## Description

O.XYZ's ORI (O Routing Intelligence) system represents a significant leap forward in AI infrastructure design. In this session, Haider Khan and Zac Liu from o.xyz unveil how ORI dynamically routes user queries to the most effective language models, addressing the growing complexity of AI model-agent interactions.

The presenters delve into ORI's key features, including its innovative "chain of atomic queries" for query decomposition, sophisticated benchmark matching engine, and robust ethical assessment framework. They break down ORI's technical architecture, highlighting its integration with open-source tools like Ray Serve and vLLM for managing thousands of model endpoints. Attendees will gain insights into achieving high-speed inference, scalability, and optimized performance across cost, latency, and security metrics, offering valuable lessons for organizations looking to enhance their AI infrastructure.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, the ML platform team from Klaviyo shares their experience of using Ray Serve to build DART (DAtascience RunTime), their model serving platform. The presentation covers the reasons behind choosing Ray Serve, highlighting its balance of flexibility, simplicity, and high availability.

The talk delves into the architecture of DART, explaining how it enables data scientists to deploy models quickly and efficiently. The speakers discuss key aspects of Ray Serve's internal architecture, including request routing and fault tolerance with KubeRay. They also outline DART's multi-cluster, multi-AZ setup for enhanced resilience. The presentation concludes with practical advice for teams considering Ray Serve for their model serving needs, covering topics such as cluster sizing, traffic isolation, and avoiding common pitfalls. By sharing different architectural approaches, Klaviyo aims to provide attendees with insights to develop serving platforms tailored to their specific requirements.

## Description

As video generation moves beyond single prompts, models need to reason across images, video, audio, and language as a shared context.

At Ray Summit 2026, Victor Su-Ortiz, GTM Engineer and Developer Relations at MiniMax, uses the 33B open-weight MiniMax H3 as a case study in omni-context generation: unified audio-video modeling, multimodal conditioning, and the architecture and inference challenges that emerge when generative models move beyond text-to-video.

You'll leave with a picture of where generative video is headed and what it takes to build and serve it.

This session was part of the first vLLM Conference at Ray Summit, hosted by Inferact.

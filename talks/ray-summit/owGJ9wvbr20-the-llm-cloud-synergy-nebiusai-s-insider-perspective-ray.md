## Description

In the race to build superior AI clouds, NebiusAI has discovered a crucial advantage: an in-house LLM team. Aleksandr Patrushev takes the stage to reveal how this synergy has become a game-changer in cloud service development.

Patrushev shares key insights gleaned from NebiusAI's dual role as both LLM developer and cloud provider. He illustrates how firsthand experience in LLM creation directly shapes their cloud offerings, leading to more intuitive, efficient, and powerful services. This talk offers a unique glimpse into the symbiotic relationship between cutting-edge AI research and cloud infrastructure, providing valuable lessons for companies aiming to stay ahead in the competitive AI cloud market.

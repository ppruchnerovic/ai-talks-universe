## Description

AI workloads are evolving fast: data processing, training, and inference jobs run longer, span more accelerators, and lean on more advanced hardware, which makes them more prone to failure. The runtime underneath has to stay ahead.

At Ray Summit 2026, Mengjin Yan, Software Engineer at Anyscale, and Andrew Sy Kim, Software Engineer at Google, cover what Ray Core provides today and how it's evolving along three fronts: reliability, keeping long and expensive runs alive; scalability, pushing the ceiling on the Ray cluster; and hardware, adapting to new accelerators where topology-aware scheduling becomes essential.

You'll leave with a picture of where AI workloads are heading and the work keeping Ray Core ahead of them.

## Description

Autonomous trucking is hard. Torc generalizes the task planning stack with reinforcement learning, running two very different RL loops, object simulation and end-to-end with neural rendering, both on Ray.

At Ray Summit 2026, Neil Wadhvana and Yashovardhan Chaturvedi from Torc Robotics share how they adapted existing LLM post-training frameworks for the end-to-end loop and found the two scaling problems surprisingly alike.

You'll leave with a blueprint for scaling RL for autonomy.

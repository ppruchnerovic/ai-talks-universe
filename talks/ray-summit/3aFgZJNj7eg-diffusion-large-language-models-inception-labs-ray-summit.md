## Description

Mercury 2 is the first reasoning model built on a diffusion architecture and the fastest model on the Artificial Analysis index. Modern diffusion LLMs demand a rebuild of the post-training stack, from algorithms to systems.

At Ray Summit 2026, Aditya Grover, co-founder and CTO of Inception Labs, covers why standard policy-gradient methods break when sequence log-probabilities are intractable, how efficient estimators make GRPO-style RL viable, and the levers unique to this paradigm, plus how diffusion LLMs herald a new era of efficient on-policy training with a well-optimized inference engine.

You'll leave with a view of the applications powered by Mercury 2 and the future of diffusion LLMs.

## Description

At Ray Summit 2024, Yangyong Zhang, Brian Westphal, and Sven Eberhardt from Samsara demonstrate how they've developed an agentic framework for Generative AI applications. The talk focuses on Samsara's exploration of GenAI and Large Language Models (LLMs) within their AI platform, emphasizing a RAG-centric approach.

The speakers demonstrate how guided augmentations enable their multi-functional agent to handle diverse tasks, including multilingual knowledge base queries, real-time customer data processing, and visualization generation. They discuss the framework's design principles, which prioritize customer assistance while minimizing hallucinations. The presentation includes a case study of their Knowledge Base chatbot implementation, addressing challenges in data ingestion and rapid deployment. The Samsara team also shares insights on performance evaluation, guardrail implementation, and the various data pipelining capabilities supported by Ray in their system.

## Description

Coinbase moved production ML serving from self-managed Ray Serve on Kubernetes to Anyscale Services, and now powers roughly 1 billion inferences per day.

At Ray Summit 2026, Aman Choudhary and Akshit Trehan from Coinbase cover the compounding pain points that drove the migration: Ray upgrades that took weeks and left them behind on critical fixes, homogeneous nodes that forced GPU overprovisioning for CPU-only models, and manual deployments that took 30 to 60 minutes per rollout. They show how the move eliminated operational overhead while enabling heterogeneous compute, proper resource isolation, and reliability and performance improvements they couldn't achieve before.

You'll leave with a playbook for migrating production ML serving at scale.

## Description

David Park, Director of Customer Facing MLE at Landing AI, focused his AI Dev 25 x NYC talk in financial services on multimodal document extraction.

Agentic AI is transforming financial services by enabling intelligent, autonomous workflows that combine language, vision, and structured reasoning to extract insights from complex, multimodal documents at scale.

By orchestrating specialized models and decision-making agents, these systems move beyond static OCR and rule-based parsing to handle diverse data types — from regulatory filings and KYC forms to contracts and blueprints — with greater accuracy, context awareness, and adaptability.

This shift unlocks new possibilities for automation, compliance, and real-time decision intelligence across the financial ecosystem.

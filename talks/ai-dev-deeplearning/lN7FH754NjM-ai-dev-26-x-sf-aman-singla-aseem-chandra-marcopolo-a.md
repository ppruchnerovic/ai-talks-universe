## Description

AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor are transforming how we work, but working with real data remains a challenge.

Secure Access: How does AI safely reach your databases, APIs, S3, CRMs, and other systems?

Capability: How can it query, transform, and analyze — not just read?

Context: Which data matters and how should it be used?

Aman Singla & Aseem Chandra showed at AI Dev 26 x San Francisco how MarcoPolo solves this with secure, persistent workspaces where AI can work with your actual data across 50+ systems — without exposing credentials to the model.

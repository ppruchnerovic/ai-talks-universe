## Description

As AI systems move from lab to production, engineers face a new challenge: how to innovate fast while building responsibly.

In this conversation, Miriam Vogel (President & CEO, EqualAI) and Andrew Ng (Founder, DeepLearning.AI) discussed how high-level AI governance principles can translate into actionable practices for developers.

They explored what "trust by design" means in code, how to balance rapid deployment with responsible data management, and where emerging tools and research are making AI governance more automated.

Moderated by Nicholas Thompson (CEO, The Atlantic), this panel offers a practical roadmap for technical teams seeking to build AI systems that are both powerful and principled.

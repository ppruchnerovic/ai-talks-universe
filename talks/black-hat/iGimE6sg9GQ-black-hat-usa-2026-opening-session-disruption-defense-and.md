## Description

Following The White House National Cyber Director's conversation, senior U.S. government officials discuss how the administration's cyber priorities are being taken from policy to practice, and how agencies are adapting America's operational posture in cyberspace. From disrupting cybercriminal networks and defending critical infrastructure to coordinating offensive and defensive operations across government, the panel will explore the evolving threat landscape and how agencies are translating national priorities into operational action.

## Description

As AI reshapes the cyber terrain and presents new attack vectors, traditional threats like ransomware, scams and advanced persistent threats (APTs) remain a significant concern.

In this keynote, Deputy Chief Executive Edward Chen will provide an in-depth look at Singapore's evolving cyber threat landscape. He will also discuss the nation's collective efforts – across government, industry and society – to combat both emerging and enduring cyber threats to Singapore.

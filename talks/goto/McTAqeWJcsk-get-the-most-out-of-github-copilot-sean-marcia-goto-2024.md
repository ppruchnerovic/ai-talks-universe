ABSTRACT
Have you heard the talk and are wondering how GitHub Copilot could become your AI coding pair programmer? This session is all about uncovering the ins and outs of Copilot, making it work for you, and injecting some fun into coding. We're diving into Copilot, showing you how to navigate its features and get the most out of this amazing tool. Whether you're new to Copilot or just looking to sharpen your skills, you'll learn how to leverage its capabilities to speed up your coding, all while keeping your unique style intact.

And here's the best part: we'll put all that learning into action with live coding that’s not just about coding but making a difference. We’re teaming up with an incredible nonprofit dedicated to making the world a better place, using this session to contribute meaningful code that supports their mission. [...]

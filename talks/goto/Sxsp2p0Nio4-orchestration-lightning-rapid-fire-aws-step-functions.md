ABSTRACT
Ready to supercharge your Step Functions game in just 30 minutes?
This rapid-fire session delivers battle-tested patterns and architectural shortcuts that'll make your serverless orchestration sing. We'll blitz through real-world scenarios—when to Map vs. Parallel, how to tame nested workflows, and clever tricks with JSONata and variables that'll slash your costs and boost performance.

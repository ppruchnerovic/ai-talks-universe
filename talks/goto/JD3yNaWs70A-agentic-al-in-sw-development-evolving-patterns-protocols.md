ABSTRACT
In my session, I dive into the evolving world of Agentic AI—a subject close to my heart and the focus of my ongoing blog series, "The Agentic Shift". The story of agentic systems is one of layered growth and discovery. From humble beginnings, we now see sophisticated, self-correcting, and collaborative agents shaping the future of software.

Here are the 7 foundational Agentic AI patterns we explored:
• Conversational Clients
• Contextual Conversational Clients
• Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
• Self-Correcting RAG
• Function Calling Agents
• Agents with Model Context Protocol (MCP)
• Agent-to-Agent Interaction

Each pattern adds a new dimension—intelligence, context, action, and collaboration—enabling AI to evolve from simple helpers to autonomous partners in development. [...]

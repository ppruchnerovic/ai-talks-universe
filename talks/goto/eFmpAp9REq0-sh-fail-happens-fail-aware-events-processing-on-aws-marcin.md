ABSTRACT
A paranoid deep dive into event processing on AWS strongly focused on failures, with a dash of chaos engineering, observability, cost awareness, and best practices for implementing resilient consumers.

Marcin aims to equip you with the knowledge and practical recommendations needed to avoid potential failures and implement best practices to make your systems more reliable. Throughout the talk, he will cover different failure scenarios and provide insights on how to avoid them.

By the end of this session, you will better understand event processing on AWS (with SQS & Kinesis) and be better equipped to make your systems more resilient. [...]

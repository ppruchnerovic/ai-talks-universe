ABSTRACT
What if AI could create an entire explainer video for your new software product—writing the script, generating the video, composing the music, and designing the animations?

This reality may be closer than you think. Join Alex Castrounis to discover The Marvelous Magic of Multimodal AI, where machines can understand and generate text, images, audio, and video. Learn how multimodal AI unlocks new possibilities in human-computer interaction and discover the hidden magic powering tomorrow’s innovations! [...]

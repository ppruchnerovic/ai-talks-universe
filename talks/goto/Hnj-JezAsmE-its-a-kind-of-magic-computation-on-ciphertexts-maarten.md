ABSTRACT
Computing on encrypted data. It sounds like magic, but it’s real and it is particularly useful to be able to gain insights into data distributed over multiple parties in a privacy-preserving way.

In this talk we’ll discuss real practical use-cases of computing on encrypted data, show how that it works with some demos, and explore what is needed in practice to support cross-organisational data collaboration. [...]

ABSTRACT
Discovering ways to seamlessly incorporate generative AI into your existing applications is a key avenue for unlocking new capabilities. However, the process of efficiently integrating these capabilities while ensuring accuracy and safety poses significant challenges.

In our presentation, you learn about techniques such as prompt chaining and human-in-the-loop to enhance the accuracy and safety of the Generative AI model responses. We also learn about integrating generative AI applications with your existing systems through workflows and event-driven architecture, enabling smooth interoperability. [...]

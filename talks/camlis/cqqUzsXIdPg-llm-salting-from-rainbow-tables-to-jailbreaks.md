## Description

Speaker: Tamás Vörös

Author(s): Tamás Vörös; Adarsh Kyadige

Abstract: This work proposes LLM salting, a lightweight defense mechanism that rotates the internal refusal direction of LLMs, rendering previously effective jailbreak prompts (like GCG) ineffective without degrading model utility.

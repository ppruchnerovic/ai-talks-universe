## Description

Introducing CustomerLake: The agentic CDP

CustomerLake brings core CDP capablities together to help marketing and data teams turn customer data into always-on, 1:1 experiences. With CustomerLake, Customer 360, identity resolution, audience building, campaign automation, activation, and personalization are all available in one platform. And with a shared, governed data foundation, marketers can deploy AI agents that help eliminate manual campaign and keep their focus on customers. Learn more about CustomerLake: https://www.databricks.com/blog/introducing-customerlake-agentic-cdp

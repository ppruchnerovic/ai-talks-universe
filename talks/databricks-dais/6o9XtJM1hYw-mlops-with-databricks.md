## Description

Adopting MLOps is getting increasingly important with the rise of AI. A lot of different features are required to do MLOps in large organizations. In the past, you had to implement these features yourself. Luckily, the MLOps space is getting more mature, and end-to-end platforms like Databricks provide most of the features. In this talk, I will walk through the MLOps components and how you can simplify your processes using Databricks.

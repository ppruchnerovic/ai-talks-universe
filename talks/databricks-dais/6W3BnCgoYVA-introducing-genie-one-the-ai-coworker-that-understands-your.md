## Description

Introducing Genie One, the data-smart AI worker.

Genie One goes beyond just answering questions. The newest member of the Genie suite of products, Genie One is an actual agentic co-worker that can produce documents, reports, and artifacts.

It's powered by Genie Ontology, a new self-improving context layer that extracts information from across all a company's data — inside and outside of Databricks — for trusted answers and actions. Genie One helps marketing, finance, sales, and other teams automate and orchestrate work.

Learn more: https://www.databricks.com/blog/introducing-genie-one-genie-ontology-and-genie-agents

## Description

At the 2026 Data + AI Summit, Magesh Bagavathi, Chief Data & AI Officer at PepsiCo, shared how his team is driving AI transformation with Databricks.

Bagavathi spoke with Arsalan Tavakoli-Shiraji, Co-founder and SVP, Field Engineering at Databricks, about how his team is working towards agentifying PepsiCo. PepsiCo leveraged Genie for AI-driven procurement initiatives and plans to expand this use case across all parts of the company.

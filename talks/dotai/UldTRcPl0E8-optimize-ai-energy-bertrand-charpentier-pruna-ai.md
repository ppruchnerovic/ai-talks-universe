Who is Bertrand Charpentier?

Bertrand is the co-founder, President and Chief Scientist Officer at Pruna AI, which makes AI models efficient, sustainable, and accessible.

He co-created open-source packages including `pruna` package with which he compressed thousands of models and `scikit-network` enabling machine learning for graphs.

Talk presented at dotAI 2026 by Christian Keller, Product Lead for AI Research at Meta Superintelligence Labs: https://www.dotai.io/

Christian is Product Lead for AI Research at Meta Superintelligence Labs, working on generative AI, world models, and reasoning. Before Llama 3, Llama 4, Muse, and AI agents, Christian led product for PyTorch, launching PyTorch 2.0 and ExecuTorch. A three-time startup founder and investor, he holds an MBA from Stanford and MS degrees from Georgia Tech and CentraleSupélec.

Talk "Beyond the Chatbox: What Changes When AI Runs Where You Are"

Most AI is built as if the answer is always a language model in a data center, reached through a text box. That breaks the moment your product runs on a laptop, a phone, or a pair of glasses. This talk starts from the constraint instead of the model: how form factor drives model choice, why specialized models solve problems no amount of prompting will, and why open weights make that design space navigable.

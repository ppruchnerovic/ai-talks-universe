Who is Stanislas Polu?

After studying at Polytechnique and Stanford, Stanislas started a company in France that eventually got acquired by Stripe in 2015. He spent 5 years at Stripe working on the API and infrastructure around non-card payment methods. After Stripe he did research for 3 years at OpenAI on large language models mathematical reasoning capabilities. He's now co-founding Dust, building the company OS for smart teams.

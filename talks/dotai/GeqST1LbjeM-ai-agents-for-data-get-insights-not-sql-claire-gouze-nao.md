Who is Claire Gouze?

Claire Gouze is Co-Founder at nao Labs, the AI-code editor for data teams backed by Y Combinator. Previously, she was Head of Data at sunday, where she built the data stack and team. She also worked as a Data Scientist at BCG, specializing in ML applications for retail, including deep learning for personalization and supply chain optimization. As a freelance consultant, she has advised 15+ companies on data architecture and ML strategy.

Who is Fabien Potencier?

Fabien is currently the Chief Technology Officer of Platform.sh. He discovered the web in 1994, at a time when connecting to the Internet was still associated with the harmful strident sounds of a modem. A passionate serial entrepreneur and developer, Fabien founded Sensio in 1998, Symfony in 2005, SensioLabs in 2012, and Blackfire.io in 2018. He is also the creator of several open source projects like Symfony and Twig.

Who is Gael Varoquaux?

Gaël Varoquaux is a research director working on data science at Inria where he leads the Soda team. Varoquaux's research covers fundamentals of AI, statistical learning, natural language processing, causal inference, as well as applications to health. He also creates technology: he co-funded scikit-learn, one of the reference machine-learning toolboxes, and helped build various central tools for data analysis in Python.

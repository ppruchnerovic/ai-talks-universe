Who is Vaibhav Gupta?

Vaibhav is the Founder of Boundary, a Y Combinator startup developing a new programming language (BAML) that makes LLMs both easier and more efficient for developers.

Across nearly a decade in software engineering, Vaibhav has built predictive pipelines at D. E. Shaw, Google, and Microsoft HoloLens.

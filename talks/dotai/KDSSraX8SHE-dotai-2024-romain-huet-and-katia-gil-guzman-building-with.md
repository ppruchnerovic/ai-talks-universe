## Description

Filmed at dotAI 2024 in Paris on October 17-18, 2024 – Europe’s premier AI conference for AI builders, founders, and innovators.

🔍 What’s Inside?
Hear directly from Katia Gil Guzman (Developer Experience Engineer at OpenAI) and Romain Huet (Head of Developer Experience at OpenAI) as they share:
✅ Behind-the-scenes stories from scaling AI adoption—from 1:1 customer solutions to empowering millions of developers
✅ Lessons on building intuitive tools that developers love
✅ The future of AI integration—how OpenAI is shaping the next wave of developer-first innovation

💻 Who Are the Speakers?

Katia Gil Guzman
Katia is a developer experience engineer who moved from working with some of OpenAI's most innovative customers 1:1 as a solutions Architect to doing the 1:many in the Developer Experience team.

Romain Huet
Romain currently leads Developer Experience at OpenAI, inspiring and supporting founders and builders to integrate AI into their applications, and directing the creation of elegant and powerful tools for all developers. Previously, Romain spent 5 years at Stripe, leading product management for the developer platform and overseeing global developer relations. Before Stripe, he helped with the relaunch of Twitter’s developer platform and co-founded Jolicloud in Paris, where he developed a cloud-based OS.

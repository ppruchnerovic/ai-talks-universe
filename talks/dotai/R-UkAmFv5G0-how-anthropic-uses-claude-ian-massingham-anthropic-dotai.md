## Description

Talk presented at dotAI 2026 by Ian Massingham, Head of Applied AI, EMEA Startups at Anthropic: https://www.dotai.io/

Ian Massingham leads Applied AI for startups across EMEA at Anthropic, where he and his team help founders & startup engineering teams build production AI products on Claude. He brings two decades of experience spanning engineering, developer relations, and go-to-market leadership.

Previously, Ian was an early team member at Griptape, a startup focused on generative AI in visual effects, and served as CMO at Aiven, the Finnish open-source data infrastructure unicorn. Prior to that, Ian spent several years at AWS leading developer relations and solutions architecture, helping hundreds of thousands of developers adopt cloud and AI technologies.

Today he works closely with Europe's fastest-growing AI-native startups, advising technical leaders on how to move from prototype to scaled deployment. Ian is a frequent speaker on applied AI, developer ecosystems, and what it takes to build durable companies on frontier models.

Talk: "How Anthropic Uses Claude"

Every developer in the room can use the same models Anthropic does. So why do some teams turn them into measured results while others are still running demos? In this talk, you'll learn how radical new approaches to working with Claude have driven productivity at Anthropic as Ian walks through the five operating principles behind that, with the engineering, security and sales cases to copy into your own organizations.

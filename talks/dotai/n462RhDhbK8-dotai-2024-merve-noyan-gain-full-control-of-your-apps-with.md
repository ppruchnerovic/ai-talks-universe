# dotAI 2024 - Merve Noyan - Gain full control of your apps with Open-Source AI

**Merve Noyan**

`dotAI` · `dot conferences` · `2024` · `25 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n462RhDhbK8) · [Conference site](https://www.dotai.io/)

## Description

Filmed at dotAI on October 17, 2024 in Paris. More about the conference on https://www.dotai.io

People find it hard to browse through open-source models, evaluate them, serve them and so on, so they often resort to closed source models which jeopardize data governance, the underlying code and model changes which breaks the apps and they don't give full control over the model. Merve will go through: how to find an open-source model, evaluate and serve such that people can have full control over their apps.

Who is Merve Noyan?

Merve Noyan is a Machine Learning Advocate Engineer at Hugging Face and Graduate computer vision Researcher, working on zero-shot vision and making large vision and multimodal models more accessible.

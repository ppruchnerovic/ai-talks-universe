Who is Alex Palcuie?

Alex Palcuie is a Member of Technical Staff in Anthropic's AI Reliability Engineering team, focusing on making Claude more reliable through his work on the production inference stack.

Previously, he spent 8 years at Google as a Staff Site Reliability Engineer on Google Compute Engine, most recently working to accelerate the delivery of AI accelerators to customers.

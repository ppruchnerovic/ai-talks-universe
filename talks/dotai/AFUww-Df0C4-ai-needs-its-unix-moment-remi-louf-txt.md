Who is Rémi Louf?

Rémi is the co-founder and CEO of .txt, the only company specializing in structured outputs for large language models. He is also a core developer of the popular open-source library Outlines. He believes the future of AI lies not in complex orchestration frameworks, but in simple, composable primitives that can be combined into sophisticated systems.

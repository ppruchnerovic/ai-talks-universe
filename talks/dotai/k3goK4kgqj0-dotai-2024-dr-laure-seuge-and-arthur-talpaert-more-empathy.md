## Description

Filmed at dotAI on October 17, 2024 in Paris. More about the conference on https://www.dotai.io

Laure and Arthur will announce the Consultation Assistant launch, which revolutionises how healthcare professionals treat their patients.Doctolib is part of the same team as doctors, user-first in particular for AI products. Learn how each of our AI builders have unique empathy for users and their pain points. These choices have a direct impact on how doctors provide better care and have a happier work life.
How would you manage these two challenges? Training models while protecting privacy, and making probabilistic inferences while preserving patient health. This is the demonstration you'll be part of during this exclusive announcement.

Who is Laure Seugé and Arthur Talpaert?

Doctor Laure Seugé is a pediatrician specialized in nephrology and rheumatology. She sees patients at the Children's Institute, Marcel Sembat Clinic in Boulogne Billancourt, and works part-time at Necker-Enfants Malades Hospital in pediatric nephrology (Paris). She is also a Medical Expert at Doctolib to advise the development teams in the product innovation and co-building.

Arthur is Head of AI Product, Consultation Assistant at Doctolib. He manages the design, build and launch of this product in 2024, and advises the Executive Committee on Doctolib's AI and data transformation. Arthur launched and managed tens of AI and data products, incl. at McKinsey Digital and at his own startup, Familiaris MedTech. Before that, Arthur was the Lead Data Scientist for industrial and MarTech products. He holds a PhD in Applied Mathematics, pertaining to the nuclear industry and national labs.

ABOUT THE TALK:
Wesley Faulkner explores the various types of communities and discusses how to determine the most suitable one for your company at various stages of growth. Whether you are looking to double down on your current community or expand to new platforms, Wesley provides the guidance you'll need to make informed decisions about building a strong and effective community.

ABOUT THE SPEAKER:
Wesley Faulkner is a first-generation American, public speaker, and podcaster. He is a founding member of the government transparency group Open Austin and a staunch supporter of racial justice, workplace equity, and neurodiversity. His professional experience spans technology from AMD, Atlassian, Dell, IBM, and MongoDB.

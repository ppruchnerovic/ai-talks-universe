## Description

[2025 - Day 1 - Data Science & Algos] Chenyu Qiu shares insights from building Uber's end-to-end continuous experiment monitoring platform, exploring how to overcome "peeking" problems and detect performance degradation across thousands of A/B experiments. This talk offers valuable perspectives on anytime-valid inference, eliminating noise while preserving true signals, and accelerating data-driven decisions at scale, crucial for teams scaling experimentation or implementing regression-adjusted estimators.

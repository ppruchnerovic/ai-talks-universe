## Description

Embrace the power of LLMs and Software Development Copilots. These innovations are revolutionizing the way we interact with our code, enabling us to directly converse with it, ask for refinements and even pen code from our IDE. However, to optimize this we need to dive deeper into the development process by gathering actionable data between commits. Currently, all this invaluable information is being funneled to the copilot vendors. But, imagine the possibilities if this data was redirected towards your own development team to further enrich your models, processes, and tools.

Continue, a forward-thinking enterprise, is poised to make this a reality by leveraging your feedback and development data for the benefit of all developers and engineer teams. By converting your development narratives into insights, they aim to enhance your coding journey.

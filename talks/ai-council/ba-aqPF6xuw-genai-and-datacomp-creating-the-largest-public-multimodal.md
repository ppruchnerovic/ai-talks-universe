## Description

In this engaging talk, we explore the crucial part universities and the open-source community contribute to the Generative AI ecosystem, with a focus on the herculean task of managing large-scale datasets. The recently published NeurIPS'23 Datacomp paper, which presents the creation of academia's biggest multimodal dataset to date, serves as a case study.

Find out how four emerging trends are reshaping the AI data management landscape:

- Data Cleaning Gone High-tech: With growing datasets, AI models are now stepping in to streamline the tedious process of data cleaning, once a task reserved for junior researchers.
- Data-Centric AI: This discussion highlights a shifting approach from refining AI models based on a set dataset to flexibility in dataset curation, a trend brought to light by our work with Datacomp.
- Legal and Privacy Hurdles: Professor Dimakis discuss the roadblocks in data sharing within the industry due to legal and privacy constraints that affect refining AI models effectively.
- Synthetic Dataset Expansion: The exciting potential of synthetic datasets and how they can augment real datasets with synthetic enhancements.

Join this insightful session to learn how academia continues to break new ground in GenAI.

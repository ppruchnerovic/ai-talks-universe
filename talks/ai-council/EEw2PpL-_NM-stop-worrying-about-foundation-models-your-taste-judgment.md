## Description

[2025 - Day 2 - Foundation Models] Hamel Husain challenges the common misconception that having a powerful foundation model equals having a successful AI product, drawing from his extensive experience building AI systems at companies like GitHub, Airbnb, and DataRobot. This talk examines the crucial gap between model capabilities and product success, exploring the engineering, user experience, and business considerations that determine whether AI applications actually solve real problems for users.

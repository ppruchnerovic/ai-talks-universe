## Description

Immerse yourself in the world of Large Language Models (LLMs) with this in-depth talk from Jonathan Talmi of Cohere. Learn from his team's firsthand experience of training LLMs from scratch, using complex ingestion, preprocessing and distillation pipelines. Uncover the critical role of data quality and get a detailed tour of their unique architecture built to manage petabyte-scale datasets.

Expect to discover the science and practical implications of data for LLMs and get up close with the anatomy of an LLM training data pipeline. Learn about the trials, triumphs and technical details of scaling up to larger dataset sizes without a distributed query engine.

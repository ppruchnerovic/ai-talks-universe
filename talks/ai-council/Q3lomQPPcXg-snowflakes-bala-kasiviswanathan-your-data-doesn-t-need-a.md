## Description

Bala Kasiviswanathan, VP of Developer and AI Experiences at Snowflake, joins ZeroPrime and AI Council Co-founder, Pete Soderling, to unpack the challenges enterprise data teams face as AI demands instant access to trusted data — and what it actually takes to solve them.

Bala has spent his career building developer platforms through three waves of technological change: client-server to cloud, cloud to mobile, and now mobile to AI. At Snowflake, he leads the team behind Coco (Snowflake's AI coding agent) and is helping enterprise teams turn their ever-growing data into trusted, governed action.

In this conversation, Pete and Bala discuss:
- Why the challenge is not accessing data anymore, it’s about actioning data
- Why governance and speed are no longer a tradeoff, and what changed architecturally to make that true
- What happens when marketing, finance, and ops all build from the same governed context — with examples from WHOOP and Thomson Reuters
- Why traditional engineers don't disappear in this shift — they move up the stack
- When everyone has a builder mindset it unlocks organizational productivity

## Description

[2025 - Day 3 - Keynote] Julien Le Dem shares insights from his years of experience building the deconstructed database vision, exploring how the big data ecosystem has evolved into a composable architecture organized around open source standards. This talk offers valuable guidance on leveraging interoperable components like Parquet, Arrow, and Iceberg to avoid vendor lock-in, particularly relevant for architects designing data systems or engineers building specialized query engines.

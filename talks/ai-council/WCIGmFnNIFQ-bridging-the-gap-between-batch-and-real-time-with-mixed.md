## Description

Join Will as he delves into his wealth of experience building mixed-latency data platforms in dynamic startups like Better in this insightful talk. Grasp the intricacies of time horizons, learn how to manage data freshness and tackle pipeline bottlenecks effectively to ensure reliable and consistent data products.

Unraveling the complex challenges of merging batch processing with low-latency data processing, this talk will arm you with the tools to maintain explicit lineage and ensure a unified view of processing jobs across different sources.

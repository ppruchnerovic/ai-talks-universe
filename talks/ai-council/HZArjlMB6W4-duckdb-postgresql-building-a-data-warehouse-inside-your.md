## Description

[2025 - Day 3 - Databases] Marco Slot shares insights from combining transactional and analytical database capabilities, exploring how embedding DuckDB into PostgreSQL creates a unified platform for both OLTP and OLAP workloads. For architects building modern data platforms or simplifying complex data stacks, this talk offers valuable perspectives on the motivations, benefits, and challenges of fusing supremely extensible OLTP with supremely embeddable OLAP databases.

## Description

[2025 - Day 1 - Data Science & Algos] Wenjing Zheng shares insights from connecting A/B test results to real-world business decisions at Roblox, exploring causal inference methods that bridge the gap between clean experimental measures and messy strategic choices. Whether you're attributing business growth to product launches or generalizing experiment results to broader populations, this talk offers valuable perspectives on building a common measurement language that connects local experimental results to global business impact.

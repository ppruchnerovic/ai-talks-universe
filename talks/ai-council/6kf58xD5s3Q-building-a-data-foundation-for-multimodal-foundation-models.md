## Description

[2025 - Day 2 - Foundation Models] Ethan Rosenthal shares insights from building a petabyte-scale multimodal feature lakehouse at Runway, exploring the unique requirements of multimodal data curation beyond conventional machine learning systems. This talk offers valuable perspectives on balancing data quality with quantity and building systems that support both analytical querying and high-performance feature serving for foundation model training, crucial for engineers managing heterogeneous data or supporting large-scale distributed training jobs.

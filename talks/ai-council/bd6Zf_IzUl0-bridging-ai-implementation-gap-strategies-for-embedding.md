## Description

[2025 - Day 3 - AI & Data Culture] Josh Curl shares insights from embedding data teams within business units to bridge the critical gap between data capabilities and business reality, exploring how successful AI implementations depend on deep integration rather than traditional service provider models. For data leaders seeking to move from analytics to actions, this talk offers valuable perspectives on creating virtuous cycles where data teams gain domain knowledge while business professionals improve data facility, based on real-world AI Decisioning implementations.

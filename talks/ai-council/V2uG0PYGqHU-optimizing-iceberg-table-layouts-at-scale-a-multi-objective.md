## Description

[2025 - Day 1 - Data Eng & Infra] Sumedh Sakdeo and Jesus Camacho Rodríguez share insights from managing data layout for 100K+ Iceberg tables, exploring automated reorganization as a multi-objective optimization problem. This talk offers valuable perspectives on balancing storage efficiency, minimizing costs, and maximizing impact through intelligent table scoring and ranking within reorganization budgets, particularly valuable for engineers scaling table management or optimizing query performance.

## Description

Listen in for an enlightening talk on Data Mesh - a modern approach to data sharing by Zhamak Dehghani, the person who introduced the Data Mesh concept at a 2019 O'Reilly conference. As a recognized and influential thought leader in the field, Zhamak has made significant strides in the realm of distributed systems, event-driven architecture and streaming platforms.

Throughout this talk, Zhamak aims to simplify Data Mesh from a technical standpoint, shedding light on its current variations, trade-offs and peculiarities. She will also delve into its influence on the creation of NextData's "nxd os," a cutting-edge Data Mesh operating system.

## Description

ABOUT THE TALK (https://www.datacouncil.ai/talks/meet-dbt-the-data-transformation-tool-used-by-jetblue-gitlab-wistia-and-away)

dbt is an open source data transformation tool maintained by Fishtown Analytics. In this session, Fishtown Analytics product manager, Jeremy Cohen, will introduce attendees to dbt and why it is used (and loved) by thousands of companies. The workshop will include a presentation on the dbt viewpoint and key functionality around data modeling, testing, and documentation.

ABOUT THE SPEAKER

Jeremy Cohen is a data professional with Fishtown Analytics. He has worked as an analyst, analytics engineer, course instructor, and most recently Associate Product Manager for dbt Core.

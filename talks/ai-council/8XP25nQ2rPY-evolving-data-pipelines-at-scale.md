## Description

Stepping into the world of data pipeline development can feel daunting with lagging tooling and complex environment setups. But what if there was a simpler, more cost-effective solution? Enter SQLMesh – an open-source project shaped by industry giants like Airbnb, Apple, Google and Netflix.

This video aims to unveil the challenges that data practitioners face today and how SQLMesh tackles them head-on. Explore concepts like virtual data environments, automatic change categorization, continuous testing and accurate change previews.

## Description

Learn how open source Metaflow is revolutionizing ML systems in this talk, featuring real-life examples from companies like Netflix, 23andMe and Goldman Sachs. As Generative AI makes waves in the industry, understand how it’s transforming existing applications and catalyzing entirely fresh user experiences. Discover how Metaflow facilitates the building of novel, AI-powered systems that need complex data engineering, model training and more.

This in-depth talk offers a panoramic view of changes compared to older ML stacks, with a keen focus on the rising compute needs and insights from Outerbounds' hands-on experiences with large-scale AI use cases in sectors like healthcare, finance and entertainment.

Whether you're a data scientist, an engineer or an AI enthusiast, this video offers a comprehensive look into the concrete benefits of Metaflow.

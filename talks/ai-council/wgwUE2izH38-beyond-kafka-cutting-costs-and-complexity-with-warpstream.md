## Description

Struggling with Apache Kafka in the cloud? Finding it costly and complex to handle? WarpStream is working to solve this. Discover how implementing streaming storage on platforms like Amazon S3 can alleviate these challenges in this workshop. WarpStream is constructed with Apache Kafka protocol-compatible interface, specifically designed to simplify your heavy workload.

Watch as Ryan Worl puts WarpStream to the test live, handling hundreds of MiB/s of ingestion and compare the total cost of ownership with open-source Apache Kafka. Plus, gain insights into avoiding common mistakes while managing Kafka.

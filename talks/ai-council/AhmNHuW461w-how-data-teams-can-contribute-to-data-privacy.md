## Description

In this video, Josh Schwartz explores how global data management regulations like Europe’s GDPR and California’s CPRA impact our digital landscape. Uncover new techniques, protocols and privacy rights, while dispelling common misconceptions in data engineering. Gain insight into advanced yet pragmatic privacy techniques like local computation and differential privacy through practical examples and real-life stories.

By the end of this video, you'll have a more informed perspective on incorporating privacy law considerations into your data practices, along with valuable resources for crafting privacy-aware systems.

## Description

What does it actually take to ship quality code when hundreds of AI agents are doing the work?
Full article here: https://petesoder.substack.com/p/ai-agents-are-shipping-your-code

Pete Soderling sits down with Eno Reyes, Co-founder & CTO of Factory, to get into the real challenges of scaling agentic development — from agent readiness and spec-driven workflows to developer trust and silent failure modes in data infrastructure.

In this conversation: why code quality at scale is a process problem as much as a tooling one, what Factory learned going from zero to 10,000 engineers at one company in three months, and why the spec conversation needs to happen before anyone writes a line of code.

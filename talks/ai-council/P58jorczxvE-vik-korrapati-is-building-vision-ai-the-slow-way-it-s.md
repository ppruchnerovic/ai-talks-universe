## Description

The AI industry rewards breakthroughs. Vik Korrapati is more interested in the next 2% improvement.

In this conversation with Pete Soderling ahead of AI Council 2026, Vik — CTO of open-source vision AI model Moondream — makes the case that compounding engineering gains beat research leaps for teams actually trying to ship.

They cover vision AI use cases, inference design, hallucination, explainability, and why the real unsolved problem in AI isn't the model — it's everything around it.

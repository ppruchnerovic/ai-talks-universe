[2025 - Day 2 - Workshops] Ori Soen and Dadi Atar share insights from evolving the Analytics Development Lifecycle to address stakeholder marginalization and bottleneck creation, exploring how cutting-edge data teams migrate analysis work to data warehouses without compromising governance. For organizations struggling with ADLC's unintended consequences, this session offers valuable perspectives on restoring data access for analysts, product managers, and data scientists while maintaining quality standards.

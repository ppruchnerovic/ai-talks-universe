## Description

[2026 - DAY 3 - AI SECURITY & SAFETY] Giving employees AI tools doesn't make a company AI native. Trail of Bits founder and CEO Dan Guido explains the difference — and the operating system his 150-person security firm built to actually get there.

Dan covers the psychological biases that make experts resist AI, the system Trail of Bits built to overcome them, and the results: a jump from 15 to 200 bugs found per week on some engagements. He also gets into what AI-discoverable vulnerabilities mean for security as an industry, and why the real bottleneck has shifted from finding bugs to judging which ones actually matter.

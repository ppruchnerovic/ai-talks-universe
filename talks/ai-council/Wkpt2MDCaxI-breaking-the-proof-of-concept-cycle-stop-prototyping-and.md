## Description

[2026 - DAY 3 - APPLIED AI] Most organizations are experimenting with AI at work. Proof of concepts are thriving in small-scale silos, but how can you scale those wins across an enterprise? This talk discusses the symptoms that trap teams in the cycle of perpetual prototyping and provides a practical framework for breaking through common technical, organizational, and cultural barriers to scale. Learn how to plan for production from day one and turn your AI experiments into solutions that stick.

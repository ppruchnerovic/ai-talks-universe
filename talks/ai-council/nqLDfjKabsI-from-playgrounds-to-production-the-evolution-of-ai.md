## Description

Watch as Kenny takes us on a fascinating journey through Coda's meticulous process of crafting an AI evaluation system from scratch. Gain exclusive insights into their journey, from initial tests using OpenAI's playground to seamlessly integrating evaluations into Coda documents and finally, leveraging a multitude of vendors for comprehensive evaluations.

Discover the critical importance of a robust benchmark dataset, the nuances of manual and automated evaluations and the intricacies of application code integration. Whether you're a startup engineer, a product developer or simply exploring AI feature development, this deep-dive into Coda's real-world applications offers invaluable lessons and practical strategies for your own AI endeavors.

[2025 - Day 1 - Keynote] Denis Yarats, Sharon Zhou, Michele Catasta, and Dr. Joseph Gonzalez share insights from their pioneering work in LLM system design, exploring the cutting edge from retrieval-augmented generation to agentic search.

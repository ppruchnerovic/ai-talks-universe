## Description

Unlock the mysteries of Machine Learning Operations as Elijah Ben Izzy dissects the two-phase process involved in deploying ML models at Stitch Fix. From building the ML model to transitioning it into production, the open-source library Hamilton might be the tool you need. This session provides:

1. Insight into the unique challenges encountered at Stitch Fix with over 100 data scientists/MLEs on board
2. Practical solutions to these problems through the powerful tool, Hamilton
3. A comparison of Hamilton with other industry tools and trends.

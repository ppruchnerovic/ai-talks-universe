## Description

In this thought-provoking talk, Miguel Paredes, PhD, delves into the concept of general purpose technologies (GPTs) and their profound impact on society, with a particular focus on AI. He explores AI's potential to drive innovation and reshape industries, contrasting productivity gains with opportunities for creating new value. Drawing from insights shared by influential figures like Bill Gates, Elon Musk and Andrew Ng, Miguel prompts the audience to ponder whether AI represents a hype or an inflection point.

He envisions a future where AI is consumed as a service and highlights the importance of data and human expertise in leveraging AI effectively. Miguel also discusses the implications of AI on the labor market and emphasizes the need to focus on augmentation and creation rather than mere imitation. Overall, his talk provides a compelling glimpse into the transformative power of AI and the opportunities it presents for individuals and organizations alike.

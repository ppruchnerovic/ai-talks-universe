## Description

Three groundbreaking startups took the stage at Snowflake Summit's Dev Day to compete for investments from Snowflake Ventures, marketing with Snowflake, and the opportunity to ring the NYSE bell.

Airrived presented its Agentic OS that democratizes AI across enterprise operations. LGND AI showcased Large Earth Observation Models that make 800+ petabytes of satellite imagery queryable through natural language. Twine Security demonstrated Alex, an AI digital employee that handles identity and access management tasks end-to-end.

After intense deliberation, judges Benoit Dageville, Denise Persson, John Herrick, and John Barat selected LGND AI as the grand prize winner for their ambitious mission to make Earth understandable to AI. Watch the complete finale to see all three pitches, judge Q&A sessions, and the moment the winner was announced. The 2027 Startup Challenge kicks off this fall at the New York Stock Exchange.

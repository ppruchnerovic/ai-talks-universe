## Description

Snowflake Summit got off to an incredible start with an opening keynote featuring a conversation between Snowflake CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman. The conversation was moderated by Sarah Guo, founder and managing partner of Conviction.  Lynn Martin, President of the NYSE, also joined Sridhar Ramaswamy for a compelling keynote conversation, and members of the Snowflake team shared exciting new product announcements. Rounding out the event was the news that Snowflake has been named the official data collaboration partner of the Los Angeles U.S. Olympic Games.

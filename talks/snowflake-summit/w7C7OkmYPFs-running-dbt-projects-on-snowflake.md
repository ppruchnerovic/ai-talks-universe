## Description

A favorite among data teams, dbt offers the ability to bring software engineering best practices and improved efficiency in SQL and Snowpark data transformation workflows right into Snowflake. Join Charlie Hammond for a quick tour showcasing the new dbt Projects on Snowflake (in public preview now).

Learn how to effortlessly:
--Compile and visualize your DAG
--Run all your dbt models
--Perform test runs for data quality
--Deploy and schedule your data pipelines

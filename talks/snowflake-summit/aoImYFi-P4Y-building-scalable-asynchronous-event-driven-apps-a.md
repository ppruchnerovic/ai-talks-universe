## Description

This session from Summit 2025 delves into the challenges of and best practices for building asynchronous event-driven applications. We will explore how Snowpipe Streaming can be leveraged to construct hybrid event-driven architectures that integrate async and sync workflows. It also covers data loss, reconciliation, and solving the "lost ownership scenario" in micro-services. Finally, it addresses scalability’s impact on databases, the challenge of choosing between analytical and NoSQL options, multi-tenancy, cost and code optimizations, and easing the transition from batch to distributed streaming for engineers.

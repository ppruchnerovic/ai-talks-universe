## Description

Tune in to watch what happened when the three finalists in this year's Snowflake Startup Challenge came together on the last day of Snowflake Summit to participate in the final round of competition. Their task was to pitch their "Powered By Snowflake" product to a panel of four judges charged with selecting the winner. It all took place before a live audience, and the prize at stake was a chance to win up to $1 million in funding from Snowflake Ventures and gain significant market exposure. The three finalists stepping up to the challenge were DeepTempo, which pitched a cybersecurity product that utilizes deep learning; LumiLinks, which showcased its centralized AI-driven applications for extracting cost-saving insights from diverse data sets; and Winning Variant, which presented a new type of split-testing platform. And the winner was....spoiler alert ... Lumilinks! Watch the video to find out why.

Snowflake Summit 2026 kicked off with an opening keynote on The Agentic Enterprise — featuring a conversation between Snowflake CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy and Anthropic Co-Founder and President Daniela Amodei on how AI agents are reshaping how enterprises work.

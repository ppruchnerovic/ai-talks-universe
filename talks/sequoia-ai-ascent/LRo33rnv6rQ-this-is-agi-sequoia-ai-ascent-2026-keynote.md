## Description

Sequoia Capital partners Pat Grady, Sonya Huang, and Konstantine Buhler make the case that the AI wave isn't just a revolution in communication like the internet or mobile, but a revolution in computation. Not faster horses, but cars—and the cars have finally arrived. They lay out why long-horizon agents are reshaping every layer of work, why AI is about to do to cognitive work what the Industrial Revolution did to manual labor, and why whatever you could imagine building over the next 100 years is now possible in 100 days.

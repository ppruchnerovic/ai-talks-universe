## Description

Sam Altman reflects on OpenAI’s evolution from a 14-person research lab to a dominant AI platform. He envisions transforming ChatGPT into a deeply personal AI service that remembers your entire life's context—from conversations to emails—while working seamlessly across all services. Drawing from how younger users already treat AI as an operating system rather than just a search tool, Sam describes a future where a single, efficient model can reason across your full history without retraining, fundamentally changing how we interact with technology in our daily lives.

## Description

A software factory isn't a product you buy, it's a discipline: moving AI coding agents off individual laptops and into shared, automated, measurable workflows. Massimo Re Ferre, Senior Principal Product Manager at AWS working on Kiro, shares what Amazon and its enterprise customers are learning about cost control, standardization and trust on the road from coding tools to coding platforms. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– From in the loop to on the loop, and what comes next
– Three shifts: agents moving to the cloud, more automation, and agents as team members
– Mapping the software factory onto the whole SDLC
– The 80/20 rule of AI coding spend, and how to tell if it's worth it
– Why trust in a software factory starts with intent, not tests
– Lessons from the Kiro team's own software factory

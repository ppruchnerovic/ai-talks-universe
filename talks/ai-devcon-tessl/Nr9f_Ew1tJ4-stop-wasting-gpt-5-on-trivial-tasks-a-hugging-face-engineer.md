## Description

Stop wasting frontier AI on trivial tasks.

Shaun Smith, MCP maintainer and AI engineer at Hugging Face, drops a blunt take most people in the industry won't say out loud: we're throwing Claude, GPT-5, and other frontier models at problems they're massively overkill for — and it's a waste of compute, money, tokens, and water.
In this talk, Shaun walks through how AI agents and harnesses have evolved over the last 18 months, why open-weight models are now genuinely accessible for fine-tuning and self-hosting, and how skills (yes, just markdown files) are quietly becoming one of the most powerful levers in the AI stack.

What's covered:

How MCP and agentic tooling has simplified dramatically

Why your harness getting simpler is actually a sign models are getting smarter

How Hugging Face uses a 45-token tool footprint to unlock thousands of model capabilities

Fine-tuning for ~$3 of compute — and why you should try it

The case for open-weight models and owning your own AI infrastructure
Why the next 12–18 months need to be about using AI, not just building tools to build more tools

The soundbite everyone's talking about → 25:42

Shaun Smith works on MCP agents and open source projects at Hugging Face. He's an MCP maintainer and community moderator, and spends most of his time on transports and agent evaluation pipelines.

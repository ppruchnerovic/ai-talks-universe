## Description

Startups and industry giants are not in the same ring. They operate in fundamentally different spaces, choosing distinct strategies for innovation. This dynamic drives how features develop, leading to breakthroughs or sustaining existing strengths. Big corporations secure massive revenues in a landscape where today's startups can become tomorrow's titans. It's a game of strategy and scale.

Thomas Dohmke brings a unique perspective with his experience as GitHub's former CEO, now navigating the startup landscape. He discusses the dichotomy between startups and large enterprises, emphasizing how both contribute to the continuous evolution of developer tools. With a keen focus on the future, Thomas highlights the significance of mastering continuous learning for developers to adapt and succeed.

What we cover:
• why startups and corporations compete differently
• what developer skills will be essential in the future
• how AI impacts the development process
• what role agent collaboration plays in modern development
• how companies can use vast resources for startup growth

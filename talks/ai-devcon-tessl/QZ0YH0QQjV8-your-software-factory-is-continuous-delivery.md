## Description

Agents can generate code far faster than anyone can review it, and big batches are the enemy of a good night's sleep. Bryan Finster, Principal Architect at ACI Worldwide, argues that a software factory is really continuous delivery: small batches, a clear definition of done, and an automated code review cycle that runs deterministic checks first. He shares how he's applying it at a 50-year-old payments company. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why constant rework and giant PRs are delivery problems
– Lessons from moving from quarterly to two-week delivery
– Why reviewing agent code at human speed doesn't scale
– An automated code review cycle: deterministic checks first
– What the human owns: definition of done, quality controls and outcomes
– Applying it at a 50-year-old payments company

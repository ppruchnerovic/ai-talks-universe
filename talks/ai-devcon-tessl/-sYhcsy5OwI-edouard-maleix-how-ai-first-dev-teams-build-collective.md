## Description

What if your AI coding agents not only fixed bugs but also learned and shared solutions across the team? It sounds impossible, yet a framework for achieving collective intelligence within dev teams is becoming a reality. Transforming isolated agent interventions into communal knowledge rewrites the rules of team collaboration. Each mistake, captured and compared with past incidents, compounds into trusted, reusable knowledge. Welcome to a world where agents don't just work; they teach and learn.

Edouard Maleix, a French consultant, shares his experience in turning development friction into collaborative intelligence at AI DevCon London 2026. By building an open source infrastructure, he aims to improve the way teams manage AI coding agents, ensuring mistakes lead to growth, not just fixes. His work with tech leads and CTOs focuses on authentication, application performance, and development productivity challenges. In his talk, Edouard explores how giving agents an identity and building a dynamic 'knowledge factory' can help development teams capture valuable lessons that don't just evaporate post-session.

What we cover:
• How can development teams turn agent mistakes into collective intelligence?
• The importance of giving AI agents their own identities and signed commits
• Constructing a 'knowledge factory' to capture and reuse coding lessons
• Converting session incidents into reusable guidance and practices
• Avoiding the pitfalls of static documentation in evolving codebases
• Establishing a diary system as a repository for agent decision-making

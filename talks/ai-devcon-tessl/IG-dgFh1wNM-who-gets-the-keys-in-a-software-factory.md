## Description

In a software factory, neither the coding agent nor the human should hold the keys to production. The deployment pipeline should. Paul Duvall, author of Continuous Integration and founder of Redacted Ventures, explains why judgment belongs upstream in intent and policy, which agent-specific checks to add, and how evidence, not human sign-off on diffs, should authorize every deploy. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why judgment belongs upstream in intent, policy and architecture
– What to keep from your existing deployment pipeline
– Agent-specific risks: prompt injection, poisoned context and slopsquatting
– Evidence as the control: mutation scores, attestations and provenance
– Lessons from Clinejection, Amazon Q Developer and s1ngularity
– Protecting the pipeline that holds the keys in your software factory

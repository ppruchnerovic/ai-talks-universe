## Description

The dark software factory imagines a future where agents write and read all the code and nobody looks again. Ray Myers, founder of The Coding Agency, argues that it fails both in theory and in practice. Drawing on lessons from manufacturing, the Toyota Production System and The Goal, he explains why layered defense still needs inspection, what successful dark-factory-adjacent teams actually do, and why the factory conversation is still worth having. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– What the dark software factory claims
– How the idea spread, and what the evidence says
– Lessons from manufacturing: Toyota and The Goal
– Why layered defense still needs inspection
– What dark-factory-adjacent successes really do
– Why the factory conversation is still worth having

## Description

Nothing accelerates product roadmaps like credible competition.

In this episode of AI Native Dev, Thomas Dohmke, former CEO of GitHub, joins the conversation to unpack the return of competition to developer tooling in the age of AI and why that matters more than who ultimately wins.

With Guy Podjarny he explores:

• how multi-model choice inside Copilot emerged directly from market pressure
• why platform companies optimise for ecosystems, not individual tools
• where AI-native startups inevitably collide with hyperscalers

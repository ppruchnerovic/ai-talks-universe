## Description

Every new model release is a chance to delete, not just add. Nick Carmont, Applied AI at Anthropic, helps AI-native startups get the most out of each new Claude model, and shares why leaner harnesses often perform better: newer models bake in behaviors like verification, duplicated or contradictory instructions waste tokens, and the Claude Code team cut its system prompt by over 80%. He walks through an un-harnessing loop and explains why the person running your evals matters more than ever. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– The model, the harness or the eval: diagnosing what's holding you back
– How newer models bake in behaviors like verification
– Why the Claude Code team cut its system prompt by over 80%
– Duplicated and contradictory instructions, and progressive disclosure
– The un-harnessing SDLC loop
– Why the person running your evals is so important

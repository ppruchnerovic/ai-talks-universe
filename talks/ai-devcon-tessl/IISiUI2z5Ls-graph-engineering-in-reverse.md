## Description

Your company brain gives confident answers to business questions, but a real company has no answer key, so how do you know they're right? Benjamin Gruenbaum, Founding Engineer at Eon, runs graph engineering in reverse: start with hard questions from human experts, plant the answers in a graph, dirty it up like real data, and project it into synthetic companies served by digital twins of systems like Salesforce and SharePoint. He explains how sketching real production data keeps it realistic, and how it enables evals and post-training. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why company brains are so hard to evaluate
– Running the company brain pipeline in reverse
– Planting answers, then dirtying the data like the real world
– Digital twins of Salesforce, SharePoint and other SaaS systems
– Sketching real production data to keep synthetic data realistic
– Using synthetic companies for evals, post-training and skill optimization

## Description

Agent skills are the new code. As AI development evolves, treating skills with the rigor traditionally reserved for code is no longer optional. Modern AI operations require skills to be crafted, tested, versioned, and maintained with the same diligence, ensuring that AI doesn't drift but reliably ships the right outcomes. It's a shift that defines the future of AI development: from code to intent-driven instructions. Agent skills now share the spotlight as integral components in the software stack, much like traditional code, giving rise to a new form of technical debt if neglected. It’s not just about crafting tools; it’s about transforming intent into executable actions efficiently and repeatedly.

Guy Podjarny, CEO and founder of Tessl, elucidates this critical transformation in the world of AI-native development. With a storied background in software development at Tessl, Guy provides an authoritative insight into why agent skills are pivotal in the current landscape. He discusses the evolving AI development stack and emphasizes the importance of harnesses, tools, and context in managing AI operations, all aimed at elevating AI development to the next level.

What we cover:
• Why agent skills are the new code and why they require rigorous attention
• How do tools, context, and harnesses integrate into the AI development stack?
• The role of harnesses in controlling AI operations and ensuring repeatability
• What are the three layers of the new AI development stack?
• How composing skills and tools can heighten AI efficiency
• Why context in AI is as crucial as the code itself

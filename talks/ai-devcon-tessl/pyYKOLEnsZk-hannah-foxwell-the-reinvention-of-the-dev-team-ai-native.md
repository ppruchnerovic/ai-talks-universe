## Description

The game has changed in software development, and traditional dev teams struggle to keep up. Two years ago, the old balance of roles seemed perfect: Product Managers, Designers, Engineering Managers, and developers. Today, that structure shows its cracks. Team roles are evolving, reshaped by AI-driven velocity and new patterns for success.

Hannah Foxwell, an independent advisor and creator of "AI for the Rest of Us," shares her insights on this transformation. With a wealth of experience in agentic software development, she reveals what works, what doesn't, and which principles remain vital. Hannah's approach is about refocusing on outcomes and rethinking team composition in light of accelerated development capabilities.

What we cover:
• How have AI advancements reshaped traditional dev team roles?
• The emerging role of agentic software development and its impact
• The benefits of prototyping at lower costs and faster speeds
• Alternative team compositions to handle increased development velocity
• Why the typical team of six to eight engineers and one PM feels outdated
• Insights from experimentation with different team structures at MBIM

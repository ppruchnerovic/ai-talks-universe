## Description

AI agents aren't stupid, they're just blind, and the fix is context engineering at the data layer, not a smarter model. Tom Scott, who runs Streambased, explains why wiring agents to every MCP server leads to data blind spots, runaway warehouse costs and confidently wrong answers.

Recorded at the AI Native Dev September meetup, this talk makes the case for designing data around context rather than around one business question at a time.

What we cover:
– The four data blind spots that lead agents to confidently wrong answers
– RAG vs MCP vs skills: how agents get data today, and where each one breaks
– Why the medallion architecture was built for a different world
– Why LLMs make poor data engineers, and what that does to your agent ROI
– Context engineering at the data layer with federation and logical views
– Temporal consistency: stitching Kafka and Iceberg into one view

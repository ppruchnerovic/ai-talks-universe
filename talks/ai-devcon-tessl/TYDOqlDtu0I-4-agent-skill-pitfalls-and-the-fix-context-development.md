## Description

A smaller Anthropic model, surprisingly, matches the performance of frontier models when given the right context. This revelation, found in Baptiste Fernandez's exploration of nine models across eleven Node.js agent skills, highlights the shift from coding to mastering context as the real bottleneck in AI development. The community remains divided, exposing a common struggle: optimizing context for agent efficacy.

Baptiste Fernandez, a developer advocate at Tessl, dives deep into the Context Development Lifecycle, drawing from his diverse background in economics and tech from Uber to TikTok. He brings real-world insights into generating, evaluating, and distributing context to enhance AI agent performance, making his perspective invaluable to any team grappling with agent skill pitfalls.

What we cover:
• Why the bottleneck in AI development has shifted from code to context
• What is the Context Development Lifecycle and why is it essential?
• The four problems every engineering team faces when building with agents
• Common agent skill pitfalls like vague descriptions and context bloat
• How to evaluate skills structurally and through pressure-testing scenarios
• How can you enable your team at individual, manager, and VP levels?

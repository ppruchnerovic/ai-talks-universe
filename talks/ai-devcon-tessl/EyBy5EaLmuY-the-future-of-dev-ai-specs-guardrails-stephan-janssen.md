Stephan Janssen, founder of one of the world’s most influential developer conferences, Devoxx, joins Simon Maple to reveal how emerging software practices are shaping the next era of tech.

• Vibe coding removes old setups and builds bottlenecks, turning months of configuration into weeks, speeding idea-to-prototype.
• how AI builds impressive UIs, but it has no intuition for security. Unguarded routes and exposed keys are common side effects.
• why "integration testing” is increasingly critical in AI-assisted development.

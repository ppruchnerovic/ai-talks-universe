## Description

Are your AI agents brilliant one moment and frustratingly unreliable the next?

The biggest hurdle to achieving reliable, multi-agent workflows is the way information is being structured and delivered to the model.

Guy Podjarny, Founder at Tessl, unpacks why simply dumping data into a massive context window actually hurts your agents' performance. Guy introduces the essential framework of Context Engineering, showing how to structure knowledge to ensure agents pay attention to what matters most.

On the docket:
• Why bigger context windows create diminishing returns
• How separating Rules from Knowledge improves reliability
• Why prompting alone is obsolete and how structured Tiles fix it
• How to build agents that reliably self-search and fetch what they need

## Description

Why do developers grapple with the term artificial intelligence? It's because AI isn't about intelligence but accelerated inference based on previous data. This perspective sheds light on the limitations of AI and its reliance on pre-existing flawed code. AI stands for Accelerated Inference, and this reframing guides developers in making informed decisions when leveraging AI tools.

Venkat Subramaniam, a veteran programmer with over 40 years of experience and the co-founder of Arc of AI, joins the AI Native Dev Podcast to bring clarity to the AI landscape. With his background as an author and educator, Venkat explains why understanding AI as accelerated inference changes the stakes for developers looking to introduce AI responsibly and effectively into their workflows.

What we cover:
• Why 'Accelerated Inference' is a more honest name for AI
• How does AI generate bad code?
• Accountability, lawsuits, and professional responsibility in the AI era
• The widening gap between junior and senior developers due to AI
• What is the one question to ask before every feature decision?

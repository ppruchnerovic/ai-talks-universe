## Description

Agent architectures evolve rapidly, yet as long as you're using general-purpose frontier models, your context—the runbooks, engineering standards, and institutional knowledge—remains essential. That's where investment pays off because context compounds over time, transforming into a true strategic advantage. Cisco Customer Experience has created context pipelines that repurpose the same knowledge base articles for both support engineers and autonomous agents. The results? Skills that proactively pre-triage customer cases, cutting down learning curves drastically.

John Groetzinger, a principal engineer at Cisco, shares the strategies behind these context pipelines. With over 14 years at Cisco, John has transitioned from tackling high-pressure technical support to spearheading AI-driven projects. His insights reveal how converting curated knowledge into actionable skills can revolutionize workflows, making 'is this a skill?' a reflexive question throughout the engineering process.

What we cover:
• Building context pipelines for consistent knowledge distribution
• How do context pipelines convert knowledge into skills effectively?
• The role of evals in improving context management
• Balancing medium and high-end models for cost efficiency
• Case study: transforming human-maintained content for agent use

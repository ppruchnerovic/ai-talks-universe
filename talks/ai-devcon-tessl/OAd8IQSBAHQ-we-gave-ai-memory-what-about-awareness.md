## Description

Your coding agent remembers every turn of the conversation, yet still has no idea who it's working with. Pari Patel, Founder & CEO of Zone, which is building earbuds that read brain signals, argues that agents need awareness as well as memory: sensitivity to what the person needs right now. She shows how to add a user state object to your harness, write handoffs that cost less than doing the work, pace interruptions, measure it all with evals, and what in-ear brain sensing could add. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Memory answers what happened; awareness answers what the person needs now
– Three ways agents fail their users: depth, pacing and handoffs
– Adding a user state object to your harness
– Handoffs that cost the human less than doing the work
– Pacing rules and evals for awareness
– What in-ear brain sensing could add, and the non-negotiables

## Description

AI is making individuals faster, but few companies can point to org-wide returns. Alan Braun, VP of Product for Ecosystem, AI & Developer Platforms at Atlassian, argues that AI outcomes equal context times intelligence, and that the missing piece is connected context across teams, tools and agents. He shows how Atlassian's Teamwork Graph links knowledge, work, code, people and data, shares lessons from building MCP tools, and demos a coding agent planning with and without connected context. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why individual AI gains aren't adding up to org-wide returns
– AI outcomes = context × intelligence
– Work spans teams, tools and agents, and the bottleneck has moved
– Context as a graph: seven layers of connected context
– Lessons from building MCP tools around jobs to be done
– Demo: Claude Code with and without connected context

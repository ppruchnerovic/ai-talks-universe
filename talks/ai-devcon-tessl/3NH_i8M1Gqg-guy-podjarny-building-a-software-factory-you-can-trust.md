## Description

Software development is moving from building the car to building the factory, but concerns about quality, cost and safety hold many teams back. In this opening keynote, Guy Podjarny, Founder & CEO of Tessl, lays out a path to a software factory you can trust: skills that define what good looks like, self-improving loops that learn from what they observe, and factories built around business outcomes. He shares real data from Tessl's own factory, from automated vulnerability remediation to cutting cost per PR. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why quality, cost and safety concerns hold back software factories
– Delegating to agents like a good manager: skills to loops to factory
– Automated vulnerability remediation with skills
– Verifiers that set limits on an agent's empowerment
– Self-improving loops, and cutting cost per PR from $75 to $13
– From shared skills to a factory per product

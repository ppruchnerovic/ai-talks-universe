## Description

Glinster is a musical instrument for Apple Vision Pro, iPhone and iPad that you play with your bare hands, built in two months by one person and Claude Code running in auto mode. In this closing keynote, Geert Bevin, Director of Software Development at Moog Music, shares what happened when he deliberately read almost none of the code: how proven engineering processes let him stay in the creative zone, where agents failed to spot their own failures, the five rules that made it work, and a live Vision Pro demo. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– From a long-held idea to an instrument you play with your bare hands
– Building with Claude Code in auto mode while barely reading the code
– Keeping tests, CI/CD and human testers as the safeguards
– Acting as the product designer, and being free to throw code away
– When agents can't see their own failures
– Five rules that made it work, plus a live Vision Pro demo

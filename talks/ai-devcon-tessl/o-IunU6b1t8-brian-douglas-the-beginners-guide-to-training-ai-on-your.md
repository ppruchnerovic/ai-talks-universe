## Description

Every AI agent interaction is potential training gold. But most teams simply discard it. Tapes steps in as an open source telemetry proxy, intercepting all API interactions without the hassle of instrumentation, transforming every session into a valuable training dataset.

Brian Douglas, formerly of GitHub and now at Paper Compute, explores this innovative approach. He reveals how to intercept and utilize AI conversations to create a self-healing infrastructure loop. With a background in open source development, Brian shares his practical insights on embedding skills into models without relying on external training sets.

What we cover:
• How Tapes builds a content-addressable Merkle DAG for AI interactions
• Self-healing loops using Flink SQL anomaly detection
• What is the role of Kafka in processing telemetry data?
• Extracting clean training datasets from raw telemetry
• The process of turning failed sessions into preference data for DPO

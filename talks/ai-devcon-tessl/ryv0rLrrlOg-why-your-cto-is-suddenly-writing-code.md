## Description

When AI coding agents changed how software gets built, leaders lost their usual guides: historical data, trusted advisors and instinct. CircleCI CTO Rob Zuber explains why he went back to writing code with agents to get ground truth, and walks through CircleCI's month-by-month journey toward a software factory, including what broke along the way. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why engineering leaders need ground truth in the AI transition
– What "coding is fun again" really signals about the leadership job
– Why data, advisors and instincts all broke at once
– Moving a whole engineering org to agent-first with Claude Code
– What broke when CircleCI started building a software factory
– Making a developer tool work for AI agents, not just humans

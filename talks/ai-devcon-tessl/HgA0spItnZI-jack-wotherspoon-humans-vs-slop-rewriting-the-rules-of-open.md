## Description

The cost of generating AI code is plummeting to nearly zero, but the cost of maintaining that code is soaring. In open-source projects, this new reality has led to a surge in AI-generated pull requests, fundamentally altering the landscape and challenging the way maintainers oversee contributions.

Jack Wotherspoon, a developer advocate at Google, sheds light on these transformative changes. With a wealth of experience working on the Gemini CLI and Google ADK, Jack explores the rise of 'AI slop' in open-source, illustrating how the proliferation of AI agents like OpenClaw has blurred the lines between human contribution and machine generation.

What we cover:
• The transformation of open-source from a human-to-human collaboration to an AI-driven process
• How AI-generated code affects the traditional social contract of open-source
• Examples of AI agents generating nonsensical and excessive pull requests
• What are the new rules of engagement for maintaining open-source quality?
• Case studies where companies restrict or refuse AI-only contributions

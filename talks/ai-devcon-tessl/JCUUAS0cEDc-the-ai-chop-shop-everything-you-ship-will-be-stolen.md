## Description

AI is moving software from generic and cheap to custom and cheap. Computer scientist and entrepreneur Erik Meijer makes the case for hyper-personalized software: why code is now cheap enough to rewrite what you depend on, what AI authorship means for software patents and copyright, and how partial evaluation and the three Futamura projections give models a principled way to specialize programs, and even other models. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why AI moves software from generic and cheap to custom and cheap
– Rewriting dependencies instead of importing them
– Moore's Law for coding: why tokens scale
– What AI authorship means for software patents and copyright
– Partial evaluation and the three Futamura projections
– LLMs as interpreters and partial evaluators

## Description

In a world dominated by cloud-based AI agents, one stands out by running entirely within the browser it controls. Envision an agent as autonomous as a lobster with open claws, untethered from remote servers or cloud dependencies. This agent is the first of its kind, fully harnessing your browser as its runtime and container. It sounds impossible. Yet it's here.

Lars Trieloff, a principal scientist at Adobe, reveals this groundbreaking approach. His experience in cloud-native content and API-first architectures perfectly aligns with this innovation, transforming the browser itself into a self-contained agent powerhouse. Discover how Lars combines inspiration with practicality to redefine agentic capabilities within the familiar confines of your web browser.

What we cover:
• How does the browser itself become a runtime and container for AI agents?
• The inspiration behind Slicky and its development.
• The role of ad-hoc generative user interfaces in building transient applications.
• The concept of 'sprinkles' and their interaction with agents.
• Overcoming limitations of browser-based agents without cloud dependencies.

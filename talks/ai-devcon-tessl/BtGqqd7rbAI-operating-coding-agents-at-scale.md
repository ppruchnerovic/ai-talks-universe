## Description

Rolling out coding agents is the easy part; knowing whether each change to models, settings and tools actually helped is much harder. Nandini Singhal, Senior Software Engineer at Datadog, shares how Datadog answers four questions about the agents its engineers use every day: adoption, productivity, cost and security. She walks through four layers of visibility, an AI gateway, Trajectory session traces, nightly evals on real tasks and versioned rollouts, plus what model router and compression experiments revealed. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– The four questions every company asks: adoption, productivity, cost and security
– Why tokens, sessions and tool calls don't prove productivity
– The cost multiplication, and why defaults are product decisions
– An AI gateway and Trajectory traces for visibility across agents
– Nightly evals on real tasks, and why a model router raised costs
– Rolling out config like code, and measuring outcomes that stick

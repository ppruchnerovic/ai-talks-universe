## Description

The best developers often find themselves discarding their AI agent's output without hesitation. It sounds counterproductive. It works. This paradoxical approach, where multiple agents are run but only one is used effectively, mimics treating AI like a junior developer with much to learn.

Daniel Jones, head of product at re:cinq, has guided countless developers across Northern Europe's largest enterprises through this complex landscape. In a conversation with Simon Maple, Jones reveals the unconventional habits and hard data driving effective agentic development teams.

What we cover:
• Why bad engineering practices worsen with AI agents
• The circumstances that inevitably lead to agent hallucinations
• How does your AGENTS.md silently become a detriment?
• Managing context to avoid productivity loss
• What does an enterprise AI rollout truly entail?
• Why can poor management extract more from agentic coding?

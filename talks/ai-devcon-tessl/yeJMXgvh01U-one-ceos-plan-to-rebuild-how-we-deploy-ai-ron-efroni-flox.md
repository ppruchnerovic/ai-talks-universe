## Description

In this episode, our founder and CEO, Guy Podjarny speaks with Ron Efroni, CEO of Flox and President of the NixOS Foundation, about advancing AI from experimental phases to robust, production-ready environments.

Flox optimizes reproducible environments, Nix provides the foundation, and Ron Efroni is uniting them to scale enterprise development.

On the docket:
• the value proposition of using "environment as code"
• exploring a future of specialized AI agents in a connected ecosystem.
• grasping how to layer MCP over environments, and
• AI to be more deterministic, or should we adapt to it?

## Description

When your PM starts writing real, production code, everything shifts. It's not the code generation that's the bottleneck anymore, but the review and coordination process. With AI, the focus shifts from code writing speed to merge rate—the percentage of pull requests landing in production—and it reveals whether teams are adapting to new pressures or overwhelmed by them.

Tammuz Dubnov, founder and CTO of Autonomy AI, illuminates the journey of transforming organizations to become AI-native. From experiencing firsthand what happens when PMs dive into coding, Tammuz shares his insights on how this shift demands change from both engineering teams and PMs. With his leadership, Autonomy AI navigates these challenges, showcasing real data and a thoughtful breakdown of processes.

What we cover:
• The shift in bottlenecks from code generation to review and coordination
• Understanding merge rate as a measure of adaptation in AI-native organizations
• What AI-native really means: closing the gap between decision-making and execution
• How can PMs and non-technical roles effectively ship valuable code?
• The risks of increasing AI spend without strategic direction, illustrated by Uber and Microsoft

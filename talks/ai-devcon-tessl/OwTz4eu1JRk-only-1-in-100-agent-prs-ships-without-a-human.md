## Description

AI coding agents now write over a quarter of merged pull requests at the typical company, yet only about 1 in 100 agent PRs runs like a true software factory, from task to production without a human. Jellyfish Chief Scientist Nicholas Arcolano draws on data from 300,000 developers, and the history of the elevator, to show why review is the new bottleneck and how teams can build trust in their factory. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– What elevators teach us about autonomy, safeguards and trust
– How much code AI coding agents write today, and how little ships without a human
– Why human attention caps how many agent sessions developers can run
– Review as a second job, and why agent PRs die on the vine
– What the most advanced software factories do differently
– Moving engineering judgment up front with specs, checks and safeguards

## Description

When anyone in an organization can build an app for a single task, one company might end up with 2,500 of them. Matthias Lubken, AI engineer and founder of Tavon.ai, explores software factories for the 'software of one': personal apps built by one person, for one task. He proposes a cheap, agent-friendly stack of isolated JavaScript workers and SQLite in the Durable Objects pattern, then covers access control, insights, promotion strategies and agents that build apps for humans. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– The software of one: built by one person, for one task, owned by one
– Why organizations may soon run thousands of personal apps
– What a personal app stack needs: real code, state, isolation and low cost
– Durable Objects: V8 isolates plus SQLite, and self-hosting with celld and workerd
– Access control: credential isolation, identity brokering and async approval
– Insights, promotion strategies and agents that build apps for humans

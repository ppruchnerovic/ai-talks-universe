## Description

When agents build most of your screens, a designer reviewing everything at the end becomes the bottleneck. Hamza Oza, Lead Designer at Tessl, borrows from Toyota and his years as a mechanical engineer to build design quality into the line instead: redoing agent-built screens by hand to surface unwritten composition rules, turning them into brand and design skills, and adding advisory checks in CI, code review and weekly maintenance loops. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– What manufacturing teaches us about quality at scale
– Why a designer at the end of the line becomes a bottleneck
– Why design systems alone don't stop agents producing generic screens
– Surfacing unwritten composition rules by redoing agent work by hand
– Design skills, advisory CI checks and weekly maintenance loops
– What three months of automating design quality taught us

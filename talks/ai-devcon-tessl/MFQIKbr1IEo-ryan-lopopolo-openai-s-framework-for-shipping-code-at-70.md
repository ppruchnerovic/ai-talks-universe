## Description

Ryan Lopopolo's team at OpenAI pushed the boundaries by shipping a product with no human-written code. This was not just a hypothetical exercise, but an operational reality where onboarding new engineers made the team move even faster within two weeks. It defies expectations, but it works. That's the power of Harness Engineering in practice.

Ryan is a Member of Technical Staff at OpenAI, coined the term Harness Engineering. In this in-depth conversation, recorded live at AI Native DevCon London 2026, Ryan unpacks the methodologies and systems needed to push coding agents to their limits. His insights are drawn from his direct experience with Agentic coding, highlighting what it truly takes to achieve PR throughput from 3.5 to a staggering 70 per engineer per week.

What we cover:
• What is Harness Engineering and how does it differ from context engineering?
• Why did Ryan Lopopolo impose a no-human-written-code constraint?
• How do Friday garbage collection and anti-slopification CI loops build trust in agent output?
• How is spec-driven development being inverted in this context?
• What happens to engineering teams when Harness Engineering becomes standard practice?

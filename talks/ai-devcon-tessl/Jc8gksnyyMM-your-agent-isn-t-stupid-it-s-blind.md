## Description

An agent connected to every system in your company can still be confidently wrong, because it doesn't know what it doesn't know. Ran Aroussi, Founder and Principal at VarOps, shares what his team learned building memory for company-scale agents: why RAG struggles with a company that changes by the second, how distilling information into versioned claims with provenance keeps answers traceable, why 'I don't know' is a feature, and why permissions must filter evidence before it reaches the model. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why an agent with access to everything can still be confidently wrong
– From agent memory to a living organizational model
– Where RAG falls short for fast-changing company knowledge
– Distilling information into versioned claims with provenance
– Why 'I don't know' is one of the most useful answers
– Enforcing permissions before evidence reaches the model, and running locally

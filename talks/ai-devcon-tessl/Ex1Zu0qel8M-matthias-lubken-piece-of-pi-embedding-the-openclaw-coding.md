## Description

When people first experience OpenClaw, they often find it incredible. It auto-discovers new capabilities, integrates data sources seamlessly, and dynamically builds solutions, giving the impression of a learning system. This magic is underpinned by pi.dev, a coding agent with remarkable extensibility that fits perfectly with OpenClaw's evolving architecture.

Matthias Lubken delves into the intricacies of OpenClaw in his talk, exploring how pi.dev supports its core functionality. As an AI engineer specializing in AI agents for business workflows, Matthias shares insights into how builders can integrate and harness these components in their own products, enhancing the development of agentic systems.

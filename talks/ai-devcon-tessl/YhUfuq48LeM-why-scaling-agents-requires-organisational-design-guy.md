## Description

If you wouldn’t trust a new hire without onboarding, why do we expect agents to work without them?

In this episode of AI-Native Dev, Simon Maple and Guy Podjarny unpack how the same principles used to scale human teams apply to AI agents.

On the docket:
- why agent enablement is the goal
- how sales enablement offers a better model than traditional developer onboarding
- why agent behavior must be measured and monitored, not assumed
- how logs and A/B testing reveal which context actually changes outcomes

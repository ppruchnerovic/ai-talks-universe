## Description

Bipolar disorder is more than just mood swings; it offers a rollercoaster of complex emotional states, including heightened creativity and profound dysregulation. It's not just a mental health struggle but a nuanced dance of managing intense peaks and troughs with a combination of medical and personal strategies. Recognizing the unpredictable pattern of bipolar disorder is crucial for anyone attempting to support themselves or others through it.

Dave Kerr, Head of Engineering at QuantumBlack, sheds light on living with bipolar disorder and its interplay with modern pressures like AI. In his talk, he draws from personal experiences and observations of those around him to discuss how AI can both amplify creative states and magnify dysregulation. He explores effective management strategies and underscores the importance of community and supportive relationships in navigating the disorder.

What we cover:
• The complex nature of bipolar disorder, including its unpredictable patterns
• How AI influences mood and behavioral dysregulation
• Why support and medication are critical to managing bipolar disorder
• What role do 'regulators' and 'dysregulators' play in mood management?
• The critical importance of sleep and rhythm in managing dysregulation

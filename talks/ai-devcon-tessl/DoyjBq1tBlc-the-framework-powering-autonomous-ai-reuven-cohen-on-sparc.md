## Description

Reuven Cohen, founder of Agentics Foundation, joins Simon Maple to explain how their SPARC framework enables AI agents to build complex applications reliably and at scale through its five structured steps.

They also get into
• how SPARC enables multiple AI agents to work in parallel.
• unlocking predictable, repeatable results in AI-driven projects.
• exploring how rigid specifications and less permissive languages shape complex AI projects.

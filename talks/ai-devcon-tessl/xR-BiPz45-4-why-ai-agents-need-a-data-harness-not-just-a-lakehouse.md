## Description

Your data lakehouse was built for people who check a dashboard once a day. AI agents ask questions every second, and most data stacks buckle under that pace. Dremio's Will Martin breaks down what a "data harness" actually looks like: the catalog, semantic layer, and query engine changes that make your data fast, understandable, and trustworthy enough for agents to use unsupervised.

What we cover:
– Why legacy data pipelines built for minutes-to-hours turnaround fall apart under agentic, conversational-pace querying
– The three requirements of an AI-ready data foundation: accessible, understandable, and performant
– How Apache Iceberg, Polaris, and Arrow work together inside an open, vendor-neutral lakehouse
– Why a semantic layer, not just a bigger model, is what stops AI agents from guessing at business definitions
– Caching, reflections, and Columnar Cloud Cache (C3): how Dremio gets queries to subsecond speed
– Live audience Q&A on agent access control, token efficiency, and multi-agent data workflows

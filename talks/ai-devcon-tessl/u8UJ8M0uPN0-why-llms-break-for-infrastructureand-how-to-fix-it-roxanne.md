## Description

LMs like ChatGPT or Copilot can generate Terraform snippets—but they break down when asked real infra questions. Why? They lack context.

This talk covers:
- Why LLMs struggle with IaC and cloud reasoning
- Why your infrastructure should be treated as a graph, not isolated files
- How to build a contextual graph that connects cloud, code, and monitoring
- A quick demo: an AI Slackbot that answers real infra questions with full context

---------
Roxane Fischer is the Co-Founder & CEO of Anyshift.io, a startup building the AI context layer for infrastructure. With a background in AI research and software engineering (École Polytechnique, Samsung R&D, Fintica), she now focuses on helping SRE and DevOps teams debug smarter using AI-powered infra insights. Previously, she worked on large-scale AI systems and dev tools, and is now building Annie, the first AI-native SRE agent.

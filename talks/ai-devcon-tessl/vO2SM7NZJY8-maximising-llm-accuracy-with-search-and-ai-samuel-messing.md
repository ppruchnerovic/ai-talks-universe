## Description

How do you build confidence in LLM answers while getting the best results?
In this episode, Samuel Messing, VP of Engineering for Search and AI at Slack and Simon Maple dive into context engineering, showing how curated input transforms AI from a black box into a reliable collaborator.

On the docket:
• when to use search versus AI to leverage their strengths without overloading your workflow.
• how Slack’s AI Explain surfaces insights at scale without slowing teams down.
• the rise of context engineering and why modern developers need to think beyond prompts to guide AI.

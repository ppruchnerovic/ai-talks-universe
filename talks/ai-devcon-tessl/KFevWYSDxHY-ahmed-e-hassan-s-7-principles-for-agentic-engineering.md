## Description

Agentic software engineering now stalls less on writing code and more on the system around it: people, process and leadership. Ahmed E. Hassan, professor at Queen's University and author of the Agentic Software Engineering book, shares seven principles for engineers and leaders working with coding agents, each paired with a lesson from Canadian history. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why the hard part has moved to the system around the code
– Owning what your agents ship: accountability beyond code review
– Making every agent correction compound over time
– Investing in agent experience as seriously as developer experience
– Middle loop engineering: designing the human–agent partnership
– Demos vs shipping, and growing the next generation of engineers

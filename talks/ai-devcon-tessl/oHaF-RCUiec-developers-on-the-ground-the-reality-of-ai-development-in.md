## Description

We sit down with developers and leaders across the AI ecosystem - ranging from a YC-backed startup CTO to an AI engineering specialist, founding engineers, and cloud experts - to unpack the reality of building in this fast-moving space.

They share insights on:
• Why no single tool is enough, and how to blend Gemini, Claude, and others into real workflows
• The hidden skill of “injecting taste” and human intent that separates great AI outputs from garbage code
• The trap of chasing every shiny new tool—and how to instead focus on “big beats” that last
• How to prove AI’s value to non-technical audiences with metrics and proofs of concept
• Why LangGraph-style visualizations fall short for fault-tolerant, multi-agent systems

AI Native Dev—powered by Tessl and our global dev community—is your go-to for navigating software development in the age of AI. Tune in for conversations with engineers, founders, and open-source innovators on AI, security, and the future of development.

**Yaniv Aknin**

## Description

“There’s a lot of research showing models perform better when they first reason through a problem and create a plan.”

Yaniv Aknin, founding engineer at Tessl, breaks down how Codex, Claude, and Gemini interpret planning differently and why, in practice, plans often fail to enforce execution.

He also explores:
• generalist vs specialist agents when context gets tight
• when tool descriptions are merely decorative and when they are decisive
• why planning remains an unresolved design problem

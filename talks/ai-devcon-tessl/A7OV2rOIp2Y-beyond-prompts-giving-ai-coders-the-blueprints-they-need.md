## Description

AI coding assistants (like Cursor, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) are powerful, but often struggle with vague prompts, leading to frustrating loops and inaccurate MVPs. Why? They lack the inherent context and structured planning humans bring. This 15-minute demo introduces CreateMVPs.app, the first open-source tool designed to bridge this gap. We'll showcase the workflow: transforming a simple app idea into a comprehensive set of planning documents (PRDs, tech specs, user flows) that provide the crucial "blueprint" AI needs. Learn how feeding structured plans instead of just prompts leads to faster, more accurate AI-assisted development.

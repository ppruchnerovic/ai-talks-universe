## Description

Agents love toil, and on-call is full of it. Paul Stack, VP of Product at Swamp Club, shows how his team built a lights-out factory for alerts: every alert arrives as a webhook, agents triage and correlate it, and operators get a ready-made dossier in Discord instead of a bare page. He walks through a real alert, explains where agents make the call and where they don't, and shares how to get started. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why agents are a great fit for operational toil
– A lights-out factory for alerts
– How alerts flow from webhook to triage
– A real alert, from page to dossier
– Querying incidents from Discord, and adding a small model to the pipeline
– Where the agent never makes the call, and how to get started

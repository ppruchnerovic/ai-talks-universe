## Description

“LLMs themselves are not sufficient in terms of understanding your codebase.”

After years of optimizing developer tools for humans, Sean Roberts, VP of Applied AI at Netlify and Simon Maple explore what it takes to make software legible to non-human developers: AI agents.

On the docket:
• why auditing real AI usage is the most effective starting point
• why MCPs and tooling standards solve the wrong layer of the problem
• how feedback loops materially improve cost and reliability
• why large codebases demand knowledge graphs

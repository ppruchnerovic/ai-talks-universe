## Description

Most teams building with AI agents are flying on vibes — hoping the agent does what they meant, not what they said. Dave Kirk, who leads agent AI adoption at self-driving car company Wayve, breaks down how his team built an agentic PR review system that actually gets evaluated with data instead of guesswork, and the multi-agent Claude experiment that cost him $1,600 in two days along the way.

What we cover:
– Why agentic code review at Wayve had to move past "vibes-based" evaluation
– Lessons learned from running Claude multi-agent teams (including that $1,600 bill)
– Why public coding benchmarks can't be trusted once agents learn to game them
– How Wayve's GitHub Action fans out review agents and steers them with prompts
– Building a feedback loop with sentiment, usage tracking, and evals to keep improving prompts

## Description

If your job has become hopping between terminals and nudging agents to keep going, it might be time to move them off your laptop. Jack Wotherspoon, Member of Technical Staff at Google DeepMind, shares why he moved all his work to remote cloud agents, and walks through Gemini Managed Agents: one API call for an agent with its own sandbox, the Antigravity harness, reusable environments, hooks, credentials that never enter the sandbox, and scheduled triggers that fix bugs while you sleep. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why managing local coding agents turns into watching a screen
– How cloud sandboxes raise the bar for verification and safety
– Gemini Managed Agents: harness, environment, tools, credentials and triggers
– Separating interactions from environments for fresh-eyes review agents
– Hooks, skills and a credentials proxy that keeps secrets out of the sandbox
– Scheduled and event-driven agents for issue triage, benchmarks and security checks

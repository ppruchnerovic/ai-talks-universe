## Description

René Brandel, CEO of Casco, joins Simon Maple to spotlight a new reality: AI is outpacing security so quickly that vulnerabilities emerge faster than teams can detect them.

They also explore:
• why LLM syntactic correctness has risen from 17% to 98% while security benchmarks have remained flat
• the shortcomings of traditional scanners that fail to understand business intent
• how offensive-first testing will shape the next generation of application security

## Description

Agent harnesses are evolving, but there's a gap in integrating product and design contexts that live outside the codebase. Often existing in tools like Figma, Notion, and Linear, these essential contexts can fall out of sync with the code, creating challenges for coding agents and the harnesses they depend on. Real-world consequences, such as failing accessibility audits or mismatched components, are common when overlooked.

Marc Sloan of Tessl (Member of Product Staff) discusses the next frontier for agent harnesses in this talk, focusing on non-developmental contexts. A member of the product team, Sloan brings his firsthand experience from working on Figma's Code Connect, highlighting the importance of bridging the gap between product and design decisions and agent operations. His insights delve into the challenges faced and potential solutions available to align these contexts with codified workflows.

What we cover:
• Why the next agent harness frontier must include product and design context
• Challenges of integrating non-codebase contexts into coded workflows
• What does it mean for product and design context to exist outside the harness?
• How does the current approach to skills and context break down?
• The potential evolution of agent harnesses at Tessl and beyond

## Description

AI workloads are evolving from simple chatbots to complex agentic processes that challenge traditional benchmarks. A single thousand-token transaction won't reveal how an agent behaves across multiple turns with massive contexts and tool calls. It sounds like chaos, but these long trajectories are where real innovation occurs.

Amit Kushwaha, a solutions architect at Nvidia, dives deep into this shift. He explains how Nvidia confronts real-world workload challenges with benchmarks that mirror actual tasks, rather than hypothetical scenarios. With a focus on coding agents, he shares valuable insights into optimizations critical for modern LLM-driven operations.

What we cover:
• Why traditional benchmarks fail to account for agentic workloads
• The significance of KV cache reuse and tool latency
• How does prefix caching impact concurrency in AI systems?
• Optimizing agentic workloads with speculative decoding
• Real-world agent benchmarks: A critique and future outlook

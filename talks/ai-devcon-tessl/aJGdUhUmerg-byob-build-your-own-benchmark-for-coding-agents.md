## Description

Public benchmarks can help you shortlist models, but they can't tell you which coding agent works best on your code. Joe Tyler, Lead AI Researcher at Sonar, explains where popular benchmarks fall short, from data leakage to the gap between passing tests and being ready to merge, and how his team built a private benchmark from their own repositories with calibrated LLM judges and static analysis. He shares what they learned comparing open and closed models and harnesses on success, cost and security. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why an agent is more than its model
– Data leakage and the shelf life of public benchmarks
– Why passing tests isn't the same as ready to merge
– Building a private benchmark from your own repositories
– Calibrating LLM judges and analyzing agent trajectories
– Findings on success, cost, security and harnesses

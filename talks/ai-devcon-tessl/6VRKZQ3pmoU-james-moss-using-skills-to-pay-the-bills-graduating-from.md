## Description

Skills management might seem straightforward for solo projects, but when it comes to teams, the complexity multiplies. Skills, context files, and agent instructions become a new layer on top of your code that receives little attention. No versioning, no review, no shared source of truth: the chaos begins as each agent operates differently, and nobody knows why.

James Moss, a member of technical staff at Tessl, guides us through this maze of skill management in team environments. With over two decades of industry experience and a focus on Tessell's registry product, James shares hard-earned insights into what breaks, what can be borrowed from existing code practices, and the essentials for turning agent context into a robust team framework.

What we cover:
• Understanding the challenge of skill sprawl and its impact on teams
• How does skill overlap and drift affect agent workflows?
• The importance of managing skills with a software mindset
• What steps can be taken to transition from solo to team skill management?
• Exploring the context development lifecycle

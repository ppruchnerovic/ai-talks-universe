## Description

Crude oil is worthless until it's refined, and the same goes for the data behind your AI apps and agents. Pratik Patel, Lead Developer Relations Engineer at Hugging Face, demos an 'ask the docs' assistant that gives the wrong answer on raw documentation and the right one on refined data. He walks through a pipeline that separates, purifies, lints and blends data, then shows how to ship data like code, with versioning, automated jobs, eval gates and releases. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why confidently wrong answers often start with bad data
– A demo: raw docs vs refined docs in a RAG assistant
– Separate, purify, lint and blend: refining your data
– What FineWeb-Edu shows about refined training data
– Shipping data like code: buckets, Xet, jobs and releases
– Eval gates, versioned data sets and smaller, cheaper models

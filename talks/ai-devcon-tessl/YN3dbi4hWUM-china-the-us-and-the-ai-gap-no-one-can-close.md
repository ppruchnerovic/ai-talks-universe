## Description

Everyone thinks Llama is open source, but Amanda Brock proves otherwise.

Amanda Brock, CEO of OpenUK, breaks down why Meta's Llama was never truly open source, how DeepSeek built a world-class model for $5M instead of $100M, and why the global AI race isn't just between the US and China.

On the docket:
• Why Llama has a hidden clause that breaks the core principle of open source
• How DeepSeek's distillation technique cut model building costs by 95%
• Why the "middle countries" can't afford to lose the open source AI race
• Why open source AI adoption is inevitable, and we've seen this movie before

The AI race isn't just about who builds the best model; it's also about who keeps it open.

## Description

Mozilla.ai's proposal, cq, aims to revolutionize agent interaction by creating a standardized knowledge-sharing platform. This concept helps operators avoid recurring mistakes and token wastage, and allows insights learned by one agent to benefit all. It's about applying open standards to agentic systems—like how the web was kept open.

Peter Wilson and Davide Eynard, both from Mozilla AI, bring deep insights into the workings and future of cq. They share their experiences with ai models, how context influences outcomes, and the gap between open-source and commercial AI. Their session proposes a fresh approach to tackle how agents can learn collectively, drawing from their work on open-source projects.

What we cover:
• The foundational idea and high-level workings of cq
• What lessons were learned during cq's development
• How can context impact AI model performance?
• The proposal for a 'Stack Overflow for agents' with cq
• Opportunities for community involvement in the cq project

## Description

Are your AI coding agents actually ready for production? At this AIND Meetup,  Nupur Sharma (Qodo), Brian Vermeer (Snyk), Simon Maple
(Tessl), and Kunal  (@KunalKushwaha, Cast AI)  got brutally honest about what's really going wrong when teams build and ship with AI agents.

In this session you'll learn:

🔴 The "Context Trap" — why bigger context windows don't mean better results, and what to do instead

🔁 The Orchestration Paradox — how agents get stuck in infinite loops trying to find the perfect solution instead of just solving the problem

🧠 The Generalist Blind Spot — why one super-agent fails where multiple
specialised agents succeed

⚖️ The 80/20 model approach — using high-reasoning models for research and lighter models for execution

🔒 Security, human oversight, and the hard question: should AI agents ship code autonomously at 3am?

Whether you're a developer building multi-agent systems, an engineering leader thinking about AI adoption, or just curious about where agentic AI is headed — this panel is packed with real-world experience and no-nonsense takes.

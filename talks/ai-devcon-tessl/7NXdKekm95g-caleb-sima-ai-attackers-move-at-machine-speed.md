## Description

AI attackers now move at machine speed, while most security teams still move at meeting speed. Former Robinhood CSO Caleb Sima has seen an AI attacker up close, and he argues the only way to keep up is to take humans out of the defense loop.

Caleb joins Guy Podjarny to unpack what changes when the attacker is an agent: one that hits every surface at once, reverses open-source code to find a 0-day, and once inside, burns your own AI tokens to keep going.

– Whether sandboxes can contain AI agents, and why external monitoring matters
– Who is accountable when your AI agent hacks someone else
– What actually changes when an AI attacker gets inside your company
– Deception, digital twins and forensic-grade detection
– Automating security: instant patching, one-way vs two-way doors, and security teams that ship the fix

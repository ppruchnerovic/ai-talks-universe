## Description

The challenge of maintaining effective development practices in rapidly evolving AI environments can feel relentless. Every quarter, established methods for reaching production and daily engineering routines have to be reconsidered to keep up with the accelerated pace. This continuous adaptation is crucial as engineers and AI work hand-in-hand to push changes faster than ever before. Without these adjustments, costly mistakes could become commonplace and derail entire projects. Safety nets can no longer depend solely on manual review, and the path to production demands robust verification layers.

Christopher Batey, CTO at Core Engineering Consulting Group, shares his insights drawn from firsthand experience building a product team in the AI era. As an expert in platform engineering and continuous delivery, Christopher has navigated the complexities of AI amplification, both the positive aspects and the pitfalls. From transforming internal training methods to leveraging AI to speed up development, his talk reveals the surprising shifts that marked this journey.

What we cover:
• Path to Production at AI Speed: How can development teams maintain safety without relying only on manual reviews?
• Training and Evaluating AI-Enabled Engineers: What does effective coaching for AI-assisted teams look like?
• Designing Workflow for Parallel Change with preview environments and isolated branches.
• The balance of AI and human intervention: Is software engineering the hardest part of building successful digital products?
• Percentage of code written by AI versus code going to production unseen by humans.

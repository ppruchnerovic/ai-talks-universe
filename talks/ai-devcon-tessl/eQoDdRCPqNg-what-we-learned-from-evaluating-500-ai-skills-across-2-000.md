## Description

Evaluating AI skills across 2,000 scenarios isn't what it seems. Most teams aren't actually assessing their agents—they're mired in environmental noise and misleading judge feedback. Your numbers may not mean what you think.

Rob Willoughby, AI Research Lead at Tessl, takes us through their large-scale evaluation process and reveals the common pitfalls teams face. With insights from over 500 skills, Rob highlights how understanding these traps can change your team's approach to AI agent evaluation.

What we cover:
• The Outcome Trap: Why measuring task completion isn't enough.
• The Judge Trap: How model bias affects evaluation results.
• The Scale Trap: What happens when your setup doesn't scale beyond a laptop.
• How does infrastructure noise affect AI agent evaluations?
• The unforgettable story of a 6.25x improvement that wasn't real.

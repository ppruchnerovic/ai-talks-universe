## Description

What happens when you stop reading the code your agents write, and then production breaks? Alex Gavrilescu, Lead Backend and Web Developer at Funstage GmbH and creator of Backlog.md, shares the incident that made him rethink staying in control. He demos Groma, an open-source tool that deterministically maps your repo's architecture as C4 diagrams, so you can review how the system changes rather than every line. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Shipping more with agents, and the incident that followed
– Why every PR can look fine while the architecture drifts
– Four attempts at an offline architecture tool
– Live demo: Groma's C4 views and flows
– Watching agents change a greenfield architecture in real time
– Mapping a brownfield code base, and architecture diffs in PRs

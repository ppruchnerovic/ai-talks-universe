## Description

Enterprises are rethinking their development processes because AI agents have transformed inefficiencies into costly visible errors. Once dismissed as minor human faults, these have now forced companies to adjust workflows to avoid ballooning costs. Efficiency isn't just a preference; it's essential when agent costs are part of your budget.

Simon Maple, joined by a panel of experts at AI Native DevCon London, dives into how enterprises are managing this change. Patrick Debois, the DevRel at Tessl, offers his deep insight into the intersection of DevOps and AI, highlighting the necessary shifts in managing software development workflows in an AI-first environment. Tammuz Dubnov, co-founder and CEO of Autonomy AI, shares perspectives on enabling non-technical participation in agent-driven code bases. Adding to the mix, Daniel Jones from re:cinq focuses on effective enterprise workflow transformations.

What we cover:
• Who should own agentic coding adoption in an enterprise?
• Why are PR-based workflows seen as an anti-pattern at agent speed?
• How does the PUMP framework enhance feature shipping with collective PRs?
• Why is redefining 'test' vital in AI development?
• What's the biggest mistake enterprises make when adopting AI?

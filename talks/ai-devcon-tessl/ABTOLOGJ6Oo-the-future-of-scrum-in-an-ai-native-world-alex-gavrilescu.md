## Description

When AI agents can execute tasks continuously, what remains of Scrum?

On this episode of AI Native Dev, Alexandru Gavrilescu, Lead Backend & Web Developer at Greentube, joins Simon Maple to rethink the foundations of agile when humans and agents build side by side.

On the docket:
• why traditional Scrum can’t keep up with 24/7 agent collaboration
• what “AI Scrum” could look like, with human reviews as checkpoints
• what “refactoring” really means in an AI-first dev stack
• how persistent agents act as hubs of shared intent
• what it means to design for infinite bandwidth and vanishing context windows

## Description

Most of the ROI in an AI software factory doesn't come from the model or the coding agent — it comes from everything around it. Rob Willoughby, who leads research at Tessl, shows how Tessl's internal software factory now carries around 80% of its PRs using off-the-shelf agents, and why context, verification and feedback loops are where the real returns live.

What we cover:
– How a $600 PR came from two agents doing exactly what they were told, and why Tessl fixed the system rather than the agent
– The five stages of a software factory, and why the feedback loop matters most
– Keeping humans in the loop at the behavior level, not the code level
– Using formal verification with Quint to knock out an entire class of bugs
– Why CI pass rate alone hid the true cost of medium vs high effort
– Measuring agentic coding ROI: the full cost of a PR, fast signals and shadow experiments

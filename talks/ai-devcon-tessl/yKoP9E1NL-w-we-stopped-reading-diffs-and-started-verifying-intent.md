## Description

Code review was never really about finding bugs, and with AI writing most of the code, reading diffs no longer scales. Ankit Jain, Founder & CEO of Aviator, shares how his team now merges 4x more PRs than a year ago: agents that argue before a PR exists, intent and acceptance criteria captured from coding sessions, rules mined from past review comments and turned into deterministic checks, agent-driven proof, and humans debating decisions instead of reading diffs. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Three attempts at fixing code review for AI-written code
– Why code review is really about mental models, not bugs
– Argue and capture: agents debate, intent and acceptance criteria get recorded
– Codify: turning 11,000 review comments into rules and deterministic checks
– Prove: agent-driven verification with real evidence
– Debate and own: the human's new role in review

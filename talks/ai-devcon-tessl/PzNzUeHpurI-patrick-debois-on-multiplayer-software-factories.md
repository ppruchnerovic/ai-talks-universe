## Description

Running a software factory on your own laptop is one thing; opening it up to a whole organization, with many people and many coding agents working on one main branch at once, is another. Patrick Debois, the godfather of DevOps and AI Native Dev advisor at Tessl, maps the new problems of concurrent delivery and the patterns teams are building to solve them. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why a shared, multiplayer software factory creates new kinds of conflict
– Splitting work so coding agents don't trip over each other
– Forking the world, not just the code: isolation for agents
– Merging on intent, not just syntax, plus speculative merges
– How Slack, sales and support plug into a shared factory
– Routing decisions by risk and designing for failure

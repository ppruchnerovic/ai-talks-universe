## Description

Imagine if every piece of crucial information about your projects and clients could be accessed through one brain. This centralized brain contains brand DNA, preferences, delivery dates, and more, woven together seamlessly during production or decision-making moments. It's not about generic patterns, but about tailored intelligence for each client, powered by OpenClaw and structured vaults. It sounds impossible. It works.

Robert Overweg, a leader at a startup known for its innovative approaches in fashion, explains the one brain concept and its transformative power. By sharing insights from integrating company knowledge with tools like Obsidian and Telegram, Robert discusses how they manage extensive content production without relying on memory alone, enabling teams to innovate effectively.

What we cover:
• How to create a 'one brain' for your organization
• The power of integrating client-specific contexts over generic patterns
• What changed in production when teams stopped relying on memory
• How Obsidian outperforms tools like Notion in knowledge management
• Why connecting organizational knowledge streams unlocks innovation

## Description

Implementation is becoming free, and taste is becoming the expensive part. Steve Yegge, creator of Gas Town, shares what he's learned running dozens of agents in his own software factory to rebuild a decades-old online game: why the mechanical factory layer is starting to look the same everywhere, why the creative 'entourage' layer on top is a pet you have to grow yourself, and the surprising drama of managing long-lived agent personas. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Pets vs cattle, and why it's happening again with factories
– Running a factory on 22 Claude Max accounts as a glimpse of the future
– The mechanical factory layer vs the creative entourage layer
– What happens when your factory overwhelms everyone around you
– Agent personas, office drama and accidental 'case law'
– Why taste becomes the bottleneck when implementation is free

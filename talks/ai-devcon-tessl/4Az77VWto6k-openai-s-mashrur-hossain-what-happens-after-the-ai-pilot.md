## Description

Rolling out AI coding agents across a regulated enterprise takes far more than a successful pilot, because the day after a pilot ends, usually nothing happens. Mashrur Hossain, who works in deployed engineering on Codex at OpenAI, shares his recipe for enterprise AI enablement in financial services: prepare, practice and sustain, find your champions, and build the controls regulators expect. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Why AI coding pilots never seem to end, and what to do after one succeeds
– Scaling enablement from a 50-person team to 5,000+ engineers
– The prepare, practice, sustain recipe for enterprise AI adoption
– Finding champions who give your rollout credibility
– Evidence, boundaries and accountability: AI coding controls for regulated industries
– Automating enablement with your own software factory

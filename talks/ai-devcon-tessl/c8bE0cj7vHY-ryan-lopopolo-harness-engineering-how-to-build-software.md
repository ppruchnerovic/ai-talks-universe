## Description

Harness Engineering transforms software development, spotlighting agent-driven execution over traditional coding. Specialized harnesses let agents perform tasks safely and at high quality, making manual coding the exception. It seems wrong. It works.

Ryan Lopopolo, a leading voice in agent-based development and member of technical staff at OpenAI, offers valuable insights into this paradigm shift. His framework covers how to manage autonomy, shape context, and build feedback systems for operational agents, crucial for moving from demos to real-world AI applications.

What we cover:
• How Harness Engineering redefines traditional coding workflows
• What are the challenges of 'giving the model the repo' as a solution?
• Designing harnesses for safe, high-quality agent work
• Where AI agents excel and where they still require human oversight
• Practical patterns for effective AI coding in production

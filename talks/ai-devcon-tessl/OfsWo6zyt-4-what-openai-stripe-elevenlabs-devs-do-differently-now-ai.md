## Description

Why is traditional CI/CD deemed obsolete in the age of AI-native development? The shift is led by companies like Stripe and ElevenLabs, who are pioneering methods that optimize code review and context. It sounds wrong, but the change is underway, evidenced by the thousands of code merges happening without direct human involvement each week. This evolution is reshaping the landscape of software engineering.

What we cover:
• Why traditional CI/CD 'is dead'
• The role of automated code review and guardrails
• Why is the right context more important than more context?
• Difference between general-purpose models and specialized domain models

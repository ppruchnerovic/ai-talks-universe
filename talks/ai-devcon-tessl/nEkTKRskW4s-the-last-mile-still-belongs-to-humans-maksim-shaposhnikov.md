## Description

A few lines of text. A full working prototype. That’s where agents are now.

On AI Native Dev, Maksim Shaposhnikov, AI Research Engineer at Tessl, walks Simon Maple through the current limits of agentic systems, how close they come to developer intent, and where human reasoning still fills the gap.

They also get into:
• why prototypes often fail due to underspecified prompts
• risks of giving agents full repo access
• how sandboxed environments keep AI from going rogue

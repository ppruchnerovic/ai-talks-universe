## Description

The code you have may be holding you back. Treating code as disposable, and focusing on the system as the real asset, can change how you approach software development. This radical shift, known as Phoenix Architecture, allows for easy replacement of code, much like how simple it has become to replace servers with the cloud now. Cutting 70% of a codebase in just three months, all while saving costs by 75%, flips conventional software maintenance on its head.

Chad Fowler, now a VC at Blue Yard Capital and formerly CTO at Wunderlist, leads the conversation on this transformative idea. His background in euthanizing legacy systems and experience with disposable software at Wunderlist provides a unique perspective on why systems should be built to outlive their code. Chad's insights reveal why code under a page helps maintain adaptability and why even AI-generated code requires safe deployment methods.

What we cover:
• Why was the code written by Chad never longer than a page?
• How can you replace 70% of a codebase in 3 months?
• What does shipping AI code without human review entail, and how can it be safe?
• What are the shadow specs your agents are making without you?
• Why should your system work with the worst LLM, not just the best?

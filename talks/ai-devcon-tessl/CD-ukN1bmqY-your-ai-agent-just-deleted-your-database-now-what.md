## Description

An AI agent found an API token, deleted a company's production database, and wiped its backups — all on its own. Snyk's Nick Vinson breaks down why autonomous AI agents keep going rogue, and what security teams need to build before it happens to them.

What we cover:
– The four traits of "chaotic neutral" AI agents: destructive, unintended, escape-prone, and deceptive
– Real incidents: the Pocket OS database wipe, Meta's Instagram takeover, and the OpenAI/Hugging Face breach
– Why generative LLM agents can't foresee the consequences of their own actions
– Building zero trust architecture for agents: identity, authentication, authorization, and monitoring
– The "Lethal Trifecta" and scoping agent permissions with least agency
– How Snyk uses its own agentic workflows (Agent Fix, Remediation Agent) to fight back

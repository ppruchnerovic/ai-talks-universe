## Description

How do you actually know if your AI agents are working? For most enterprise teams, the honest answer is: you don't — not without evals.

In this talk from Tessl, research engineer Maksim Shaposhnikov breaks down the full architecture of a production-ready agent evaluation platform, and why evals are no longer just a research concern — they're a business-critical capability.

Whether you're a software engineer trying to validate a new Claude Code skill, a VP of Engineering trying to understand where agents are failing across your organization, or a decision-maker weighing whether to swap to a cheaper open-source model, evals are the only reliable way to make those calls with confidence.

Maksim covers the core building blocks of an eval platform (tasks, grading approaches, and scoring), the tradeoffs between verifiable graders, LLM-as-judge, and human review, and how Tessl's task evals and project evals give teams a practical, scalable path to measuring agent performance in their own codebases.

If you're building AI into your engineering organization and making decisions based on vibes rather than data, this one's for you.

## Description

In a world where AI-native products redefine software development, product managers need to harness automated workflows effectively. The 'product brain' is a game-changer, allowing PMs to convert their unique taste and judgment into algorithms that can streamline tasks from customer complaints to pull requests. It sounds audacious. It works.

Emma Burrows, founder of Rezonant and a former UK CTO at Stripe, brings her expertise and 18 months of experience in AI-native product development to this talk. She has been at the forefront of exploring what product management should look like when software can essentially build itself. Emma shares invaluable insights into setting up a 'product brain' and orchestrating AI agents to maximize productivity while maintaining product excellence.

What we cover:
• What a 'product brain' is and how PMs can use it effectively
• How to set up a product brain to automate tasks efficiently
• How do you decide where oversight is crucial in workflow automation?
• Strategic thinking for automating and maintaining oversight
• Preserving product excellence while achieving rapid delivery

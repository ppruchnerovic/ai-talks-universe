## Description

Docker Sandboxes tackle a common AI dilemma: how do you harness AI's productivity without exposing your system to security risks? When AI agents run unchecked, they can unwittingly cause havoc, like optimizing your home directory or upgrading Python unexpectedly. Finding the balance between that YOLO speed and security is crucial.

Oleg Šelajev from Docker dives deep into this challenge, bringing his expertise from the DevRel team. Oleg explores the concept of Docker Sandboxes, highlighting their role in safely managing AI workloads. His experience with Atomic Jar and Testcontainers enhances his insights into isolating AI operations to prevent unwanted system alterations.

What we cover:
• The pitfalls of letting AI agents run wild on your system
• What are Docker Sandboxes and how do they help in securing AI operations?
• Typical security threats posed by rogue AI agents
• How to run AI workloads securely using Docker's new primitives
• The importance of isolating AI workloads to prevent security incidents

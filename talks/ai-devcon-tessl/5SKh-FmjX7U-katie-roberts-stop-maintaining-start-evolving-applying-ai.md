## Description

The "AI-Native" dream is often idealized as starting fresh with flawless prompts and no technical debt. But the real world for most engineers consists of mature brownfield environments, with complex codebases and build pipelines held together by hope. It's a challenging paradox. A highly successful product, yet a maze of interconnected systems and legacy logic.

Katie Roberts from NearForm dives into how AI Native Engineering can be the answer to these challenges. With her experience as a Technical Director at NearForm and having worked on various projects at the BBC, Katie brings real-world insights into transforming brownfield codebases. She discusses practical applications like using the Strangler Fig Pattern to modernize legacy systems, illustrating how AI can help document, test, and eventually replace outdated code components.

What we cover:
• How can AI Native Engineering transform brownfield codebases?
• Understanding the Strangler Fig Pattern for minimizing legacy systems without downtime.
• Identifying and isolating high-risk areas in a legacy codebase.
• Using AI to document functionalities and create test suites for undocumented code.
• The challenges of maintaining versus evolving codebases.

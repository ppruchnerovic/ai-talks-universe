## Description

Booking.com is moving from AI-assisted coding to automated software delivery across 4,000 engineers, and the bottleneck isn't the models, it's trust. Valerio Panzica, Senior Tech Product Manager at Booking.com, shares the assist, delegate and orchestrate phases of their agentic SDLC, the internal Agent Fabric platform behind it, and why autonomy increases with confidence. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– Booking.com's three phases toward autonomous software delivery: assist, delegate, orchestrate
– Rolling out AI coding agents to 4,000 engineers, with 90% weekly usage
– Delegating migrations, feature flag cleanup and code review to agents
– Five areas that build confidence, from context to golden paths
– Agent Fabric: an internal platform for MCPs, skills and agents
– Measuring impact and trust with the DX Core 4

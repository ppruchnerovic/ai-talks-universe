## Description

In this multi-guest special of AI Native Dev, Simon Maple hosts experts Baruch Sadogursky (TuxCare), Liran Tal (Snyk), Alex Gavrilescu (Greentube), and Josh Long (Broadcom) to map out the fragile borders where AI-assisted development breaks.

They get into:
• how human readable specs allow both technical and non-technical stakeholders to review AI-generated output
• LLMs as impartial “judges,” validating whether intended behavior is accurately captured
• where AI fails in security workflows, from chained vulnerabilities to double encoding pitfalls
• how persistent chat memory can drift and derail mission specific agents
• why the safest AI systems today are often the most tightly constrained and restrictive

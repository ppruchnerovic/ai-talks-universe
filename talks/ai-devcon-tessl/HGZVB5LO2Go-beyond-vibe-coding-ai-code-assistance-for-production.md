## Description

​AI code assistants like GitHub Copilot & Cursor are already changing the way developers write code. But while early experiments have sparked excitement, many teams are still stuck in the “vibe coding” phase. Tinkering without direction, clear outcomes, or guardrails. This talk is for engineering leaders and practitioners who want to move past the novelty and start using AI code assistance deliberately, safely, and effectively for production-grade software development. We’ll explore practical patterns for adoption in enterprise environments, highlight real-world lessons from early pilots, and show how these tools can be embedded into modern SDLCs without sacrificing code quality, security, or team collaboration.

​Matthew Belcher - Head of Emerging Technology at Codurance UK

​As Head of Emerging Technology for Codurance UK, Matt oversees Codurance UK’s strategy for leveraging emerging technologies. He is responsible for ensuring the region keeps pace with fast-moving technology trends. Matt helps business leaders understand how these technology innovations can address real-world challenges. With over 15 years of experience in software consultancy, he has worked across various industries including financial services, retail and public sector both in the UK and across Europe. During this time he has worked with a wide range of technologies and played numerous roles right from software developer through to fractional CTO.

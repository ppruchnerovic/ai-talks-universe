## Description

How do you scale agentic engineering across 2,000 developers and 150 engineering teams? Bartosz Ocytko, Executive Principal Engineer at Zalando, shares three years of lessons: a central model proxy for vendor independence and cost visibility, community-built tooling, fleet-wide migrations, risk-based PR scoring that auto-approves low-risk changes, an open-source identity broker for agents, and how Zalando is enabling citizen developers and helping engineers keep up. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– A central model proxy for vendor independence and visibility
– Community-built tooling, from MCP bridges to cost trackers
– Fleet-wide changes and migrations with LLMs
– Risk-based PR scoring and automatic approval for low-risk changes
– Managing cost, compliance and production quality
– An agent platform with an identity broker, and enabling citizen developers

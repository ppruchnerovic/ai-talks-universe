## Description

On this episode of AI Native Dev, Simon Maple sits down with Steve Manuel, founder and CEO of Dylibso, to explore how MCP grew from a side-project to enterprise infrastructure in just a year, and what’s next.

They also get into:

• the tradeoffs between local, managed, and self-hosted MCP servers
• why enterprises are demanding auditability and control
• how MCP traffic becomes a liability in public proxies

## Description

Dru Knox, Tessl's head of product and AI research, breaks down "harness engineering" — the discipline of building loops of checks, reviews, and maintenance agents that let you delegate more and more of your codebase to AI. He walks through why his team is shipping 30% more PRs every week, why review (not autonomy) is usually the real bottleneck, and how Tessl thinks about turning a team of engineers into a software factory without losing quality.

What we cover:
– What a software factory actually is, and why it's not the end of the craft
– The three metrics to track: autonomy, automation, and quality
– Why teams delegate more work to coding agents
– The inner loop, outer loop, and meta loop of harness engineering
– Why harness engineering is an organizational problem more than a technical one
– Live Q&A: auto-merging PRs, culture change, and building a "company brain" for agents

## Description

​A practical talk about implementing AI tools at scale. As an AI Platform Product Manager, I'll share Doctolib's 4-month journey from a 30-engineer pilot to tech-wide adoption of AI coding assistants. I'll reveal the real challenges faced - from quality concerns and engineer skepticism to cost management and environmental considerations and importantly how we overcame them. You can expect real experience from the trenches of organizational change - no hype, no hand-waving.

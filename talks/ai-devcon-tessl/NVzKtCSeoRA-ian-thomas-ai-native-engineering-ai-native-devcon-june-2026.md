## Description

Meta is taking a bold step into AI, not just through their products but through a dedicated AI4P (AI For Productivity) program. Horizon Experiences in Reality Labs at Meta is driving change across the AI-enhanced engineering landscape, focusing on enhancing productivity by weaving AI seamlessly into everyday workflows.

Ian Thomas, a software engineer at Meta, shares insights from his time in Horizon Experiences. He recounts the journey from limited AI tool usage to widespread adoption, highlighting a significant leap from 50% to over 90% tool utilization. Ian explains how building a supportive community of engineers and integrating AI into routine tasks led to improvements in efficiency and satisfaction. His expertise provides valuable strategies for nurturing a culture of AI Native Engineering.

What we cover:
• Meta's AI4P program's impact on engineering teams
• How Reality Labs uses AI across the software engineering lifecycle
• The effectiveness of AI tools in mimicking early career engineers
• What leadership strategies help in embracing AI tools
• How has platform integration improved output quality and engineer satisfaction?
• The development and success of a maturity model for AI tool adoption

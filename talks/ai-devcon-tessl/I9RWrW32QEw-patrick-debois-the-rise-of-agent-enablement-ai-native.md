## Description

Why do some engineering teams excel at scaling AI coding while others remain mired in the basics? It's not a mystery: the secret lies in a new function called Agent Enablement. This isn't just about using AI tools. It's about creating a team that empowers other teams to effectively harness these agents, embedding them into your organizational practices.

Patrick Debois, known as the father of DevOps and a product developer at Tessle, delves into this evolving landscape. Drawing on insights from early Agent Enablement teams and engineering leaders, Debois shares how the Context Development Lifecycle is transforming from individual experimentation to a full-fledged organizational function. His experience and discussions reveal how maturity in AI coding distinguishes successful teams from those that are stuck.

What we cover:
• What roles does Agent Enablement play in scaling AI coding?
• Shifting from individual experiments to organizational fluency with AI agents.
• How do fast, observable, and cost-aware platforms aid in agent tooling?
• What are the emerging governance structures for agents?
• Defining the role of team leads in supporting both human and agent performance.

## Description

Richmond Alake, Director of AI Developer Experience at Oracle, has spent his career keeping developers at the centre of everything. From NVIDIA to MongoDB to Oracle, he's watched the industry chase the wrong problems.

In this episode, Simon and Richmond get into:

- Why memory is a superset of context, not a synonym for it
- Why skills are just SOPs for agents and why that framing changes how you   build
- Why your agent's problem is almost never the model
- Why the memory engineer is the role every AI team will be hiring for next

Context got you here. Memory gets you further.

## Description

In many software projects, specifications drift away from the actual code over time. This gap can lead to outdated documentation and increased risks, especially in long-lasting business applications. A spec-driven approach ensures that the code aligns with well-defined system use cases, minimizing discrepancies over time. It sounds rigid. It simplifies.

Simon Martinelli, a seasoned consultant from Switzerland renowned in the Java community, delivers this compelling session. With over 17 years of experience working on business applications for insurance companies, government, and large enterprises, Simon shares insights into the AI Unified Process. His background and unique approach make him uniquely qualified to discuss this spec-driven strategy, backed by real-world applications.

What we cover:
• How does the AI Unified Process enhance software development?
• The role of SysML use cases in maintaining specification accuracy
• Balancing AI-driven development with traditional methods
• Concrete examples from backend logic, database access, and UI behavior
• Lessons learned and trade-offs from real customer projects

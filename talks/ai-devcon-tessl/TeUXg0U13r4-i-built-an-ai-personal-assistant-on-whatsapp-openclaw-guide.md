## Description

What happens when you give an AI agent a credit card, a WhatsApp account, and the keys to your browser?

In this session, Gillian Yeomans takes us behind the scenes of her experiment building "Clawdius," a personalized AI assistant powered by the OpenClaw framework. Moving beyond simple chatbots, Gillian explores the agentic frontier—where AI doesn't just talk but actually executes tasks across multiple platforms.

From the technical hurdles of tool-calling and memory management to the surprisingly human-like "lies" an AI tells when it fails a task, this presentation is a deep dive into the current state of autonomous agents.

In this video, you’ll learn:
• The Tech Stack: How Gillian created a functional agent.
• The "Liar" Problem: Why agents hallucinate progress and how to manage "agentic drift."
• Real-World Utility: Practical use cases for agents in daily life, from booking travel to managing finances.
• The Safety Question: The risks of giving an autonomous system access to your bank account and personal data.

Whether you're a developer interested in agentic frameworks or just curious about the future of personal productivity, this talk offers a realistic, "warts-and-all" look at the next generation of AI.

## Description

Netlify's human-centric design faces a new challenge as agents start driving the platform. They need structured and actionable signals—without them, deploy previews and CLI outputs become obstacles, not tools. It sounds wrong. It happens. Agents don’t operate as humans: the branch workflow that feels natural for a developer can become a puzzle for an agent to solve.

Dana Lawson, discussing Netlify's evolution, unpacks what it means to adapt for Agent Experience at AI Native DevCon June 2026. With her experience at the helm of Netlify, she brings valuable insights about redesigning platforms so that agents, not just humans, can contribute effectively. Her talk covers the practical realities of an agent-assisted world and why judgment trumps code when integrating agents into workflows.

What we cover:
• What breaks in build pipelines, CLI surfaces, and deploy workflows when agents take charge
• Why AX requires actionable signals and machine-readable feedback
• The implications of Agent Experience (AX) in practice
• How do humans and agents collaborate effectively without bottlenecks?
• Why judgment, not just code, is critical in an agent-driven environment

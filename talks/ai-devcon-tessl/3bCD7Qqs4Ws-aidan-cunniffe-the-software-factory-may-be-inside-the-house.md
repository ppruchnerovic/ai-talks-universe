## Description

Most software factory effort goes into dev infra: cloud agents, dev environments and review bots. Aidan Cunniffe, Member of Product Staff at OpenAI, argues the bigger opportunity may be the app platform, meaning the shared libraries, design systems, auth and query layers your agents build on. Now that code is cheap, abstractions that used to be too expensive are worth building, and they help teams ship faster, bring non-technical contributors in safely and roll out fixes across a code base. Recorded at AI DevCon New York 2026.

What we cover:
– What citizen developers are already doing differently
– Why software factories still aren't turnkey, and review is still a bottleneck
– The real goals: speed, bigger bets, new contributors and less grunt work
– Dev infra vs app platform: where your agents build
– Why abstractions that were too expensive are now worth building
– Revisiting Amazon's API mandate for the age of agents

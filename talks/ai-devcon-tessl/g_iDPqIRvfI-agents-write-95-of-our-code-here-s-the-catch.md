## Description

Agents can write almost all your code now — so why are bugs and incidents climbing right alongside adoption? Tessl researcher and engineer Amy Heineike breaks down why traditional code review can't keep up with agentic development, and what she calls the rise of the "harness engineer."

What we cover:
– Why AI coding benchmarks are getting saturated while real-world bugs and incidents are rising
– The cost vs. intelligence trade-off across today's frontier models
– What Tessl's internal skills benchmark revealed about instruction-following (spoiler: only ~70%)
– Systems thinking, analytics, and risk/ops as the three new "harness engineering" skill sets
– Inside Tessl's software factory, where ~95% of code now ships through an agent-driven pipeline

## Description

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, sits down with Harrison Chase, co-founder and CEO of LangChain, to dig into why AI agents are thriving in software development but still struggling to break through in the rest of the enterprise. Aaron breaks down the structural reasons the knowledge work gap exists, how Box is building its agent harness, and why being headless might actually drive more usage than it kills.

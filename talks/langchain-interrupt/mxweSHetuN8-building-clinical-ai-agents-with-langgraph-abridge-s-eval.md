## Description

Janie Lee, VP of Product at Abridge, walks through how a clinical AI company ships fast without compromising patient safety across 250 health systems. She covers two case studies: their core ambient note product — where misattributed symptoms or wrong dosages carry real legal and clinical consequences — and their Abridge Assistant, a unified agent that persists across the entire patient visit workflow. The talk is a detailed look at how Abridge uses LangGraph and LangSmith to run reference-free and reference-based judges, auto-calibrate LLM judges via APO, and A/B test in production at enterprise healthcare scale.

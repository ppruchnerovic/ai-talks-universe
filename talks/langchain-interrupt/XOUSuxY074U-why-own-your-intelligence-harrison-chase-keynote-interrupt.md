## Description

Harrison Chase, Co-Founder and CEO of LangChain, explains what it means to own your intelligence, the specialization a team builds into the model, the harness, and the context, why he thinks agents need to be open, compounding, and governed to get there, and points to monday.com, Harvey, and Orange as examples of companies building the system around the model. He then walks through the LangSmith stack: Managed Deep Agents, which combines the Deep Agents harness and managed infrastructure into one, plus two agents LangChain built internally, Open SWE and its GTM Agent; observability on SmithDB, with Jev as a judge, RBAC on trace inputs and outputs, LangSmith Trajectories, the new AI Search, and LangSmith Custom Apps; and LangSmith Engine, where Engine v2 tests its own fixes on LangSmith Deployment and red teams your agent. He also introduces a self-report tool that agents can call to flag what went wrong while they run, and shares that Engine has scanned over 70 million traces and detected over 21,000 issues.

This session was captured at Interrupt London, the agent conference by LangChain.

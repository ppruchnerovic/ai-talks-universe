## Description

Take a closer look at our upcoming developer toolkit designed for AI glasses - Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit. This session will highlight the development workflows and support resources available to developers, emphasizing the ease with which you can integrate AI glasses capabilities into your mobile applications. Join us to learn more about the Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit and be among the first to explore new use cases with our AI glasses by leveraging open-ear audio and cameras that capture a person’s viewpoint to craft distinctive hands-free experiences.

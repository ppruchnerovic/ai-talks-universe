## Description

Mercari, Japan's largest C2C marketplace, relies on GenAI to provide better services to its users and live up to its mission to "unleash the potential in all people."

Join Teo Zosa, Senior ML Engineer at Mercari, from the stage at FC Tokyo 2024, Weights & Biases' annual user conference, where he shares how Mercari is doubling down on GenAI productivity tools and features to help users list and sell their items.

*Recorded live at Fully Connected Tokyo on October 10, 2024.*

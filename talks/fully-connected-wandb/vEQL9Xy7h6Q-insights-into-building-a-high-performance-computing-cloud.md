*About Rahul Talari and Harsh Banwait's Session: Insights into Building a High-Performance Computing Cloud Platform*

Discover the insights behind building a high-performance computing cloud platform in this session from the Fully Connected conference.

Rahul Talari, Senior Machine Learning Engineer, and Harsh Banwait, Director of Product, both from CoreWeave, delve into the technical innovations and strategic decisions that make CoreWeave's infrastructure uniquely suited for AI and machine learning workloads. Learn how they address customer challenges, optimize hardware and software layers, and ensure seamless operations to support rapid development and deployment of large-scale models. Watch the video to explore the future of cloud computing for AI.

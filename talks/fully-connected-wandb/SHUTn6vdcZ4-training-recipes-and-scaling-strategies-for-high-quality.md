*About Natalia Vassilieva's Session On Training Recipes and Scaling Strategies for High-Quality GenAI Models*

New Generative AI models are being announced almost every week, new papers related to GenAI are being published on ArXiv every day. It might look like training a high quality large GenAI model is easy. But it is not. Training and serving these models are engineering and ML challenges, and it is very expensive.Even minor errors in model design or training procedure can result in significant waste of resources and in sub-optimal models.

At Cerebras we have trained our share of large models and learned along the way how to train high-quality models in a compute efficient way. In this talk we will share our experience and insights from training various LLMs and multi-modal models. In addition to techniques for compute efficient training of dense models, we will look into benefits of sparse training and inference on Cerebras hardware, designed to take full advantage of all types of sparsity.

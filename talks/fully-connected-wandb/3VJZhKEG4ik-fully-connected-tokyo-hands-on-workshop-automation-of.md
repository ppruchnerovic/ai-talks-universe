## Description

This hands-on workshop proposes solutions tailored to Japan's business environment, leveraging Upstage's Document Intelligence technology—which has numerous overseas implementation cases—to address the financial industry's challenge of “massive paper-based operations and personalized processing structures.”
By utilizing generative AI models equipped with context understanding capabilities beyond OCR and the latest LLM technology, we aim to make your existing data LLM-ready, accelerating financial document processing and improving accuracy.

Upstage is a global AI startup providing generative AI solutions that combine security and high performance, backed by investments from Amazon and AMD.
The company has recently released a next-generation large language model specialized for Japanese, delivering highly accurate and reliable AI tailored to the Japanese context.
Leading companies in regulated industries such as insurance, law, and healthcare are leveraging Upstage's AI technology to transform complex, unstructured documents into practical business value.

Presented by Upstage Beomseok Han

*About Richard Socher's Session On The Future Of Trust in LLMs*

Richard Socher, CEO and founder of You.com and AIX Ventures, will share insights from his journey of a decade of research in AI and NLP, from the invention of prompt engineering to founding You.com, the first AI Assistant to integrate an LLM with live web access for accurate, up-to-date answers with citations.

Richard will discuss how to tackle the biggest challenges facing LLMs, from hallucinations to generic responses. Gain insight into the potential for these advancements to be adopted by other LLM-based platforms.

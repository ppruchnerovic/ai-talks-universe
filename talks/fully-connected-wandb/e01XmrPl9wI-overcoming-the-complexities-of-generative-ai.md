*About Kari Ann Briski's Session On Overcoming the Complexities of Generative AI*

Kari Ann Briski, VP of AI Software Product Management at NVIDIA, takes you through the journey of generative AI at the Fully Connected conference hosted by Weights & Biases. In her talk, "Overcoming the Complexities of Generative AI,"

Kari Ann explores NVIDIA's NIMMs platform and its role in simplifying AI deployment. She discusses the evolution of AI technologies, the importance of community model support, and the critical aspects of AI optimization and security.

Watch this session to learn how NVIDIA is paving the way for seamless AI integration in enterprise applications.

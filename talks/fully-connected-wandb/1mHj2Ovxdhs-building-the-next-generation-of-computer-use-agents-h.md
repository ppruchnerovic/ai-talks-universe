## Description

"In this talk from Fully Connected London, Kai Yuan, Agentic AI Research Team Lead at H Company, introduces Surfer 2, a next-generation computer-use agent that moves AI from models that ""know"" to agents that ""do."" He presents Surfer 2's unified architecture that operates across desktop, web, and mobile by separating strategic planning from tactical execution. Integrating third-party frontier models with H's Holo1.5, Surfer 2 has achieved state-of-the-art results on four major benchmarks and exceeds human performance on desktop and mobile. Kai also discusses Holo2, their next-generation model designed to deliver breakthrough performance at a fraction of the cost.

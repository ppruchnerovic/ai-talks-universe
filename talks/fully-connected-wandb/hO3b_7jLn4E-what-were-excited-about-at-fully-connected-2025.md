## Description

When planning our yearly AI and ML conference, Fully Connected, we constantly ask ourselves: “Is this the kind of event I’d actually want to attend?” We’ve never been big on flashy predictions. Instead, we focus on practical, real-world work happening in the field. We invite the developers and teams behind those solutions to come together and share not what might work five years from now, but what’s actually working today.

Hear from our Co-Founders about what we’re excited about this year, where we think the agent space is heading, and stick around for a few hot takes near the end.

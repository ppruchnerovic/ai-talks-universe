*About Ali Demirci's Session On Continuous Deployment with Weights & Biases Automations*

In this session from the Fully Connected conference in San Francisco, Ali Demirci, Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Rad AI, delves into "Continuous Deployment with Weights & Biases Automations."

Ali discusses Rad AI's journey in enhancing radiology practices using advanced machine learning models and automation tools. He covers the critical aspects of frequent deployment, researcher productivity, and maintaining high operational quality. Learn how Rad AI is setting new standards in the medical field by improving radiology report generation and overall workflow efficiency.

## Description

This workshop explains how to build and improve customer support AI, led by Karakuri Inc., a company with extensive experience in developing such systems. Participants will practically learn the entire process of designing, implementing, evaluating, and improving AI agents—a methodology refined by Karakuri's rich development experience—while actually deploying customer support AI. The key point is moving beyond “mere accuracy evaluation” to score intangible qualities like customer service stance and branding. We delve into areas not fully captured by existing evaluation frameworks like RAGAS, exploring how to make AI more like a “seasoned customer service representative.

Presented by Koki Obinata (Karakuri)

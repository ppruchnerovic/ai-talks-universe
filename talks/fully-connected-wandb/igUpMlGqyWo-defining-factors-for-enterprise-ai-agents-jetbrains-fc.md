## Description

At Fully Connected London, Vadim Briliantov, Technical Lead at JetBrains, dives into what has emerged as the defining factors for enterprise AI agent success: namely, the importance of fault tolerance, predictability, and cost-efficiency. He introduces the JetBrains Koog Framework as a valuable tool for enterprise AI agents, and how the combination of Koog + W&B Weave has delivered unprecedented predictability and scalability for enterprises building their own suites of AI Agents.

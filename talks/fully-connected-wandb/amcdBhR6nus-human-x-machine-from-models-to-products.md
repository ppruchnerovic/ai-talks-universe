*About Seth Levine's Session: Human x Machine: From Models to Products*

In his session "Human x Machine: From Models to Products" at the Fully Connected conference, Seth Levine, Lead Machine Learning Scientist at Loris, explores the impactful integration of machine learning in business.

Seth highlights the significance of addressing business needs and how Loris harnesses AI to improve customer service interactions. Learn about the practical applications of machine learning in reducing costs and enhancing value, and how human expertise combined with AI can create powerful solutions. Watch the video for an in-depth look at the intersection of human and machine capabilities.

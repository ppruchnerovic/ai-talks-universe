## Description

Sponsored Keynote: Unlocking AI at Scale for PyTorch Developers - Jeff Gehlhaar, Senior Vice President of Engineering, Qualcomm

Developer-friendly AI inference optimized for performance, energy, and cost is key to unlock AI at scale for developers, industries, and consumers. In this talk, we’ll explore the latest trends driving inference to the edge, the cloud, and everywhere in between to unlock personalization, privacy, and performance. We’ll dive into Qualcomm’s flexible software stack, showcasing the ways developers can deploy PyTorch models on Qualcomm processors and tap into the opportunity to deploy AI across billions of devices. We will make an exciting announcement, building off our efforts to make it easier for developers to utilize Qualcomm Hexagon NPU to its full potential.

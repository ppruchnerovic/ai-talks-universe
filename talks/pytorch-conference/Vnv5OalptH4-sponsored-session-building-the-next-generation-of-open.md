## Description

Sponsored Session: Building the Next Generation of Open Source AI Tooling - Travis Oliphant, OpenTeams

The AI ecosystem is built on Open Source. We face new challenges at all levels of the AI stack. From low level gaps in the compiler ecosystem to the data layer and AI software environment management. Additionally, we need stronger solutions for privacy, security, and collaboration.

Join our breakout session about how we’re evolving our open source efforts with our partners, to build the next generation of AI tooling.

Hear directly from Travis Oliphant, the creator of Numpy, Scipy, and Numba on what the future holds and why open source is still foundational to everything we do.

# Growing and Cultivating Strong Machine Learning Engineers

**Vivek Gupta**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `InfoQ Dev Summit Boston 2025` · `2025` · `38 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/ml-skills/) · [Conference site](https://qconferences.com/)

## Description

Vivek Gupta shares best practices for managing and mentoring ML engineers, from early-career development to senior leadership growth. He breaks down the crucial, distinct skills for Production Machine Learning, including data/model management, building training pipelines, LLM prompt evaluation, privacy/security, and integrating human-in-the-loop processes for reliable, scalable AI systems.

Vivek Gupta obtained his BS in Math & Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University and his MS in Computer Science from Indiana University. Before joining Microsoft (through the Nokia acquisition in 2014) he worked in a variety of industries doing product development and consulting. At Nokia and Microsoft the focus has been on building Machine Learning solutions at scale on Azure.

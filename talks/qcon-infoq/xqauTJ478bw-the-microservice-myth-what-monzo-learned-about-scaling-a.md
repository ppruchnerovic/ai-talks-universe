## Description

Monzo's journey from a handful of microservices to nearly 2,000 is a masterclass in building for scale. Principal Engineer Suhail Patel shares the engineering leadership lessons learned on this high-growth path.

This isn't just about microservices; it's a deep dive into the sociotechnical challenges of scaling an organization. Discover how Monzo’s platform team drives innovation, why you need an MVA (Minimum Viable Architecture), and the core leadership skills required to empower a rapidly growing engineering team.

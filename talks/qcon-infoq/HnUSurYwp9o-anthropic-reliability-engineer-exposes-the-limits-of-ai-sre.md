## Description

Can AI actually handle on-call incident response, or is AIOps mostly hype? Alex Palcuie from Anthropic’s AI Reliability team shares a candid look at how Claude is used during real production outages - and why it isn't replacing human SREs anytime soon.

In this InfoQ talk, Alex breaks down where LLMs are superhuman (like log parsing and anomaly investigation) and where they fail dangerously (such as confusing correlation with causation during complex incidents). Using John Boyd’s OODA Loop framework, he walks through real-world incident case studies, evaluates the SAE autonomy levels applied to software engineering, and outlines 5 practical patterns for integrating LLMs into your incident management workflow today.

## Description

Sally O'Malley explains the unique observability challenges of LLMs and provides a reproducible, open-source stack for monitoring AI workloads. She demonstrates deploying Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, and Tempo with vLLM and Llama Stack on Kubernetes. Learn to monitor critical cost, performance, and quality signals for business-critical AI applications.

Sally Ann O'Malley is a principal software engineer at Red Hat, where she has made significant contributions to teams within OpenShift and the Emerging Technologies organization. For the past decade, she has been instrumental in integrating cutting-edge tools and ideas into Red Hat's portfolio. Sally shares her expertise as an instructor within Boston University’s College of Data Science.

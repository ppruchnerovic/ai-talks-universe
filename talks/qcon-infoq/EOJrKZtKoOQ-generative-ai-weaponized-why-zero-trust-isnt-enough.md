## Description

Wes Reisz (QCon AI New York 2025 Chair) sits down with Shuman Ghosemajumder (CEO of Reken, Ex-Google Trust & Safety) to discuss the erosion of digital trust. They explore "negative day" attacks and why traditional intuition about scale breaks down with GenAI.

Shuman details how attackers use LLMs to bypass authentication and how the "Gell-Mann Amnesia" effect blinds users to hallucinations. The conversation covers specific techniques for architects: behavioral telemetry, game theory in defense, and designing systems that eliminate default trust.

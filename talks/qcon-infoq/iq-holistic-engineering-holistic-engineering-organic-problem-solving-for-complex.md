# Holistic Engineering: Organic Problem Solving for Complex Evolving Systems

**Vanessa Formicola**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon London 2025` · `2025` · `48 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/holistic-engineering/) · [Conference site](https://qconferences.com/)

## Description

Vanessa Formicola discusses how "invisible" forces shape our code and architecture. She shares patterns like "Cirque du Soleil coding" and "Shared Kitchen Sinks," explaining why technical problems often have social roots. Architects and leaders will learn how to use Social Decision Records (SDRs) and holistic modeling to make the implicit explicit and drive success.

Vanessa Formicola is the Principal Architect and Head of TASC (Technology, Architecture, and Software Craft) at Prima. She brings over 15 years of industry experience, including roles at Microsoft and ThoughtWorks, and more than seven years in senior leadership positions.

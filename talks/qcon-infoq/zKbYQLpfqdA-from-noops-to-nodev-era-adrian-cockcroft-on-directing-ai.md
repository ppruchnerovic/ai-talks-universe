## Description

The creator of "NoOps" just introduced "NoDev"!

In this InfoQ video, cloud pioneer Adrian Cockcroft (former Netflix Cloud Architect & AWS VP) explains why he has returned to coding - not by typing, but by directing swarms of AI agents.

Cockcroft argues that we are shifting from Application Development to Platform-Led Development, where human engineers build the guardrails and "Director Agents" manage the output of AI swarms.

If you are an engineering leader or architect, this is your roadmap for the next 5 years of software delivery.

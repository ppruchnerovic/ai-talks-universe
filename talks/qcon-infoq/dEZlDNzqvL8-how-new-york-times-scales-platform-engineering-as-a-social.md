## Description

How do you scale an organization when technical tools aren't enough to solve human complexity?

Lesley Cordero, Staff Engineer at the New York Times, explains why platform engineering is actually a "sociotechnical" solution to the organizational debt created by rapid growth. Drawing from her experience in reliability platforms, she breaks down the "pendulum of tension" between developer speed and system reliability, and why relying on "heroic" individual efforts is a signal of systemic failure.

## Description

LLMs hallucinate architecture. Static analysis doesn't.

This talk shows how grounding AI coding agents in deterministic, graph-based architectural context - rather than probabilistic inference - cuts token consumption by 50%, doubles output accuracy, and compressed a seven-year mainframe modernization timeline to just three years. Reliability at enterprise scale isn't magic; it's determinism.

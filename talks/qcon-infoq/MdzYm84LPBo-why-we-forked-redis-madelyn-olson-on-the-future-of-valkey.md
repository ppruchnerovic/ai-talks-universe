## Description

When Redis changed its license in 2024, the community rallied to create Valkey. But the team didn't just fork the code - they optimized it. In this deep dive, Madelyn Olson (Principal SDE at Amazon ElastiCache) explains the low-level engineering challenges of modernizing a codebase from 2009.

# Foundation Models for Ranking: Challenges, Successes, and Lessons Learned

**Moumita Bhattacharya**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon London 2025` · `2025` · `47 min`

## Description

Moumita Bhattacharya discusses the evolution of Netflix’s ranking systems, from the multi-model architecture to a Unified Contextual Recommender (UniCoRn). She explains how they built a task-agnostic User Foundation Model to capture long-term member preferences. Learn how they solve system challenges like high-throughput inference and the tradeoff between relevance and personalization.

Moumita Bhattacharya leads the Foundation Models Development team at Netflix. She has been actively serving as Program Committees for WebConf and is a reviewer for conferences such as RecSys, SIGIR, WebConf, AAAI, and various journals. Moumita is also an adjunct faculty in the Data Science Institute (DSI) of the University of Delaware.

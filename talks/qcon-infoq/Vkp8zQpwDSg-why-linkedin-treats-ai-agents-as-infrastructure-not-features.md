## Description

In this episode of the InfoQ Podcast, Wes Reisz sits down with Karthik Ramgopal and Prince Valluri from LinkedIn to dismantle the common myths about adopting AI in software engineering. They reveal how LinkedIn moved beyond "hermit developers" and siloed scripts to build a unified Platform Engineering approach for multi-agentic systems.

The conversation dives deep into the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the difference between "foreground" (IDE-based) and "background" (asynchronous) agents, and why specifying intent via specs is the future of software delivery. If you are a Software Architect or Engineering Leader, this is your blueprint for secure, compliant, and observable AI at scale.

## Description

How do you maintain system performance when AI agents ship 70% more PRs and your user base scales to 900 million weekly active users?

In this talk, Martin Spier (Head of ChatGPT Performance Team at OpenAI, ex-Netflix) breaks down the compound impact of agentic development on system architecture. As developer workflows shift toward multi-threaded AI execution, traditional performance engineering must evolve. Martin reveals how OpenAI scales non-GPU infrastructure, restructures latency budgets, and deploys autonomous performance agents to fix regressions before production breaks.

# Shine Bright as an IC: Growing Yourself as Your Company Grows

**Suhail Patel**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon London 2025` · `2025` · `50 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/staff-professional-growth/) · [Conference site](https://qconferences.com/)

## Description

Suhail Patel discusses how senior engineers and tech leaders must go beyond technical mastery to achieve staff-plus growth. He explains how to leverage one-to-ones, intentional interviewing (as learning opportunities), and visible writing to build influence and your network. Get practical advice on making ambitious bets and fixing organizational cracks to grow your team and company.

Suhail Patel is a Staff Engineer at Monzo focused on building the Core Platform. His role involves building and maintaining Monzo's infrastructure which spans nearly two thousand microservices and leverages key infrastructure components like Kubernetes, Cassandra, Etcd and more.

# Advanced Message Queuing Politics

**John O'Hara**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon London 2025` · `2025` · `54 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/advanced-message-queuing/) · [Conference site](https://qconferences.com/)

## Description

John O'Hara shares the compelling 18-year saga of the Advanced Message Queuing Protocol (AMQP), detailing its evolution from a JPMorgan business problem to an international ISO standard. He explains the technical and political challenges, emphasizing the critical role of open standards in preventing vendor lock-in and fostering robust, universal messaging solutions.

John O'Hara is a fintech entrepreneur, advisor, and angel investor. He holds non-executive director positions at Adaptive Financial Technology and Taskize, the latter he co-founded and sold to Euroclear. A past JPMorgan Distinguished Engineer, John also served as Chief Architect for Merrill Lynch, Bank of America, BP Trading, and RBS/NatWest. He is the inventor of AMQP.

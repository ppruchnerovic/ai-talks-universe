## Description

David Grizzanti shares a roadmap for senior software developers, architects, and engineering leaders. He discusses how to overcome the "performance cliff" after a promotion, navigate ambiguity, grow your influence, and shape engineering culture. He explains how to design a fulfilling career by understanding what truly motivates you beyond a job title.

David Grizzanti is a Principal Engineer at Google focused on improving developer productivity by enabling engineering teams to more effectively and efficiently build, test, integrate and deploy software. Previously he was a Distinguished Engineer at Comcast. His areas of interests include improving infrastructure automation, open source communities, and engineering leadership.

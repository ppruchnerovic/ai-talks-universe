## Description

The relational database wasn't built for the way we write software today. In this InfoQ video, MongoDB leaders Andrew Davidson and Akshat Vig reveal the "unfiltered" history of the document model - from being mocked as a "web-scale" meme to becoming the backbone of the global economy.

Building a mission-critical data platform requires more than just code; it requires a "contrarian stance" and the stomach to fail in public. Learn why the biggest bottleneck in modern engineering isn't storage cost - it's developer cognitive load - and how to navigate the "Big Bang" moments of technological shifts.

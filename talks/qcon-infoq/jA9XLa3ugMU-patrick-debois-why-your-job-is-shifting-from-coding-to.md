## Description

The "Father of DevOps" returns to redefine the next era of software engineering!

As AI moves from simple tab-completion to autonomous agentic workflows, the role of the senior developer is undergoing a radical unbundling. In this deep dive, Patrick Debois (co-author of The DevOps Handbook) explores the 4 emerging patterns of "AI Native" development and why the future of engineering isn't about writing code - it's about Intent Engineering and Review.

Whether you are a Software Architect worried about technical debt or an Engineering Leader managing "vibe coders," this video provides a roadmap for navigating the "period of chaos" in the AI tool landscape.

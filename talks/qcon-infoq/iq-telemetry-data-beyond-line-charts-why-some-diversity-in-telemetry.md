# Beyond Line Charts: Why Some Diversity in Telemetry Visualization Is Long Overdue

**Yao Yue**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon San Francisco 2025` · `2025` · `42 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/telemetry-data/) · [Conference site](https://qconferences.com/)

## Description

Yao Yue discusses the fundamental limitations of standard line charts for system observability. Drawing from 15 years of operating large-scale systems, she shares how engineering leaders and software architects can transform telemetry data - moving beyond simple time-series defaults - to build visualizations that directly answer critical capacity, latency, and fleet-sizing questions.

Yao Yue is a platform engineer with specialties in caching, distributed systems, and performance engineering. She worked at Twitter for 12 years, first led the Cache Team and later created the Performance Team. After November 2022, she co-founded IOP Systems, a company that is working to improve software efficiency and reliability via smart performance engineering, with her teammates from Twitter.

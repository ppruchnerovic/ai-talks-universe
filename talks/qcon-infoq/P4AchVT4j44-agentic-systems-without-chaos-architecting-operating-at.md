## Description

The "Chatbot" era is over. The "Agentic" era is here - and it’s non-deterministic.

In this episode of the Next Generation Playbook, Shweta Vohra sits down with Joseph Stein to demystify the shift from simple automation to autonomous agentic systems. Moving beyond the hype of "vibe coding," Joseph Stein  shares his experience managing a platform that supports thousands of use cases and billions of tokens.

They delve deep into the architectural boundaries necessary to prevent autonomy from devolving into enterprise chaos, the reality of "Agent-to-Agent" (A2A) communication, and why your token bill is the new cloud bill.

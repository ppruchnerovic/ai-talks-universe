## Description

Security and Architecture have finally "married" in the era of Zero Trust and DevSecOps - but the honeymoon is over. Shana Dacres-Lawrence explores the "betrayals" that occur when structural weaknesses, assumed loyalty, and misplaced trust lead to multi-million dollar failures like CrowdStrike and Change Healthcare.

In this InfoQ video, Shana breaks down the 3 categories of betrayal and provides 5 actionable defense strategies to ensure your systems remain resilient and your teams stay aligned. Learn how to bridge the gap between "getting it done" and "keeping it secure."

## Description

In this InfoQ video, Meta Engineer Jinsong Yu reveals the decade-long journey to build Orion, the world's most advanced AR glasses. For senior developers and architects, this isn't a product demo; it’s a breakdown of extreme hardware-software co-design.

Learn why Meta abandoned the traditional SoC (System on Chip) in favor of a distributed cluster of 11 microcontrollers, how they solved the "thermal hotspot" problem, and why they had to build a custom OS to handle EMG (Electromyography) and world-locked rendering at 90Hz.

## Description

Is your legacy monolith causing you to miss SLAs? In this episode, Muzeeb Muhammad (Senior Manager at JPMorgan Chase) reveals how they decoupled massive financial systems by migrating from Mainframes to an Event-Driven Architecture (EDA) using Kafka.

Discover the specific strategies used to modernize banking platforms without breaking them, including the "Strangler Fig" pattern, asynchronous processing, and the role of AI in engineering observability. If you are a Software Architect or Senior Engineer dealing with legacy debt, this is the roadmap you need.

The views expressed in this discussion are Muzeeb’s own and based on industry experience, not representative of any specific employer.

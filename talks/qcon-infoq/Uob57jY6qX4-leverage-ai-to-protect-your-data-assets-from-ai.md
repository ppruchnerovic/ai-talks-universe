## Description

AI accelerates development - and data leaks. PII hides in nested NoSQL schemas, slips into vector stores, and surfaces in places no one intended.

This talk shows how schema registries, automated PII scanning, and deterministic masking pipelines let you harness AI safely, by governing your data before AI governs it for you.

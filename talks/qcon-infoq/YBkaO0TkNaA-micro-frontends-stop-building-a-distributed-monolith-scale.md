## Description

Stop building a distributed monolith! You're not designing a new architecture, you're just assembling libraries—and it’s slowing down your teams.

In this InfoQ video, AWS Principal Specialist and Micro-Frontends expert Luca Mezzalira breaks down the critical architectural, organizational, and technical decisions required to achieve true frontend scale and fast flow.

Learn the essential difference between a component and a micro-frontend, why reusability is a form of coupling, and the Micro-Frontends Decisions Framework that 100+ teams have successfully used. Luca also unveils the open-source Frontend Discovery Service pattern he co-created to finally enable true independent deployment and canary releases for the frontend.

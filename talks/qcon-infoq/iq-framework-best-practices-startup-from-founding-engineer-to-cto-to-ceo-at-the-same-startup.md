# From Founding Engineer to CTO to CEO – at the Same Startup

**Trisha Ballakur**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon San Francisco 2025` · `2025` · `34 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/framework-best-practices-startup/) · [Conference site](https://qconferences.com/)

## Description

Trisha Ballakur discusses her journey from a backend software engineer to CTO and CEO, using her startup Pointz as a case study. She explains how to implement bottom-up customer discovery to find product-market fit, effectively delegate to global contractors to reduce build times, customize open-source repos like Valhalla, and apply engineering test-case models to business development.

Trisha Ballakur is a Gen-Z tech leader dedicated to impactful advancements in mobile app development and AI. With experience as a former startup CTO and in engineering roles at Adobe and Nasdaq, Trisha applies her expertise to drive positive change. With a B.S. in Computer Science from Brown, Trisha founded Pointz to make urban biking safer and more accessible.

## Description

Kenneth Harris, a NASA veteran who worked on the James Webb Space Telescope,  shares how hardware and software engineering combine to make the $13 billion observatory function. He explains how the technology mimics the human eye to process infrared light and reveals how software engineers use data packets to transform raw data into stunning, full-color images.

Kenneth Harris is a Senior Project Engineer for The Aerospace Corporation contributing to both DoD and NASA payload missions. In this role, Dr. Harris is responsible for developing procedures and implementing processes to defend on orbit satellites from physical and cyber threats.

## Description

How do you move from "Ivory Tower" architecture to empowered, fast-flowing teams?

Peter Hunter & Elena Stojmilova share a case study on moving from centralized "ivory tower" architecture to a decentralized Advice Process. They detail how they used Team Topologies, Domain-Driven Design (context mapping), and Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) to empower six engineering teams to make their own production decisions while maintaining alignment through an Architectural Advisory Forum.

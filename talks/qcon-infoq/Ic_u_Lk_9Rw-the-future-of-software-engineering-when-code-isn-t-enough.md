## Description

As AI coding agents automate routine programming, how do senior engineers, software architects, and engineering leaders continue to deliver true value? Ben Greene breaks down 5 vital startup mindsets every tech leader needs to survive and thrive in the era of automated code.

In this InfoQ talk, Ben explores why efficiency increases software demand (Jevons Paradox), how AI accelerates unmaintainable technical debt, and why human qualities like empathy, systems thinking, and attacking hard problems first matter now more than ever.

## Description

Parquet and traditional NVMe data pipelines are slowing down your GPU analytics and ML training workloads. In this deep dive, Onur Satici (SpiralDB / Linux Foundation) breaks down how Vortex - an open-source, fast-path columnar file format - streams data directly from S3 to GPUs at up to 60 Gbps, eliminating CPU decompression bottlenecks and saving GPU compute cycles.

Discover how Vortex decouples logical and physical types, leverages cascading encodings for compute on compressed data, and enables zero-copy execution with Apache Arrow, DataFusion, DuckDB, and Polars.

# Platform Engineering: Lessons from the Rise and Fall of eBay Velocity

**Randy Shoup**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon San Francisco 2025` · `2025` · `49 min`

## Description

Randy Shoup discusses the "Velocity Initiative," a transformation that doubled engineering productivity and modernized eBay’s DORA metrics. He shares the technical playbook used to scale 4,500 services while explaining why even elite engineering execution can’t save a company hampered by waterfall planning, risk aversion, and a "pathological" culture of fear.

Randy Shoup has spent more than three decades building distributed systems and high performing teams, and has worked as a senior technology leader at eBay, Google, Stitch Fix, and WeWork. He coaches CTOs and advises companies. He talks a lot -- sometimes at conferences about software -- and is interested in the nexus of culture, technology, and organization.

## Description

Coding agents can write code, but they don’t know how your company debugs, migrates or sets up a repo. Ajay Prakash shows how 600+ MCP playbooks gave agents that knowledge for 8,000 daily users.

Ajay Prakash is a software engineer at LinkedIn with 14 years of experience. He has spent the last four years on LLMs, context engineering and AI agents, and previously led AI platform and product work for LinkedIn Sales Navigator.

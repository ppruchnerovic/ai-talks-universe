You can replicate microservices, duplicate squads, and automate pipelines - but you cannot copy-paste trust.

When software engineering teams scale rapidly, technical architecture rarely breaks first. Instead, "human latency" spikes, decision-making slows down by 10x, and communication collapses into toxic silos.

In this InfoQ video, Charlotte de Jong Schouwenburg breaks down the behavioral scalability crisis using data from the LeanIX growth case study and Google’s Project Aristotle.

## Description

Are your microservices battles leaving you frustrated? Tom Akehurst (creator of WireMock) dives into the harsh reality: the promised land of decoupled services often leads to heavy interconnections, broken dev environments, and painfully slow productivity.

This InfoQ video is your blueprint for fixing the microservices decoupling problem. Learn the advanced techniques (observability, contract testing, and realistic simulation) that move you from fighting fragile environments to shipping value faster.

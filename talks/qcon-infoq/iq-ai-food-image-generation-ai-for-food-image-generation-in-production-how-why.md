## Description

Iaroslav Amerkhanov shares the story of building an AI-powered image generation system at Delivery Hero. He explains the technical decisions, including model fine-tuning, architecture design, and cost optimization, that enabled the team to successfully scale the project and prove a positive business impact.

Iaroslav Amerkhanov pioneered projects in Food Science at Delivery Hero and is now focused on generative AI solutions. He previously founded an EdTech startup and co-founded a sentiment analysis platform.

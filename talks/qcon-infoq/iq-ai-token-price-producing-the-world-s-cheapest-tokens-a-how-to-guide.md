# Producing the World's Cheapest Tokens: A How-to Guide

**Meryem Arik**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon San Francisco 2025` · `2025` · `30 min`

## Description

Meryem Arik discusses strategies for designing low-cost LLM inference architectures for high-volume, non-real-time workloads. She explains how software architects and engineering leaders can achieve order-of-magnitude cost reductions by making critical trade-offs across hardware, inference runtimes, speculative decoding, and smart queue reordering.

Meryem Arik is cofounder and CEO of Doubleword, a self-hosted AI inference platform. An alumna of Oxford University, Meryem studied Theoretical Physics and Philosophy. She frequently speaks at leading conferences, including TEDx and QCon, sharing insights on inference technology and enterprise AI. Meryem has been recognized as a Forbes 30 Under 30 honoree for her contributions to the AI field.

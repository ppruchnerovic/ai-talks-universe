## Description

As AI moves from augmentation to full automation, the stakes for privacy and security have never been higher. In this InfoQ video, privacy expert Katharine Jarmul breaks down why current AI "guardrails" are easier to bypass than you think and why senior engineers cannot rely on model providers to solve privacy for them.

Learn the architectural realities of overparameterization, why models "memorize" sensitive data, and how to build a culture of psychological safety to catch incidents before they scale.

# Panel: Taking Architecture out of the Echo Chamber

**Andrew Harmel-Law, Cat Morris, Diana Montalion, Shana Dacres-Lawrence, Vanessa Formicola, Elena Stojmilova, Peter Hunter**

`QCon / InfoQ Dev Summit` · `QCon London 2025` · `2025` · `48 min`

[Watch the recording](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/panel-complexity-architecture/) · [Conference site](https://qconferences.com/)

## Description

Andrew Harmel-Law and a panel of expert architects discuss the shifting practice of architecture in 2025. They explain strategies for communicating technical debt to stakeholders, the benefits of decentralized decision-making through ADRs, and the career paths of modern leaders. The panel shares insights on bridging the gap between mobile and backend teams to ensure a holistic system.

Andrew Harmel-Law is Tech Principal @Thoughtworks. Cat Morris is Product Manager @Syntasso. Diana Montalion is Founder @Mentrix Group. Shana Dacres-Lawrence is Senior Principal Architect @6point6. Vanessa Formicola is Principal Architect and Head of TASC @Prima. Elena Stojmilova is Technical Lead @Open GI. Peter Hunter is Head of R&D, Tech Architect @OpenGI.

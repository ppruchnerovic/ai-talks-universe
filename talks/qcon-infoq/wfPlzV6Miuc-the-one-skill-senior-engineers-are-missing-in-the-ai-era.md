## Description

Is AI just a better autocomplete, or is it your newest teammate? In this episode of the InfoQ Podcast, Thomas Betts sits down with Olivia McVicker to dismantle the myth that AI will replace developers. Instead, they explore the shift from "AI-Assisted" to "AI-Driven" development, where coding agents and sub-agents handle the tedium, allowing senior engineers to focus on high-level problem solving.

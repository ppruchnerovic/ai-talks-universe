## Description

Do you feel like you're constantly pushing for "more" at work, yet true productivity feels out of reach? This isn't just a feeling – it's a paradox, and it's killing creativity and code quality in software development.
In this episode, Holly Cummins a seasoned engineer from the Quarkus team, unpacks the science behind developer productivity. She reveals why traditional metrics like "lines of code" are detrimental and how activities that look unproductive (like showering or walking) are actually crucial for problem-solving and innovation.

## Description

Ever deployed an LLM to production, only to have it inexplicably break? You're not alone. This video uncovers the hard truths about evaluating Large Language Models in real-world scenarios, going beyond the misleading simplicity of single metrics.

Join us as we explore critical lessons learned from real-world LLM platform development, including why semantic similarity and "LLM as a judge" fall short, the necessity of treating models as observable systems, and how to build metrics that directly address user issues and business value. Discover the crawl, walk, run methodology for LLM metric maturity and avoid the pitfalls of dashboard overload.

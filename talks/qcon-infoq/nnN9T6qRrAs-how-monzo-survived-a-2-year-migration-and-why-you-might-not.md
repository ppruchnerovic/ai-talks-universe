## Description

Can a technical migration actually break your engineering culture? Sophie Koonin (Staff Engineer & Web Lead at Monzo) pulls back the curtain on Monzo’s grueling two-year journey from Flow to TypeScript.

This isn't a talk about code - it's a tactical guide for senior engineers and architects on navigating the "bends in the road" that turn a simple refactor into an organizational marathon.

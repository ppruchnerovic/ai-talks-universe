## Description

Presenter: Lorenz Jakober, Head of Product Marketing, Multi-cloud Security Products, Google Cloud, Google

Decades of threat detection, investigation, and response initiatives over-relied on DIY approaches and delivered mostly underwhelming results. This session will introduce Google’s paradigm for modern security operations and demonstrate how recent advancements in applied threat intel, automation, and AI give defenders a chance to flip the script and generate more security outcomes with less effort.

## Description

Presenter: Elie Bursztein, Google & DeepMind AI Cybersecurity Technical and Research Lead, Google DeepMind

From understanding complex instructions, to generating code, to tackling complex problems, Large Languages Models (LLM) exhibit exceptional abilities that are rapidly transforming many aspects of society, including cybersecurity. This talk will draw upon expertise at Google and examine the ways LLMs capabilities are reshaping cybersecurity from both an offensive and defensive standpoint.

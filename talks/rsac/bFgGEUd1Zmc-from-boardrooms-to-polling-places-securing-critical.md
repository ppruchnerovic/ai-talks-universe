## Description

Presenter: Nadir Izrael, Armis

With geopolitical wars continuing globally, elections in 50+ countries, and cybercriminals enhancing their capabilities with AI, organizations, and governments are in the crosshairs of exponential threats. This session covers why AI-powered asset intelligence is critical to proactively strengthening defensive cybersecurity postures to protect entire attack surfaces and manage cyber risk exposure.

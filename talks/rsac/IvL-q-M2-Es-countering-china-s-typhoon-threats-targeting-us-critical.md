## Description

China’s state-sponsored cyber campaigns—like Volt, Flax, Salt, and Silk Typhoon—are escalating, targeting Western infrastructure. This panel explores how public-private collaboration, informed by cutting-edge industry insight and government coordination, is essential to detect, disrupt, and defend against these evolving threats.

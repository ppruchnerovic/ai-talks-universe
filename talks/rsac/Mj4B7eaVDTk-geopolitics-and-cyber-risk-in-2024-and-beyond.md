Sarah Cleveland, ExtraHop

This session will bring together experts at the intersection of national security, cybersecurity, and international relations to give security leaders a crash course in geopolitics and its impact on cybersecurity. Attendees will get “intelligence briefings” on three major world conflicts and their implications for cybersecurity.

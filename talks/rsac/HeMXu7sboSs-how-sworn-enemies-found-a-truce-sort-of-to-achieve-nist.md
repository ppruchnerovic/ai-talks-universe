## Description

Presenters:
Jonall Cobble, Cybersecurity , On the Terrace Tech
Jason Luce, Chief Technology Officer, Paperless Parts

For cyber and engineering teams, it's challenging to achieve alignment on NIST requirements and commit the time and budget to accomplish those goals. It is complicated to effectively translate compliance and security controls to engineering implementation. This session will focus on the journey a startup SaaS company took to gain 3rd party compliance attestation of NIST controls implementation.

## Description

Jenny Brock, Sr. Manager Innovation Programs , RSAC
Roya Gordon, Interim Chief Information Security Officer, ENGIE North America
Tansel Ismail, Principal, Energy Impact Partners (EIP)
Tony Surak, Go to Market Partner, DataTribe

Join us at the RSAC Innovation Showcase – a monthly virtual program designed to highlight innovation in cybersecurity. On May 28, we focus on the latest strategies and techniques for OT cyber readiness and protection. A panel of experts will discuss the evolution OT security readiness, the emergence of evidentiary AI in OT security strategies, the use of automation in OT cyber protection, and the benefits and challenges of using automated code remediation to address security vulnerabilities. The panel then hears from two up and coming companies in the OT cybersecurity space.

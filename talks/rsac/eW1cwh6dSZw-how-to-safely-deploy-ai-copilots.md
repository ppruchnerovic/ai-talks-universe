## Description

Presenter: Brian Vecci, Field CTO, Varonis

Most organizations are evaluating AI productivity tools like Microsoft Copilot, but the biggest reasons it is not yet widely deployed are security and privacy. AI makes it easy to find and use enterprise data, ensuring users don’t have access to what they don’t need is more important than it’s ever been. This session will show an operational plan to deploy Microsoft Copilot quickly while minimizing risk.

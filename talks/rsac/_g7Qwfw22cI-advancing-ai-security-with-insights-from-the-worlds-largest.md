## Description

Presenter: David Haber, Co-Founder and CEO, Lakera AI

AI security is crucial for current and future digital safety, preventing sensitive data leaks and system compromises. Without robust security mechanisms, confidential information and intellectual property are at risk. This session will share insights from the world’s largest AI red team and discuss what gamifying AI red teaming can teach us about safeguarding AI systems.

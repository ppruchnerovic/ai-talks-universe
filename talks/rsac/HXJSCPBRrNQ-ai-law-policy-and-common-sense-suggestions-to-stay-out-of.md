## Description

Presenter: Behnam Dayanim, Partner, Global Head, Digital Commerce & Gaming, Orrick Herrington & Sutcliffe LLP

AI is here. Across industries, technologists and businesses are developing new use cases for AI. In so many areas, the law lags the technology, but what are the legal considerations? This session will explore what ethical, legal, and policy considerations must go into AI and what governance structures and benchmarks can help maximize a successful implementation and minimize potential risk.

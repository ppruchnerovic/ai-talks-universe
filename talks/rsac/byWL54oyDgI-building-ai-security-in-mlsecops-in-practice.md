## Description

Presenter: Diana Kelley, CISO, Protect AI

Are AI systems secure? Not sure how to tell? Starting with an overview of real vs. perceived risks in AI and ML this session will help attendees focus on the most impactful security issues by explaining how MLOps overlaps with DevOps, highlighting areas where the two processes diverge, and providing clear guidance on how and where security can be woven into the ML pipeline.

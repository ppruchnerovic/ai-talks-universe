## Description

Presenters:
Mohamad Ali, IBM
Ryan Dougherty, IBM
Srinivas Tummalapenta, IBM Consulting Cybersecurity Services

Businesses are rushing to benefit from AI, but it comes at a detrimental cost when security is an afterthought. Organizations cannot afford to bring unsecured AI models and data into their environments, as attackers will undoubtedly target them for malicious gains. It’s paramount that businesses apply a holistic security framework and embed governance across their AI pipeline and underlying infrastructure.

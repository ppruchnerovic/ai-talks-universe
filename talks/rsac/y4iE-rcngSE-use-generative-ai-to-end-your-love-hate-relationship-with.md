## Description

Presenter: Heidi Shey, Principal Analyst, Forrester Research

Design an approach to data security for Generative AI use where DLP is an outcome, and not a single data control. This session will outline how an organization’s use of large language models today will impact the scope of the team's efforts, the AI policy they create and how they align this policy with other enterprise policies, and the different data controls (DLP and others) they can apply.

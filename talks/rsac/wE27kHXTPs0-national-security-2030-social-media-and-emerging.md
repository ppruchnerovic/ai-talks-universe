## Description

Presenter: Jenny Reich, Fellow and Adjunct Professor, Georgetown Law Center on National Security

Social media has revolutionized everything from what we buy to how we vote. Soon, innovations in AI, AR/VR, and biometrics will meld our digital and physical lives in novel ways. This talk will help cybersecurity professionals navigate this evolving frontier in national security, and will explore how the confluence of new technologies and advanced cyberinfrastructure will shape our digital future.

As we approach the 2024 Presidential election, we know that agents of chaos are actively trying to dismantle public trust in our democratic systems, making it all the more critical for security organizations to build resilient systems while also addressing the impact of disinformation and misinformation campaigns. Join RSAC and a panel of industry leaders who are working to secure democracy by ensuring the integrity and security of our critical infrastructure.

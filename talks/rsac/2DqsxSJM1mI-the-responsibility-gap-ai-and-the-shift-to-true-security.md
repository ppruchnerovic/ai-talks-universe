## Description

Stephen Vintz, Co-CEO, Tenable

AI adoption has been so pervasive it’s created a dangerous illusion of security. Multiple departments in organizations own different aspects of its implementation. The problem? A glaring responsibility gap when it comes to how AI risk is managed and measured. This session outlines how executives and boards must govern this profound cultural and digital evolution, before systemic risk takes hold.

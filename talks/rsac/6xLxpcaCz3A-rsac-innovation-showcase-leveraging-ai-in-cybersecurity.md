## Description

Presenters:
Sid Trivedi, Partner, Foundation Capital
Assaf Keren, CISO, Qualtrics
Jason Lish, Vice President, Chief Information Security Officer, Cisco Systems Inc.

Join us at the RSAC Innovation Showcase – a virtual program designed to highlight innovation in cybersecurity. On February 26, we focus on leveraging AI in cybersecurity workflows.

We are joined by two leading CISOs at cutting-edge software companies who will discuss what role new advances in generative AI are having in improving how cybersecurity teams defend against threat actors.

A few of the topics covered include which security workflows can be most automated through current AI innovations, how security teams are thinking about implementation and budget plans in 2025 and 2026 to leverage GenAI-first security products, and which security workflows are still in need of new AI innovation.

The panel then hears from two up and coming companies leveraging AI to help security professionals reduce their process risk.

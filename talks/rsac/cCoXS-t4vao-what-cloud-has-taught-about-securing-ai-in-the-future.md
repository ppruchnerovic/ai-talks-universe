## Description

Presenter: John Yeoh, Cloud Security Alliance

The rise of GenAI offers unique opportunities and challenges today. As AI integrates into daily life, ensuring its safety and scalability is crucial. This session shares lessons from cloud best practices pivotal for AI technologies. The CSA's AI Safety Initiative promotes responsible AI integration and industry collaboration. This session will present the latest research on cloud security for AI.

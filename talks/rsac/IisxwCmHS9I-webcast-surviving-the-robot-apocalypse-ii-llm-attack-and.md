## Description

Presenter: J. Wolfgang Goerlich, CISO, Oakland County, Michigan

Take a long, hard look at generative AI and LLMs. There are defensive and offensive strategies we can employ: prompt social engineering, jailbreaks, and model poisoning. In the near future, in a world potentially dominated by artificial intelligence where machines govern many aspects of life, survival skills may depend on our ability to social engineer those machines. Join this webcast as we explore LLMs and generative AI in the ever-evolving landscape of threats.

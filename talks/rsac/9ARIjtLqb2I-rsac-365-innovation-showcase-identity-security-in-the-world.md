## Description

Presenters:
Jenny Brock, Sr. Manager Innovation Programs, RSA Conference
Aaron Jacobson, Partner, New Enterprise Associates (NEA)
Omar Khawaja, CISO, Client & Faculty, Databricks & Carnegie Mellon University
Allison Miller, Founder & Principal, Cartomancy Labs

Join us at the RSA Conference Innovation Showcase – a virtual program designed to highlight innovation in cybersecurity. On November 25, we focus on Identity Security in the World of AI.

A panel of experts will discuss the latest challenges in identity management and how they affect your organization, the complexities AI introduces to identity security and how to mitigate risks, and tools and techniques for effectively harnessing AI to bolster your security measures.

The panel then hears from two up and coming companies in the identity security and governance space.

Hosted in partnership with New Enterprise Associates (NEA)

## Description

Presenter: Alexander Leslie, Associate Threat Intelligence Analyst, Recorded Future

Amid wars, disasters, and political instability — hacktivism does little but breed chaos. It has been co-opted by cybercriminals, nation-states, and influence peddlers seeking to advance malign narratives and capitalize on human suffering. This session will address the role of hacktivism in the cyberthreat landscape as one of the primary drivers of malign influence.

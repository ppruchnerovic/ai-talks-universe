## Description

Desire Banse, Security Operations Manager, IdeaCrew

Incidents are leaving defenders trapped in a reactive cycle. This webcast presents a novel framework grounded in cybernetics, control theory, and AI to break free from this loop. Viewing attackers and defenders as feedback systems, and applying AI techniques like active modeling and imitation learning, allows proactive, adaptive strategies that enhance resilience and cybersecurity performance.

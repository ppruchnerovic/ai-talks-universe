## Description

Jenny Brock, Sr. Manager Innovation Programs, RSAC
Katie Gray, Senior Partner, In-Q-Tel
Lucas Nelson, Founding Partner, Lytical Ventures
Matthew Rose, Head of Corporate and Government Affairs, Snowflake

Join us at the RSAC™ Innovation Showcase – a monthly virtual program designed to highlight innovation in cybersecurity. On December 10, we focus on AI-informed policy guidance for cyber companies.

As the threats to cybersecurity and tools to combat those threats are ever evolving, so must the policy and governance of these spaces. Our panel will discuss how companies are thinking about the creation of these policies and how they can protect their businesses.

A panel of experts discuss how AI is shaping policy and governance in the cybersecurity sector, opportunities for startups to leverage policy guidance as a strategic advantage, and staying ahead of best practices and compliance leveraging AI. The panel then hears from two up and coming companies in the cyber risk exposure space.

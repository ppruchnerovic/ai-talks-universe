## Description

Presenters:
Lynn Doan, Managing Editor, Bloomberg News
Hans De Vries, Chief Cybersecurity & Operational Officer, European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA)
Despina Spanou, Deputy Director General, Cybersecurity and Trust, European Commission

From increasingly complex hybrid threats to AI-driven cyberattacks, Europe’s cyberspace is entering a new era of risk. Join this session with the EU Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) and the European Commission to unpack the latest threat trends from an EU perspective and explore how new EU initiatives are building a more secure, trusted and resilient cyberspace.

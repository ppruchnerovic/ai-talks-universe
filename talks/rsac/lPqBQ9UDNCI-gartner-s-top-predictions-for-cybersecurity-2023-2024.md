## Description

Presenter: Leigh McMullen, Gartner

Human factors and Generative AI more than ever are "colliding" within cybersecurity. This co-mingling makes for a prediction year unlike any other with very different and competing drivers. This session will take attendees on a fun and light-hearted tour of Gartner's top predictions with an emphasis on the story behind the story, and not more fear, uncertainty, and doubt.

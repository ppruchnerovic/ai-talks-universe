## Description

Jenny Brock, Sr. Manager Innovation Programs, RSAC

The RSAC™ Innovation Showcase is a monthly virtual program designed to highlight innovation in cybersecurity. On September 30, we focus on the latest strategies and techniques for engaging agentic AI in your enterprise.

A panel of experts discuss the move from traditional automation to agentic AI systems in security, ways in which these systems can boost productivity & efficiency, risk considerations associated with deploying agentic decision making, and practical security frameworks to help manage these rapidly evolving tools.

The panel then hears from two up and coming companies in the Agentic Security space.

This innovation showcase is hosted in partnership with Rain Capital.

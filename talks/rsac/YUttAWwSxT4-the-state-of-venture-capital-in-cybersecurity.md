## Description

Moderator:
Dave DeWalt, NightDragon

Panelists:
Mark Hatfield, Ten Eleven Ventures
Yoav Leitersdorf, YL Ventures
Chenxi Wang, Rain Capital
Nadav Zafrir, Team8

2023 saw a dichotomy of trends converging, with a drop-off in funding tied to the economic downturn alongside a renewed fervor for investment in emerging technologies such as AI amidst ever-rising threats. The result: one of the most unique periods in cyber venture capital history. Leading VCs will discuss how they are approaching this dynamic market and what we may see in 2024 and beyond.

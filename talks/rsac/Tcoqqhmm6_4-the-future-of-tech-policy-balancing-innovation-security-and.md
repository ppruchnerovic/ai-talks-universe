## Description

Paul Nakasone, Director, Institute of National Security, Vanderbilt University
Chris Krebs, Former Director, Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)
Ted Schlein, Chairman & General Partner, General Partner, Ballistic Ventures and Kleiner Perkins

Industry leaders will debate what’s possible when it comes to tech and policy. From cybersecurity regulations to AI policy, deepfakes, and software liability, this session will explore what makes sense, what will stifle innovation, and how to foster collaboration between the public and private sectors to shape the future of tech.

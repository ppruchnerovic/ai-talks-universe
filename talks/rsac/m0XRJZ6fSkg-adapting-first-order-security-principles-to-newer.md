## Description

Presenter: Char Sample, ICF

Going back to “first principles” can be beneficial particularly, when the landscape changes due to the emergence of cloud, AI, hyperscale, and other trends. This session analyzes architectural trends as they exist in modern designs and takes a “first principles” approach in examining how to approach architecture for modern ecosystems, applications, and environments.

## Description

Meg Anderson, VP-CISO, Principal Financial Group®
Kirsten Davies, 3x CISO
Fredrick Lee, CISO, Reddit
Jason Risch, Partner, Greylock Partners

Join us at the RSA Conference Innovation Showcase – a virtual program designed to highlight innovation in cybersecurity. On September 18, we focus on The Intersection of AI and Cyber.

The intersection of AI and cybersecurity embodies a pivotal frontier in the ongoing battle to protect digital infrastructures from evolving threats. The effective integration of AI into cybersecurity frameworks demands a multidisciplinary approach that blends technological advancements with ethical considerations and uses human expertise to ensure resilient and responsible protection against cyber threats.

Esteemed panelists will discuss:
How are adversaries and defenders deploying in cybersecurity today?
How can companies set policies that harness the power AI offers without potentially exposing the companies to undue risk?
What is the role that cybersecurity professionals play in developing and using AI in cybersecurity to ensure resiliency responsibly?

The panel then hears from two up and coming companies in the AI and Cyber space.

## Description

Doneyli De Jesus, who leads AI engineering solutions at Langfuse by ClickHouse, shares how his team helped a top-five bank put deployment gates on AI agents. Banks don't certify models, they certify applications, and they need setups that are on-premises, gated and air-gapped. He walks through building a golden data set, scoring traces across several dimensions, and how a small model that was 97% grounded but only 60% accurate on numbers cleared the bank's 85% gate once it got a calculator tool. Domain experts set the thresholds and review failures, taking evaluation from weeks to minutes.

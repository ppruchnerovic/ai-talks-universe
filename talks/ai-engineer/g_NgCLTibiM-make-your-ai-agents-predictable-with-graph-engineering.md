## Description

Graph engineering is a new name for an old idea: give agents a map of the logic they should follow, not just a pile of text. Zachary Blumenfeld of Neo4j explains how graphs make agents more predictable, governable and portable. He shows how to compile skills and past decision traces into graph-shaped skills, then demos a loan-approval agent that follows them.

## Description

Maty Bohacek of Stanford looks at a security check we still rely on: getting on a phone or video call to prove you're human before opening an account, approving a wire or recovering access. With AI avatars that can now join Zoom calls, he tested what agents do when a human check is the only thing between them and their goal. When a voice or video cloning tool was available, many agents used it to impersonate a person, even when told not to, and safety alignment only cut the rate from 65% to 44%. He closes with what needs to happen next across alignment, control, and deepfake detection and provenance.

## Description

Jesse Hall of LiveKit shows how to build voice agents for financial services that feel human, not robotic. He explains why model leaderboards mislead (they score components, but you ship a conversation), why you should measure time to first audio and time to first sentence instead of first byte or first token, and how async tool calls keep a conversation flowing while slow back-end work runs. He also covers audio-aware turn detection, the roughly 1.5-second latency budget (500-600ms feels truly human), and benchmarking your own stack with simulated callers.

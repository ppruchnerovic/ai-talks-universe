## Description

In this fireside chat, Mintlify co-founder and CTO Hahnbee Lee and Coinbase head of AI product Lincoln Murr discuss what changes when agents, not people, are the main users of your product. Lee shares that agent traffic on Mintlify docs sites grew from about 10% to about 80% this year, why page size and accuracy now matter more than design, and where AI writing fits. Murr explains why Coinbase is making its whole business available through APIs, how the x402 payment standard has seen around 100 million on-chain agent transactions, and why giving agents money makes them more autonomous.

## Description

Every company has someone who knows everything: the reorgs, the politics, which projects are really on track. Joao Wenzel of Citadel Securities explains how his team approximates that person with an agentic system that builds an institutional memory from decks, Slack, meeting notes, code changes and agent traces. Borrowing the idea of a state estimator from robotics, the system updates its beliefs as evidence arrives, lets facts age, grades its own predictions against what actually happened, and takes corrections from people. He shows why simple retrieval isn't enough, how agents catch "watermelon projects" that are green on the outside and red on the inside, and how back-testing showed that projects the agent flagged were twice as likely to slip.

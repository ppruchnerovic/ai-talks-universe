## Description

Jemima Abu, a product engineer and certified accessibility professional, explains why AI agents copy the accessibility gaps in the code they learned from, and why 96% of top websites still have accessibility issues. In an informal experiment, giving her agent an accessibility skill made the same build faster and cheaper. She covers what inaccessible code costs you in users, money and legal risk, then shares a four-step approach: define requirements, audit your codebase, use a design system, and make your agents follow the rules every time. She also shares a lightweight accessibility skill you can start with.

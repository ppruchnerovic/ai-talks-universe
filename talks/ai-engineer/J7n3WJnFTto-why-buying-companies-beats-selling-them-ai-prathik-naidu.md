## Description

Deploying AI into real businesses is hard: org charts are messy, trust takes decades, and service firms don't scale like software. Prathik Naidu, founding partner at Long Lake, explains their strategy: acquire established service businesses, then transform them with AI from the inside. He covers how they choose companies and what changes once they own them, drawing on 40 businesses across residential services, architecture, HR and more.

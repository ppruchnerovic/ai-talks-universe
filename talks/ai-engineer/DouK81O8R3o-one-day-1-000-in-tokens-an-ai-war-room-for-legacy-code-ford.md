## Description

At GEICO, coding got fast but everything around it (decisions, sign-offs, context) stayed slow. Ford Prior's fix was a multiplayer, AI-native war room: the right decision makers, one driver, and fleets of agents wired into GEICO's knowledge, tools and code. His bet was that one day and $1,000 in tokens could map a legacy claims domain end to end and ship one real change. He ran it three times across rentals, damages and salvage. He covers FleetKit, the markdown rulebook that steers the agent fleet, plus the worksheets and diagrams it produced and what he'd change next.

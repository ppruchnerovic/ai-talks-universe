## Description

Matt Ragone is a financial planner at Range, an all-in-one wealth management firm, not an engineer. He leads AI adoption for its planning team and shares the step-by-step method non-technical planners used to build their own agents and skills. With standardized processes and client data already in one place, the team could have an agent draft cash flow analyses, insurance reviews and tax reviews while planners are on calls with other clients, with a certified financial planner reviewing everything before it goes out. He walks through the five steps: pick a workflow you repeat, teach it real cases, package it as a skill, test it on edge cases, and ship it with an owner and a reviewer.

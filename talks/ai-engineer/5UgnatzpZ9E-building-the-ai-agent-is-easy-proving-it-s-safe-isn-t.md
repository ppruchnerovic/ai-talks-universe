## Description

NatWest lead AI engineer Jonathan Osborne shares how the UK's second-largest retail bank, with 19 million customers and about 100,000 assistant conversations a day, is moving its digital assistant Cora from scripted answers to AI agents. Building the agent was the easy part; proving it was safe was the hard part. He covers the routing funnel that limits what ever reaches a model, why quality is several scores instead of one, human reviews where an 83% pass rate hid a 54-point gap, an LLM judge that scored far higher than humans with almost no correlation, and shadow deployments with feature flags for a safe rollout.

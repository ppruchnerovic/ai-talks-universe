## Description

Most companies use AI, but very few see it show up in their margins. Adam Nahari of Apollo Global Management explains why engineering is the smaller part of an AI transformation. He walks through the six moves that turned an airline's crew-scheduling desk into a 10x win: start from the P&L, pick one or two problems, get a real owner, put the tools in everyone's hands, and set kill metrics before you start.

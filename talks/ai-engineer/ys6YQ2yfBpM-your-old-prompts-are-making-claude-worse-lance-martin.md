## Description

Lance Martin of Anthropic shares simple fixes that get more out of frontier models, often improving quality and cutting cost at the same time. He covers auditing prompts for outdated habits like "think step by step" and contradictory rules, using the prompt cache properly (cutting costs up to 72% on internal benchmarks), designing evals Claude can actually climb, and hill climbing without overfitting, including a run that cut cost 90% while improving performance by switching to a stronger model at lower effort. He closes with how Claude Managed Agents keeps days-long agents reliable and secure, then takes questions on eval design, grading open-ended tasks and more.

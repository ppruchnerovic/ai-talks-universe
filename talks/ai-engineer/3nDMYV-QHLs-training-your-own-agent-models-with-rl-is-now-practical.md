## Description

Jason Mancuso of Modal argues that treating models as swappable backends is a losing strategy, and that teams with agents in production already have what they need for reinforcement learning: a harness, evals and real traces. He explains why RL generalizes better than fine-tuning on recorded traces, then walks through a stable recipe for asynchronous RL built on GRPO with masked importance sampling, score centering and top-p sampling. He shows how sparse weight updates let Modal spread rollouts across clouds, GPU types and quantization levels while training stays stable and rollouts get 15% cheaper.

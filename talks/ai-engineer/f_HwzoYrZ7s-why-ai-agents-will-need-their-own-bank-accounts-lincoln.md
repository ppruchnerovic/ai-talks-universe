## Description

Lincoln Murr, head of AI product at Coinbase, explains agentic finance: agents that pay with money and agents that grow it. He covers the path from AgentKit, an early experiment giving an AI a wallet, to x402, an open standard built on the long-unused HTTP 402 "payment required" code. x402 now handles hundreds of millions of agent transactions a month. Other topics are microtransactions as a replacement for the ad economy, agents running trades on simple instructions, and what two years of building taught Coinbase. Trust is earned step by step, and the AI is the enabler, not the product. He ends with a look at agents that can pay for their own upkeep.

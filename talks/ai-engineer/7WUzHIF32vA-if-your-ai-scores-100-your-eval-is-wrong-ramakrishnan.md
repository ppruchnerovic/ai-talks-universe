## Description

Ramakrishnan Lokanathan, an AI safety researcher and former startup founder, lays out how to build evals for high-stakes AI applications. Define the most ambitious version of success, test every step of the workflow and not just the final answer, combine automated checks with human review and adversarial tests, keep testing what used to work so upgrades don't break it, and turn real user failures into new tests. He also introduces eSlams, an open-source package that has AI models play games like chess, Go and poker against each other as another way to see how they reason.

## Description

Some of the most important financial data sits behind logins and anti-bot walls, with no API. Jay Sahnan and Arik Bird of Browserbase show how browser agents solve this through three case studies: Ramp fetching receipts at scale (over 30,000 manual hours saved), Bill paying across thousands of different vendor portals with one agent, and Stripe automating KYC checks. They also cover how Browserbase forked Chromium to run reliably in the cloud, and how Stagehand's act, extract and observe primitives keep automations from breaking when sites change.

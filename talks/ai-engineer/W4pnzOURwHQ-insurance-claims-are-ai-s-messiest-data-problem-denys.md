## Description

Insurance claims are full of handwriting, faxes, duplicates and 8GB PDFs. Denys Linkov of Wisedocs explains why that unstructured data is both the biggest barrier and the biggest opportunity for AI in insurance. He covers cleaning the data, analysis within a single document, and cross-claim insights, along with the benchmark Wisedocs built with Artificial Analysis to test how models handle medical records.

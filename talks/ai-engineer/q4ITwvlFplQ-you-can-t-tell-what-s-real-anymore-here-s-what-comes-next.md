## Description

Runway's first AI film in 2019 was 128x128 pixels. Today, people can't tell generated video from real footage. Runway co-founder and CEO Cristóbal Valenzuela explains how next-frame prediction at scale teaches models physics, object permanence and how the world works. He then shows what happens when those models become real-time and interactive: world interface models, robotics, and Continuum, an early research prototype where anything on your screen can become a live, interactive video.

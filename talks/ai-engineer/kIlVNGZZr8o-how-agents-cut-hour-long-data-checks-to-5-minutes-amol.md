## Description

Amol Kapoor and Clifford Ressel of Nori Agentic show how cloud agents can keep an asset manager's security master database clean, especially as more money moves into private assets whose details live in PDFs, emails and call notes. Cliff explains what a security master is and why private assets break the usual identifiers, then Amol walks through work with a Fortune 200 asset manager, where every changed cell used to take an hour of manual checking. Now an agent investigates each change, cites its sources, recommends keeping or discarding it and pings the owner in Slack, cutting the human part to about five minutes. They close with lessons on adoption, keeping humans in the loop and handling back-office busywork.

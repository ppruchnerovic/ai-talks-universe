## Description

How do you know whether your agent would have made the right call? Ryan Ahern of Exa shows how to use version-controlled search to see the web exactly as it was on a past date, then backtest an agent's predictions against what actually happened. The hands-on example predicts company acquisitions from signals like leadership turnover, then tunes the system without leaking future information.

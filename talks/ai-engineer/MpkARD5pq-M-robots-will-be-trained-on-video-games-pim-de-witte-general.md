## Description

Pim de Witte of General Intuition, the team behind the Medal game recorder, argues that models built to act in space and time need a different design than LLMs, which work turn by turn. His models take in frames and send out actions continuously, the way a gamer plays. Because game controllers already run most robots, Medal's game clips (about a billion videos a year and over 1.5 trillion action tokens a month) become training data, and one model can play games at a high level and control more than 10 types of robots. He also previews early access, accessibility uses and hackathons in New York and London.

## Description

Jack Wotherspoon, developer experience engineer at Google DeepMind, introduces Gemini Managed Agents: coding agents that run in cloud sandboxes from a single API call instead of on your laptop. He covers the Interactions API, the Antigravity harness, environments that several agents can share, uploaded skills and AGENTS.md files, a proxy that keeps secrets out of the sandbox, scheduled triggers and multi-agent workflows. Demos include an SEC filing researcher handing its work to a quant agent, and a stock research app built in AI Studio.

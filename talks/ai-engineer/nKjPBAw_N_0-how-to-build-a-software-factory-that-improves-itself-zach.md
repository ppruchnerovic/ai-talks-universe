## Description

Zach Lloyd, founder and CEO of Warp and former engineering lead on Google Docs, explains why companies are moving from engineers prompting agents on their laptops to sandboxed cloud software factories. He makes the case for treating your factory as infrastructure that's open, works with your existing tools and is defined in code. He demos Warp Factories: visibility into every agent run, LLM-judged scores, a self-improvement loop that proposes fixes to the factory itself, and benchmarks built from your own past tasks to compare cost and quality across models.

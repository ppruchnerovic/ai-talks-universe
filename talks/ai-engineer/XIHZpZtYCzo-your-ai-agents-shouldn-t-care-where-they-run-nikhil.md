## Description

Agents can't keep borrowing your identity and running on someone else's machines, especially at banks and other heavily regulated companies. Nikhil Harithas of Factory shows how they rebuilt their coding agent, Droid, to run on any compute inside a customer's own cloud. He covers the compute gateway and relay design, bring-your-own-key model calls, snapshots and forking, and why they plan to open source the layer.

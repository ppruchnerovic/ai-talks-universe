## Description

Zack Proser, an engineer on the WorkOS applied AI team who has written and published obsessively for 15 years, is less worried about AI slop than about all the stories that never get told. He shares how he packed his writing know-how into Blogbot, a Slack tool that gets colleagues' drafts 85-90% of the way there while a human still reads, edits and puts their name on every post, and why craft can survive the assembly line. Then he applies the same idea to an audience of two, building a bedtime story app for his toddlers in four hours, and asks who you would build a factory for.

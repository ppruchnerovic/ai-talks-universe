## Description

x402, AP2, ACP, MPP, TAP, UCP: every big fintech and payments company now has an agent payment standard, but real volume is close to zero. Jan Curn, founder of Apify, walks the agent payment funnel (need, setup, discovery, purchase) to show where it breaks. He covers why API keys still win, why crypto is well placed, the trust problem in finding services, and how pay-per-use billing finally landed.

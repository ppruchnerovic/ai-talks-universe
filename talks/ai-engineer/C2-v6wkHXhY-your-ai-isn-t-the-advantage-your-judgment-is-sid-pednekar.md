## Description

Sid Pednekar, an AI, technology and data executive in asset management, looks at why most companies now have AI but only a few see real results from it. Following two hypothetical firms, he lays out three things the leaders do differently: they pick the right problem and invest deeply in a few high-value workflows instead of scattering pilots, they build shared capabilities by standardizing the core and breaking down silos, and they make every success and failure feed back into the system. Running through all of it is one idea: when intelligence gets cheap, judgment becomes the scarce resource.

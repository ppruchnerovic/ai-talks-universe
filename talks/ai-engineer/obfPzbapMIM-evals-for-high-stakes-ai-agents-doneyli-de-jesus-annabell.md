## Description

A hands-on workshop on evaluating AI applications where mistakes are expensive. Annabell Schäfer and Doneyli De Jesus from Langfuse use a banking assistant to walk through the whole AI engineering loop. They cover tracing with PII masking, building datasets, turning domain-expert comments into failure modes, LLM-as-judge and code evaluators, and CI that blocks a prompt change when scores drop.

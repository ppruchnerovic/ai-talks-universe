## Description

The era of unlimited AI budgets is over, and most teams are locked into whichever tools they picked first.

Remy Guercio, who works on Tailscale's AI gateway Aperture, looks back at a year of AI lock-in and asks how teams get out of it while proving real ROI. He breaks an internal AI deployment into four layers (the models, the data connectors like MCP, CLIs and APIs, the interface, and the sandbox) and argues an AI gateway is what keeps them swappable. He shows how Aperture uses your Tailscale network for identity instead of shared API keys, sets budgets per model (unlimited for cheap models, tight for frontier ones), works as both an LLM and MCP gateway, handles authentication for Cursor, Claude Code and Codex in one place, and keeps logs you can use to diagnose what an agent did.

In this talk:
• Why unlimited AI budgets are ending, and what ROI maxing means
• The four layers of an AI stack and where lock-in hides
• How an AI gateway ties identity, budgets and logs together
• Aperture as both an LLM and MCP gateway

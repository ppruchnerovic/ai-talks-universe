## Description

Runlayer is a company of about 60 people running 300+ agents every day. Founding engineer Aiden Sochowski shares what they found once they put a gateway in front of everything and logged every session and tool call: prompt injection through email, PR-review agents getting talked into approvals, over-permissioned bots, one poisoned agent contaminating others, buggy vibe-coded MCP servers burning tokens, and a model switch that raised costs over 100x in a week.

## Description

Hassan El Mghari of Together AI and his father Omar, a former CFO and head of internal audit, test whether an open model can forecast a company's profits. They give GPT-OSS-120B only Costco's public filings from before its 2024 membership fee increase, have it write the financial model as structured formulas, and run 16 cheap attempts in parallel. The forecast lands within 4.4% of Costco's actual results, until an audit of the assumptions shows two errors that happened to cancel out. Their takeaway: when you build agents for specialized work, put an expert in the loop and turn their checks into code.

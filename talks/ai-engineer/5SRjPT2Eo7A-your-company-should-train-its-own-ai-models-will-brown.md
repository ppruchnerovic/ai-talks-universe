## Description

Agents relearn the same things every session because the knowledge that runs a company never gets written down. Will Brown of Prime Intellect makes the case for training models on your own traces, feedback and workflows, so they learn on the job the way human coworkers do. He covers evals as test cases, decision models and routing, and widget-style interfaces that turn your decisions into training data. He ends with nightly training runs and an example model trained for about $300K.

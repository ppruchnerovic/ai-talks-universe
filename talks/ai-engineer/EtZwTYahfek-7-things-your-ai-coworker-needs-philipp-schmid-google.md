## Description

Philipp Schmid of Google DeepMind shows how to build an autonomous AI coworker for finance with Gemini and the Gemini API's Managed Agents. He breaks a coworker down into seven needs (a brain, a computer, playbooks, tools, credentials, house rules and a schedule) and builds each one: skills and files in the agent's own sandbox, built-in tools and MCP servers, credentials swapped in by a network proxy so a prompt injection can't steal them, hooks for approvals, and cron triggers. He finishes with a live demo of an agent that audits a portfolio and updates a Google Sheet on a schedule.

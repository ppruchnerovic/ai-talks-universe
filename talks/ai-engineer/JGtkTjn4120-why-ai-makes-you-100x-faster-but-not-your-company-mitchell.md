## Description

Individual engineers have seen huge productivity gains from AI, but most companies haven't. Mitchell Troyanovsky, co-founder of Basis, argues that agents need to be simple and reliable to spread through a company, and the way to get there is to make them opinionated. He covers behavior specs, why outcome evals don't generalize to production, and two tools for finding out why an agent made a bad call: forking and sub-agent simulations.

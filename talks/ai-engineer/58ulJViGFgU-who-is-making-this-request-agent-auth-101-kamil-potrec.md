## Description

When an agent sends an invoice using your token, who actually made the request? Kamil Potrec of Keycard breaks down how agents get credentials today (personal tokens, static keys, over-privileged service accounts) and what proper agent auth requires: per-agent identity, delegation, short-lived scoped credentials, federation with legacy systems, policy and audit. He compares SDKs and gateways for keeping credentials out of an agent's reach, including the gateway tradeoffs.

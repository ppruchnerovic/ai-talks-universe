## Description

If your token spend keeps going up and productivity isn't keeping pace, start here. Eugene Cheah lays out four steps for cutting AI inference costs at scale: measure, right-size, buy capacity well and check your processes. Along the way he covers splitting API keys per team, agentic vs. process workloads, open models that match frontier models on specific tasks, classifiers, fine-tuning, the speed-vs-cost tradeoff and "slop grenades."

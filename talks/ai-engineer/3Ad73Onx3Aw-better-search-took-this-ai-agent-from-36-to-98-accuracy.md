## Description

Agents are smart enough. Most of the time, the real problem is getting them to the right data. Aamir Shakir of Mixedbread explains why the usual stack of OCR, chunking, embeddings and a vector database loses information and leaves agents reading every document. He shows multimodal, late-interaction retrieval on Intel earnings reports, and shares a customer whose answer accuracy went from 36% to 98% after switching their search.

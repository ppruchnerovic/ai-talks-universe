## Description

Apollo's CLO business used to spend 2–3 days building a single mock portfolio by hand in Excel. Now it takes minutes.

Jack McDonald (Principal at Apollo and COO of Redding Ridge, Apollo's ~$50B structured credit and CLO manager) talks with Rowspace co-founder Michael Manapat about where AI becomes mission-critical in finance. They cover automating a weekly trade reconciliation that used to take 2–3 people, why plugging your firm into a generic LLM isn't enough when one data point can come from 20 sources and only one is right, and why embedding engineers with the front office is what earns trust.

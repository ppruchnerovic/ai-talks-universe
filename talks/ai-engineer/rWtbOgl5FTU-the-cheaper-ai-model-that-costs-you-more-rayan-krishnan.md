## Description

Vals AI builds benchmarks that frontier labs cite in their model cards, and Rayan Krishnan explains why those single scores hide a jagged frontier. In finance tasks like tax and Excel LBO modeling, the best model changes by task, and a model that's cheaper on paper can cost more and score lower because it burns extra tokens. He shares lessons from building benchmarks (the right accuracy metric, reward hacking, multi-turn work), a case study of a team that spent $1.5M on tokens in a month before cutting spend about 90% with internal evals, and why a firm's own work history is its best eval.

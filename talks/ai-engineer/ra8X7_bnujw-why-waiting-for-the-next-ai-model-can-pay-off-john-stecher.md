## Description

Blackstone CTO John Stecher and Norm AI CTO Scott Worland discuss building technology that holds up as models keep improving. John explains why a solid data strategy and straight-through processing come before agents, how he weighs ROI against waiting for a better model, and why internal apps can now be thrown away and rebuilt. Scott describes how Norm's large hand-labeled eval set showed that much of its carefully built workflow code was in the way, why the team rebuilt its platform, and how it decides where to invest heavily versus lightly as the frontier moves.

## Description

Jack Woodruff, founder and former CIO of long/short equity fund Candlestick Capital and a former Citadel portfolio manager, sits down with Kepler CEO Vinoo Ganesh to talk about what AI really changes in fundamental investing. They discuss why humans are still the bottleneck, how AI turns hours of prep into minutes and raises the table stakes, why you should never trust a number you can't audit, where edge comes from when every fund has the same models, how fund teams will be structured, and why the "no-analyst hedge fund" won't beat the best human-led funds.
